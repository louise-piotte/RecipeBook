## Recommendation

Propose identity-only **pa-kimchi**, a named Korean green-onion kimchi type. Korea.net describes it as a scallion-based named variety, and a Korea.net/World Institute of Kimchi recipe identifies pa kimchi as green-onion kimchi. Keep it separate from generic kimchi and from other named kimchi types.

Use English display **pa-kimchi** with the single source-supported English alias **green onion kimchi**. Use the provisional fr-CA display **pa-kimchi (kimchi à l’oignon vert)**, with no French alias. The parenthetical is a descriptive construction using the OQLF-preferred general term oignon vert; it is not an OQLF-established term for this subtype. The Korean sources do not settle the exact Allium species, so do not attach a botanical species or turn ciboule, chives, or échalote into aliases.

The sources support a named prepared-food identity, not a fixed formula, preparation form, or universal measure. Do not add measurements, densities, unit mappings, conversions, yields, recipe links, or substitution rules.

## Identity and bilingual boundary

Korea.net’s kimchi article names pa-kimchi as a scallion-based type among distinct named kimchi varieties. A Republic of Korea K-Info Hub article likewise identifies pa kimchi as green-onion kimchi, and Korea.net’s 2021 recipe spells out pa (green onion) kimchi. These sources support the named identity and the narrow English alias; they do not establish a standard recipe or ingredient ratio.

For French display, OQLF supports oignon vert as the preferred Québec term for the general vegetable. Its entry also explains that ordinary Québec use can cover either immature onion or ciboule and warns of overlap with échalote. The ingredient sources do not specify the botanical species. Keep the borrowed name pa-kimchi first, use the descriptive parenthetical kimchi à l’oignon vert provisionally, and add no French alias, ciboule claim, chive claim, or Latin binomial.

The existing OQLF kimchi source supports only the parent kimchi name and its listed spellings. It does not standardize a French name for pa-kimchi. Preserve its source ID and existing record ownership without treating it as subtype evidence.
