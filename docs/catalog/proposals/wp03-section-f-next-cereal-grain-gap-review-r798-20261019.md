## Candidate and evidence boundary

The bounded candidate is a ready-to-eat cereal listing titled **Post Great Grains Cereal Nut Medley**, indexed as a 385 g product by Longo’s through Uber Eats Canada [S04]. It is not a review of generic granola, muesli, nuts, or the active Cranberry Almond Crunch cereal. Keep any future Great Grains product separate from component ingredients and from other finished cereals.

Post Foods Canada’s 2025 annual report says the Niagara Falls plant produces cereals including Great Grains cereal [S02]. This is current primary evidence of Canadian Great Grains production at the brand level, but it does not give a product roster, identify Nut Medley, establish that this specific SKU is current, or give its formulation. The current Post Consumer Brands Canada brand directory lists its displayed Canadian brands without Great Grains [S01]. That omission is a directory observation, not proof that the product was discontinued or that retailers do not carry imports.

The current Post Consumer Brands Great Grains page on the company’s U.S. site describes a finished ready-to-eat cereal family and lists six variants: Banana Nut Crunch, Blueberry Nut Crunch, Cranberry Almond Crunch, Crunchy Pecan, Raisins, Dates & Pecans, and Red Berry Almond Crunch [S03]. It does not list Nut Medley. Because this is the U.S. brand page, its roster cannot settle the separate Canadian product listing or establish a Canadian French name. Do not equate Nut Medley with Raisins, Dates & Pecans, Crunchy Pecan, or any U.S. variant.

A Québec health guide published in 2018 includes the phrase “great grains mélange de noix” in a list of ready-to-eat cereals [S05]. This is useful historical Québec French wording, but it is not a current manufacturer title, package label, UPC-matched retailer listing, or proof that this phrase identifies the present 385 g Nut Medley item. Do not promote it to a canonical French display or alias.

The Longo’s Uber Eats Canada result supports that a product with the English Nut Medley name and 385 g presentation has been listed by a Canadian retailer [S04]. The page is location-dependent, showed “Unavailable near you” in the indexed result, and could not be opened directly because of a cache miss. It provides no French product name, ingredient declaration, UPC, current store availability, or label serving. Treat 385 g as package mass only.
