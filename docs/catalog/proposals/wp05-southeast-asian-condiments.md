## Candidate dispositions and proposed records
All entries below are proposals only. Where OQLF French is unavailable, the French descriptor is transparent/source-derived, not claimed as a standardized term. No unlisted aliases are proposed.

- Existing ingredient-ref-fish-sauce, display fish sauce / sauce de poisson: retain. Proposed aliasesFr: nuoc-mâm, nuoc-mam, nuocmam, nuoc nam, sauce au poisson, nam pla. aliasesEn: nuoc mam, nuoc nam, nuoc-nam, nam pla, patis. OQLF lists the Vietnamese/Thai names as preferred equivalents for the generic fish condiment; Philippine PNS/FDA calls fish sauce “Patis”. These aliases apply only to generic liquid fish sauce: never merge with fish-flavoured sauce, bagoong, or other fish/shrimp pastes. Review any future exact regional standards separately.
- Existing ingredient-ref-sriracha-sauce, sriracha sauce / sauce sriracha: retain. aliasesFr: sriracha. aliasesEn: sriracha, sriacha, sriacha sauce (OQLF lists the latter spelling variants). Keep separate from sambal, sweet chili sauce, and generic chili paste.
- Add bottled oyster sauce / sauce d’huître; possible OQLF spelling alias sauce d’huîtres. Scope is bottled concentrated condiment; exclude homemade sauce aux huîtres, expressly distinguished by OQLF.
- Add two distinct Indonesian identities: kecap manis (Indonesian sweet soy sauce) / kecap manis (sauce de soya sucrée indonésienne); kecap asin (Indonesian salty soy sauce) / kecap asin (sauce de soya salée indonésienne). BSN has separate sweet and salty kecap standards. Not aliases of generic soy sauce or each other.
- Existing curry paste and red curry paste stay. Add green curry paste / pâte de cari vert; yellow curry paste / pâte de cari jaune; Panang curry paste / pâte de cari Panang; Massaman curry paste / pâte de curry Massaman. Pantai collection distinguishes these products; French Massamun product and OQLF cari/curry entry inform naming. Named prepared paste identities, not aliases of generic paste/colors. Existing red ref needs review, not duplication. Pantai spells its product Massamun; alias to Massaman remains pending exact equivalence review.
- Add Thai chili paste with soya bean oil (nam prik pao) / pâte de piment thaïlandaise à l’huile de soya (nam prik pao): Pantai identifies its named oily/cooked compound product as nam prik pao. Recipe is vendor-specific; do not generalize formula.
- Add separate compounds sambal oelek / sambal oelek (pâte de piment); sambal matah / sambal matah (sauce pimentée balinaise crue); sambal bajak / sambal bajak (sauce pimentée indonésienne cuite); sambal terasi / sambal terasi (sauce pimentée à la pâte de crevettes fermentées). Huy Fong supports sambal oelek; Indonesian tourism ministry distinguishes raw matah, cooked bajak and sambal terasi. These are distinct compounds; sambal terasi is not the terasi paste. Checklist repeats belacan/terasi in two bullets; that is one underlying ingredient candidate each. “badjak” unresolved/inactive: direct official spelling found is bajak, so no badjak alias.
- Fermented-paste proposals, each separate: belacan (Malaysian shrimp paste) / belacan (pâte de crevettes fermentées malaisienne); terasi (Indonesian shrimp paste) / terasi (pâte de crevettes fermentées indonésienne); bagoong alamang (Philippine fermented shrimp paste) / bagoong alamang (pâte de crevettes fermentées philippine); bagoong isda (Philippine fermented fish paste) / bagoong isda (pâte de poisson fermentée philippine); prahok (Cambodian fermented fish paste) / prahok (pâte de poisson fermentée cambodgienne). PNS/NCBI distinguish bagoong fish/shrimp pastes from liquid patis; BSN identifies terasi udang; UPM research documents belacan as shrimp paste; Cambodian AKP identifies prahok as fermented fish. No paste aliases to fish sauce, nor aliases among these named regional pastes.
- kapi, ngapi, unspecified bagoong and other fish/shrimp ferments: unresolved/inactive. Thai FDA confirms a Thai shrimp-paste product, but the source inspected did not confirm romanized kapi; WorldFish documents ngapi variants by fish and method. Unqualified bagoong is ambiguous fish vs shrimp. Do not cross-alias with belacan/terasi.
- Add Thai sweet chili sauce / sauce thaïlandaise aux piments doux (Pantai product evidence); add peanut sauce / sauce aux arachides (Singapore National Heritage Board records satay with peanut dip). Treat as variable compound families; neither aliases sriracha nor satay paste/sauce.
- Add laksa paste / pâte de laksa (pâte d’épices pour laksa), supported by Prima Taste product. Their kit separates paste, premix and sambal. Rendang paste and satay paste unresolved/inactive: Prima calls its products complete sauce or sauce/marinade/basting-oil kits, not pastes.
- Add banana ketchup / ketchup à la banane (PNS/FDA product standard; distinct from generic ketchup); add toyomansi (soy sauce with calamansi) / toyomansi (sauce de soya à la calamansi), a compound identified by NutriAsia. Do not alias toyomansi to soy sauce or calamansi. Other calamansi juices/extracts/concentrates unresolved without exact-form product evidence.
- Add separate coconut vinegar / vinaigre de coco and cane vinegar / vinaigre de canne à sucre. NutriAsia product pages identify cane-vinegar source and spiced Sukang-Hang as spiced Sukang Tuba (coconut vinegar). Neither aliases generic vinegar; spiced vinegar is a compound. Philippine FDA vinegar guidance located was a draft and is not relied on as an active standard.
- Add kaya (coconut-and-egg jam) / kaya (confiture de lait de coco et d’œuf) as a compound spread; Singapore Roots documents egg, coconut milk, sugar and distinct pandan-infusion vs caramelized-sugar styles. Add only source-specific Kampong Speu palm sugar syrup / sirop de sucre de palme de Kampong Speu (GI spec lists syrup form), not a universal palm syrup. Kaya is not pandan or coconut syrup; green/brown styles are not aliases. Pandan extract/essence/paste unresolved: cited source establishes pandan leaf used in cooking but not a prepared extract.
- Additional Lao, Cambodian, Burmese, Indonesian, Malaysian, Filipino, Vietnamese, Thai, Singaporean, Bruneian and Timorese sweep: unresolved gap; no blanket addition or checklist completion until names/forms receive specific authoritative sources.

Category proposal: condiment, sauce, paste and sambal candidates SAUCE_AND_CONDIMENT; kaya may be that category; Kampong Speu palm sugar syrup SUGAR_AND_SWEETENER (subject to schema-owner review).
