ICAR-CCARI identifies kokum as *Garcinia indica* and lists it as a fruit/spice crop [S01]. The Indian Institute of Spices Research says the dried rind is used as a souring agent and distinguishes Kokum (*G. indica*) from Malabar tamarind (*G. cambogia*) [S02]. The Spices Board of India lists Kokam as *G. indica* and says the dried salted rind is used as a curry condiment [S05]. A Canadian product listing called “Zaika Kokum” gives the same dried-rind product boundary [S03]. These sources support a dried-rind product identity, not every fruit, juice, extract, or commercial product derived from kokum.

Use a descriptive French label, `écorce séchée de kokum`, retaining the borrowed name `kokum`. Health Canada’s French-language plant list includes `Kokum (Garcinia indica)` [reused source WP03-HI-GAP-20261011-S02], and a French-language food listing uses `kokum séché` and `amsul` for the dried rind [S04]. The latter is a French-language retailer in France, not a Québec terminology authority; no OQLF GDT entry for this culinary product was found. The display describes the product part and state without presenting it as an official term.

Keep kokum separate from Sri Lankan goraka, tamarind, Malabar tamarind, kokum butter, kokum juice, kokum syrups, Agal, and prepared beverages such as sol kadhi. ICAR-CCARI lists several of those as distinct post-harvest products [S01]. Do not use `Garcinia` or `Malabar tamarind` as aliases.

## Measurements and substitution evidence

The 200 g quantity on the Canadian listing is a package size, not a recipe portion or conversion [S03]. The reviewed sources supply no transferable density, unit mapping, portion relationship, or yield. No numeric behavior is proposed.

The French-language retailer suggests replacing tamarind with kokum in “most recipes” at roughly equivalent quantity and mentions coconut-based dishes [S04]. That broad note does not define a specific recipe, ingredient role, cooking method, preparation state, or salt adjustment. Record it as blocked substitution evidence only; do not activate a global or contextual rule from this claim.

Broader regional coverage and goraka remain open.
