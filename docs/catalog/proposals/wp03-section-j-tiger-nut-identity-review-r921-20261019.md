# R921: Generic tiger-nut identity

## Decision

Add identity-only `tiger nut / souchet comestible` for the edible tuber of *Cyperus esculentus*.
Use the TERMIUM-supported aliases `tigernut` and `chufa` in English and `amande de terre`,
`noix tigrée`, and `noix de tigre` in French. Classify it as `VEGETABLE_AND_AROMATIC`.

## Identity boundary

The bilingual TERMIUM food-industry entry directly defines the tiger nut as the edible tuber of
*C. esculentus*. PROSEA documents the species as cultivated for edible tubers eaten raw, cooked, or
roasted and distinguishes it from the related *C. rotundus*. This supports a generic ingredient
identity without treating it as a botanical nut.

Keep the whole tuber separate from the existing `tigernut flour` and `defatted tigernut flour`
references. Keep plant/weed names such as `yellow nutsedge` out of culinary aliases. Do not represent
oil, extracted beverage, roasted coffee substitute, powder, or other preparations as aliases or
forms in this identity-only update.

## Evidence and scope

- [TERMIUM Plus, TIGER NUT](https://www.btb.termiumplus.gc.ca/tpv2alpha/alpha-eng.html?i=&index=ent&lang=eng&srchtxt=TIGER+NUT): food-industry subject, edible-tuber definition, and paired English/French terms.
- [PROSEA, Plants yielding non-seed carbohydrates, *Cyperus*](https://prosea.prota4u.org/view.aspx?id=3224): species, edible-tuber use, and *C. esculentus* / *C. rotundus* boundary.
- [North Carolina State Extension Gardener Plant Toolbox, *Cyperus esculentus*](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/cyperus-esculentus/): common names and edible-tuber context. Its preparation examples are not generalized.

No source-specific measurement, recipe amount, density, conversion, yield, preparation, or
substitution is transferred. The bundled recipe corpus has no tiger-nut ingredient line. Add no
forms, measurements, recipe links, conversions, yields, or substitutions.

cereal roster clauses and named Anson Mills wheat-meal lead remain out of scope because
