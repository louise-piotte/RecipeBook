## Identity, portion, and substitution boundary

The brand is a formulated ready-to-eat corn cereal product, not a generic puffed-grain identity. Keep it separate from plain puffed corn or other puffed-grain references, peanut butter, cocoa, and any chocolate- or peanut-butter-flavoured cereal. No exact recipe ingredient wording or process makes this product interchangeable with a component. No reviewed source supports a recipe measure, household portion, conversion, yield, or replacement amount. The guide’s case pack weights and Metro’s package net weights are package context only; do not derive a serving or volume mapping from them.

The R173/R717 precedent does not resolve this hold. R173 used the Canadian manufacturer’s P101125/709 g/formula match plus Well.ca bilingual product listing to establish one named Bob’s Red Mill product even without a UPC on the retailer page. R717 separately retained a portion observation tied explicitly to Healthy Planet SKU A0063910 and marked the product match as not UPC-verified. Here the manufacturer has already specified bilingual Canadian variant names and GTIN mapping, but an indexed retailer contradicts two of those assignments and lists a further variant not present in the Canadian source set. No product-specific portion statement was found. The hold is about family/variant identity and roster completeness, not a rule that a retailer identity must always show a UPC.
