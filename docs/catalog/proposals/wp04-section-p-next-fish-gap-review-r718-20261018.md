- **Candidate set:** Northern Rock Sole / *Lepidopsetta polyxystra* (CFIA TSN 616392), Southern Rock Sole / *L. bilineata* (CFIA TSN 172917), and Common Dab / *Limanda limanda* (CFIA TSN 172881).
- **Scope:** The minimum three source-backed species identities that make both `rock sole` and `limande` return multiple distinct references under the current picker. This does not add a product form or modify the existing generic sole/flounder groups.
- **Recommendation:** Keep all three outside active references and the shared research ledger for now. But Common Dab's required canonical display contains `dab`; phrase-first search would make the broader `dab` query return Common Dab alone. CFIA assigns Dab/Rusty Dab and Sand Dab names to additional flatfish, and the prior R126 review explicitly held the Yellowtail Flounder dab terms because of that broad query. The three companions resolve the two requested terms but leave the same misleading-single-result boundary for `dab`; do not reopen R126 or invent a generic dab group in this species review. No manifest-listed recipe expresses species-specific demand.

Requirements FR-7 calls for ingredient search in both French and English and for recipe lines to link to ingredient references. The three bilingual identities would make the requested `rock sole` and `limande` searches visibly multi-result. The resulting single-reference `dab` search is still a material picker boundary. ## Bounded identity and source findings

The CFIA Fish List result for TSN 616392 pairs *Lepidopsetta polyxystra* with **Northern Rock Sole / limande du nord**. For TSN 172917, *L. bilineata*, it lists **Rock Sole / Plie**, **Sole / Sole**, and **Flounder / Fausse Limande**, plus the French-only name **Fausse Limande Du Pacifique**. For TSN 172881, *Limanda limanda*, it lists **Common Dab / Dab Commun**. The same list assigns **Dab** and **Rusty Dab** to *Limanda ferruginea* and **Sand Dab** to *Hippoglossoides platessoides*. CFIA describes its listed English and French names as acceptable in Canada for fish labelling and supplies the corresponding scientific names and TSNs. This is Canadian name-and-taxon evidence; it does not establish a current Québec retail SKU, recipe demand, product state, or interchangeability. [S01]

NOAA's Groundfish Survey Species Code Manual distinguishes *L. polyxystra* as **northern rock sole** and *L. bilineata* as **southern rock sole**. NOAA Fisheries' seafood profile puts both species under the broader heading **Rock Sole** and also lists names such as **Sole** and **Flounder**. Together, these sources support two distinct species displays while showing why `rock sole` itself is broader. The seafood page has a misspelling in its opening binomial line (`Lepidopsetta billineta`); the code manual and CFIA give the spelling *L. bilineata*. NOAA sources provide U.S. fishery and naming context, not Canadian labelling or evidence of Québec retail availability. [S02, S07]

The conditional bilingual displays are **Northern Rock Sole / limande du nord**, **Southern Rock Sole / fausse limande du Pacifique**, and **Common Dab / dab commun**. The first is the exact CFIA pair. The Southern Rock Sole English name comes from NOAA's species code manual, while the French display is CFIA's accepted French name for *L. bilineata*; CFIA's row does not pair that French-only name with an English label. Common Dab / dab commun is CFIA's exact pair. To make the French picker query `limande` return *L. limanda* too, that row would need the source-backed French alias **limande**: the OQLF-hosted GDT entry maps `limande` to English **dab** and Latin *Limanda limanda*, but credits the entry to the Association française de normalisation (1967), not OQLF. The GDT mapping supports the taxon-to-term link, not a current Québec retail name. A targeted OQLF GDT search did not surface an entry for the full phrase **limande du nord**; this is a search observation, not proof that no Québec usage exists. ITIS also lists the French common name for valid species *L. polyxystra*, but its record is taxonomic evidence rather than a Québec terminology decision or consumer label. [S01, S03, S06, S07]

Keep **limande-sole** out of any candidate aliases. The OQLF GDT entry for that compound maps it to **lemon sole**, *Microstomus kitt*; Fisheries and Oceans Canada's current French profile uses **limande-sole** for Pacific Dover Sole, *Microstomus pacificus*. Neither term identifies *L. polyxystra*. These neighboring records illustrate a naming collision, not a synonym relationship. [S04, S05]

The sources support the three species identities and names above. Do not add **Sole**, **Flounder / Plie**, **limande-sole**, or a generic `rock sole` alias. The `limande` alias belongs only to Common Dab in this conditional projection; Northern and Southern Rock Sole match it through their full French displays. The research found no supported cut, frozen/fresh state, quantity, measure, density, edible yield, recipe line, or directed substitution for any of the three species. No measurement or substitution inference is made from fishery or taxonomic sources.

It would also make `dab` return only Common Dab, although CFIA applies Dab-containing names to multiple flatfish; R126 already documented and held that short-query boundary. The candidate set therefore does not safely clear the current-picker hold. Keep R277's generic sole result, R280's flounder/plie result, R658's Yellowfin Sole identity, and the R126 Yellowtail Flounder hold unchanged. Add no reference, form, source record, alias, recipe link, measurement, density, unit mapping, conversion, yield, or substitution. Leave the Section P finfish and regional-fish parent open.
