- **Reviewed:** 2026-10-11
- **Disposition:** Retain `pine nut butter / beurre de pignon de pin`; keep French aliases empty.

## Recommendation

Retain the existing French display `beurre de pignon de pin`. OQLF's food entry recognizes `pignon`, `pigne`, and `noix de pin` as French names for pine seed; the Government of Québec consumer page also uses *pignons de pin* and identifies *noix de pin* as an alternate name. The current Balsam Natural Canadian product listing uses the exact compound *Beurre de pignon de pin* in its French product details for a pine-nut butter. Together these sources support the existing display as clear Québec-facing wording.

The full compound is not an OQLF-preferred term and the marketplace listing is third-party product text, so the review does not claim formal standardization. Keep `aliasesFr` empty; no additional exact French pine-nut-butter label was validated. No display edit, alias, form, recipe link, measurement, conversion, yield, or substitution is proposed.
