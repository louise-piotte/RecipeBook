# WP05 Koikuchi and Usukuchi Soy-Sauce Styles — Independent Québec French Review

## Review record

- **Reviewed:** 2026-10-11.
- **Scope:** Independent fr-CA wording review of only the two French displays proposed in [the koikuchi/usukuchi identity packet](wp05-koikuchi-usukuchi-soy-style-review-20261011.md).
- **Source IDs:** `WP05-KOIKUCHI-USUKUCHI-QC-WORDING-20261011-S01` through `S07`. This source-ID prefix was not found in the catalog ledger or proposal directory before this review.
- **Disposition:** Accept both displays as written, with the evidence limits below. This review does not resolve any other part of the parent packet.

## Decisions

| Proposed identity | Proposed fr-CA display | Review | Rationale and limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Koikuchi soy sauce | `sauce soya de type koikuchi` | **Accept** | `Sauce soya` is the Québec-preferred family wording. Current Québec sources use `koikuchi` as a named Japanese style, including a local product description that says `sauce soya foncée ... de type koikuchi`. The proposed display keeps the source-attested `de type koikuchi` construction but drops `foncée`, avoiding an exact collision with the separate Chinese-style dark-soy row. The complete proposed phrase is a transparent, source-supported construction rather than an exact product title or an OQLF term entry. [S01–S03, S05] |
| Usukuchi soy sauce | `sauce soya pâle (usukuchi)` | **Accept** | A current Québec product page titles its product `Usukuchi | Sauce soya pâle`; Québec culinary material also calls usukuchi `plus claire`. The parenthetical preserves the Japanese style name and makes the color wording specific. Keep `sauce soya claire` out of aliases because it is already the exact display of the Chinese-style light-soy row and is used on a Montréal retailer's usukuchi listing. [S01–S04, S06] |

Keep the proposed `sauce soya` base in both displays. OQLF lists `sauce soya` and `sauce de soya` as preferred terms and notes that `sauce soja` / `sauce de soja` are more prevalent in Europe. The submitted `soya` wording is therefore appropriate for Québec. OQLF's record also documents the general light/dark vocabulary but does not define the Japanese terms `koikuchi` or `usukuchi`; the exact Japanese style names are supported here by Québec product and culinary sources. [S01–S02]

## Collision check relevant to these displays

The active ingredient-reference catalog currently assigns:

- `ingredient-ref-light-soy-sauce` — `light soy sauce / sauce soya claire`.
- `ingredient-ref-dark-soy-sauce` — `dark soy sauce / sauce soya foncée`.
- `ingredient-ref-soy-sauce` — `soy sauce / sauce soya`, with French alias `sauce soja` and English alias `shoyu`.

Neither accepted display is an exact key owned by those rows. The local product phrases `sauce soya claire` and `sauce soya foncée` remain unsuitable as global aliases for usukuchi and koikuchi respectively, even though local retailers use those glosses for individual Japanese products. The proposed labels preserve the family term while keeping each named Japanese style distinguishable in exact lookup.

## Review notes

- **Koikuchi:** Accept `sauce soya de type koikuchi`. Do not replace it with `sauce soya foncée`, which collides with the active dark-soy display and would hide the Japanese style boundary.
- **Usukuchi:** Accept `sauce soya pâle (usukuchi)`. Do not replace it with `sauce soya claire`, which is already the active light-soy display and appears as a broad retail translation for one usukuchi item.
- Both displays are supported by current Québec usage, while the OQLF evidence supports the shared base term. Neither display is claimed to be an OQLF-standardized compound.
- This is the requested independent Québec French review of the two displays. No further bilingual-review hold is recorded for these exact strings.
