# WP05 Section Z — Goya Sofrito and Recaito cooking-base review (R253)

- **Reviewed:** 2026-10-16.
- **Disposition:** Recommend two separate, brand-qualified `SAUCE_AND_CONDIMENT` identities for the current Goya Sofrito and Recaito cooking-base products.
- **Scope:** The manufacturer’s tomato Sofrito base and Recaito base sold as packaged products. This does not define generic homemade sofrito, regional recipe formulas, or a broad cilantro/culantro identity.

## Recommendation

Add `Goya Sofrito Tomato Cooking Base / base de cuisson Goya au sofrito de tomate` and `Goya Recaito Cooking Base / base de cuisson Goya au recaito` as separate product references. Keep the French displays provisional: no Québec product label or OQLF decision for these complete product names was located. Preserve the manufacturer’s product names as borrowed terms; do not claim an OQLF ruling.

Goya describes Sofrito as a tomato, pepper, cilantro, onion, garlic, and spice cooking base. Goya describes Recaito as a Puerto Rican cooking base; its ingredient list names `CULANTRO`, while the marketing paragraph and some retailer titles say cilantro. Keep the two products separate. Do not add `cilantro`, `culantro`, `coriandre`, or herb aliases to Recaito, and do not map either product to the existing fresh `culantro / chicória-do-Pará` identity. [S01–S02, S06–S07]

If accepted, use empty aliases, null densities, and empty unit mappings. Preserve the manufacturer’s 6 oz, 12 oz, and 24 oz jar sizes only as separate product-specific package observations. Retain Canadian retailer package statements separately without converting units or treating them as kitchen measures. Add no forms, recipe links, reusable measures, conversions, preparation yields, or substitutions. [S01–S07]

## Catalog, picker, and recipe audit

The live active-reference catalog has no Goya Sofrito or Goya Recaito product row. The existing `ingredient-ref-culantro` is a fresh-herb identity, not a branded prepared cooking base. Existing Haitian epis and Trinidadian green seasoning remain separate regional mixtures. No active reference or form owns the proposed product titles, and no legacy identity or earlier focused proposal/batch owns either Goya product.

Under the current picker, `Goya Sofrito Tomato Cooking Base` and `base de cuisson Goya au sofrito de tomate` will return the Sofrito row; `Goya Recaito Cooking Base` and `base de cuisson Goya au recaito` will return the Recaito row. Short `sofrito` and `recaito` queries will each find the corresponding Goya product. Do not add generic aliases or herb-component names.

The manifest-listed package contains 114 recipes and 1,123 ingredient rows. The candidate names, `sofrito`, `recaito`, and the Goya product titles have no matching recipe line or file. No recipe allocation is proposed.

## Package-data boundary

Goya lists 6 oz, 12 oz, and 24 oz sizes for each base. Keep each as a separate manufacturer package observation for its own product. The Canadian Sofrito listing reports a 340 g jar; the Canadian Recaito listing displays 12 oz (340 g) and an indexed backorder notice. The live inventory states could not be confirmed during review. Preserve package wording as source-specific evidence; do not infer current availability or convert between ounces and grams. [S01–S07]

No source supports a reusable household measure, density, unit mapping, generic preparation yield, or substitution. Goya’s general use descriptions are not a validated exchange rule between Sofrito and Recaito or between either product and fresh herbs.
