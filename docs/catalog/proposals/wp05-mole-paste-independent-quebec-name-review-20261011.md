# WP05 mole paste: independent Québec French name review

## Scope and decision

- **Review and source-access date:** 2026-10-11.
- **Scope:** Independently reassess only the provisional French display proposed in [the broader mole-paste boundary review](wp05-mole-paste-quebec-boundary-review-20261011.md). This is a wording and product-state follow-up, not a second identity review.
- **Status:** Proposal only. No catalog, ledger, plan, roadmap, recipe, code, test, or README file is changed.

**Decision: `mole en pâte` is defensible only as a provisional, descriptive fr-CA display; it is not verified Québec terminology.** The exact phrase was not found in the targeted OQLF or Québec retailer searches. Current Québec product pages use the borrowed headword *mole* without stating the product form. Canadian official trademark records provide two better-attested alternatives—`pâte pour mole` and, historically, `pâte de mole`—but those are goods descriptions in trademark records, not OQLF recommendations or confirmed Québec package wording. Keep `mole en pâte` marked provisional if a French display is needed now; do not add the alternatives as aliases or present any of the three as settled Québec terminology.

If the catalog requires an externally attested French product label before release, the French display remains unresolved pending a bilingual package or Québec product listing that explicitly names the paste state.

## Name and product-state boundary

- Retain `mole en pâte` only with an explicit **provisional** status. Its value is that it preserves the headword used by Québec retailers while signaling that the catalog item applies to paste-state products.
- Do not add `pâte pour mole`, `pâte de mole`, `sauce mole`, `sauce au mole`, `Mole mexicain`, or `Mole verde` as aliases. The first two are alternative descriptions with limited evidence; sauce and unqualified retailer titles do not guarantee paste state.
- Count a product as in scope only when its recipe line, package, or product source explicitly identifies the item as mole paste or equivalent paste-state product. The words *mole*, *mole rouge*, *mole verde*, or *sauce mole* alone do not establish that state.
- The Metro and Tropica titles show that French Québec retail naming can preserve the Spanish-derived headword without a translated state qualifier. The prior broad audit’s La Hacienda listing distinguishes English product names for mole sauce and mole paste locally, but provides no French label; it does not settle the French display.
- The Walmart page and the CIPO goods statements establish no preparation ratio, dilution behavior, ingredient list, common formulation, or conversion. None is proposed here.

## Recipe and catalog relevance

The broader proposal’s full scan of the 114 bundled recipes found no mole, mole-paste, or mole-sauce ingredient rows. This naming follow-up identifies no new recipe line, amount, or catalog behavior; it makes no recipe-specific claim.

## Final recommendation

For this proposal, keep the display as **`mole en pâte` — provisional, descriptive wording only**. Current evidence justifies making the state explicit, but does not establish a settled Québec French form. `Pâte pour mole` has stronger current official Canadian goods-description evidence than `pâte de mole`; neither has been confirmed as Québec retail or OQLF terminology. Do not treat any alternative or sauce wording as a synonym for the paste-state item, and revisit the display if an actual Québec package label or authoritative terminology entry becomes available.
