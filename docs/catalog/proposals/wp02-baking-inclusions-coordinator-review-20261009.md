## Measurement and substitution limits

No source reviewed supports a general density, cup-to-weight mapping, package yield, or substitution rule for these identities. The direct recipes give only these scoped amounts: sugar pearls “to taste”; sprinkles optional with no amount; 7 oz marshmallow crème; and 1/2 cup diced white marshmallows. Preserve those recipe lines without deriving catalog conversions. A manufacturer’s portion or package size, where shown, applies to that branded product and is not a generic conversion. Butterscotch chips, caramel bits, toffee bits, nougat, ginger confit, candied citrus, and glacé cherries have no exact ingredient demand in the bundled corpus. No cross-product swaps are recommended.
