# WP02 Section 04 B4 R140 — elephant-foot-yam flour product-boundary hold

- **Reviewed:** 2026-10-15.
- **Status:** proposal only; no active catalog, form, ledger, plan, roadmap, generated, or recipe file was changed.
- **Plan pointer:** the open parent at `docs/RecipeBook-global-ingredient-catalog-plan.txt:2396–2397` is “Green-banana, plantain, breadfruit, tigernut, yam, taro, and other established regional root/fruit flours.” `roadmap.md` directs ongoing work through the global catalog plan; it has no separate elephant-foot-yam-flour item.
- **Candidate:** flour made from corms of *Amorphophallus paeoniifolius*. Primary studies call it elephant foot yam flour (EFYF). This is non-extracted corm flour, not isolated *A. paeoniifolius* starch.
- **Prior-work boundary:** WP02 B3 R117 reviewed and held isolated *A. paeoniifolius* corm starch as `candidate-elephant-foot-yam-starch`; it did not review a flour identity. R140 does not reopen the R117 starch decision or repeat the R136–R139 tigernut, *Artocarpus camansi*, jackfruit, or pearl-millet starch reviews.
- **Proposal-local source prefix:** `WP02-B4-ELEPHANT-FOOT-YAM-FLOUR-R140-20261015-S`. A repository search after drafting found this prefix only in this proposal and no research-ledger match.

## Recommendation

**Hold; no active catalog or ledger delta.** Primary research establishes a food-science flour made by drying and milling *A. paeoniifolius* corm slices, but the current Canadian product evidence reviewed here is for frozen whole-corm pieces, not a standalone flour package. Current exact searches did not locate a Canadian or Québec *A. paeoniifolius* flour label or supplier. The French wording is also unresolved for flour: OQLF defines `igname` for *Dioscorea*, while Health Canada's natural-health-product database and one Québec-facing frozen-corm listing use elephant-yam wording for *A. paeoniifolius*. Neither source is a Québec flour-product label or a terminology ruling for this flour.

Keep this candidate distinct from the held isolated-starch fraction, the existing *Dioscorea* `yam flour / farine d’igname` reference, and the existing *Amorphophallus* konjac-flour reference. Do not create `ingredient-ref-elephant-foot-yam-flour`, a flour ledger candidate, alias, form, measurement, density, mapping, recipe link, conversion, yield, or substitution until product scope and French naming are independently supported.

## Numeric, form, and substitution evidence

- **Research preparation:** Suriya et al. produced the flour from corms purchased in a Puducherry local market. Their procedure washed, peeled, trimmed, sliced, blanched in 0.1% sodium metabisulfite at 70 °C for five minutes, cooled, drained, dried at 40 °C for 12 hours, ground, and sieved through 60 mesh. This defines the experiment sample; it is not a household preparation guide or a Canadian product specification. [S05]
- **Bulk density:** the same study reports 0.55 ± 0.03 g/mL for its *A. paeoniifolius* flour sample; the wheat/elephant-foot-yam blend was 0.51 ± 0.02 g/mL. These are sample- and method-specific research values, not package-specific data or a validated household cup conversion. Add no `defaultDensity` or unit mapping. [S05]
- **Substitution:** the study tested cookies in which EFYF substituted refined wheat flour at levels up to 70%; reported sensory acceptability applies to that study's cookie formulations and sample. It does not establish a universal wheat-flour replacement ratio, a general baking rule, or a directed catalog substitution. No bundled recipe requests this flour. Add no substitution rule or recipe link. [S05]
- **Forms and conversion:** non-extracted corm flour and extracted starch are distinct ingredient identities, not forms of one identity. No same-ingredient flour-to-starch conversion, cooking state, or preparation form is supported. Add no form, conversion, or yield.

## Exact proposed delta

**No JSON delta.** Keep the non-extracted *A. paeoniifolius* corm-flour candidate outside active references and the research ledger until a current Canadian/Québec standalone food-flour product and product-specific Québec French wording establish a stable identity. Do not reserve `ingredient-ref-elephant-foot-yam-flour`; do not append a flour candidate to the ledger; and do not add names, aliases, forms, measurements, density, mappings, conversions, yields, recipe links, or substitutions.

## Reconsideration conditions

1. Reopen only when a current Canadian or Québec supplier/package identifies standalone *A. paeoniifolius* corm flour, and an independent Québec review supports the flour-specific French display.
2. Before any future row, recheck the picker with the proposed bilingual display. Preserve the distinction from *Dioscorea* `yam flour / farine d’igname` and *A. konjac* konjac flour; do not use shared generic aliases to bridge those identities.
3. Require a separate product-matched source for any density, household measure, form, conversion, yield, recipe link, or substitution. Keep Suriya et al.'s density and cookie ratio as bounded laboratory evidence only.
4. Keep the B4 parent open. This hold does not alter or close Section 04 B3 or any previous R136–R139 decision.
