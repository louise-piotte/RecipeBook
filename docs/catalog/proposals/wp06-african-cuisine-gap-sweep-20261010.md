## Scope, source standard, and prior-work reconciliation

Section 05 of the global catalog plan still lists North, West, Central, Horn/East, and Southern African cuisine as open. Section Z separately names West/Central African palm and seed preparations, Ethiopian/Eritrean niter kibbeh and awaze, and additional African sauces and souring ingredients. I found no earlier dedicated African cuisine gap-sweep proposal. This packet fills that research gap only for the candidates and boundaries it actually reviews; it does not close either checklist.

The source set prioritizes the Office québécois de la langue française (OQLF), FAO, Nigerian and Ethiopian public research/training bodies, peer-reviewed regional food research, and CIFOR-ICRAF. Display names retain source-native terms where no well-supported Québec French equivalent was found. A descriptive French rendering is marked provisional. A proposed identity does not imply a safe preparation instruction, measurement, density, form, yield, conversion, recipe relink, or substitution.

Existing African-related work that remains in force:

- **Egusi:** `ingredient-ref-egusi-seeds` / `egusi seeds` / `graines d’egusi` is active. Its source record treats egusi as a multi-species seed category and keeps seed forms distinct. This packet does not revisit it or map ogbono to it. See `docs/catalog/proposals/wp03-egusi-melon-seeds-identity-review-20261009.md`.
- **Fonio:** white and black fonio are active as separate grain identities; teff and teff flour are also active. These are not proposed again.
- **Dried oyster and Yokhoss:** `ingredient-ref-dried-oyster` / `dried oyster` / `huître séchée` is active. Yokhoss remains a source-scoped Senegalese market term and is not proposed as a global alias. See `docs/catalog/proposals/wp04-dried-oyster-boundary-review-20261009.md` and `docs/catalog/batches/ingredient-catalog-continuation-20261009/README.md`.
- **Bottarga / botargo:** the WP04 review retains `botargo` only with its North African source scope and defers a global bottarga identity without recipe demand. This packet does not reopen that boundary or add a roe candidate. See `docs/catalog/proposals/wp04-bottarga-coordinator-recheck-20261009.md`.
- **North African/MENA products:** harissa, chermoula, amba, zhug, shatta, pomegranate molasses, date syrup, tkemali, ajika, and other items accepted in the WP05 MENA packets remain with their current owners. Preserved lemon remains unmodeled; it is not proposed here. See `docs/catalog/proposals/wp05-mena-pantry-batch-20261008.md`, `docs/catalog/proposals/wp05-south-central-asia-mena-followup.md`, and `docs/catalog/batches/wp05-mena-condiments-followup.md`.
- **Other African source-led rows:** baobab fruit pulp is active from Section M; `crude palm oil (azeite de dendê)` is active from the South American sweep. Their geographic histories do not justify duplicate identities here. See `docs/catalog/proposals/wp03-section-m-sapote-baobab-followup-20261010.md` and `docs/catalog/batches/wp06-crude-palm-oil-identity-20261009/README.md`.
- The ginger-garlic paste proposal mentions the African peanut stew only as recipe demand for separate garlic and ground ginger lines. It is not an African cuisine sweep. See `docs/catalog/proposals/wp05-ginger-garlic-paste-identity-20261009.md`.
