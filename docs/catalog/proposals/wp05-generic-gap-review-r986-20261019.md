## Recommendation

Propose a separate, identity-only generic reference for **yeolmu-kimchi**, the named Korean kimchi prepared with young summer radish. Use `yeolmu-kimchi` as the provisional fr-CA display as well as the English display; no Québec French term for this subtype was verified. Add only the source-attested English aliases `yeolmu kimchi`, `yeolmukimchi`, and `young summer radish kimchi`. Keep it distinct from generic kimchi, other named kimchi types, raw radish and radish leaves, and salt-and-vinegar pickled radish. Do not treat a source’s dish description or brine use as a form, measure, or substitution.

Korea Tourism Organization pages name yeolmu-kimchi as a kimchi type made with young summer radish and distinguish it from cabbage, diced-radish, water, and cucumber kimchi types. The dedicated page describes the young summer radish as having a small root, thick stem, and leaves; it says these are cleaned, salted, and seasoned, with some water added before ripening. This supports a distinct named preparation identity, not a fixed formula across households or products. [S01–S02]

## Naming and proposed aliases

The Korea Tourism Organization’s dedicated page gives `Young Summer Radish Kimchi (열무김치 / Yeolmukimchi)`. Its kimchi overview also names `Yeolmu kimchi (Young summer radish kimchi)`. These support the English display/aliases and spelling variants. A 2014 original study title uses `Young Radish (Yulmoo) Kimchi`; that source-specific transliteration is not proposed as a global alias because the current official English naming is sufficient and narrower. [S01–S03]

OQLF’s `kimchi` entry supports the Québec French parent term and defines kimchi generally as a seasoned, fermented vegetable preparation served as an accompaniment; it does not name yeolmu-kimchi. Keep the specific subtype name borrowed in `nameFr` provisionally. Add no French descriptive translation, no raw `yeolmu` or `young summer radish` alias, and no broad `radish kimchi`, `young radish kimchi`, `water kimchi`, or `summer kimchi` alias. [WP05-EAST-S06, reused; S01–S02]

## Evidence limits; measures and substitutions

Those observations belong to its study groups; they do not establish reusable kitchen measures, density, unit mappings, a universal process, or recipe yield. [S03]

If integrated, keep no forms, density, unit mappings, measurement observations, conversions, yields, or recipe-derived measures; use `measurementState: researched_no_supported_value`. ## Unsupported limits

The evidence supports one named Korean kimchi type and a provisional borrowed French display. It does not establish a single required balance of root, stem, or leaves; whether each preparation includes the same amounts of these parts; a universal seasoning formula; mandatory inclusion or exclusion of brine; fermentation stage; storage method or duration; finished mass; serving weight; or food-safety conditions. Keep all preparation particulars and study results at the source that reports them.

No reviewed source establishes equivalence with generic radish kimchi, Korean pickled radish, water kimchi as a broad family, or any other named kimchi type. The R986 sources make no claims about retail availability or commercial product formulations; no commercial roster was researched.
