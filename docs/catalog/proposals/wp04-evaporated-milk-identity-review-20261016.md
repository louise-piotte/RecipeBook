# WP04 evaporated-milk identity review — 2026-10-16

## Disposition

Keep the existing identity `ingredient-ref-evaporated-milk` with the bilingual display names
`evaporated milk / lait évaporé`. Canadian compositional standards distinguish evaporated milk from
sweetened condensed milk, evaporated skim milk, and evaporated partly skimmed milk. The current
Canadian Carnation product page also uses `Lait évaporé` as its product title.

The OQLF entry `produit laitier concentré` is a broader category and records an older terminology
preference against generic `lait évaporé` and `lait condensé`. It does not establish a better current
retail product name. Keep the specific Canadian standard and manufacturer wording in the catalog;
do not add `lait concentré` or `lait condensé` as aliases because those broad forms could overlap
with sweetened condensed milk.

## Catalog boundary

- Verify the existing identity only; do not create another reference.
- Keep sweetened condensed milk and evaporated skim/partly skimmed products distinct.
- Add no aliases, forms, measures, density, unit mappings, conversions, yields, recipe links, or
  substitution rules.
- Leave the recipe's existing `354 ml` evaporated-milk line unchanged.
- The related whole-milk substitution-table finding remains blocked because the sources do not test
  bundled recipes, and their source-cup amounts are not conversions to RecipeBook's unit basis.
