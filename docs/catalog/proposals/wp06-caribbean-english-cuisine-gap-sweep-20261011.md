# WP06 English-Speaking Caribbean Cuisine Gap Sweep

**Status:** proposal-only; no shared catalog, ledger, recipe, workflow, plan, roadmap, code, or test files are changed by this packet.
**Prepared and sources checked:** 2026-10-11
**Scope:** a bounded sample of Jamaica, Barbados, Trinidad and Tobago, The Bahamas, Saint Lucia, and Saint Vincent and the Grenadines. This is not an exhaustive survey of English-speaking Caribbean islands, communities, or diaspora cuisines.

## Method and current-state audit

The review read the repository instructions, WP06 acceptance text and Section 05 checklist in `docs/RecipeBook-global-ingredient-catalog-plan.txt`, the live seed, all manifest-listed recipes, the current forms and legacy candidate data, the research ledger, and the relevant WP05/WP06 packets. The inspected package contained 954 active ingredient references, 53 ingredient forms, 75 legacy candidates, and 114 recipes with 1,123 ingredient rows. The ledger contained 1,223 records, 3,679 source entries, and 2,993 distinct source IDs.

Candidate names were compared with active canonical names and aliases, form labels and match terms, and legacy candidate names and aliases using `normalize_search_key` from `tools/seed_package/bundled_seed_package.py`. Existing exact owners include breadfruit, cassava, banana, plantain banana, allspice, cornmeal, okra, chickpeas, guava, green onions, ground cumin, cumin seeds, culantro, amaranth greens, taro leaves, coconut milk, cassareep, and Trinidadian green seasoning. The regional terms below that do not already have an exact owner include ackee, pigeon pea, roselle calyces, Scotch bonnet pepper, and Trinidadian pimento pepper. The prior WP03 legume packet already lists `ingredient-ref-pigeon-peas` as a candidate; this sweep routes new evidence to that candidate instead of proposing a duplicate.

The exact normalized-key scan found no active reference, form, or legacy owner for the source terms `ackee`, `pigeon pea`, `gungo peas`, `callaloo bush`, `green fig`, `roselle`, `chadon beni`, `shadon beni`, `bandania`, `geera powder`, `channa`, `ochro`, `pimento pepper`, `goat pepper`, `mauby`, or `falernum`. Exact matches for already represented foods were treated as current coverage, not additions. A whole-token scan of all 1,123 ingredient rows found no Caribbean name or dish phrase from the benchmark table below. Generic rows for ingredients such as cumin or chickpeas do not demonstrate coverage of Caribbean recipes.

The recipe-set fingerprint follows the current WP06 packet convention: sorted recipe basenames, each followed by NUL, exact recipe bytes, then NUL. These hashes pin the audited snapshot; repeat the read-only check before any later integration if shared files have changed.

## Coverage gaps and follow-up boundary

This source set reaches savory dishes, baking and dessert, seasoning and condiment use, festive foods, and beverages across the sample. It is sufficient to identify several missing local terms and boundary conflicts, but not to close Section 05 or claim complete Caribbean coverage.

Open work includes additional original recipe coverage from Dominica, Grenada, Antigua and Barbuda, Saint Kitts and Nevis, Montserrat, and other Eastern Caribbean islands; more Bahamian and Barbadian household recipes; community and diaspora naming in Canada; and a Québec French product-name review for the proposed ackee, roselle calyces, Scotch bonnet, and pimento pepper identities. The current sample does not establish a standard product definition for mauby or falernum, a universal callaloo identity, or a single treatment of pigeon-pea states. It also lacks a systematic study of preserved products, pickling, and household condiments for every island. These remain named research gaps, not implied catalog coverage.

For any later integration, repeat the normalized-key and recipe-line audit against the then-current package. Preserve recipe publisher measurements within their own source records; this proposal sets no quantities, conversions, density, yield, forms, or substitution behavior. It does not propose recipe links because the current 114-recipe corpus has no matching Caribbean dish or regional ingredient line.
