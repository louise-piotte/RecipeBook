# WP03 Section U — perilla leaf / shiso identity review (R335, 2026-10-16)

- **Status:** Accepted and integrated by root on 2026-10-16; see the [R335 integration README](../batches/wp03-section-u-perilla-leaves-identity-integration-r335-20261016/README.md). The original proposal records the source review and recommended scope.
- **Plan anchor:** Section U, “Fresh herbs botanical aromatics and regional flavorings,” line 7622 of `docs/RecipeBook-global-ingredient-catalog-plan.txt`. The open herb list names “shiso/perilla.”
- **Candidate:** Culinary leaves of *Perilla frutescens*, sold and used as perilla leaves or shiso leaves.
- **Coordination:** R327, R330, and R332 cover Chios mastic, mahlab, and lemon verbena; this review does not reopen them. The integrated Section I perilla-seed identity is seed-only. WP05’s recent shibazuke packet expressly leaves fresh shiso/perilla leaves for a separate review with Québec display evidence, which this proposal supplies.
- **Disposition:** Advance one identity-only HERB reference for culinary perilla leaves, displayed as `perilla leaves / feuilles de périlla`. Keep the seed, oil, powder, preserved dishes, and cultivar-specific products separate.

## Recommendation

Add one base ingredient identity for the edible leaves of *Perilla frutescens*. Kew accepts *P. frutescens* as a species and *P. frutescens* var. *crispa* as an accepted variety, recording “shiso” among the variety’s common names. OMAFRA’s bilingual crop profile identifies *P. frutescens*, names shiso, and documents culinary use of its leaves. Health Canada separately lists whole or minimally processed perilla leaf as a food ingredient. These sources establish a leaf identity without extending the seed identity or making cultivar-specific products interchangeable. [S01–S04]

| Field | Proposed value |
|---|---|
| Proposed ID | **ingredient-ref-perilla-leaves** (unreserved) |
| Category | HERB |
| English display | perilla leaves |
| Québec French display | feuilles de périlla |
| English aliases | perilla leaf; shiso leaves |
| French aliases | feuille de périlla; shiso |
| Scope | Culinary leaves of *Perilla frutescens* |
| Forms | None |
| Recipe links | None |
| Density, mappings, conversions, yields, active substitutions | None |

The Québec display is supported as local usage, not as an OQLF ruling: a Québec horticultural retailer uses `Périlla` and “also called shiso,” while a Montréal restaurant writes `feuilles de périlla` for a Korean leaf-wrap ingredient. Santé Canada’s French list independently uses the singular `Feuille de perilla`; its spelling is unaccented there, and the picker’s accent folding covers both spellings. The OQLF search located only an unrelated 1973 industrial entry for `huile de périlla`, not a culinary leaf term. [S04–S07]

## Identity and product boundary

| Term or product | Disposition | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Culinary perilla leaves, *Perilla frutescens* | Proposed identity | Scope to the edible leaf. Sources document leaves used raw or cooked and sold individually for culinary use. This identity does not add a use instruction, measure, or food-safety conclusion. [S03–S04] |
| `Shiso leaves` | English alias | OMAFRA lists shiso as a common name for *P. frutescens*; MAFF documents Japanese shiso leaves in a regional food context. The alias does not assert that every regional variety or culinary use is identical. [S02–S03, S08] |
| Green and purple/red leaves; *P. frutescens* var. *crispa* | Cultivar/product distinction | Green and purple-leafed perilla are sold for different end uses and markets. Kew accepts var. *crispa*, and MAFF describes a region-specific red shiso product. Do not add separate colour forms, or imply that colour, variety, and regional products are freely interchangeable. [S02–S03, S08] |
| Perilla seeds | Already covered separately | `ingredient-ref-perilla-seeds` is scoped to the edible seeds. Shared species does not make seeds and leaves one picker identity. The R seed review explicitly left leaves/shiso for separate work. [S09; live catalog] |
| Perilla leaf oil, perilla seed oil, extracts, and essential oil | Separate processed products | Health Canada lists leaf oil separately from leaf and seed entries; OMAFRA also describes oil and essential-oil uses. Do not map an oil or extract to the leaf identity. [S03–S04] |
| Perilla seed powder; sowing seed | Separate products | The existing seed review found Québec retail powder and horticultural seed products; neither establishes a leaf form or alias. Keep powdered seeds and seed for planting outside this leaf reference. [S09] |
| Shibazuke and other pickles | Separate prepared foods | The MAFF page uses shiso leaves in one regional pickle context. WP05’s shibazuke review establishes a named prepared preserve, not an alias, leaf form, or transferable leaf amount. [S08, S10] |
| `sesame leaves`, `wild sesame`, `Chinese basil`, and other broad common names | Do not add as aliases | Sources report multiple regional/common names, but these names also risk confusion with sesame or basil products and do not give a consistent Québec-facing leaf label. Keep the source wording in this review only. [S03] |

The proposed identity is at the botanical species and edible-part level. It retains green/purple and regional usage as unresolved product distinctions instead of turning colour or cuisine into forms. It does not include medicinal uses, teas, infusions, oils, supplements, flowers, or non-food plant material.

## Coordination and prior decisions

The integrated Section I review created `ingredient-ref-perilla-seeds` for edible *P. frutescens* seeds and explicitly excluded leaves/shiso. This proposal uses its cross-reference as evidence of an uncovered part-specific identity, not as a reason to merge leaves and seeds. [S09]

WP05’s 2026-10-16 shibazuke review says the live catalog lacks a fresh shiso identity and marks “fresh shiso/perilla leaves” unresolved and outside that preserve review; it calls for a separate review with Québec display evidence. The current proposal answers that request and leaves shibazuke’s product identity untouched. [S10]

Section U still lists “shiso/perilla” as open. This proposal does not revisit integrated rice paddy herb, pandan, makrut lime leaves, lovage, lemon balm, Chios mastic, mahlab, or lemon verbena.

## Exact picker projection

`IngredientReferenceSearchIndex` normalizes case, accents, and punctuation, checks complete normalized word phrases within names and aliases first, and uses substring matching only if no complete phrase matches. Forms contribute search terms, but this proposal adds no form.

The current catalog has only the perilla-seed reference for this candidate family. With the proposed row and aliases above, expected results are:

| Query | Projected result |
|---|---|
| `perilla leaves` | `ingredient-ref-perilla-leaves` only; no form hint |
| `perilla leaf` | `ingredient-ref-perilla-leaves` only; no form hint |
| `feuilles de périlla` / `feuille de périlla` | `ingredient-ref-perilla-leaves` only; accents normalize; no form hint |
| `shiso leaves` / `shiso` | `ingredient-ref-perilla-leaves` only; `shiso` occurs as a complete word in the alias; no form hint |
| `perilla` / `périlla` | `ingredient-ref-perilla-leaves` and `ingredient-ref-perilla-seeds`; both names contain this complete word, so the picker keeps both results |
| `perilla seeds` | `ingredient-ref-perilla-seeds` only |
| Botanical names | No result; do not add *Perilla frutescens* or its variety names as picker aliases |

The short `perilla`/`périlla` query is intentionally ambiguous because the same plant yields separate edible leaves and seeds. The complete bilingual part-qualified phrases return their respective identities alone.

## Measures and substitutions

The sources support culinary use but do not give a transferable leaf quantity, household measure, mass-per-volume value, density, or leaf-to-dried conversion. The MAFF shibazuke page’s ingredient list says “Perilla Seeds 1kg,” while its preparation steps say to use shiso leaves; this internally inconsistent recipe line is excluded from leaf measurement evidence. Do not infer a recipe amount from it. [S08]

No source supports a substitution between perilla leaves and basil, mint, sesame, or another herb. No measure, density, form, mapping, conversion, yield, recipe link, or active substitution is proposed.

## Remaining limits

This proposal advances only a species-level culinary leaf identity and source-backed English/Québec French search wording. It does not adjudicate separate Korean and Japanese retail cultivars, green/red color variants, oils, seed powder, dried leaf forms, or measures. It does not claim an OQLF-approved leaf term and does not close Section U’s wider herb, regional aromatic, or form-evidence work.
