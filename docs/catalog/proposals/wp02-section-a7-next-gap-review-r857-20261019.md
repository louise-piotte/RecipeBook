## Recommendation

Keep the InGrain Pastificio statement as an **unallocated, source-local lead**. On its “Organic Kamut” page, which describes Kamut as Khorasan, it says that “this grain” substitutes for durum semolina in all of the pastificio’s pastas. Preserve that direction as stated: **Khorasan/Organic Kamut grain → durum semolina in InGrain’s own pastas**. The page does not say that Khorasan semolina replaces durum semolina, identify a Khorasan-semolina SKU or grade, give an amount, or provide a recipe or process crosswalk. Do not reverse the direction or map the source grain to `ingredient-ref-khorasan-semolina`.

The target-specific manufacturer and research sources establish Khorasan semolina products and semolina use, but do not supply a transferable substitution rule. The 2006 pasta study compares specified Khorasan and durum semolina treatments; it is a processing trial, not an instruction to replace one ingredient with another in a home recipe. The official KAMUT page’s general alternative statement is about KAMUT brand wheat **flour** and modern wheat flour; it separately lists semolina, re-milled semolina, and whole semolina. That flour statement cannot be transferred to the semolina row.

Do not record a no-supported-substitute finding yet. The only explicit replacement claim found is attached to a grain-level product wording that does not establish a semolina form. A ledger finding on the semolina row would imply an unsupported product mapping. Keep the row’s substitution state open until semolina-specific evidence or a source-backed grain-to-semolina crosswalk is available.
