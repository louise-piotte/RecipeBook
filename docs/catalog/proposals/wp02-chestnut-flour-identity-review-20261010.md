# WP02 chestnut-flour identity review

## Scope and coordinator decision

- **Review date:** 2026-10-10
- **Scope:** edible chestnut flour versus chestnuts, chestnut powder, and water-chestnut flour.
- **Disposition:** accept one identity-only chestnut-flour product.
- **Coordinator integration:** add `ingredient-ref-chestnut-flour` as `chestnut flour / farine de châtaigne` in `FLOUR_AND_STARCH`, with no aliases, recipe links, forms, measurements, densities, unit mappings, conversions, yields, or substitutions.

## Catalog and recipe audit

The audit covered 679 active references, 34 forms, 84 legacy candidates, 865 research-ledger records, and all 114 manifest-listed recipes (1,123 ingredient rows). Exact normalized checks found no owner for `chestnut flour` or `farine de châtaigne` in the active references, forms, ledger, or legacy candidates. Neither phrase appears in a bundled recipe ingredient line or recipe text. No recipe relink is proposed.

## Evidence and limits

| Source | Support and limits |
| --- | --- |
| [Markal, Chestnut Flour](https://www.markal.fr/en/product/farcc500-chestnut-flour) | The producer says chestnuts are dried, peeled, sorted, then stone-ground to a fine powder, and lists organic chestnuts as the ingredient. It supports one edible-chestnut flour product and baking uses, not every chestnut species or milling specification. |
| [Foricher, flour products](https://www.foricher.com/en/our-flours) | The mill lists `Farine Châtaigne` and labels it in English as `Chestnut flour`. This confirms the bilingual product class at a flour manufacturer; its listing does not establish broader aliases or measurements. |
| [SIAL Paris, SAGI chestnut products](https://www.sialparis.com/fr-FR/EXHIBITORS-2024/Produit/Chataignes-et-farine-de-chataigne-SAGI-SRL) | The producer listing uses `farine de châtaigne` and describes drying, peeling, and stone milling. This supports French product wording and a second source's production description, not a Québec terminology ruling. |

## Accepted identity

| Field | Accepted value |
| --- | --- |
| Stable ID | `ingredient-ref-chestnut-flour` |
| English display | `chestnut flour` |
| Québec French display | `farine de châtaigne` |
| Aliases | none |
| Category | `FLOUR_AND_STARCH` |

Keep edible whole chestnuts, sweetened chestnut paste, chestnut powder where the source does not call it flour, and water-chestnut products separate. Do not alias `singhara atta`, which identifies a water-chestnut flour product, or assume a universal roast or grind. The French display is source-supported and remains subject to independent Québec terminology review. No measurement or substitution inference is made.
