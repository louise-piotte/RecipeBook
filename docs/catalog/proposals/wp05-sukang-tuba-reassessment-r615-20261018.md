# WP05 — Sukang Tuba boundary reassessment (R615)

- **Status:** Re-reviewed; keep `Sukang Tuba` unresolved and out of active catalog data.
- **Reviewed:** 2026-10-18.
- **Decision:** Do not add `Sukang Tuba` as an alias on generic coconut vinegar or as a separate identity in this review.
- **Scope:** Reassess the R612 hold using Philippine primary sources, a Canadian culinary-use example, and current French product wording. No ingredient-reference or form data changed.

This is French product wording outside Québec, not evidence of a Québec market-wide label. |

The review continues to support the R612 caution: the authoritative coconut-sap definition, a Toronto culinary translation, regional spiced usage, and a French trade listing do not together establish one transferable retail scope or a Québec bilingual product boundary. Keep `Sukang Tuba` out of aliases on generic coconut vinegar and cane vinegar. Do not create a separate active identity, equate it with generic palm vinegar, or fold spiced products into plain vinegar. Revisit only with a Québec-facing product listing or comparably direct bilingual market evidence.

The 114 bundled recipes (1,123 ingredient rows) contain no `Sukang Tuba` ingredient line. No forms, measurements, recipe links, conversions, yields, or substitutions were added.
