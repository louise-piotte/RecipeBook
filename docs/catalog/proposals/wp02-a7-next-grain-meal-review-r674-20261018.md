## Recommendation

Keep the active reference, aliases, and picker behavior unchanged. Add the Delishar recipe as a **source-scoped research lead** on the existing ledger record, but do not record its `1 cup / 128 g` as a measurement observation or treat its all-purpose-flour option as a supported substitution for this row. The recipe calls its ingredient “whole wheat/meal flour,” equates whole wheat and wholemeal flour, and does not specify a coarse meal grade. The active row is specifically the whole-wheat-meal term OQLF pairs with coarse whole wheat flour. The source therefore does not establish that its flour is the cataloged meal.

The integration sets `measurementState` and `substitutionResearchState` to `blocked_pending_evidence`; it adds no measurement observation and adds one blocked, recipe-scoped substitution finding explaining the unresolved grind match. The recipe explicitly says all-purpose flour may replace its whole wheat ingredient in this muffin formula, but that statement cannot be attached to the cataloged whole-wheat meal without confirming the ingredient's grade. No active rule, form, density, unit mapping, recipe link, yield, or conversion was added.
