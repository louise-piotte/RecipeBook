# WP02 Section 04 B3 — native waxy tapioca starch identity review (R196)

- **Reviewed:** 2026-10-16.
- **Status:** Integrated as an identity-only reference and research-ledger record; see the R196 batch README. The French display remains provisional.
- **Open checklist:** WP02 Section 04 B3, [Water-chestnut flour and other documented regional starches](../../RecipeBook-global-ingredient-catalog-plan.txt#L2513). The parent remains open.
- **Candidate:** Starch isolated from waxy cassava (*Manihot esculenta*), marketed as waxy tapioca starch. Keep it separate from ordinary tapioca starch, modified tapioca starch, resistant tapioca starch, whole-root cassava flour, tapioca pearls, and Brazilian polvilho products.
- **New source-ID prefix:** `WP02-SECTION-04-B3-NATIVE-WAXY-TAPIOCA-STARCH-R196-20261016`. The prefix and candidate ID were checked against the live ledger and proposal/batch archive. Reused source IDs retain their existing URL mappings; no existing URL is registered under a second R196 ID.

## Recommendation

**Add identity-only `native waxy tapioca starch / amidon de tapioca cireux natif`** as `ingredient-ref-native-waxy-tapioca-starch` in `FLOUR_AND_STARCH`, with one source-attested English alias, `waxy cassava starch`. A current Canadian B2B supplier explicitly lists waxy tapioca starch as a sourcing option. Its page states that the waxy-starch record covers native grades and distinguishes them from modified starches. The supplier confirms the offered grade, origin, documents, package, and availability only by quotation, so this is evidence of a Canadian food-ingredient sourcing class, not a fixed SKU, consumer package, or inventory guarantee. [ALLZONE-R192, S03]

Primary research independently identifies waxy cassava starch as an isolated food starch from selected waxy cassava varieties and reports composition and functional differences from ordinary cassava starch. The English alias is supported by that research. The proposed French display is a provisional descriptive construction from OQLF's preferred general term `amidon`, Québec's ingredient wording `amidon de tapioca`, and the Canadian terminology `manioc cireux`; no current Québec product label gives the complete waxy-tapioca phrase. Keep independent Québec review open before integration. [OQLF-AMIDON, OQLF-TAPIOCA, S01–S04]

Do not add a form, density, unit mapping, recipe link, conversion, yield, or reusable substitution. The corpus has no matching demand, and the reviewed research does not provide household measurements or a general recipe replacement ratio.
