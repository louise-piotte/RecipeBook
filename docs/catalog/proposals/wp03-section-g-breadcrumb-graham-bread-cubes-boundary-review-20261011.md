## Measurement and substitution boundaries

No default density, unit mapping, package yield, or conversion should be added for any class here.

- Health Canada's CNF food code 4069 reports `bread crumbs, dry, grated, plain` as 27.4 g per 60 mL, 45.6 g per 100 mL, and 114.1 g per 250 mL. This is one source-specific dried-plain-crumb observation, not a packing standard. RecipeBook's [`cup` unit](../../../app/src/main/assets/seed/bundled-library/units.v1.json) is 236.5882 mL, not 250 mL. Do not copy the 250 mL observation to a `cup`, scale it to the app cup, or apply it to fresh crumbs, panko, coarse crumbs, or another bread type without a method and product match.
- The three Graham recipe amounts (175 g, 2 cups, and 1 1/2 cups) are source recipe quantities, not a conversion basis. Current Graham crumb pack sizes likewise do not establish grams per cup or recipe yield.
- The Québec turkey-stuffing recipe's 1 L (4 cups) of bread cubes is a recipe amount for that preparation, not a cube size or mass equivalence. Retail cube package weights and the 567 g stuffing-crouton package are product-specific quantities only.
- Do not infer substitutions among fresh and dried crumbs, panko, cracker crumbs, Graham crumbs, matzo meal, bread cubes, croutons, or boxed stuffing mix. They differ by source material, moisture, grind/cut, texture, seasoning, and/or composition. A single recipe or retailer description is not a general conversion or safe contextual substitution rule.
