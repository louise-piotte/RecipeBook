## Evidence and limits

R723 established the base Canadian product as `Froot Loops cereal / Céréales Froot Loops` with paired WK Kellogg Canada product pages and explicitly left measurements unresearched ([R723 proposal](wp03-section-f-next-open-gap-review-r723-20261018.md)). The English product page currently identifies **Froot Loops® Cereal**, shows 825 g as the selected SmartLabel package, and links to SmartLabel. Its linked English label identifies GTIN `00064100595705`, a package of **825 g**, and states **“Per 1 cup (39 g)”** in Nutrition Facts. The French product page currently identifies **Céréales Froot Loops®** and links to the French SmartLabel record. That record identifies GTIN `00064100144699`, a package of **780 g**, and states **“pour 1 tasse (39 g)”**. The French page's general size row also lists 825 g, while its nutrition selector points to the 780 g SmartLabel record; use the exact SmartLabel GTIN/package fields and do not infer a conversion or equivalence between package sizes.

These are two distinct GTIN-specific label records, not one UPC in two languages. Preserve the separate 825 g and 780 g package descriptions and retain two observations even though the serving declarations agree. Package net mass is not portion mass or package yield. The label pages report a serving, but specify no cup-filling convention, density, or general volume-to-mass conversion. Both SmartLabel pages say product information can change and direct readers to the package for current information; their last-update field renders a template token rather than a usable date. The English page was returned with a four-day-old crawl indicator, while the French page was returned with a five-month-old crawl indicator. Treat both as date-limited manufacturer records, with the French current-retail status especially uncertain, and verify against a current package before reusing either as a current label claim.

The existing English and French WK Kellogg product-page sources are already owned by the R723 ledger record and should be reused as product/link context rather than registered again. The new SmartLabel URLs below have no exact ledger source owner. Their source handles are proposal-local until integration.
