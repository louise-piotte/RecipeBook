## Product and taxonomy boundary

ADM’s product sheets separately identify Native Wheat Starch and Pregel Wheat Starch. The 2024 product sheet lists Paygel 290 and Paygel 300 under Pregel Wheat Starch and describes that product class as cold-water soluble, high viscosity, and requiring no cooking. Its applications include fillings, breading, sauces, and snacks. The 2023 starch portfolio independently lists Paygel 290 as Pregel Wheat Starch. These are supplier product-family descriptions; they do not show a consumer-size starch package or a general household use ratio. [S01–S02]

The 2024 sheet also lists Pregel Wheat Flour (Aquatex) separately as precooked wheat flour. Do not merge that flour with Paygel wheat starch or attach the candidate to the existing wheat-flour identity. A Metro Québec product page names both “amidon de blé prégélatinisé” and “amidon de blé” in the same French ingredient declaration, supporting separate finished-food label terms. This is evidence of a labelled ingredient in a finished product, not a standalone Canadian starch package or proof of current in-store stock. [S01, S03]

Keep the integrated reference distinct from:

| Existing or adjacent identity | Boundary |
|---|---|
| Ordinary wheat starch, ingredient-ref-wheat-starch | Existing broad wheat-starch row; keep its amidon de froment alias. Do not assume the ordinary row is a universal alias for a named pregel product. |
| Modified wheat starch, ingredient-ref-modified-wheat-starch | R121 review and integration established this as a separate broad identity. The reviewed pregel sources do not make “modified” a synonym for “pregelatinized.” |
| Resistant wheat starch, ingredient-ref-resistant-wheat-starch | R122 covers a chemically specified resistant class. Do not fold resistant or cross-linked products into this proposal. |
| Pregelatinized rice starch, ingredient-ref-pregelatinized-rice-starch | R125 is the same type of source-qualified process distinction for rice. Wheat is a different botanical source and the wheat product evidence is independently reviewed. |
| Pregel wheat flour, including Aquatex | Flour product, not starch. No alias, form, or equivalence is proposed. |

The reviewed sources do not establish whether every pregelatinized wheat starch is chemically unmodified or whether every modified wheat starch may also be pregelatinized. Preserve only the exact distinctions the sources support. In particular, do not infer “native,” “unmodified,” or “resistant” from the integrated name.

## English and Québec French wording

The French display **amidon de blé prégélatinisé** is directly attested in the French ingredient statement on Metro’s Québec grocery listing. The same statement also lists ordinary amidon de blé separately. [S03]

OQLF’s Grand dictionnaire terminologique entry prefers **amidon prégélatinisé** and also lists **amidon gélatinisé**. It defines the term generically as starch treated by drying to become soluble in cold water. Its English translations include pre-gelatinized starch, pregelatinized starch, and pregelatinised starch. The OQLF entry is general to starch and does not itself supply the wheat-specific French phrase; the Metro declaration supplies that wheat qualifier. [S04]

Use the English display **pregelatinized wheat starch**. Keep these English search aliases: **pre-gelatinized wheat starch** and **pregelatinised wheat starch**, based on OQLF’s spelling variants, and **pregel wheat starch**, ADM’s exact English product-category wording. Leave French aliases empty because the display is directly present in a Québec product ingredient statement. [S01, S03–S04]

Do not alias the brand names Paygel 290 or Paygel 300, generic “pregelatinized starch,” “precooked wheat starch,” “instant wheat starch,” “cold-water soluble wheat starch,” or the generic OQLF alternative “amidon gélatinisé.” The brand names identify specific products; the generic terms do not preserve the exact source and product boundary of this wheat-starch entry. The OQLF translation list is not a reason to make every generic technical synonym a wheat-specific picker alias.
