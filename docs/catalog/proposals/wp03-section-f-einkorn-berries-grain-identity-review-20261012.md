# WP03 Section F: einkorn-berries grain identity review (2026-10-12)

## Review record

- **Reviewed:** 2026-10-12.
- **Status:** Integrated on 2026-10-12 as identity-only `ingredient-ref-einkorn-berries`; the shared catalog, ledger, generated catalog, plan, roadmap, and integration README record the accepted result. No app or emulator tests were run for this database-JSON addition.
- **Scope:** A separate identity for the edible whole kernels of cultivated einkorn, *Triticum monococcum* subsp. *monococcum*, sold as einkorn berries / petit épeautre. This review does not reopen the generic farro hold or the existing einkorn-flour identity.
- **Accepted decision:** Add an identity-only `GRAIN_AND_CEREAL` reference as **`einkorn berries / grains de petit épeautre`**, with no aliases, forms, measurements, recipe links, conversions, yields, or substitutions. Canadian terminology sources separate cultivated einkorn from emmer and spelt; current Canadian products use `einkorn berries`; and Québec retailers offer the grain in French under `grains de petit épeautre` and `petit épeautre décortiqué`. The French display is supported by local product naming, not an OQLF ruling.
- **Proposed ID:** `ingredient-ref-einkorn-berries` (unallocated in the audited catalog, forms, legacy candidates, and ledger).
- **Plan context:** Section F already keeps generic `farro` unresolved across einkorn, emmer, and spelt and has distinct species-specific grain rows. Its wheat-berries decision also says to keep that broad product separate from species-specific wheat grains ([plan lines 1883–1889](../../RecipeBook-global-ingredient-catalog-plan.txt#L1976)). This packet narrows to a named einkorn food grain and leaves the generic farro decision intact.

## Identity and phrase boundary

The Canadian Food Inspection Agency's cereal-seed procedure treats einkorn, emmer, and spelt as separate covered-wheat kinds [S05]. Health Canada's crop-group table separately lists domesticated einkorn (*T. monococcum* subsp. *monococcum*) and wild einkorn (*T. monococcum* subsp. *aegilopoides*) [existing source `WP03-S181`]. TERMIUM's einkorn record maps *T. monococcum* to English `Einkorn` and French `petit épeautre` and `engrain` [S04]. The current Québec product listings apply `petit épeautre` to a food grain, with one listing explicitly naming the grain product and another identifying the cultivated species [S01–S02].

| Phrase or neighboring identity | Decision | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| `einkorn berries` | **Use as English display.** | Current Canadian food listings sell edible whole einkorn as berries. Do not import organic, origin, package-size, or supplier claims into the display. |
| `grains de petit épeautre` | **Use as fr-CA display.** | Exact wording appears in a Québec retail product title; a second Québec seller lists `petit épeautre décortiqué biologique` in its grains category and identifies its product as *T. monococcum*. This is Québec product usage, not an OQLF-preferred-term ruling. |
| `einkorn grain`, `einkorn kernels`, `whole einkorn` | **Do not add aliases in this packet.** | The sources establish the grain identity but the Canadian consumer listings reviewed use `einkorn berries`; no recipe search demand requires extra English wording. The display already contains the exact product name. |
| `einkorn wheat berries` | **Keep as source wording; do not alias.** | A longer alias would also match the shorter `wheat berries` query in the current picker. That query belongs to the existing broad `wheat berries / grains de blé entiers` row. |
| `einkorn` / `petit épeautre` by themselves | **Do not add as aliases.** | These species terms already occur within the separate flour display and the proposed grain display. A broad query may show both products; the complete `einkorn flour` / `farine de petit épeautre` and `einkorn berries` / `grains de petit épeautre` phrases keep product scope explicit. |
| `engrain`, `small spelt`, `spelt`, `épeautre`, `farro` | **Do not add.** | TERMIUM records `engrain` and `small spelt` for einkorn, but they are not the current Québec food-product titles. `épeautre` belongs to common spelt, and `farro` spans einkorn, emmer, and spelt plus grain/flour contexts. Preserve the existing farro candidate and spelt identities. |
| `wheat berries / grains de blé entiers` | **Leave unchanged.** | The existing row remains the broader wheat-kernel product. This proposal does not use it as an alias for einkorn, nor infer that every wheat-berry product is einkorn. |
| Einkorn flour / `farine de petit épeautre` | **Leave unchanged and separate.** | The existing `ingredient-ref-einkorn-flour` row is a verified milling product. Whole edible kernels and flour are separate catalog identities, not forms or aliases of one another. |
| Emmer / spelt / wild einkorn | **Keep separate.** | Emmer (*T. turgidum* subsp. *dicoccon*), spelt (*T. aestivum* subsp. *spelta*), and wild einkorn are distinct from the proposed cultivated einkorn grain. |

The picker boundary above is a code-path inference, not a live picker test. [`IngredientSearch.kt`](../../../app/src/main/java/app/recipebook/domain/localization/IngredientSearch.kt#L23) matches a query's complete normalized words within a longer indexed term before its partial-match fallback. If `einkorn wheat berries` were added as an alias, searching `wheat berries` would also return the species-qualified row. Broad `einkorn` and `petit épeautre` queries returning both the grain and flour is expected; fully qualified queries distinguish them.
