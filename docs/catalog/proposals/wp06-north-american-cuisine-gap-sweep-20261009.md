## Scope and reading rules

This is a source-backed gap sweep, not a fixed-quota list and not a claim to cover all North American foodways. It separates Québec culinary usage, broader Canadian and United States material, and named Indigenous communities/traditions. “Indigenous” is not treated as one cuisine. The source set includes, among other contexts, Mi’kmaq of Nova Scotia, Métis of Batoche, Ojibwe and Dakota food references, Inuvialuit sources, Inuit community contributors from Kinngait and Mittimatalik, Cherokee sources from the Eastern Band of the Cherokee Indians, and O’odham and Piipaash sources from the Salt River Pima-Maricopa Indian Community. Each citation supports only the region, author, community, and product scope it actually names.

Candidate dispositions use the plan’s distinctions: existing identity, alias, distinct identity, compound/product family, unresolved, or out-of-scope. “Advance” means advance for coordinator review; it does not reserve an ID or integrate a row. English or fr-CA wording is marked provisional when the available evidence does not establish Québec usage. Native and community-specific names are retained as source-reported terms, not promoted into global aliases without the relevant language/community review.

The live roadmap still describes WP05 as active and carries the earlier 457-reference / 624-ledger-record snapshot. The seed package and ledger currently have the post-integration counts below. This proposal records the live files and does not update the roadmap or plan.

### Recommendation grouping

**Advance to coordinator identity review:** maple sugar; maple butter; tire d’érable; fruit ketchup; cretons; Saskatoon berry; tepary beans; mesquite pod flour; ramps; chayote/mirliton; filé powder; seal meat; caribou; Arctic char; eel. This is a set of candidate decisions, not a request to integrate all of them. The animal products should route through WP04; the prepared spreads/confections need category and ingredient-vs-prepared-food review.

**Keep existing or under another packet:** maple syrup, wild rice, dried yellow peas, generic ketchup, and coffee are existing identities. Fava beans/gourganes remain with the WP03 plant/legume batch. No candidate in this sweep requires relinking a recipe.

**Reject as broad aliases or global equivalences:** fruit ketchup → ketchup; maple butter → dairy butter; maple sugar → granulated/brown sugar; ramps → garden leek; manoomin/psín → unscoped universal aliases; chayote/mirliton → unrelated squash; bannock/luski/bangs → one interchangeable bread; seal fat → generic oil; any ingredient-to-ingredient gumbo thickener replacement. These boundaries do not reject the named foods; they reject unsupported aliasing or substitution.

**Keep unresolved:** maple flakes, grated maple sugar, maple water, maple vinegar, maple alcohol, green-tomato/cranberry ketchup boundaries, cretons’ animal/plant subfamilies, chokecherry, manoomin/psín alias scope, O’odham product-specific flour naming, ciolim, aqpiit, rendered seal fat, exact fr-CA terms, and Andouille/tasso/roux review.

## Measurement, substitution, and preservation boundaries

Identity evidence is separate from measurement and substitution. This packet imports no source quantity, density, unit mapping, package yield, dry-to-cooked yield, or substitution ratio. Where a linked recipe publishes paired units, those remain recipe-specific and are not transferred to a catalog identity. The only quantity repeated in the audit is the live corpus line “2 cups dried yellow peas”; its source row already targets the existing yellow-pea identity and provides no additional conversion here.

The Eel Stew page’s fish alternative is a single Parks Canada recipe adaptation. SFA describes variation in gumbo thickening and optional filé, which does not prove interchangeable quantities or universal replacement. No other substitution is proposed. Fruit ketchup’s source recipe and jar language do not establish safe canning, shelf stability, or preservation equivalence. No food-safety or dietary claim is made.
