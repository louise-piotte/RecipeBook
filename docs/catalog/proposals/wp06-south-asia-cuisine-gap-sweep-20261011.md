# WP06 South Asia cuisine gap sweep proposal — 2026-10-11

**Status:** proposal only; three identity-only candidates for coordinator review. No seed, ledger, recipe, test, code, plan, or checklist changes are requested by this packet.
**Scope:** India by region, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Bhutan, and Maldives; savory dishes, breads/baking and desserts, condiments/preserved foods, festive foods, and cooking beverages where represented in the reviewed sample.
**Research date:** 2026-10-11. External sources below were opened or rechecked online on that date; each source entry gives its URL and access date.

## Scope, live-state reconciliation, and benchmark

This is a bounded source-led sweep, not a claim of complete South Asian coverage. India is represented through Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Bengal/Eastern India, Tamil Nadu/Southern India, and the western Konkan coast. Bangladesh shares Bengali source contexts with West Bengal, but its recipe evidence is treated as source-specific. The Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Bhutan, and Maldives samples each use regional or official sources plus selected recipe/product evidence. A small sample cannot establish a national formula or absence of an ingredient.

The live split seed package read on 2026-10-11 contains **913 ingredient references, 51 ingredient forms, and 114 bundled recipe files / 1,123 ingredient lines**. Exact phrase scanning of those ingredient lines for the new identities, prior held candidates, and the kokum-family names recorded below returned **zero hits**. These external sources therefore demonstrate regional usage, not RecipeBook recipe demand. No recipe link is proposed.

The current live rows checked for collisions include `ingredient-ref-gooseberry` (`gooseberry / groseille à maquereau`), `ingredient-ref-paneer`, `ingredient-ref-pandan-leaves`, `ingredient-ref-millet-flour`, `ingredient-ref-finger-millet-flour`, `ingredient-ref-pearl-millet-flour`, `ingredient-ref-fenugreek-leaves`, `ingredient-ref-date-syrup`, `ingredient-ref-cane-jaggery`, `ingredient-ref-tamarind`, `ingredient-ref-pomegranate`, and `ingredient-ref-pomegranate-molasses`. These rows are not duplicate coverage for the species- or product-bounded candidates below: in particular, generic gooseberry does not resolve Indian gooseberry, fresh pomegranate does not resolve dried anardana, and active date syrup or cane jaggery does not resolve regional date-palm products.

The prior WP06 packet and its integration batch were reconciled before this review. The following twelve identities are already integrated and are not repeated as new rows: `ingredient-ref-cumin-seeds`, `ingredient-ref-curry-leaves`, `ingredient-ref-fenugreek-seeds`, `ingredient-ref-ajwain`, `ingredient-ref-nigella-seeds`, `ingredient-ref-large-cardamom`, `ingredient-ref-ghee`, `ingredient-ref-mustard-oil`, `ingredient-ref-finger-millet-flour`, `ingredient-ref-panch-phoron`, `ingredient-ref-rihaakuru`, and `ingredient-ref-timur-pepper` [P01, P02]. Generic millet flour, previously held in that packet, is now active as `ingredient-ref-millet-flour` in the later WP02 regional-grain work [P03]. Keep generic, finger-, and pearl-millet flour distinct.

The relevant WP02/WP05 reviews also remain in force: amchur powder, ginger-garlic paste, sweet mango chutney, mango and lime pickles, pandan leaves, and the prior MENA/Caucasus condiment rows are not new here. Mixed pickle and kasundi remain pending/unintegrated candidates; this packet does not reopen them. See the referenced prior WP05 packets for their exact source records and decisions [P04].
