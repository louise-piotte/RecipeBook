# WP05 independent review: Québec French display for Mexican adobo sauce

## Scope and recommendation

- **Review and source-access date:** 2026-10-11.
- **Scope:** Independently assess the proposed French display `sauce adobo mexicaine` for the narrow Mexican adobo sauce candidate in the [WP05 boundary proposal](wp05-adobo-sauce-quebec-boundary-review-20261011.md). This is a display review only.
- **Recommendation:** **Accept `sauce adobo mexicaine` as provisional wording only.** No better-attested alternate surfaced. The exact phrase was not found as a Québec product label or an OQLF term for a standalone Mexican sauce, so it should not be represented as established or officially approved Québec terminology.

The OQLF recognizes the masculine noun `adobo`, but its Latin American entry defines a spicy marinade of vinegar, garlic, and dried chiles. A separate OQLF entry defines the Filipino dish also called `adobo`. Neither entry names a standalone Mexican sauce product. [S01–S02]

Québec recipe French does use `sauce adobo`; one Metro recipe describes a chipotle pepper “dans une sauce adobo.” That supports the phrase as locally intelligible food language, while placing it in the chipotle-in-adobo context. It does not verify the exact product label `sauce adobo mexicaine`. [S03]

The proposed phrase is a transparent display: `sauce` names the wet product state, `adobo` preserves the food name, and `mexicaine` scopes the regional sense. Keep the qualifier attached. Do not add bare `adobo` or `sauce adobo` as aliases, since Québec sources apply the term to a Filipino cooking sauce or dish, dry seasoning, paste, and the sauce accompanying chipotles. [S02, S04–S08]

## Wording decision and limits

| Field | Independent review |
|---|---|
| English display | `Mexican adobo sauce` (as stated in the source proposal) |
| Québec French display | `sauce adobo mexicaine` — acceptable as a provisional catalog display only |
| Aliases | None; do not add `adobo` or `sauce adobo` |
| Alternate wording | None recommended. `sauce mexicaine à l’adobo` is not better supported by the sources reviewed and can suggest a sauce flavored with adobo rather than the named sauce product. |
| Product boundary | Separately identified wet Mexican adobo sauce only; exclude Filipino adobo dishes and cooking sauces, dry adobo seasoning, adobo paste, and chipotles packaged in adobo sauce. |

If the catalog requires an established Québec label, hold the French display until a current Québec bilingual package, manufacturer label, or authoritative terminology source directly documents the standalone Mexican sauce. If provisional display wording is permitted, retain `sauce adobo mexicaine` with no alias and mark it provisional. This review makes no claim that the exact phrase is absent from every retailer or package; the search was bounded, and retail listings change by location and over time.

The source proposal reports no standalone adobo-sauce line in the 114-recipe corpus and one existing `chipotle in adobo` line (`007#3`). This display review found no new recipe evidence and does not recommend relinking that line or changing any measurement behavior. [S10]
