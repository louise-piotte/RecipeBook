# WP03 Section F R40 — Barkoukch product-boundary hold

- **Reviewed:** 2026-10-13.
- **Status:** integrated documentation-only hold; no catalog or ledger data changed.
- **Open checklist:** the regional cereal-mix and preprocessed-grain item remains open at [the live Section F checklist](../../RecipeBook-global-ingredient-catalog-plan.txt#L2555).
- **Candidate:** Moroccan `Barkoukch`, currently marketed as `Barkoukch blé dur` / `Hard Wheat Barkoukch`; separate sellers also list `Barkoukch blé complet` / `Whole Wheat Barkoukch`.
- **Decision:** keep Barkoukch unallocated pending a clearer product boundary from the active `pearl couscous / couscous perlé` identity and a Québec-facing French product name. Do not add Barkoukch as an alias to either couscous identity. This review allocates no reference, candidate, ledger record, form, measure, recipe link, or substitution.
- **Provisional naming evidence:** current English marketplace wording is `Hard Wheat Barkoukch`; current Moroccan French seller wording includes `Barkoukch blé dur` and describes it as `couscous à gros grains`. These are source-attested names/descriptions, not an OQLF ruling or a verified Québec display. If a later review accepts a distinct product identity, `Barkoukch (Moroccan large-grain couscous) / Barkoukch (couscous marocain à gros grains)` is a descriptive candidate only.

## Why this is a distinct open candidate

The live Section F checklist has no Barkoukch-specific child decision. R38 reviews protected five-cereal `Keskes Khoumassi`; R39 holds the separate Southern Moroccan `Toulati` product. Neither reviews this durum-wheat/whole-wheat product family. The focused plan, roadmap, proposal/batch archive, active reference catalog, forms, legacy identities, research ledger, and recipe package were searched for `Barkoukch`, `Barkoukch blé dur`, `Hard Wheat Barkoukch`, `Whole Wheat Barkoukch`, and spelling neighbors. No prior Barkoukch decision or exact active owner was found.

Current Moroccan seller pages identify Barkoukch as a hand-rolled or large-grain cereal product and show separate durum-wheat and whole-wheat listings [S01–S05]. The evidence is enough to keep it in the Section F review, but not enough to decide whether the catalog should model Barkoukch as a distinct regional product or as a regional preparation within the existing pearl-couscous product family. A Québec government directory already describes one Québec-market large-pearl-couscous SKU and lists `Israeli`, `Jerusalem`, `Mograbieh`, and `ptitim` as names for that product [S06]. That record does not mention Barkoukch or establish equivalence. OQLF's existing definition of generic `couscous` is agglomerated durum-wheat semolina, steamed and dried [S07]; it likewise does not define Barkoukch.

The French display cannot yet be called Québec-preferred. Targeted searches of Québec terminology and Canadian/Québec retail sources found no Barkoukch-specific Québec label. The Moroccan French wording and an English-language Moroccan marketplace title support source wording only. Keep `Barkoukch`, `berkoukes`, `berkoukech`, `berkoukch`, `Aïch`, `avazine`, `mardoud`, and `mhammsa` unassigned across rows: the sources do not establish that these names identify one product across regions. A recent Algerian study documents `Aïch` and several locally reported names, but it studies eastern Algeria and does not crosswalk those names to the Moroccan Barkoukch product [S08]. UNESCO's couscous nomination also notes a thicker `bercoukès` variant with its own conditions and traditions, but does not identify it as this seller's Barkoukch [S09].

## Measurement, form, and substitution limits

- **Composition and variants:** one Ghalla product page names natural durum-wheat semolina for its specific `Barkoukch Blé Dur` SKU; Dar Sebti and Khayr Lablad separately list durum-wheat and whole-wheat versions [S01–S03]. Do not infer that all Barkoukch products share one flour composition or map whole wheat as a form of the durum-wheat SKU.
- **Package size:** the source pages list 500 g and/or 1 kg packages. Those are SKU package amounts only, not household measures, servings, density, unit mappings, or cooking yield. Ghalla's page marked its item unavailable when checked; its package listing is not a current availability claim [S02].
- **Forms and measurements:** activate none. Hand-rolling and natural drying are documented for a Moroccan seller's products, but a common product state, moisture specification, cooking method, and reusable mass-volume conversion are not established across the candidate family. Do not transfer the Québec pearl-couscous product's ingredients, 45 g nutrition panel, or 15–25-minute cooking direction to Barkoukch [S06].
- **Substitutions:** add no rule. No reviewed source validates a ratio or method for replacing pearl couscous, generic couscous, pasta, barley couscous, Khoumassi, or another grain product with Barkoukch.
