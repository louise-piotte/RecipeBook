## Why this is a distinct open regional boundary

The open [Section P checklist](../../RecipeBook-global-ingredient-catalog-plan.txt#L6041) lists fish powder, fish balls, and surimi while keeping regional seafood review open. The [broader progress note](../../RecipeBook-global-ingredient-catalog-plan.txt#L12319) calls for continuing that work. Existing decisions already cover fish balls, fish tofu, Bonga dried fish powder, crab-flavoured pollock flakes, and the recent canned-crab form review. Earlier reviews also held generic fish powder and unqualified surimi. `Tuyo` is a separate Filipino dried-fish term, so it is a distinct candidate within the regional seafood part of the gap rather than a duplicate of those formed, powdered, surimi, imitation-crab, or canned products.

A focused search of prior proposal and batch Markdown found no `tuyo`, `tuyong isda`, or `tuyô` review. The evidence establishes a recognized regional dried-salted fish name and Canadian listings using it, but it does not establish one species-independent catalog scope or a Québec French product name. The bundled corpus also has no matching request. Keep the candidate unallocated until a recipe or exact product request selects the relevant fish and product state.
