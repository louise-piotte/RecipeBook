# WP02 B2 R184 — *Artocarpus camansi* breadnut-seed flour boundary hold

- **Reviewed:** 2026-10-16.
- **Status:** Integrated hold (2026-10-16). No active reference or ledger record was added; plan and roadmap tracking are updated in the [R184 integration report](../batches/wp02-wp03-wp04-r184-gap-review-integration-20261016/README.md).
- **Plan fit:** WP02 Section 04 B2 remains open for alternative flours. This review addresses flour milled from edible seeds of *Artocarpus camansi* (breadnut), a product distinct from the *A. altilis* flesh flour and the *A. camansi* seed-starch candidate. The R137 review addressed seed starch, not flour. R180 addressed seed flour from *Brosimum alicastrum*, not this accepted *Artocarpus* species. The R78 breadfruit-flour proposal mentions breadnut seed flour as a separate excluded material; it did not assess whether the seed flour has a current Canadian product identity or Québec French name.
- **Proposal-local references:** This hold creates no active or legacy ID and no shared ledger source ID. Existing Kew, Maxi, and primary-study sources are reused from the R137 / breadfruit-flour ledger evidence as noted below.

## Recommendation

**Hold *A. camansi* seed flour outside active references and the research ledger until a current Canadian/Québec culinary flour product and product-matched Québec French flour name are established.** Primary research demonstrates seed flour and tests it in flour blends, and Kew recognizes *A. camansi* as a distinct accepted species. Maxi's English page titles the whole-produce SKU “Breadnut”; its Québec French page for the same product number, `20866306001_KG`, titles it “Châtaigne.” Both pages describe whole produce to be cooked, and neither identifies the species or a seed-flour product. The product description on the French page also uses `fruit à pain`, reinforcing that these retail terms do not establish the seed-flour identity. The Québec-facing whole-produce title is therefore known, but a product-matched Québec French name for the proposed flour remains unresolved. The accent-folded `châtaigne` title also collides with existing chestnut product names. [S01–S04]

Do not add or reserve `ingredient-ref-artocarpus-camansi-seed-flour`; do not assign `breadnut flour`, `breadnut seed flour`, `kamansi flour`, `kluwih flour`, `chataigne flour`, or constructed French flour phrases as displays or aliases. Keep *A. camansi* flour distinct from *A. altilis* fruit-flesh flour and starch, *A. camansi* seed starch, fresh Maxi “Breadnut,” and *B. alicastrum* / Maya-nut flour. No forms, measurements, mappings, recipe links, conversions, yields, or substitutions are proposed.

Reopen if a Canadian/Québec human-food package or supplier specification explicitly identifies culinary flour made from *A. camansi* seeds and a Québec-facing label or authoritative terminology source supports the French name for that same flour.

## Numeric, form, and substitution boundaries

- **Forms:** Add none. The studies’ cooking, dehulling, drying, milling, and sieving procedures describe how particular research samples were prepared; they do not establish consumer forms or reusable flour preparation states. [S02]
- **Measures and density:** Add none. No product-matched Canadian/Québec flour household measure, cup weight, or bulk density was found. Do not transfer Maxi’s whole-produce nutrition serving or study composition data into a mass-volume mapping or default density. [S02–S03]
- **Substitution:** Add none. Alcon et al.’s 50:50 flour blend and other individual study formulations are recipe/formula-specific research. They do not validate a general replacement ratio for wheat flour, breadfruit flour, or another seed flour. No global or contextual substitution, yield, or recipe-line link is supported. [S02]

## Reopen conditions

1. Locate a current Canadian or Québec consumer flour package or supplier specification explicitly identifying culinary flour from *A. camansi* seeds.
2. Confirm a product-matched Québec French name; do not infer one from `breadnut`, Caribbean `chataigne`, `fruit à pain`, or plant-name terminology.
3. Reassess every proposed display/alias against the live picker, especially broad `breadnut` and accent-folded `châtaigne`, before considering a reference.
4. Reassess forms, measurements, recipe demand, and substitutions against the actual flour product. Keep laboratory composition and formula percentages source-specific.
5. Leave WP02 Section 04 B2 open; this hold does not close the alternative-flour coverage item.
