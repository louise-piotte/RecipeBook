# WP03 Section I — edible lotus seed and fox nut identity boundary review

- **Reviewed:** 2026-10-10
- **Status:** Proposal only; no catalog, ledger, plan, recipe, or code changes are part of this review.
- **Scope:** Edible seeds of sacred lotus (*Nelumbo nucifera*) against fox nut/makhana (*Euryale ferox*), including product states, aliases, related lotus-root records, measurement evidence, and bundled-recipe demand.

## Recommendation

Treat *Nelumbo nucifera* edible lotus seeds and *Euryale ferox* fox nut/makhana as two distinct ingredient identities. Kew accepts them as different species in different families: *Nelumbo nucifera* in Nelumbonaceae and *Euryale ferox* in Nymphaeaceae. Kew and France’s National Museum of Natural History describe edible seed use for *N. nucifera*; Indian commodity guidance and the European Commission identify fox nut/makhana as *E. ferox*. The EU specification defines the familiar roasted-popped snack as the edible kernel released from the *E. ferox* seed.

Recommend a species-anchored, identity-only *N. nucifera* candidate, with these provisional displays:

| Candidate | Exact display | Exact qualified aliases to consider | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Edible sacred-lotus seeds | English: **sacred lotus seeds (Nelumbo nucifera)**; fr-CA: **graines de lotus sacré (Nelumbo nucifera)** | English: **seeds of Nelumbo nucifera**, **Nelumbo nucifera seeds**; French: **graines de Nelumbo nucifera**, **graine de lotus sacré** | Botanical identity and food use are supported. The French display is a transparent, provisional fr-CA description, not an OQLF-approved term; independent Québec terminology review remains open. |

Keep the following boundaries:

1. Do not merge *N. nucifera* seeds with *E. ferox* makhana, fox nuts, or gorgon nuts. Do not add shared aliases between them.
2. Do not add lotus-seed aliases to the existing makhana identity or its roasted-popped form. In particular, leave **lotus seed(s)** and **graines de lotus soufflées** off the makhana aliases. Canadian retail listings use the latter wording for branded makhana, but that does not make the species or product identical.
3. Keep **lotus root / racine de lotus** and lotus-root starch separate from the seeds. The root listing refers to a rhizome vegetable; the starch is an extracted product.
4. Treat food-grade culinary seeds as distinct from planting seeds sold for germination and from the dry lotus receptacle used as an ornamental bouquet. Species alone cannot establish that a package is food grade.
5. Do not add a form, active measurement mapping, density, process yield, substitution, or recipe link in this packet. Source-specific measure observations exist for broad *Nelumbo* seed records, but recipe demand and product-detail coverage do not support a general catalog conversion.

For the proposed lotus identity, keep bare **lotus seed(s)**, **lotus nut(s)**, and **graines de lotus** out of alias fields pending Québec review of the documented retail collision. The display itself should retain the species qualifier. Species-qualified aliases above are the safe candidates. Do not put **makhana**, **fox nut(s)**, **gorgon nut**, or **graines de makhana** on the lotus identity.

## Current catalog, ledger, and proposal audit

Snapshot read on 2026-10-10:

- The bundled package contains **712 ingredient references** and **34 ingredient forms**. The only active reference names containing lotus are ingredient-ref-lotus-root and ingredient-ref-lotus-root-starch. The existing makhana reference is ingredient-ref-makhana-seeds with English aliases fox nut, fox nuts, and gorgon nut; its form is ingredient-form-makhana-roasted-popped, labeled “roasted, popped makhana kernels” / “graines de makhana grillées et soufflées.”
- The legacy candidate file contains **84 candidates** and no match for lotus seed, lotus seeds, lotus nut, makhana, fox nut, gorgon nut, *Euryale*, or *Nelumbo*.
- The research ledger contains **901 records** and **8 unit-conversion entries**. Relevant records cover the lotus-root vegetable, lotus-root starch, makhana seed identity, and makhana roasted-popped form. There is no lotus-seed identity or form record and no matching lotus-seed unit-conversion record.
- No dedicated *Nelumbo* seed proposal was found. The directly relevant prior proposal, [WP03 makhana / fox nut identity review](wp03-makhana-fox-nut-identity-20261009.md), was integrated on 2026-10-09. It expressly excluded lotus seed(s) and graines de lotus soufflées as makhana aliases, documented the retailer collision, and said any later lotus-seed identity should be species qualified. The WP03 tree-nut coordinator review also keeps generic “nuts” generic and fox nut/makhana apart from tree nuts. The WP02 regional-starches gap review keeps lotus-root starch separate from the rhizome vegetable; it does not cover edible seeds.

The bare exact key lotus seed has no current reference or legacy owner. A partial lotus query can still surface the current lotus-root and lotus-root-starch references. The species-qualified display is therefore important even though the seed identity is new.

## Search notes and limitations

- Reviewed the active Section I checklist and the current reference, form, legacy-candidate, and research-ledger files on 2026-10-10.
- Compared all reference names and aliases, form labels and match terms, legacy names and aliases, and related ledger records for the scoped common names and botanical names.
- Searched Kew for both accepted taxa; searched official French and English terms for lotus seed and makhana; checked OQLF for a Québec French seed term. No OQLF entry for lotus seed or makhana was located.
- Rechecked the retail collision in the prior proposal; browser retrieval of the Metro and Walmart pages failed on 2026-10-10, so their prior observation is retained with an explicit date and source limitation.
- This proposal does not infer Canadian regulatory status from the EU regulation, food-grade status from ornamental or planting material, or recipe behavior from botanical/nutrient data.

**Proposed decision:** accept the taxonomic/product boundary and species-qualified lotus-seed candidate; keep the makhana identity and roasted-popped form unchanged; hold French alias finalization for independent Québec review; add no forms or active measurements until a recipe or tighter product-specific need justifies them.
