## Evidence and naming boundary

The Indian Institute of Millets Research's finger-millet leaflet identifies *Eleusine coracana* and lists `Ragulu` among its Telugu common names. This is primary crop-name evidence for the grain alias; it does not name a flour or establish a Canadian/Québec label. [S01]

Akkadi Roots' current maker page identifies its product as “Finger Millet Flour” and says this flour is known as “Ragulu Pindi (Telugu).” The same description ties the regional phrase to finger-millet flour, so it supports the qualified alias rather than a new flour identity. This exact phrase was not located on a Canadian or Québec product page; the existing Canadian evidence supports the broader finger-millet-flour identity and its retained French display, not this Telugu alias. Use the maker page for naming only; make no stock or package-availability claim. The source is already attached to the flour ledger record for the earlier `Mandua Atta` review, but its current ledger locator/support text does not yet capture `Ragulu Pindi`; refresh that same entry if the proposal is integrated. [S02]

The current French display pair remains `finger millet / éleusine cultivée` and `finger millet flour / farine de ragi`. Existing Health Canada and Québec-context sources support `éleusine cultivée`, and an existing Canadian French-facing flour listing supports `farine de ragi`. No French alias, translation of the Telugu terms, or display change is proposed. [S03–S04]

Keep this as a regional searchable-name addition. Do not create a new reference, flour grade, form, or recipe link. Do not add `ragulu flour`, `ragulu atta`, `ragula pindi`, `ragi pindi`, or bare `pindi`: these spellings/constructions are not needed to represent the exact source-backed pair in this review. In particular, a different current maker spells its product wording `Ragula Pindi`; do not normalize that distinct romanization into this exact `Ragulu` decision without a separate spelling review.
