- **Date:** 2026-10-10
- **Status:** Proposal only. No catalog, ledger, recipe, test, plan, or roadmap changes are made by this packet.
- **Scope:** Reconcile the East African/Horn and North African fat, fermented dairy, starter, and preservation candidates in the WP06 sweep and Section Z. Review the open Eritrean tesmi / Ethiopian niter kibbeh boundary. Awaze is already integrated and excluded.

The active reference and ledger retain that boundary. The later awaze review says niter kibbeh is already integrated and outside that review; awaze is integrated and remains excluded here.

Section Z still has the parent “Ethiopian/Eritrean niter kibbeh and awaze; additional North/Southern/East African sauces and souring ingredients” open. Its awaze child is checked, but no separate niter-kibbeh child is checked even though the prior WP06 checkpoint integrated the Ethiopian reference. This packet records that plan state and does not edit it. The broader WP06 Section 05 regional item also remains open.

The earlier African sweep routed Moroccan smen to WP05’s Middle Eastern/North African fermented-dairy scope. This review supplies source evidence for that route but does not change ownership. WP05 still holds preserved lemon until recipe need and a precise salt-preserved product form are established. Awaze, unrelated starch/grain work, and animal-product reviews remain outside this packet.

## Current references, ledger, and recipe demand

The active ingredient-ref-niter-kibbeh row is named “niter kibbeh” in English and French, with no aliases, no density, empty unit mappings, and no form. Its ledger record is verified; measurement and substitution research remain not_yet_researched. Its accepted decision is Ethiopia-specific and says not to generalize the Ethiopian source to Eritrea or treat it as a standard formula. The recorded source is the Ethiopian Ministry of Labor and Skills module, December 2022, under existing source ID WP06-AFRICA-GAP-20261010-S14.

Relevant active neighbors remain distinct: ghee (ghee / beurre de ghee, English alias “ghee butter”), unsalted and salted butter, buttermilk (babeurre), plain and Greek-style yogurt, sour cream (crème sure), lemon, tamarind, and generic sourdough starter (sourdough starter / levain naturel). No current form label or match term exactly matches the candidate regional names. The ledger mentions niter kibbeh only in the existing niter and ghee boundary decisions; it has no separate tesmi, smen, ergo, or ersho record. The legacy candidate file has no exact whole-name match for the reviewed candidates.

All 114 manifest-listed recipe files and all 1,123 ingredient rows were scanned with Unicode normalization, accent removal, punctuation-to-space conversion, and whole-phrase matching. There are **zero full-file matches and zero ingredient-line matches** for niter kibbeh, niter kibe, niter qibe, nit'ir qibe, tesmi, smen, sman, semn, semneh, ergo, dhanaan, ititu, arrera, hazo, kocho, enset, injera, ersho, lben, rayeb, preserved lemon, preserved lemons, citron confit, clarified butter, or spiced butter.

These generic rows do not establish demand for a regional identity or a substitution. The only Africa-titled recipe is the African peanut stew; its peanut-butter and garnish lines are unrelated to this fat/souring review.

## Candidate decisions for coordinator review

| Candidate / bilingual wording | Evidence-based boundary | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| **Existing niter kibbeh** — EN niter kibbeh; fr-CA niter kibbeh. “Beurre clarifié épicé” is an explanatory rendering, not an alias. | The Ethiopian Ministry module and Montréal food profile support an Ethiopian spiced clarified-butter identity. Keep distinct from generic ghee, clarified butter, and plain butter. | Keep the existing row and current no-alias state. No new identity. Do not add niter kibe, niter qibe, or nit'ir qibe without a stronger transliteration source. |
| **Tesmi** — candidate EN tesmi (Eritrean spiced clarified butter); provisional fr-CA tesmi (beurre clarifié épicé érythréen). | Eritrean food-culture material defines tesmi as clarified butter with onion, garlic, and mild spices, and describes its use with ge’at. A Danish cultural-center document’s indexed heading crosswalks Tigrinya tesmi with Amharic niter kibbeh, while a 2014 Tigray food-consumption questionnaire lists “Butter (Tesmi)” without defining spice or product state. The evidence supports the Eritrean name, but does not settle whether it should be an alias of niter kibbeh or a separate identity. | **Hold.** Add neither a tesmi alias to the Ethiopia-scoped reference nor a separate row. Request a terminology/product-scope decision with a second direct Eritrean source before mapping. |
| **Smen** — candidate EN smen (Moroccan fermented butter); provisional fr-CA smen (beurre fermenté marocain). | A Moroccan dairy review distinguishes smen (fermented butter) from fresh butter, fermented milk, coagulated fermented milk, and cheese. A French-language recipe uses the borrowed term with the gloss “beurre fermenté.” Keep distinct from ghee, niter kibbeh, plain butter, lben, and rayeb. | **Route to WP05 Section Y** as the earlier African sweep directed. This is a source-backed identity candidate, not a WP06 addition; no alias, form, numeric data, or recipe link is proposed. |
| **Ergo** — candidate EN ergo (Ethiopian fermented milk); provisional fr-CA ergo (lait fermenté éthiopien). | A 2024 Ethiopian fermented-milk review identifies ergo as spontaneously fermented whole milk and distinguishes it from ititu, ayib, qibe, arrera, and hazo. It does not establish ergo as a general recipe acid or replacement for yogurt, buttermilk, or sour cream. | **Hold for recipe demand and naming review.** No exact recipe demand exists in this package. Do not alias it to yogurt, buttermilk, or sour cream. |
| **Ersho** — candidate EN ersho (Ethiopian injera starter); provisional fr-CA ersho (ferment de départ éthiopien pour l’injera). | A 2024 study describes ersho as fluid saved from previously fermented injera sourdough and used as a back-slopping starter. This is a source-named regional starter, not injera itself and not a general souring agent. | **Hold for WP02 starter-owner review.** The current generic sourdough-starter row and its naming boundary are under separate review. Do not add an alias, new identity, or baker’s-yeast substitution in this packet. |
| **Lben, rayeb, ititu, arrera, hazo, and dhanaan** | Regional dairy sources distinguish fermented milk, curd, buttermilk, spiced buttermilk, and camel-milk products. Existing generic dairy rows do not absorb these names. | Keep each term source-scoped. No alias, new identity, recipe link, or substitution is proposed here. |
| **Preserved lemon / citron confit** | The existing WP05 hold remains: no matching recipe need and no exact salt-preserved product form established for catalog use. | Leave unmodeled under the existing WP05 decision. No preservation instruction or replacement rule is proposed. |
