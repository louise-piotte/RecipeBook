## Identity boundary

Include the food-grade flour made by milling defatted corn germ or a defatted corn-germ meal. The literature uses this named flour in cookies, muffins, bread, and pasta experiments. The identity should remain qualified as **defatted**: unqualified “corn germ flour” can blur full-fat germ, differently processed germ fractions, and oil-extraction residues.

This candidate is not a general corn-germ identity and does not assert a uniform commercial specification. Research samples and milling streams vary, and the studies do not establish a standardized residual-oil level or a single process. The 1983 study describes milling and screening a commercial defatted meal into a food-grade flour; that process and its analyzed composition belong to that study. The 1973 and 1974 food-formulation findings likewise remain specific to their experimental materials and formulas.

Keep nearby catalog identities unchanged:

- `ingredient-ref-toasted-corn-germ` is the separately qualified toasted human-food ingredient. It is not an alias or form of this defatted flour.
- `ingredient-ref-corn-oil` retains the existing French alias `huile de germes de maïs`; do not move or duplicate that alias.
- `ingredient-ref-corn-bran`, `ingredient-ref-cornmeal`, `ingredient-ref-corn-grits`, `ingredient-ref-hominy-grits`, `ingredient-ref-cornstarch`, and `ingredient-ref-whole-kernel-corn-flour` remain distinct by fraction or preparation.
- Feed-grade corn-germ meal/cake and variable oil-milling residues are not promoted as human-food synonyms. The existing CFIA feed-table source describes a dry-milled, mechanically extracted corn-germ meal that may contain some or all of the remaining corn kernel [existing source `WP03-SECTIONF-TOASTED-CORN-GERM-20261014-R95-S09`].

The French display is provisional and descriptive, not a Québec terminology ruling. The reviewed French-language manufacturer page uses `farine de germe de maïs déshuilé` for its ingredient and food-industry applications [S04]. Use that wording only as a candidate display; do not transfer the page's cold-extraction or cryomilling details to the generic identity. No aliases are proposed, including unqualified `corn germ flour`, `maize germ flour`, or `farine de germe de maïs`.
