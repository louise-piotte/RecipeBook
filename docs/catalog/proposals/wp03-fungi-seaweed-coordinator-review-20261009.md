## Product forms, measurements, and substitutions

1. **Keep mushroom state local to the named identity.** The bundle has generic mushrooms in line-level fresh-like uses and a separate ground/dried candy-cap identity. It does not establish a generic dried-mushroom form, canned mushroom row, powder row, or reconstituted product. For future lines, distinguish fresh, dried, ground/powdered, canned, and reconstituted when the recipe/product actually says so. A soaking liquid is a separate preparation ingredient or by-product, not a synonym for the mushroom.
2. **The candy-cap source quantity is recipe-specific.** The linked source recipe uses 1/4 oz dried candy caps, crushed or ground, while the bundled row is 7 g [S26]. Preserve that line, but do not create a catalog oz-to-g conversion, density, or dried-to-fresh yield from it.
3. **A vendor reconstitution instruction does not establish a universal conversion.** Far West Fungi recommends reconstituting its dried mushrooms in warm liquid for 15–20 minutes and then using them in applications calling for fresh mushrooms [S27]. This is maker guidance; it gives no mass/volume yield, and it does not establish equivalence across mushroom species. The recipe's ground dried candy caps are not the vendor's whole dried product. Do not create a general dried-to-fresh substitution or soak-yield mapping from this statement.
4. **Keep seaweed hydration yield separate from density.** OQLF defines nori as a processed, pressed, dried product [S14]. The FAO material describes sea grapes being sold as a wet, salted product that is washed/desalted for use [S22]. These source-specific states support keeping dry, wet/salted, fresh, and reconstituted products distinct when they occur; they do not supply a general recipe conversion. If a later recipe set needs a hydration yield, record a source- and species/product-specific yield separately from density.
5. **Huitlacoche may appear in multiple commercial forms, but none is in this bundle.** A scholarly review reports fresh, canned, and lyophilized/dehydrated market forms [S28]. Add such a form only when the product or recipe uses it; do not infer a common yield or conversion from its fungal identity.
6. **No substitution is supported.** No active substitution rule exists, and the four ingredient rows do not establish replacements among button/cremini/portobello, any named fungi, candy caps, seaweeds, algae, or huitlacoche. Shared fungal biology or a common umami use is not enough to justify interchangeability. Preserve the species/product distinction and leave substitution records unset.
