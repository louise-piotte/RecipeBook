## Pre-integration snapshot and audit

The live package before this proposal contained **1,087 ingredient references**, **79 forms**, **75 legacy candidates**, **1,386 research-ledger records** with **4,613 source entries**, and **114 manifest-listed recipes**. The recipe files contained **1,123 ingredient rows**.

I checked every live reference display and alias, every form label and match term, the legacy candidate list, related A7 ledger records and decisions, and every manifest-listed recipe ingredient row. Exact new-term lookups found no active reference, form, or legacy-candidate owner for `brown rice farina`, `whole grain brown rice farina`, `brown rice meal`, `whole grain rye meal`, `millet meal`, `triticale meal`, `malted wheat meal`, `whole grain wheat farina`, `spelt meal`, or `purple wheat meal`. The known live neighbors and partial-name overlaps are itemized below; an exact miss does not imply that a phrase is an independent identity.

The recipe-demand scan searched ingredient-row `originalText`, `ingredientName`, `preparation`, and `notes` across all 114 manifest-listed files for the reviewed meal, farina, malted/toasted-flour, and held A7 terms. It found **0 matching rows in 0 recipes**. No recipe links or relinks are proposed.

### Exact catalog neighbors and collision notes

- `ingredient-ref-rye-meal` is the exact live owner of `rye meal`; its current EN aliases are `coarse rye meal`, `pumpernickel meal`, and `pumpernickel flour`, and its fr-CA display/aliases are `gruau de seigle`, `semoule de seigle`, and `farine à pumpernickel`. `whole grain rye meal` has no exact current owner; no alias is proposed because its grind is not stated in the Canadian ingredient declarations.
- `ingredient-ref-wheat-meal` is named `whole wheat meal / farine de blé entier brute` and has `farine de blé complet brute` as a French alias. Bare `wheat meal` has no exact owner, although that string occurs inside the narrower English display. This substring overlap does not resolve generic wheat meal.
- `ingredient-ref-scottish-oatmeal` owns `Scottish oatmeal / gruau écossais`, with `Scotch oatmeal` and `Scottish oats` as EN aliases. It does not own generic `oatmeal` or `oat meal`.
- `ingredient-ref-brown-rice` (`brown rice / riz brun`), `ingredient-ref-brown-rice-flour` (`brown rice flour / farine de riz brun`), `ingredient-ref-rice-flour`, and `ingredient-ref-rice-cereal` (`crispy rice cereal / céréales de riz croustillantes`) are the live neighboring rows. None owns brown rice farina or brown rice meal.
- `ingredient-ref-millet`, `ingredient-ref-common-millet`, `ingredient-ref-pearl-millet`, and the generic, pearl, and finger millet-flour rows remain species- and product-distinct. None owns `millet meal`.
- `ingredient-ref-triticale-grain` owns `triticale`; no reviewed meal term is an alias on that row.
- No active row owns the exact key `farina`. A substring lookup for `farina` also encounters the four Italian flour-grade aliases `farina di grano tenero tipo 0/00/1/2`; do not add bare `farina` to any of them or to a generic wheat row.
- `ingredient-ref-malt-flour` owns `malt flour / farine maltée`; `ingredient-ref-roasted-barley-flour` owns `toasted barley flour`. These active exact terms remain distinct from cereal-specific malted flour and from malted wheat meal.
- There were no exact phrase matches in the 75 legacy candidate entries for the new candidates listed above. The ledger retains `candidate-barley-meal`, `candidate-dry-oat-meal`, `candidate-hattai-ko-mugikogashi`, and `candidate-toasted-rye-flour`; the wheat-meal record remains narrowly accepted for `whole wheat meal` while bare `wheat meal` stays unresolved. The earlier generic-malt candidate is merged into the active `ingredient-ref-malt-flour` row.

## Limits and review notes

- The brown-rice-farina row is the only new identity recommendation. Its French display is taken from a current Canadian manufacturer product list; **an independent Québec French reviewer should accept, revise, or hold that display before integration**, because the GDT sources do not establish a generic French term for this rice product. If the reviewer rejects the manufacturer wording for a generic catalog row, leave the candidate held rather than inventing `farina de riz brun` or `semoule de riz brun`.
- `whole grain rye meal` remains unassigned as an exact search phrase until a product source states a process boundary compatible with the active coarse rye-meal row. No French term is proposed for it.
- The marketplace millet page and old abandoned trademark file are used only to explain why a global millet-meal/flour equivalence is unsupported.
- No densities, volume-to-mass mappings, yield values, substitutions, or recipe links were added or recommended. No application tests were run; this is a research proposal only.
