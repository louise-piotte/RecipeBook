# WP02 hazelnut-flour identity review

## Scope and coordinator decision

- **Review date:** 2026-10-10
- **Scope:** hazelnut flour versus whole hazelnuts, hazelnut meal, and process-qualified flour products.
- **Disposition:** accept a narrow identity-only `hazelnut flour / farine de noisette` entry. Keep hazelnut meal, defatted flour, and roasting qualifiers out of global aliases.
- **Coordinator integration:** add `ingredient-ref-hazelnut-flour` in `FLOUR_AND_STARCH`, with no aliases, recipe links, forms, measurements, densities, unit mappings, conversions, yields, or substitutions.

## Catalog and recipe audit

The audit covered 679 active references, 34 forms, 84 legacy candidates, 865 research-ledger records, and all 114 manifest-listed recipes (1,123 ingredient rows). Exact normalized checks found no owner for `hazelnut flour` or `farine de noisette` in the active references, forms, ledger, or legacy candidates. Neither phrase appears in a bundled recipe ingredient line or recipe text. No recipe relink is proposed.

## Evidence and limits

| Source | Support and limits |
| --- | --- |
| [Noccioro, Hazelnut Flour](https://www.noccioro.it/en/prodotti/farina-di-nocciole/) | The producer names a single-ingredient flour made from roasted hazelnuts, finely ground. This confirms a food product sold as hazelnut flour; it does not set a universal roast, particle-size, or fat-content standard. |
| [ABC Emballuxe, 2025 product catalogue](https://abcemballuxe.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Catalogue-ABC-EMBALLUXE_NOEL-XMAS-06-2025V1.pdf) | The confectionery supplier lists `Farine de noisette Royale / Royale hazelnut flour` as a separate product. This supports bilingual product wording in a supplier catalogue, not its origin, composition, or the milling specification of every hazelnut flour. |
| [Edora Hazelnuts, Hazelnut Flour](https://edorahazelnuts.com/products/hazelnut-flour/) | The manufacturer describes controlled particle size and lot-level fat checks and notes project-specific defatted options. This cautions against inferring a universal fat or roast specification from the display name. |

## Accepted identity

| Field | Accepted value |
| --- | --- |
| Stable ID | `ingredient-ref-hazelnut-flour` |
| English display | `hazelnut flour` |
| Proposed fr-CA display | `farine de noisette` |
| Aliases | none |
| Category | `FLOUR_AND_STARCH` |

Keep whole hazelnuts, hazelnut meal, defatted hazelnut flour, roasted-hazelnut flour, and hazelnut powder outside aliases until product scope is reviewed. The accepted row records the source-supported marketed ingredient name; it does not prescribe fat level, roast, or grind. No measurement or substitution inference is made.

The French product wording is source-attested but not an OQLF ruling; keep it open for independent Québec terminology review.
