# WP04 Section P — frozen tempura fish product-boundary review (R224)

- **Status:** Review disposition accepted and integrated on 2026-10-16 as a documentation-only boundary hold; see the R219–R225 gap-review integration README. No catalog or ledger data was allocated.
- **Open work:** Section P's fish-product coverage remains open under the broader item at [the catalog plan](../../RecipeBook-global-ingredient-catalog-plan.txt#L6374). This packet reviews one unowned prepared-fish boundary after R221. It does not reopen any existing held candidate.
- **Recommendation:** Hold an unqualified frozen tempura-fish identity outside the active catalog until a bundled recipe requests it or picker search can preserve the broader meaning of `tempura`.
- **Reviewed product:** High Liner's Canadian-market frozen tempura-battered fish, Metro product **061763041341**. The English retailer display is **Frozen Tempura Battered Fish**; Metro's French listing is **Poisson en pâte tempura surgelé**.
- **Boundary:** Keep this product separate from tempura fish fries (a separately named 24-portion SKU), tempura-battered species-specific fillets, breaded fish sticks, and fish prepared at home with tempura batter. Do not infer a fish species from the unqualified product name.

## Findings

Metro's current bilingual grocery listings show a High Liner 700 g frozen fish product made from minced fish fillets. Metro identifies pollock or haddock in the ingredients and reports 12 portions for product 061763041341. High Liner's product page confirms the 700 g package and the pollock-or-haddock minced-fillet declaration. These are SKU facts, not a stable recipe measure or a species identity. Metro's page currently notes that this SKU is unavailable at its selected store; the listing supports the product name and package description, not local in-stock availability. [S02–S05]

The same retailer and manufacturer distinguish adjacent products. Metro lists High Liner fish fries in tempura batter as another 700 g product, UPC 061763041273, with 24 portions. High Liner also lists a separate tempura-battered haddock fillet product. Those names and product structures do not support merging fries or species-specific fillets into the proposed 12-portion retail item. Frozen breaded fish sticks remain a separate shape/product group; R221's CFIA evidence also notes that breaded fish portions may be made from minced fish or slices. [S06–S07; reused R221 S04]

OQLF defines *tempura* broadly as a Japanese dish of fish, seafood, or vegetables fried in batter. A frozen fish item is therefore only one possible result for a bare `tempura` request. The live picker normalizes accents and punctuation, prefers complete word phrases, and falls back to partial matches. At this audit, the active catalog has 1,614 references and 122 forms; exact queries for `tempura`, `fish in tempura batter`, `frozen tempura battered fish`, `poisson en pâte tempura`, and `poisson en pâte tempura surgelé` return no result. `fish` returns six existing references and `poisson` returns nine. [S01]

If a reference with either sourced display were added now, the complete-word search would make bare `tempura` return that one fish product. The exact product phrases would also return it, but a singleton `tempura` result would imply that the broader dish term means this one frozen pollock-or-haddock product. That picker behavior and the absence of recipe demand outweigh the current product listing as a reason to allocate a global identity. Do not add a reference, alias, form, or candidate record in this review.
