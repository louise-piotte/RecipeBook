# WP06 East Asia cuisine gap sweep proposal

**Research date:** 2026-10-11
**Status:** Proposal only; awaiting root review. No catalog, ledger, recipe, plan, roadmap, code, or test files are changed by this packet.
**Scope:** China by major culinary region, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Japan, Korea, and Mongolia, as named in Section 05 of the global ingredient catalog plan.

## Recommendation

This bounded sweep recommends two identity-only catalog actions:

1. Promote the existing legacy candidate **ingredient-ref-five-spice-powder** as **five-spice powder / cinq épices chinoises** in **BAKING_AND_SPICE**. Keep aliases empty. Direct Canadian manufacturer and Québec retailer evidence support the bilingual product wording; Taiwan and Hakka regional sources document seasoning use under their own wording without establishing that all similarly named blends are identical. [S04, S15–S18]
2. Add **matcha / matcha** as a separate identity-only row in **OTHER**, with only the French alias **thé matcha**. Japanese agriculture sources describe powdered Japanese tea as a beverage and confectionery ingredient, and a Québec tea retailer uses matcha and thé matcha with the French descriptor poudre de thé vert. [S09–S12]

Neither recommendation has an exact bundled recipe ingredient line. These are external regional-coverage additions, not recipe-demand additions. No forms, measurements, densities, unit mappings, conversions, yields, recipe links, or substitutions are proposed. All other source mentions below are already covered, named compounds, aliases/variants that cannot be activated safely, or unresolved/held items routed to their existing category reviews.

This packet is a source-grounded sample rather than an exhaustive ethnographic or market survey. It records coverage across savory dishes, sweets and baking, condiments and preserves, festive foods, and relevant beverages, but does not support closing WP06 regional coverage as a whole.
