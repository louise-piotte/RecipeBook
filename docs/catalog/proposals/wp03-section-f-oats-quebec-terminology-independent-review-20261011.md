# WP03 Section F — independent Québec French review of quick and instant oats

- **Review date:** 2026-10-11.
- **Review type:** independent terminology and market-name recheck of two existing oat references.
- **Disposition:** retain both active bilingual displays. Keep quick oats and plain instant oat flakes as distinct identities. Make no alias or catalog-name changes.
- **Shared changes:** added two current Québec retail source entries to the existing quick-oats and instant-oats ledger records. No ingredient reference, form, recipe, measurement, conversion, yield, or substitution changed.

## Review decision

Retain `quick oats / flocons d'avoine à cuisson rapide`. TERMIUM Plus lists `flocons d'avoine à cuisson rapide` and `avoine à cuisson rapide` as correct French terms for quick oats, and a Québec retailer currently uses the exact longer compound in product titles. Keep `avoine à cuisson rapide` as the existing French alias. OQLF recommends `flocon d'avoine` for the base processed product and advises against `avoine roulée`; its processing note supports the useful distinction that thinner, more crushed and steamed flakes cook faster.

Retain `instant oats / flocons d'avoine à cuisson instantanée` as a separate identity with no aliases. TERMIUM maps the English class to the current French display and describes more extensively processed flakes. The same record also lists `flocons d'avoine minute`, but the fresh Québec retail check did not establish that shorter wording as a plain-flake ingredient name. A Québec retailer uses `Gruau instantané` for formulated Quaker instant-oatmeal products, including flavoured products. OQLF defines Québec `gruau` as a cooked oatmeal dish. Keep `gruau instantané` and `flocons d'avoine minute` off the plain-flake row until an exact product context resolves their scope.

The independent review therefore confirms the current terminology and product boundary. It does not supply a general measure, density, cooking state, recipe link, or substitute. The recipe corpus contains two direct quick-oats rows (024#6 and 047#4) and no instant-oats ingredient line.

## Scope preserved

- Keep quick oats, instant oats, rolled oats, oat groats, steel-cut oats, oat flour, and prepared oatmeal distinct.
- Keep `quick-cooking oats` and `avoine à cuisson rapide` on the existing quick-oats identity.
- Keep the existing instant-oats alias lists empty. Do not add `flocons d'avoine minute`, `gruau instantané`, `instant oatmeal`, or sachet names to plain instant flakes.
- Preserve the two existing quick-oats recipe links and quantities. The bundle has no instant-oats line.
- No forms, densities, unit mappings, observations, conversions, cooking yields, or substitutions were added.
