# WP03 Section N proposal: sea vegetables and algae product boundaries

- **Reviewed:** 2026-10-09
- **Status:** proposal only. This task creates this packet only. No seed data, recipes, catalog ledger, legacy candidates, plan, roadmap, code, tests, or existing proposal packet was changed.
- **Scope:** nori, kombu, wakame, dulse, kelp, arame, sea lettuce, aonori, sea grapes, and the closely related Atlantic-wakame / winged-kelp product boundary.
- **Plan state:** Section N remains open. This packet does not close the sea-vegetable checklist or the separate forms and hydration-yield work.
- **Excluded:** recipe interpretations or relinks, dried/fresh/reconstituted forms except for the product definition inherent to nori, hydration yields, measures, conversions, substitutions, and additions to the shared research ledger.

## Recommendation

Carry six narrowly named identity candidates forward: **nori, kombu, arame, sea lettuce, dulse, and Alaria esculenta sold as Atlantic wakame/winged kelp**. Hold generic **wakame, kelp, aonori, and sea grapes** until the product and taxonomic scope is resolved. Keep them as ingredient identities only; none of the proposed names establishes a form transition or a numeric conversion.

## Search and normalized-key collision audit

I followed `normalizeIngredientSearchKey`: lowercase, NFKD normalization, folding `œ`/`æ`/`ß` and `&`, stripping combining marks, replacing punctuation with spaces, and collapsing whitespace. The app first returns full normalized word-phrase matches across reference and form search terms; only if none exist does it fall back to substring matching. I ran the proposed exact names, aliases, variant spellings, and hold terms against live active reference and form search terms. Each currently returns **no active reference/form result**. Exact normalized keys also have no collisions in references, forms, legacy candidates, or ledger record IDs.

Expected broad-query overlap after adding the scoped rows should remain visible in the picker: `laitue` may return generic lettuce, sea lettuce, and nori through `laitue pourpre`; `lettuce` may return generic lettuce and sea lettuce; `kelp` will match the explicitly named `winged kelp` Alaria identity. Those substring/token overlaps are not synonym claims. Keep full product terms such as `laitue de mer`, `laitue pourpre`, `Atlantic wakame`, and `wakamé atlantique` on their scoped identities. A future sea-grapes row would also share broad words with the existing generic grapes/raisins references, which is another reason not to make `grapes`, `raisins`, `green caviar`, or `lato` global aliases without a narrower source decision.

The relevant active catalog remains unrelated to these candidates: generic lettuce, grapes, and raisins exist, but no seaweed/algae reference or form exists. No legacy candidate or ledger identifier is being revived. The legacy candidate set includes unrelated ingredients only.
