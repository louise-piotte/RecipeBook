# WP02 B2 R177 — Pili nut flour identity review

- **Reviewed:** 2026-10-16.
- **Status:** Proposal-only at authoring. This file was the sole change from the reviewer; no catalog JSON, ledger, roadmap, plan, generated file, code, or batch record was edited by the proposal.
- **Plan fit:** New nut-flour candidate under the open WP02 B2 alternative-flour coverage. The current checklist's nut-flour work names almond, hazelnut, coconut, chestnut, and peanut; the focused archive search found no prior pili-nut-flour review. This review is separate from the WP06/Section I whole-pili-nut identity and from pili-pulp flour research.
- **Proposal-local source IDs:** `WP02-B2-PILI-NUT-FLOUR-R177-20261016-S01` through `S05`. These are provenance labels only and are not shared-ledger records.

## Recommendation

**Add identity-only `pili nut flour / farine de noix de pili` as a provisional French display.** A recent primary food-science study explicitly uses *Canarium ovatum* pili-nut flour in cookie formulations. Québec-facing evidence already supports `noix de pili` for the edible nut; the proposed French flour name is a transparent product description built from that term, not an exact OQLF-approved flour name. Keep the candidate distinct from the separate *Canarium ovatum* pulp flour made from the surrounding fruit pulp, and from `pili-pili`, the unrelated pepper term. No Canadian or Québec retail flour was found, so this is an identity-only research-backed addition rather than a retail-availability claim. Add no aliases, forms, recipe links, measurements, conversions, yields, or substitutions. [S01–S05]

## Proposed identity and exclusions

- **English display:** `pili nut flour`
- **French display:** `farine de noix de pili` (descriptive and provisional for Québec; based on the established `noix de pili` term)
- **Identity scope:** flour made from edible pili nut kernel of *Canarium ovatum*.
- **Do not include:** pili pulp flour, whole pili nuts, pili-nut butter, generic `pili flour`, or the unrelated chili-pepper term `pili-pili`.
- **Aliases:** none. In particular, keep bare `pili`, `pili flour`, `pili pulp flour`, `pili nut meal`, `pilinut flour`, and `farine de pili` inactive pending product-specific support.

## Ownership and picker audit

The active catalog has one exact neighboring owner: `ingredient-ref-pili-nuts`, displayed as `pili nuts / noix de pili`, with the English aliases `pili nut` and `pilinut`. No active reference or form owns `pili nut flour`, `pili flour`, or `farine de noix de pili`; no legacy-import candidate owns the proposed identity or phrase. The only relevant ledger record is for whole *Canarium ovatum* pili nuts. It contains an existing Québec retailer listing and terminology sources for the nut; it has no flour candidate or flour source. The focused proposal/batch archive found no pili-nut-flour identity decision. Other archive references are the whole-nut identity, a broad patent ingredient list, or unrelated flour products. No new source ID or URL in this proposal duplicates a current ledger entry; the already-ledgered Québec retailer, TERMIUM, and OQLF records should be reused if a later integration needs those facts.

`IngredientSearch.kt` prefers complete word-phrase matches. If added with the proposed displays, `pili nut flour` and `farine de noix de pili` would resolve to the new flour row; broad `pili` and `noix de pili` queries would show both the existing whole-nut and flour rows. Do not add the short alias `pili`, which would broaden the whole-pili query unnecessarily. Keep `pili-pili` out of all aliases: OQLF defines that Québec French term for a very hot African pepper, and punctuation folding makes it a separate `pili pili` search phrase, not a name for *Canarium ovatum*.

## Bundled recipe demand

A manifest-ordered scan of the current 114 bundled recipe files and all 1,123 ingredient rows found no mentions of `pili nut flour`, `pili flour`, `pili pulp flour`, `farine de noix de pili`, or *Canarium ovatum* in recipe text, and no link to a flour candidate. This result is bounded to the current bundled package.

## Numeric and substitution boundaries

The 2025 cookie study reports experimental blend levels of 10%, 20%, and 30% pili nut flour in wheat-flour cookies. Those are formula-specific tested levels; the study's preferred 30% formulation is not a general wheat-flour substitution ratio. The paper supplies no transferable household volume-to-mass measure, bulk density, unit mapping, or preparation yield for the flour. The separate pili-pulp-flour thesis reports research-only density, yield, and functional values for a different fruit part and must not be transferred to nut flour. No forms, measurements, conversions, yields, recipe links, or active/global/contextual substitutions are proposed.
