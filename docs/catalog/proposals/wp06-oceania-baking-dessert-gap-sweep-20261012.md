# WP06 Section 05 — bounded Oceania baking and dessert gap sweep

**Date:** 2026-10-12  
**Status:** Proposal-only, evidence-led sample. This review does not complete the Australia, Aotearoa/New Zealand, and Pacific checklist.

## Scope and disposition

This sweep samples baking, breads, desserts, and baking ingredients from Australia, Aotearoa/New Zealand, Samoa, Tonga, and Fiji. It checks exact catalog ownership and all bundled recipe ingredient rows, and records only candidates supported by the sources below. It does not claim coverage of all Oceania cuisines or Indigenous food traditions.

No canonical reference, form, legacy candidate, recipe, ledger record, checklist, or plan was changed. Recommendations are identity, alias, spelling variant, compound/dish, or unresolved. No forms, measures, densities, conversions, yields, or substitutions are proposed.

## Catalog, proposal, and recipe audit

I searched active reference names and aliases, form labels and match terms, and legacy candidate names for the selected names and orthographic variants. None has an exact current owner. `Mānuka honey` also has no exact owner, although the generic `ingredient-ref-honey` owner exists. The selected terms also have no exact hit in prior proposal or batch documents. Finger lime is deliberately excluded: it already has the active `ingredient-ref-finger-lime` owner and its reviewed French aliases in the [WP03 citrus integration packet](../batches/wp03-wp04-citrus-mushroom-finfish-identity-integration-20261009/README.md).

The exact search covered all 1,123 ingredient rows in all 114 manifest-listed recipe files, using `originalText`, `ingredientName`, and `ingredientRefId`. It found no line naming the selected Australian, Māori, Samoan, Tongan, or Fijian candidates, including the spelling variants in the findings table. A separate exact check also found no `mānuka` / `manuka` recipe line. The corpus therefore gives no direct recipe-line demand for these additions.

Related generic owners already exist: sweet potato (`ingredient-ref-sweet-potato`, FR `patate douce`), sourdough starter (`ingredient-ref-sourdough-starter`, FR `levain naturel`), breadfruit and breadfruit flour, cassava root/flour/leaves, coconut and its specific products, taro, and taro leaves/flour. The full corpus has two generic sweet-potato rows, both outside this regional sample (African peanut stew and Québec pea soup). It has 10 coconut rows, three coconut-milk rows, one coconut-oil row, and one cream-of-coconut row, all linked to their existing owners. These do not constitute demand for the source-named regional dishes or products. No recipe row currently calls for rewena starter, wattleseed, quandong, lemon myrtle, horopito, kawakawa, or the named Pacific desserts.

## Québec French terminology notes

Exact-name searches in the [OQLF Grand dictionnaire](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/) and [TERMIUM Plus](https://www.btb.termiumplus.gc.ca/tpv2alpha/alpha-fra.html?lang=fra) did not surface a verifiable Québec French entry for the selected Indigenous or regional names. This is a bounded search result, not proof that neither database contains a related broader term. Forward `myrte citronnée` for lemon myrtle, supported by French technical botanical/herb terminology [S14–S15], to WP05 with the regional baking evidence; this packet does not make its identity decision. Retain the source names `wattleseed`, `quandong`, `horopito`, `kawakawa`, `kūmara`, and `rēwena` where no Québec equivalent was verified. For quandong, record `pêche du désert` only as a qualified secondary alias from Codex [S16]. The suggested `culture de rēwena` is an explanatory French label, not a sourced standard term.

## Remaining misses and limits

This packet reviews a bounded baking/dessert sample only. It does not cover the full native-food inventory of Australia (DAFF itself lists numerous commercial species and much wider Indigenous food use), all Māori baking or plant-food traditions, or the broader Pacific. Other Australian bush foods and native seeds, additional Māori roots and wild plants, other regionalized forms and names, and Pacific cuisines beyond the Samoa/Tonga/Fiji sample remain unreviewed. Lemon myrtle, horopito, and kawakawa are handed to WP05 for identity review; mānuka honey is handed to WP02 sugar/sweetener review. Pikopiko remains unresolved because the common culinary name is not species-bounded. Strawberry gum sugar remains product-level because its plant/source composition is unspecified. No new target ingredient appears in the bundled corpus, so there is no recipe link to propose. WP06 Section 05 remains open.
