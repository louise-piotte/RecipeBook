# WP02 Section 04 B3 R137 — breadnut seed starch product-boundary hold

- **Reviewed:** 2026-10-15.
- **Status:** Proposal-only hold. This review changes no shared catalog JSON, ledger, checklist, roadmap, generated file, recipe, or application file.
- **Checklist:** WP02 Section 04 B3, `[ ] Water-chestnut flour and other documented regional starches.` The parent remains open.
- **Candidate:** starch isolated from edible seeds of *Artocarpus camansi* Blanco, a distinct accepted species from *A. altilis* breadfruit flesh.
- **Proposal-local source-ID prefix:** `WP02-B3-BREADNUT-SEED-STARCH-R137-20261015-S`. The prefix and candidate wording were checked against the live reference catalog, forms, ledger, legacy identities, plan, and proposal/batch archive. No focused *A. camansi* seed-starch decision or source-prefix owner was found.

## Recommendation: hold, no delta

Keep *A. camansi* seed starch outside the active picker and research ledger until a current Canadian or Québec human-food product source identifies a marketed isolated seed-starch product and supports a product-specific Québec French name. Primary studies do establish a separately extracted seed-starch fraction. They characterize laboratory samples prepared with differing extraction treatments; they do not establish a retail food specification, Canadian starch product, household measure, or recipe substitution.

A current Maxi listing for a whole produce item named “Breadnut” is relevant local context, but it does not identify a botanical species, describes cooking the whole item's flesh, and is currently out of stock at the Laval Laurentides store. It does not support assigning the isolated seed fraction to that SKU. Exact-phrase Canada/Québec searches found no standalone breadnut seed-starch listing. This is a bounded search result, not proof that no such product is sold.

The English word **breadnut** is not a safe unqualified identity label: Kew also records it for seed-bearing *A. altilis* varieties, while the Government of Canada terminology database uses **breadnut tree** for *Brosimum alicastrum*. Québec terminology sources support **fruit à pain** for *A. altilis*, not *A. camansi*. Caribbean agricultural literature uses **chataigne** for *A. camansi*, but that regional fruit/seed wording is not a Québec starch-product name. The candidate French construction **amidon de graines de breadnut** is intelligible but unattested as a Québec product term. Keep it inactive. [S06, S08–S09, S11]

**No JSON delta is proposed.** Do not add or reserve an ingredient-reference ID or ledger candidate. Add no alias, form, measurement observation, default density, unit mapping, recipe link, conversion, yield, or substitution. Reconsider if a current Canadian or Québec label or supplier specification identifies food-grade isolated *A. camansi* seed starch and a Québec French product name is independently supported.

It reports the product out of stock at the selected Maxi Laval Laurentides store when checked. The page contains no scientific name, no seed-starch ingredient, and no extracted-starch processing claim. It is therefore evidence for a Québec-facing whole-product listing, not for a seed starch or its taxonomy. [S05]

The search does not establish that such a product is unavailable through every retailer or supplier.

## Measurements, forms, substitutions, and recipe demand

- **Measurements and yields:** Indarti et al. report laboratory starch recovery for kluwih seeds that differs between their extraction procedures, including 18.2% without sodium-metabisulfite immersion and 24.4% after alkaline immersion. Hidayat et al. vary sodium-metabisulfite concentration and report sample-specific starch content, moisture, amylose, and granule-size results. These extraction/sample values are not a household mass-volume pair, commercial package measure, bulk density, or standardized home extraction yield; keep them out of active conversion data. Maxi's 1 oz (28 g) nutrition serving is for the whole produce SKU, not extracted starch. [S02–S03, S05]
- **Forms:** Add none. Wet extraction, chemical immersion, settling, washing, and drying are research processing steps, not a consumer form family with supported same-ingredient conversions. [S02–S03]
- **Substitutions:** Add none. The primary studies characterize starch and extraction properties; they do not validate a recipe-level replacement ratio against breadnut seed flour, breadfruit starch, corn, potato, tapioca, or another starch. Do not infer equivalence from general thickening or material-application language. [S02–S03]
- **Recipe demand:** A manifest-ordered read-only scan of all **114 recipe files and 1,123 ingredient rows** found zero occurrences in recipe content or ingredient lines of `breadnut`, `bread nut`, `kamansi`, `kluwih`, `Artocarpus camansi`, `breadnut starch`, `breadnut seed starch`, or `amidon de graines de breadnut`. No recipe line links to a candidate ID. No recipe link, relink, or substitution is proposed.

## Reconsideration conditions

1. Locate a current Canadian or Québec human-food label or supplier specification for isolated *A. camansi* seed starch, distinct from whole produce, flour, starch-rich blends, and packaging/industrial starch.
2. Confirm the French product name from a Québec label or authoritative Québec terminology source; do not infer it from the fruit name or from Caribbean usage.
3. Preserve the boundary from *A. altilis* flesh starch and flour, and keep bare `breadnut`, `breadfruit`, `fruit à pain`, `châtaigne`, `noix-pain`, `kamansi`, and `kluwih` off any future row unless a product-specific source and picker review justify each term.
4. Recheck measures, forms, substitutions, and recipe demand separately. Do not transfer lab extraction yield or the Maxi whole-produce 28 g serving to starch.
5. Leave WP02 Section 04 B3 open; this review does not complete the regional-starch checklist item.
