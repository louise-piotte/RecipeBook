## Why the prior hold is reconsidered

The October 10 WP03 pearl-millet review left `sajje` inactive because its reviewed evidence did not establish the spelling. Round 11 then used an ICAR–IIMR crosswalk and a product page for Telugu `Sajjalu`, deliberately leaving Kannada `Sajje` outside that decision. Round 14 made the separate Tamil `Kambu` decision and again did not allocate `Sajje`.

The evidence now closes that exact gap. ICAR–IIMR's live common-name page places Kannada `Sajje` under Pearl Millet and shows that the page was updated on 2026-07-07. A product page localized for Canadian orders names Pearl Millet Flour in English and gives the Kannada product name `ಸಜ್ಜೆ ಹಿಟ್ಟು (Sajje Hittu)`. A Karnataka state agriculture table also pairs `Bajra` and `Sajje` and lists flour as a processing opportunity. These sources support the two bounded aliases without making `Sajje` interchangeable with `Sajjalu` or `Kambu`. [WP02-A6-KANGNI-FOXTAIL-FLOUR-ALIAS-20261013-S01; WP02-A6-SAJJALU-PEARL-MILLET-20261013-R11-S02; S01]

The Canada-facing evidence is limited. The Kalagura Gampa listing uses a `/ca` page and Canadian-dollar price but identifies its help centre and address in Telangana, India. It is not a Canadian manufacturer or a Québec French label. A second `silkrute.ca` listing uses Canadian dollars and includes `Pearl Millet Sajje` in the title of a pearl-millet-flour product, but the search result reports no stock for its selected options and the page could not be directly fetched during this review. Neither page is used to claim Canadian manufacture, Québec availability, or a French name. [WP02-A6-SAJJALU-PEARL-MILLET-20261013-R11-S02; S02]
