# WP05 pandan Québec French terminology review (R345, 2026-10-16)

- **Status:** Completed independent fr-CA review; retain the existing leaf and powdered-leaf displays.
- **Plan anchor:** WP03 Section K culinary leaves and WP05 Section X pandan products.
- **Scope:** Review `feuilles de pandan` and `poudre de feuilles de pandan` as catalog display wording. This does not reopen the plant identity or powdered-leaf product evidence.
- **Disposition:** Keep both labels unchanged. Add no aliases, forms, recipe links, or numeric behavior.

## Decision

Retain `pandan leaves / feuilles de pandan` for the culinary leaf identity and `pandan leaf powder / poudre de feuilles de pandan` for its existing dry powdered form. The Québec recipe source uses `feuilles de pandan` directly, and a Canada-targeted French product listing uses the exact powder wording. The phrases are transparent, grammatical French descriptions. No OQLF-preferred pandan term was found in a targeted GDT search, so neither display is presented as an OQLF standard.

The evidence supports the current, narrow displays. It does not justify broadening labels or adding bare `pandan`, `poudre de pandan`, or `pandan powder` as aliases. Those shorter terms can span leaf, flavoring, extract, and compound preparations. The current `pandan` query already returns both the leaf identity and the separate McCormick pandan flavor identity.

## Remaining boundaries

This review accepts the existing displays as conservative, descriptive fr-CA wording. It does not claim either phrase is standardized by OQLF or broadly preferred across Québec retailers. The distinct identity and composition boundaries for juice, extract powders, essences, pastes, and compound flavor products remain open under their existing plan owners.
