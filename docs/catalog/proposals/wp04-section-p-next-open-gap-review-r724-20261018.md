## Canadian and Québec name scope

CFIA describes its Fish List as the source of English and French common names acceptable for Canadian fish labelling, paired to scientific names and taxonomic serial numbers. It also explains that a blank French name means no applicable French name was found in its references. In the current result list:

- *Mullus* spp. (TSN 169416) has separate rows for **Goatfish / Rouget-barbet** and **Red Mullet / [French blank]**.
- *Upeneichthys* spp. (TSN 169462) has the same separate **Goatfish / Rouget-barbet** and **Red Mullet / [French blank]** rows.
- *Mullus surmuletus* (TSN 169418) has **Mullet / [French blank]** and a separate **Surmullet / Rouget de roche** row. The species-qualified French term does not name the full *Mullus* + *Upeneichthys* Red Mullet group.

The co-occurrence of `Goatfish / Rouget-barbet` and `Red Mullet / [blank]` on the same genus-level taxa does not make CFIA’s `Rouget-barbet` row a French translation of its separate Red Mullet row. CFIA records the common-name rows independently and explicitly says a blank cell means it did not find an applicable French name for that row.

The OQLF GDT record headed [`rouget-barbet`](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/3297706/rouget-barbet) is in zoology/ichthyology and edible-fish domains, defines a fish in family Mullidae, and gives English `red mullet` / `mullet` and Latin *Mullus*. However, the record names **Association française de normalisation, 1968** as its author. It is an old terminology record hosted in the OQLF GDT, not an OQLF statement that this is the current normalized Québec fish-product label; its *Mullus* scope also does not establish the separate *Upeneichthys* part of the Canadian group.

The OQLF’s Québec edible-fish entry for [`rouget`](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/1198981/rouget) has OQLF as author and identifies its preferred term `rouget` with *Pseudupeneus* sp.; its market note names *P. maculatus* and *P. prayensis*. It lists `barbet` and `rouget-barbet` only as terms used in some contexts and says those appellations were not retained by the Québec fish-product terminology committee. This makes `rouget` unsafe as a Red Mullet alias and weakens `rouget-barbet` as the Québec display for a multi-genus group. The entry was last updated in 2011; it does not describe current inventory.

Do not substitute `rouget de roche` for the group: CFIA attaches it to the species-specific *M. surmuletus* row, not the *Mullus* + *Upeneichthys* group. Do not use broad `goatfish` as a group alias: CFIA also uses Goatfish for other Mullidae genera, and R63 separately documents the Québec *Pseudupeneus* / `rouget` boundary. Do not merge the candidate with grey/striped mullets in family Mugilidae or with other CFIA `Mullet` rows.

## Food-market evidence and limits

Seacore Seafood, a Canadian Vaughan supplier, currently lists **Red Mullet** and prints the product-family scope as **Mullus Upeneichthys**. The page says the family is available whole, fresh or frozen, wild, with stated product origins of the U.S.A. and Brazil. This supports a Canadian supplier’s use of the English group label and a multi-genus product family. It does not name an exact species, provide a paired French product label, establish current item-level stock, or establish Québec retail availability. The whole/fresh/frozen/origin claims remain supplier-level only.

Montréal’s [United Seafood whole-fish page](https://www.unitedseafood.ca/wholefish.html) lists **Red Mullet** in its current whole-fish selection, but gives no binomial, origin, or taxonomic scope for that entry. It supports Québec-facing use of the English market wording only; it cannot identify the fish as *Mullus* or *Upeneichthys*.

Montréal’s [Fou des Îles page](https://foudesiles.ca/) lists **Rouget Barbet** among whole Mediterranean fish in its “new arrival” text. The listing gives no scientific name or matching Red Mullet label and does not establish which CFIA genus or species is sold. It corroborates local use of the French phrase, but not its acceptance as a Québec-normalized name for the two-genus group. The page’s arrival wording is not a stock guarantee.

No product-matched bilingual Québec source was found that identifies a Red Mullet item as one or both of the CFIA genera and supplies a Québec French label for the same scope. Retail terms without a binomial are not enough to connect a specific product to the group.

## Picker projection

The live matcher first returns complete normalized word phrases across reference displays, aliases, and attached forms, then falls back to substring matches. Current exact queries below return no Red Mullet-related result because there is no active owner. A purely illustrative row using the candidate group phrase plus `rouget-barbet` would make the bilingual term searchable, but would produce the following singleton projections:

| Query | Current live result | With illustrative `Red Mullet (name used for multiple fish species) / rouget-barbet (nom utilisé pour plusieurs espèces de poissons)` display | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| `red mullet` | None | The illustrative group only | The English phrase is source-backed as a multi-species supplier group; the user-visible qualifier would state that scope. |
| `mullet` | None | The illustrative group only | This broad token would select the Red Mullet group alone despite CFIA’s wider `Mullet` usage, including *Mugil*, *Mullus*, and *Moxostoma* rows described in R129. |
| `rouget-barbet` | None | The illustrative group only | The phrase is not a safe Québec-normalized product label for this group; OQLF’s marketing-term note is contrary evidence. |
| `rouget` | None | The illustrative group only | Word-phrase matching treats the first word of `rouget-barbet` as a match; it would route OQLF’s preferred *Pseudupeneus* term to a different fish group. |
| `surmullet`; `rouget de roche`; `Mullus`; `Upeneichthys`; `goatfish` | None | None with the illustrated display and no aliases | Keep these species, taxon, or broader family terms unassigned. |

This projection does not recommend the illustrative row. A French descriptive label such as a family or genus phrase would not be a source-attested Québec fish-product name for the same group. Aliases cannot fix a misleading display term because the display itself participates in picker matching. Hold until a Québec-retained name or a product-matched bilingual source establishes the full group scope.

## Recommendation

Keep the CFIA Red Mullet market group unallocated in the active catalog, legacy-import identities, and research ledger. Canadian evidence supports an English group spanning *Mullus* and *Upeneichthys*, but CFIA leaves the French cells blank on the Red Mullet rows. The apparent French companion `rouget-barbet` comes from separate CFIA Goatfish rows, an old AFNOR-authored GDT record scoped to *Mullus*, and Québec retail wording without a taxon; OQLF also says `rouget-barbet` was not retained for fish-product marketing and scopes preferred `rouget` to *Pseudupeneus*. The evidence therefore does not establish one Québec French name with the same scope as Red Mullet.

Add no reference, alias, legacy candidate, ledger source, form, measure, density, unit mapping, conversion, yield, recipe link, or substitution. Do not change R63 or R129. Revisit only if an authoritative Québec fish-product source or a current, species-scoped bilingual product listing supplies a French display coextensive with the Red Mullet group.
