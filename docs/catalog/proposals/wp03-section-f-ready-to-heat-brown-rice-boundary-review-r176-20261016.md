# WP03 Section F R176 — Bistro Express ready-to-heat brown-rice product boundary (2026-10-16)

**Recommendation: hold a separate catalog identity and aliases.** BEN'S ORIGINAL BISTRO EXPRESS Wholegrain Brown Rice is a current Canadian brown-rice pouch that is heated before serving. A Québec Metro listing uses the French title **Riz brun à grains entiers, Bistro Express**. The product is specific and well attested, but adding its bilingual title as an identity would make ordinary `brown rice` / `riz brun` searches return the branded ready-to-heat product beside the generic brown-rice ingredient. Keep the product and its evidence source-bounded until the picker can preserve the product qualifier or a catalog-level rule establishes that packaged cooked products belong under a more precise, safely searchable identity.

This is a new product-state review under the still-open Section F regional cereal-mix and preprocessed-grain item ([plan](../../RecipeBook-global-ingredient-catalog-plan.txt#L3510)). It addresses one Canadian ready-to-heat brown-rice SKU only. It does not reopen R174's hulless-oat/oat-rice decision or R175's Red River Cream of Whole Wheat review.

## Product boundary

The Canadian manufacturer describes its 250 g pouch as wholegrain brown rice to heat in a microwave for 90 seconds or in a skillet [S01]. Metro Québec lists the same product in French as `Riz brun à grains entiers, Bistro Express`; its listed ingredients are water, wholegrain parboiled brown rice, and canola oil [S02]. This establishes a ready-to-heat product presentation, not a generic recipe equivalent for dry brown rice or an ingredient-only brown-rice product.

Keep the SKU distinct in evidence from:

- generic `brown rice / riz brun`, which remains the owner of ordinary brown-rice queries;
- `quick-cooking rice / riz à cuisson rapide`, whose reviewed scope is dry, cooked-and-dehydrated rice sold for final preparation, rather than this ready-to-heat pouch;
- parboiled rice, which is a processing identity and does not itself mean fully cooked or microwave-ready;
- flavored Bistro Express rice and multi-ingredient ready-rice products, which are excluded from this plain brown-rice product review.

No new shared catalog, ledger, plan, roadmap, or batch data is proposed. The brand and SKU name remain proposal-local evidence only.

- The ingredient list includes water and canola oil with the brown rice. It does not establish that the retail pouch is equivalent by mass or volume to generic cooked brown rice in a recipe.
- Add no active measure, default density, unit mapping, form, cooking yield, dry-to-cooked conversion, or recipe link.
- No reviewed source establishes a reusable substitution ratio or method between the pouch, generic cooked brown rice, quick-cooking dry rice, or parboiled rice. Add no global or contextual substitution rule.

## Disposition

Hold this one ready-to-heat brown-rice pouch outside the active reference catalog and ledger for now. Preserve the manufacturer and Québec retail evidence in this proposal; keep generic brown rice, quick-cooking rice, and parboiled rice unchanged. Revisit if the picker can preserve the full product qualifier for narrow searches, or if the catalog's product-scope policy explicitly accepts this class of packaged cooked rice without degrading generic brown-rice search.
