## Recommendation

Propose identity-only **koji-zuke (vegetables pickled with koji)**. MAFF defines the *koji-zuke* pickle class to include vegetables and seafood pickled in koji, or in koji with additions such as sugars, mirin, and spices. This proposal limits the identity to vegetables. The National Institute of Health and Nutrition food-list appendix has one specifically named eggplant *koji-zuke* entry made with rice koji; treat that as an example only, not a universal grain, substrate, or recipe specification. [S01–S02]

Keep the candidate separate from `shio-koji` as a seasoning ingredient, `miso-zuke` as a miso-based pickle, and `nukazuke` as a rice-bran pickle. Keep separately named foods such as bettara-zuke and sagohachi-zuke out of the aliases: MAFF lists them as named examples, not synonyms for this proposed catalog label. Do not extend the vegetable row to fish or other non-vegetable foods in MAFF's broader class. [S01]

Use the source-attested English spelling `koji-zuke` with a vegetable-scope description. For fr-CA, use the borrowed name plus a provisional descriptive gloss; no Québec-standardized French term was found. Add no aliases, forms, density, unit mappings, reusable measures, recipe links, conversions, yields, or active substitutions.

## Identity and bilingual boundary

MAFF's national pickles classification describes *koji-zuke* as vegetables or certain seafood pickled in koji, sometimes with sugars, mirin, or spices added to the koji material. It lists *koji-zuke* separately from miso-zuke, rice-bran pickles, sake-lees pickles, soy-sauce pickles, and other methods. The source's broader category includes non-vegetarian foods; this proposal intentionally limits the catalog row to vegetables. It does not impose one vegetable list or one addition formula. [S01]

The NIBN appendix lists `Eggplant (Koji-zuke: pickled small oval type with rice koji)` among vegetable pickles. This supports the existence of a named vegetable example and the English spelling `Koji-zuke`; it does not show that all koji-zuke uses rice koji, small oval eggplant, or the same preparation. [S02]

The displays should read:

| Locale | Proposed display | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|
| English | `koji-zuke (vegetables pickled with koji)` | `Koji-zuke` appears in the NIBN food-list entry. The parenthetical makes this proposed row's vegetable scope explicit. |
| French (Canada) | `koji-zuke (légumes marinés au koji)` | Provisional descriptive fr-CA wording. No Québec authority or reviewed French-language source standardizes this generic preserve name; the parenthetical is not an alias. |

Do not add aliases `koji`, `rice koji`, `shio-koji`, `miso-zuke`, `nukazuke`, `Japanese pickles`, `bettara-zuke`, or `sagohachi-zuke`. The single NIBN rice-koji eggplant entry cannot support a catalog-wide rice-koji constraint or synonym.

The cited MAFF and NIBN URLs are already present under R1047's `ingredient-ref-misozuke-vegetables`. Their currently recorded source descriptions remain attached to that row. If accepted, add fresh locators `WP05-SECTIONW-KOJI-ZUKE-VEGETABLES-R1050-20261019-S01` and `...-S02`, with evidence statements limited to the generic koji-zuke classification and the specific NIBN eggplant example, respectively.
