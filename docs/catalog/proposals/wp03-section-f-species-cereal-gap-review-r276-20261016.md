## Species and French-name evidence

FSSAI's current online cereals standard covers whole or dehulled millets and lists `Barnyard Millet (Samakechawal or Sanwa or Jhangora)` as *Echinochloa crus-galli* and *E. colona* [S01]. The 2025 ICAR–Indian Institute of Millets Research manual describes barnyard millet as including Indian *E. frumentacea* and Japanese *E. esculenta*, both cultivated as food grains [S02]. Health Canada's crop-group page lists `Millet, barnyard (*E. frumentacea*)` within cereal grains [S03]. These primary sources establish real human-food and agricultural use of the name, but they do not supply a shared taxonomic crosswalk between the different named sets. Do not treat a crop-group classification, historical name, or unqualified Canadian retail label as proof that all products called barnyard millet are one species.

Québec French evidence remains insufficient for the broad class. The OQLF entry gives `millet`, `mil`, and `petit mil` as broad terms and explicitly says `millet` may group several species; it does not name barnyard millet [S04]. Québec's AgriRéseau cover-crop guide uses `millet japonais` for *E. frumentacea* in an agricultural context [S05]. FAO's French millet page uses `millet japonais` for *E. esculenta* [S06]. The terms are therefore source- and species-sensitive, and neither source is a Québec human-food label.

The newly located Spice Divine Ottawa listing gives a direct French title, `Millet des champs S&V (non poli) 1 kg`, for the English product `S&V Barnyard Millet (Unpolished) 2.2Lb` [S07–S08]. The product page has no botanical declaration or Québec terminology claim; the French body later uses `millet japonais`, and the listing was shown out of stock in its indexed snapshot. The nearby Burlington retailer's current S&V collection lists a separate `S&V Barnyard Millet` product, but no species or French label [existing WP02-A6 source `WP02-A6-BARNYARD-FLOUR-20261012-S01`]. The Ottawa title can be retained as product-specific research wording; it cannot be promoted to a general fr-CA display or alias from this evidence.
