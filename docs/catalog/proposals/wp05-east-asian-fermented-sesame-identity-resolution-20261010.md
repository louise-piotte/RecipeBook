# WP05 East Asian fermented-soy and sesame-paste identity resolution — 2026-10-10

- **Packet type:** proposal only; not integrated.
- **Scope:** douchi; white and red fermented bean curd; Chinese-style sesame paste; the broader unqualified sesame-paste phrase.
- **Prepared and source-access date:** 2026-10-10.
- **Recipe scope:** every recipe listed in the live bundled-library manifest.
- **Source IDs:** WP05-EAS-ID-20261010-S01 through WP05-EAS-ID-20261010-S15. This prefix was checked against the live research ledger and proposal/batch files before use; no collision was found.

## Recommendation

Accept four bounded identities with no recipe links:

| Candidate | Proposed English display | Proposed French display | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Douchi | douchi | douchi | English alias only: fermented black soybeans. Do not add black beans, fermented black beans, haricots noirs fermentés, or a sauce name. |
| White fermented bean curd | white fermented bean curd | tofu blanc fermenté | No generic fermented bean curd or fermented tofu alias. |
| Red fermented bean curd | red fermented bean curd | tofu fermenté rouge | No generic fermented bean curd, fermented tofu, or unqualified red bean curd alias. |
| Chinese-style sesame paste | Chinese sesame paste | pâte de sésame à la chinoise | Do not add the unqualified aliases sesame paste, pâte de sésame, sesame sauce, or tahini. |

Keep unqualified **fermented bean curd / fermented tofu** and **sesame paste / pâte de sésame** on hold. The colour-qualified curd products are individually named in manufacturer and Canadian French evidence, while generic fermented-curd wording can name either product. The sesame sources support a narrow Chinese-style paste and a Canadian French display term, but do not justify mapping every sesame paste or prepared sesame sauce to that identity.

These are identity proposals only. Do not add preparation forms, densities, unit mappings, package yields, measurements, conversions, substitutions, or recipe-line links. Ingredient and process details on a source product page remain product-specific.

## Candidate dispositions and exact limits

| Candidate | Disposition | Recommended scope and limits |
|---|---|---|
| **Douchi** | **ACCEPT, narrow** | Propose one douchi identity, with douchi as the English and French display and fermented black soybeans as the sole English alias. HONGSING identifies douchi as a fermented, salted black-soybean condiment [S02]; the Canadian Intellectual Property Office glosses Douchi as fermented black soybeans and its French trademark journal uses douchi [S03–S04]. Do not add the ambiguous phrases fermented black beans, salted black beans, black beans, haricots noirs fermentés, or soja noir fermenté. Do not alias to prepared black bean sauce, chili-black-bean sauce, or doubanjiang. The regulator also lists douchi and fermented bean curd as separate categories [S01]. |
| **White fermented bean curd** | **ACCEPT, colour-qualified** | Propose white fermented bean curd / tofu blanc fermenté. Wei-Chuan lists white fermented bean curd in seasoning sauce as a standalone packaged product [S06]. Lee Kum Kee Canada uses tofu blanc fermenté for an ingredient in Chu Hou paste [S07], providing current Canadian French wording; it does not make Chu Hou paste the same identity. Do not alias to plain tofu, tofu fermenté, generic fermented bean curd, or red fermented bean curd. Do not generalize the source product’s brine or ingredients to all brands. |
| **Red fermented bean curd** | **ACCEPT, colour-qualified** | Propose red fermented bean curd / tofu fermenté rouge. Zheng Sheng Food lists a red fermented bean-curd product as a manufacturer product [S08]. Montreal retailer SUE Foods uses the French retail title tofu fermenté rouge for a Koon Yick Wah Kee product [S09]; its page was marked coming soon when accessed, so it supports wording and product listing, not current stock. Lee Kum Kee separately lists red fermented bean curd among sauce ingredients [S10]. Do not add unqualified red bean curd, red bean paste, generic fermented tofu, or the white product’s name as aliases. Product recipes and red colourants vary; no formula is proposed. |
| **Unqualified fermented bean curd / fermented tofu** | **HOLD** | Do not add a parent row or alias the unqualified phrase to either colour-qualified identity. White and red are both independently documented products and are listed separately by the manufacturer [S10]. Preserve recipe text that does not specify colour rather than inferring a variant. |
| **Chinese-style sesame paste** | **ACCEPT, style-qualified** | Propose Chinese sesame paste / pâte de sésame à la chinoise, with no extra aliases. LCBO’s Canadian English and French versions of one sesame-noodle recipe use Chinese sesame paste / pâte de sésame à la chinoise and allow tahini in that recipe’s dressing [S11]. Manufacturer Foreway sells a traditional sesame sauce (paste) made from milled white sesame and specifically recommends it as an ingredient for Chinese sesame noodles; its product menu lists that category separately from its Tahini product [S12–S14]. This evidence supports a narrow product style and display, not a universal formulation or global substitution. |
| **Unqualified sesame paste / pâte de sésame** | **HOLD** | Do not add these broad phrases as aliases or map them to tahini. The source-backed Chinese-style term is more specific; the Canadian LCBO recipe’s one-recipe “or tahini” option is not a general identity crosswalk. Keep sesame seeds, sesame oil, tahini / beurre de sésame, and prepared sesame sauce separate [S05, S11–S15]. |

For accepted identities, add only the paired displays and the single stated douchi English alias. Do not add alternate spellings or broader French synonyms from non-Canadian sources. The named ingredient terms do not imply that every product has one recipe, brine, colourant, texture, or use.
