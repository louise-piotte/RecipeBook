# WP05 Section W — regional preserved and fermented vegetable boundary review

- **Packet type:** review plus root-integrated identity batch.
- **Reviewed:** 2026-10-12.
- **Scope:** open Section W preserved/fermented vegetable gaps remaining after the completed zha cai, Yibin ya cai, pickled radish, takuan, fermented bamboo-shoot, and Korean kimchi/jeotgal work. Checked the active plan, related proposals and integration READMEs, references, forms, legacy candidates, ledger, and all 114 manifest-listed recipes.
- **Disposition:** integrated identity-only references for generic `pickled mustard greens` and named Japanese `takana-zuke`. Kept broader preserved-mustard wording and other named regional preserves separate until their vegetable, preparation, and Québec French label are source-bounded. No exact recipe line requests either integrated identity.
- **Testing:** no tests or emulator checks were run because this change only adds database JSON data under `AGENTS.md`.

## Exact-name ownership and recipe demand

The pre-integration exact-key audit found no owner for either ID or full display pair. The only near-name vegetable owner was the raw `ingredient-ref-mustard-greens / mustard greens / feuilles de moutarde`; the preservation qualifier was absent. The active `ingredient-ref-pickled-radish / pickled radish / radis mariné` remains its own salt-and-vinegar class. No exact candidate name appeared in the 86 ingredient forms or 66 legacy candidates. The two accepted names now have distinct normalized displays; in particular, the generic French phrase appears only on the generic preserve. Existing ledger evidence for sinki and Hunan radish peel belongs to the active pickled-radish boundary record and its earlier source IDs; this review does not alter those records.

I scanned every ingredient row in all **114** manifest-listed recipes (**1,123** rows), including `originalText`, `ingredientName`, and linked reference IDs. The scan found **zero** exact or source-variant matches for `pickled mustard greens`, `preserved mustard greens`, `feuilles de moutarde marinées`, `takana`, `takana-zuke`, `takana shiozuke`, `suan cai`, `suan tsai`, `xue cai`, `mei cai`, `mei gan cai`, `dongcai`, `paocai`, `sinki`, `radish peel`, and the Hunan white-radish-peel wording. Fresh mustard-greens, cabbage, and radish wording was not upgraded into preserved-product demand. No recipe link is proposed.
