# WP02 Section 04 B3 R195 — waxy sorghum starch product-boundary hold

- **Reviewed:** 2026-10-16.
- **Status:** Integrated as a documentation-only hold. The proposal, checklist sub-item, roadmap, and integration README record the decision; no active reference, ledger candidate, recipe link, or application file was added.
- **Checklist:** WP02 Section 04 B3, [Water-chestnut flour and other documented regional starches](../../RecipeBook-global-ingredient-catalog-plan.txt#L2513). The parent remains open. This packet reviews the unallocated waxy-sorghum-starch subtype; it does not reopen waxy maize (R192), waxy rice (R189), generic sorghum starch (R133), or the water-chestnut ambiguity.
- **Scope:** Starch experimentally isolated from waxy sorghum grain, most commonly Sorghum bicolor in the studies reviewed. The packet asks whether this amylopectin-rich starch subtype merits an active identity separate from the generic sorghum-starch class.
- **New proposal-local source-ID prefix:** WP02-SECTION-04-B3-WAXY-SORGHUM-STARCH-R195-20261016. The prefix and candidate ID were checked against the live catalog, ledger, legacy identities, checklist, and proposal/batch archive. No R195 source IDs or exact waxy-sorghum-starch owner were found.
- **Source reuse:** Reuse existing source IDs for the exact OQLF and current Québec sorghum-flour URLs. New IDs S01–S03 each map to one new URL. No source ID is assigned to more than one URL.

## Recommendation

**Hold a separate waxy-sorghum-starch reference outside the active catalog and ledger.** Primary research identifies and isolates waxy sorghum starch as a starch type with properties that differ from normal sorghum starch. However, current Canadian and Québec evidence reviewed here identifies sorghum flour or a natural-health-product ingredient record, not a current culinary waxy-sorghum-starch product. OQLF supports the broader Québec terms sorghum starch and waxy cereal starch, but the complete French compound for this source has not been established as a Québec product name. The existing generic sorghum-starch reference already owns the species-class term.

Do not add an alias to the generic sorghum-starch row, add a second active reference, or create a ledger candidate now. Keep the current sorghum-starch identity unchanged. Reopen if a current Canadian or Québec human-food package, supplier specification, or food-service product source identifies waxy sorghum starch as a marketed grade and a Québec terminology review confirms its French display. This is a bounded market and naming hold, not a claim that waxy sorghum starch is unavailable elsewhere.

## Canadian and Québec product evidence

A bounded public search on 2026-10-16 used the phrases waxy sorghum starch, sorghum starch Canada, waxy sorghum starch Canada, amidon de sorgho cireux, and amidon de sorgho Québec, with Canadian, Québec, and retailer qualifiers. It found research and broader ingredient references, but no current Canadian or Québec human-food package or supplier page for waxy sorghum starch. This is a bounded non-discovery, not proof of market-wide absence.

The adjacent Canadian/Québec evidence does not close the gap:

- The Québec Food Directory's current gluten-free all-purpose flour blend lists farine de sorgho alongside tapioca and potato starch. It names sorghum flour as one blend component, not sorghum starch. Its cup-for-cup direction applies to the whole blend. [WP02-B3-SORGHUM-STARCH-R133-20261015-S03]
- Mako Foods Exotik's current Québec-facing listing describes a 700 g package as 100% sorghum flour. This establishes local flour wording and availability only; it does not identify waxy sorghum or extracted starch. The package size is not a recipe measure. [WP02-B3-SORGHUM-STARCH-R133-20261015-S04]
- Health Canada's Natural Health Products Ingredients Database change log records Sorghum Starch and the preparation methods Dry and Powdered in a 2021 update. This is natural-health-product ingredient vocabulary, not a culinary-food package, food-grade specification, or evidence of current Canadian retail stock. [S03]
- OQLF's French term amidon de sorgho is broad to the Sorghum genus, and its separate waxy-starch entry gives general cereal terminology. Neither entry validates a marketed waxy-sorghum product or the full French compound proposed for review. [WP02-B3-SORGHUM-STARCH-R133-20261015-S01; WP02-SECTION-04-B3-NATIVE-WAXY-RICE-STARCH-R189-20261016-S03]

## Measures, density, forms, and substitutions

No reusable measurement or substitution behavior is supported.

- The 2008 comparison reports amylose content, lab isolation recovery, thermal properties, rheology, and test-specific starch-water dispersions for particular samples. These are sample and protocol observations, not household portions, g/mL density, package servings, or recipe conversions. Do not transfer the study's extraction recovery or experimental starch-water ratios into catalog mappings. [S02]
- The 2025 paper compares methods for isolating starch from waxy sorghum grain. Extraction methods and laboratory results do not define a user-facing form, bulk density, volume-to-mass mapping, or cooking measure. [S01]
- Neither primary study tests waxy sorghum starch as a general replacement for maize, rice, tapioca, potato, or another starch in recipes. Food-ingredient potential or thickening behavior is not a validated substitution ratio.
- Keep defaultDensity null, unitMappings empty, forms absent, and substitution rules absent unless later product-specific or recipe-specific evidence supports them.

4. Add a measure, density, conversion, form, yield, or substitution only with separate evidence that supports that exact behavior and scope.
5. Keep the Section 04 B3 parent open. This documentation-only hold requires no software tests.
