# WP02 Section 04 B3 R142 — foxtail-millet starch product-boundary hold

- **Reviewed:** 2026-10-15.
- **Status:** Proposal-only hold. No shared catalog, research-ledger, checklist, roadmap, generated, recipe, or application file was changed.
- **Checklist:** [WP02 Section 04 B3](../../RecipeBook-global-ingredient-catalog-plan.txt#L2209), `[ ] Water-chestnut flour and other documented regional starches.` The parent remains open after the R141 lentil-starch integration.
- **Candidate:** Food starch isolated from foxtail millet, *Setaria italica* (synonym *Panicum italicum* in OQLF’s GDT entry).
- **Scope:** The isolated starch fraction, distinct from foxtail millet grain, whole-grain flour, and dry-milled foxtail millet rava. This is also distinct from R139’s pearl-millet (*Pennisetum glaucum*) starch review.
- **Proposal-local source-ID prefix:** `WP02-B3-FOXTAIL-MILLET-STARCH-R142-20261015-S`. The exact candidate, hypothetical reference ID, and source prefix were checked against current references, forms, ledger, legacy identities, plan, and proposal/batch archive; no focused foxtail-starch decision or allocated ID/source prefix was found.

## Recommendation

**Hold foxtail millet starch outside the active catalog and ledger.** Primary research establishes an extractable *Setaria italica* starch fraction and documents food-science properties. The current Canadian and Canadian French-facing product pages reviewed name foxtail millet grain, flour, or rava. The bounded product search found no standalone Canadian/Québec human-food foxtail-millet-starch package or supplier page. Those adjacent products do not establish a marketed starch identity.

OQLF gives the Québec names **millet d’Italie**, **sétaire d’Italie**, and **millet des oiseaux** for *Setaria italica*, and identifies **foxtail millet** in English. Its entry is for the plant and warns that *millet des oiseaux* also refers to bird feed. OQLF’s 2026 entry prefers **amidon** for starch generally and reserves **fécule** more specifically for starch in tubers or underground organs. Neither entry attests a product name such as **amidon de millet d’Italie**. That compound is only a possible descriptive construction, not a validated Québec label. [S01–S02]

Do not add a reference, ledger candidate, aliases, forms, density, unit mappings, measurements, recipe links, conversions, yields, or substitutions. Do not merge this candidate with pearl millet, proso millet, finger millet, whole foxtail millet, foxtail flour, or foxtail rava.

- FreshOnTheGo Grocery in Scarborough, Ontario, lists **Foxtail Thinai Millet** in its millet assortment, **Foxtail flour** in the same assortment, and a separate **Foxtail Millet Rava** product under its flour/sooji listings. The rava product page is currently marked out of stock. Rava remains a dry-milled grain product, not an extracted-starch product. [S06]
- The Canadian French-language retail listing **Ingrano Millet de Sétaire Bio 1Lb** describes whole millet grain as *millet à queue de renard* and *millet des oiseaux*. The page is marked out of stock, and neither its title nor copy identifies starch. It is market wording for the whole grain, not a Québec starch label. [S07]
- Targeted current searches on 2026-10-15 for `foxtail millet starch`, `foxtail starch`, `thinai starch`, `amidon de millet d’Italie`, `amidon de sétaire d’Italie`, and *Setaria italica* starch across Canadian/Québec retailer and product-directory results found research pages and grain/flour listings but no standalone human-food starch package. This bounded non-discovery does not prove that no such product exists. [S08]

## Measurements, forms, and substitution limits

- The cited studies characterize isolated laboratory samples and report extraction or functional measurements tied to specific cultivars, methods, or waxy/non-waxy states. They do not establish bulk density, a cup/spoon mass, a package serving, or a reusable kitchen conversion for a Canadian product. Do not create `defaultDensity`, unit mappings, or a package-yield record. [S03–S04]
- Laboratory extraction is not a catalog form conversion from whole grain or flour to starch. Add no forms, form rules, or preparation yields.
- Functional or thickening properties do not establish a tested culinary replacement for corn, potato, tapioca, or another starch. No product-specific household recipe substitution was found; add no global or contextual substitution.
- The bundled corpus has no starch line to link. The rava and flour entries are separate products and cannot supply recipe demand or measurements for extracted starch.

## Exact proposed delta

**No JSON delta.** Keep foxtail millet starch outside the active references and research ledger. Do not reserve `ingredient-ref-foxtail-millet-starch` or create a candidate record. Keep Section 04 B3 open.

Reconsider only after a current Canadian/Québec human-food source identifies a standalone foxtail-millet-starch product and a product-specific Québec French term is independently reviewed. Any future measurement, form, conversion, yield, recipe link, or substitution requires evidence specific to that product and culinary use.
