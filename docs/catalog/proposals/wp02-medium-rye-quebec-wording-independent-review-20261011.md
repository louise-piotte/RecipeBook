# Independent fr-CA wording review: medium rye flour

- **Review date:** 2026-10-11
- **Scope:** French display wording only for the proposed `medium rye flour` reference.
- **Disposition:** **Accepted and integrated** as the fr-CA display for `ingredient-ref-medium-rye-flour`.
- **Proposal-local source IDs:** `WP02-MED-RYE-FRCA-20261011-S01` through `S04`; checked for reuse in the proposal set before drafting.
- **Change scope:** This review did not edit shared files; the coordinator integrated the accepted identity in `docs/catalog/batches/wp02-medium-rye-flour-integration-20261011/README.md`.

## Recommendation

Keep the proposed bilingual pair:

| Field | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| English display | `medium rye flour` |
| fr-CA display | `farine de seigle moyenne` |
| Additional French aliases | None supported by this review |

**Rationale:** The Government of Canada Translation Bureau’s TERMIUM Plus lists the exact pair `medium rye flour / farine de seigle moyenne`, marks the French term correct, and identifies it as feminine. A federal bilingual bakery glossary repeats the same term. `Moyenne` agrees with the feminine noun `farine`; the post-noun placement also matches the attested form. No sourced reason supports replacing it with `farine moyenne de seigle` or adding a variant alias. [S01, S02]

The OQLF entry reviewed here covers the generic `farine de seigle` and discusses flour colour, but does not name the medium grade. Its limited scope neither endorses nor contradicts the federal grade term. A current Walmart.ca French marketplace listing uses `farine de seigle biologique moyenne`, but the item is sold by a third-party seller and Walmart says seller and third-party product details are not separately verified. Treat that as weak evidence that the phrase appears in Canadian commerce, not as an authoritative Québec label or the basis for this recommendation. [S03, S04]

This wording decision does not establish a uniform milling threshold, composition, extraction rate, or product specification for medium rye flour. Keep the separate product-boundary limits in the product-boundary proposal; do not add further French synonyms from this terminology review.

## Review notes

- **Accept** the exact proposed display `farine de seigle moyenne`; do not add `seigle moyen` or alternate word order as aliases.
- The reviewed OQLF entry supports the generic head term only. This review does not claim that OQLF has officially preferred or specifically reviewed the compound.
- No direct Québec manufacturer or mill package label in native French was located in this focused search. The product listing is recorded with its marketplace limitation.
- No tests were run; this is a source-based wording review and changes no application or database data.
