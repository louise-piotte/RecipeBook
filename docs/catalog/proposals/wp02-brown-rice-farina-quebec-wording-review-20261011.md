# Brown-rice-farina Québec French display review

- **Reviewed:** 2026-10-11
- **Status:** Independent fr-CA wording review; proposal only.
- **Target:** Proposed `ingredient-ref-brown-rice-farina` in [the A7 residual grain-meal follow-up](wp02-a7-residual-grain-meals-followup-20261011.md), with proposed fr-CA display `céréales chaudes crémeuses au riz brun`.
- **Scope:** Decide whether this wording is suitable as a generic catalog display, while distinguishing it from Bob’s Red Mill’s documented Canadian product name. No shared catalog, recipe, ledger, plan, roadmap, or integration file was changed.
- **Proposal-local source IDs:** `WP02-BROWN-RICE-FARINA-QUEBEC-WORDING-20261011-S01` through `S05`. These IDs are unique to this review; `[S01]` etc. abbreviate the full prefix.

## Recommendation

**Hold `céréales chaudes crémeuses au riz brun` as the generic fr-CA display. Accept it as the exact Canadian French product title for Bob’s Red Mill’s matching brown-rice hot cereal.** The phrase is grammatical and is attested by both the manufacturer’s current Canadian French product list and a Québec retailer listing. Those sources document a product name for one branded SKU; they do not establish a general Québec term for *brown rice farina*.

The OQLF material located for *farina* associates the English term with wheat semolina and defines *semoule* in wheat-milling contexts [S03]. TERMIUM’s milling entry likewise defines English *farina* as a wheat product, though its French definition is internally inconsistent with its English definition [S04]. Neither source supplies a rice-farina equivalent. The available evidence therefore does not support replacing the held phrase with `semoule de riz brun`, `farina de riz brun`, or another generic translation. No alternative generic fr-CA display is recommended here.

The proposed target is not yet present in the live seed references; this review concerns only the proposed ID and display in the A7 packet. If the row is retained as a generic ingredient identity, leave its fr-CA display unresolved pending independent terminology review. If it is instead represented specifically as Bob’s product, the phrase may be used as a source-attested product name with that narrow provenance.

## Decision and limits

- **Product-specific wording:** Accept `céréales chaudes crémeuses au riz brun` for the Bob’s Red Mill product identified in S01. La Moisson’s current Québec listing corroborates this exact wording in local retail use [S02].
- **Generic catalog wording:** Hold the same phrase for a generic `brown rice farina` reference. `Céréales chaudes` names a preparation/product category and `crémeuses` is part of the manufacturer’s product title; neither source shows that the complete phrase is a general ingredient-class term.
- **No replacement proposed:** The authority evidence is wheat-specific, while product sources only give the branded wording. A direct rendering such as `farina de riz brun` or `semoule de riz brun` would be an unsupported extrapolation here. Keep the proposed row’s generic fr-CA display unavailable until terminology support is found.
- **No extra alias:** Do not add the product title as a generic French alias on the basis of this review. The iHerb variant reinforces that retailer title phrasing can vary [S05].
