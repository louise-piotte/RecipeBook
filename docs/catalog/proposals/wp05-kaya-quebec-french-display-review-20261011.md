# WP05 kaya — Québec French display review

- **Reviewed:** 2026-10-11
- **Disposition:** Retain the existing `kaya / kaya` display and English-only `kaya jam` alias. Keep French aliases empty.
- **Scope:** Québec-facing French name for the existing egg-and-coconut kaya spread; no identity or recipe integration is proposed.

## Recommendation

Keep the canonical pair `kaya / kaya`. A current Montréal restaurant menu retains *kaya* in the French item name *Kaya toast et oeuf d’onsen* and describes the spread as *tartinade à la noix de coco*. A Québec travel article and Vacances Air Canada’s Canadian French page use *confiture de noix de coco* as explanatory prose. These descriptions show that readers may benefit from a coconut-spread gloss, but the phrases are broad and do not name kaya specifically. OQLF defines *confiture* as a fruit preparation; kaya is an egg-and-coconut spread. Keep the native product name visible and do not add either descriptive phrase as a French alias.

Retain `kaya jam` as the sole English alias. Keep `coconut jam`, `coconut egg jam`, `confiture de noix de coco`, and `tartinade à la noix de coco` out of canonical aliases. Existing evidence identifies pandan and caramel versions as kaya styles, not separate ingredient identities. This review adds no catalog reference, alias, form, recipe link, measurement, conversion, yield, or substitution.

This is a Québec-oriented naming decision based on current local menu language and French-Canadian descriptive use. It does not claim OQLF standardization for *kaya*.
