# WP02 B2 R180 — Maya nut seed flour Canada/Québec boundary hold

- **Reviewed:** 2026-10-16.
- **Status:** Proposal only. This is the only file changed; no catalog, ledger, plan, roadmap, generated, code, or batch file was edited.
- **Plan fit:** One unreviewed B2 seed-derived flour candidate under the still-open Section 04 B2 flour checklist. The candidate is flour made from *Brosimum alicastrum* seeds, called Maya nut or ramón in the reviewed sources. R137 reviewed *Artocarpus camansi* seed starch; its mention of *B. alicastrum* / `breadnut tree` is a common-name collision boundary, not a flour decision for this species.
- **Proposal-local source IDs:** `WP02-B2-MAYA-NUT-FLOUR-R180-20261016-S01` through `S05`. The Health Canada crop-group source is already represented in the ledger and is cited as reused evidence; no new ledger entry is proposed.

## Recommendation

**Hold a separate Maya nut seed-flour identity and all aliases pending a current Canadian/Québec flour product and product-matched Québec French flour name.** Primary studies and a current US manufacturer establish roasted or dried-and-milled *B. alicastrum* seed flour as a real food product. The Canadian-facing product found is sold as roasted ramón powder and positioned mainly as a beverage; it does not establish a Canadian flour grade or a Québec flour label. The current MayaNut™ flour listing is US-branded and out of stock at review. TERMIUM supplies `noix-pain` only as a botanical French name for *B. alicastrum*, not a flour-product term. Do not treat the Canadian powder as flour, repurpose `noix-pain` as a product name, or add `breadnut` as an alias. [S01–S05]

Reopen if a current Canadian/Québec consumer product explicitly identifies culinary flour from *B. alicastrum* seeds and supports a French display for that same flour. Keep a future flour distinct from roasted beverage powder, whole fruit/seeds, *Artocarpus* breadnut seed products, and breadfruit flour. No active reference, alias, form, ledger candidate, recipe link, measurement, conversion, yield, or substitution is proposed.

## Numeric, form, and substitution boundaries

- **Measures and density:** The Canadian roasted-powder listing’s package weights and any preparation serving directions are source-specific to that beverage powder. They do not establish a flour cup weight, flour density, unit mapping, or reusable conversion. The MayaNut flour listing gives a 1/2 lb package but no household measure or mass-volume pairing. Add no active density, mapping, portion, or conversion. [S01, S03]
- **Research values:** Maldonado-Robledo et al. compare sun-dried and hot-air-dried seed flours and test particular cookie formulas; the reported replacement levels are formulation-specific. Other studies use their own seed sourcing, drying, roasting, grind, and recipe conditions. Do not convert experimental composition, absorption, or formulation percentages into a generic flour measurement or catalog behavior. [S02, S05]
- **Forms:** Add none. Lightly roasted baking flour and dark roasted beverage powder are described as different commercial products by the maker; the available Canadian listing does not define its powder as a form of the flour. Whole seeds, flour, and roasted beverage powder remain separate product states unless direct product evidence connects them. [S01, S03]
- **Substitutions:** Add none. A study’s partial wheat-flour replacement in one cookie or tortilla formula is not a general wheat replacement ratio and does not establish substitution against other seed or nut flours. Do not add a global or contextual substitution, yield, or recipe-line link. [S02, S05]

## Reopen criteria

1. Locate a current Canadian/Québec consumer flour product or supplier specification explicitly identifying culinary flour from *B. alicastrum* seeds, and distinguish it from roasted drink powder.
2. Confirm a product-matched Québec French name; do not infer flour terminology from the botanical `noix-pain` entry or from English-only international listings.
3. Keep any future flour separate from *Artocarpus* breadnut/breadfruit products, whole *B. alicastrum* seeds, and roasted beverage powder; review every alias for picker collisions before activation.
4. Reassess measurements, forms, recipe demand, and substitutions independently against the actual consumer flour. Do not transfer package size or study-specific formulation percentages into reusable data.
5. Leave the broad WP02 B2 checklist open; this hold does not complete the flour-coverage item.

## Root integration disposition

**Accepted as a hold.** Keep Maya nut/ramón seed flour, its proposed names, and all aliases outside
the active references and research ledger. The Canadian-facing roasted ramón powder does not establish
a culinary flour product, and the current flour listing does not establish Canadian availability or a
product-matched Québec French name. No candidate mention appears in the bundled recipe corpus. The B2
flour-coverage item remains open.
