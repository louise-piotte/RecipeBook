# Independent Québec French review: Shiratamako display

- **Review date:** 2026-10-11
- **Disposition:** Provisional accept for the current fr-CA display; evidence supports internal Québec French review.
- **Scope:** Review only the display `farine de riz gluant Shiratamako` on `ingredient-ref-shiratamako-glutinous-rice-flour`. This is not a review of the product identity, aliases, measurements, recipe links, or substitutions.
- **Status:** Proposal only. No catalog, ledger, plan, roadmap, code, test, or existing proposal files changed.

## Conclusion

Keep **`farine de riz gluant Shiratamako`** as the provisional Québec French display. `Riz gluant` is a Québec-preferred term for the rice, and Télé-Québec uses the exact descriptive gloss `shiratamako (farine de riz gluant)` and the construction `farine de type shiratamako`. These sources support the current catalog wording as clear, natural descriptive French, with the Japanese product name retained as the distinguishing term.

This is not an OQLF entry for the full phrase or a claim that the phrase appears on a manufacturer’s French package. A current Canadian French retail page uses the close title `Farine Tamasan Shiratamako` and describes the product as flour made from Japanese glutinous rice. The Québec retailer located in this search attests `farine de riz gluant` for a generic Thai product, not Shiratamako itself. The exact display is therefore well supported for internal Québec review but remains a descriptive, product-qualified catalog label rather than officialized terminology.

This display review makes no Mochiko equivalence or substitution claim. Télé-Québec’s recipe page includes a replacement note for its named recipe; that note does not establish general interchangeability and is outside this wording decision.

## Review decision

The existing display accurately places the recognized Québec descriptor first and the Japanese product name last. The direct Télé-Québec usage makes it suitable for the internal Québec French review. I recommend **provisional acceptance of the current string** for this identity. The remaining qualification is terminological: neither OQLF nor the reviewed Canadian package listing standardizes the complete compound as an official term.

Keep `Shiratamako` in the display as a product name. Do not add it as a French alias to generic glutinous rice flour, and do not infer that the displayed product is interchangeable with Mochiko or every other glutinous-rice flour. No change to the shared catalog or ledger is proposed here.
