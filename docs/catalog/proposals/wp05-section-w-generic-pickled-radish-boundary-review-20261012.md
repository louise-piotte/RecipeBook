# WP05 Section W — generic pickled-radish boundary review

- **Packet type:** proposal only; no catalog integration performed.
- **Reviewed:** 2026-10-12.
- **Scope:** the open Section W pickled-radish candidate, checked against the active plan, existing East Asian preserve proposals and integration records, current references/forms/legacy candidates/ledger, and all manifest-listed recipe files.
- **Recommendation:** propose one identity-only generic salt-and-vinegar radish preserve, bounded to the Canadian pickles/relishes class. Keep the identity separate from takuan, named Korean radish products, fermented radish preserves, bamboo shoots, and the named Chinese mustard preserves. There is no bundled recipe-line demand.
- **Write scope:** this proposal file only. No catalog, recipe, ledger, plan, roadmap, or test file was changed. No tests were run.

## Recommendation

| Proposed ID | English display | Proposed fr-CA display | Aliases | Category | Supported scope |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ingredient-ref-pickled-radish` | `pickled radish` | `radis mariné` | None | `SAUCE_AND_CONDIMENT` | A generic radish preserve explicitly presented as a salt-and-vinegar pickle. Canada's compositional standard defines pickles/relishes as fruit or vegetable products prepared with salt and vinegar; spices, seasonings, sugars, and vegetable oil are permitted. This is a product-class boundary, not one recipe or formula. [S01–S03] |

The bilingual naming is supportable for that narrow class. The OQLF gives Québec/Canada French `marinades` and `conserves au vinaigre` for English `pickles`, and discourages the English borrowing `pickles`. A current Canadian French-language trademark-journal goods list uses the compound `radis mariné`; that attests the wording but is not an OQLF terminology ruling. Keep `radis mariné` as a concise display, with no extra aliases. [S02–S03]

The row should not mean every preserved or seasoned radish. In particular, the generic phrase does not settle the preparation of a named regional product. A product may be sweetened and still fit the Canadian salt-and-vinegar pickle class; sweetness alone is not a reason to split it. However, do not assign a named Korean or Japanese product to this identity based only on a retailer's generic `pickled radish` descriptor. The source does not establish that every such SKU has the same preservation process. [S01, S05–S08]

No forms, preparation states, density, unit mappings, measurements, package or cooking yields, conversions, substitutions, or recipe links are proposed. The current recipe corpus has no exact demand. Section W remains open for the distinct regional products listed below.

| Sinki and other regional fermented radish products | No active owner for `sinki`. [S09–S10] See the [bamboo boundary review](wp05-section-w-fermented-bamboo-shoot-boundary-review-20261012.md) and [integration record](../batches/wp05-section-w-fermented-bamboo-shoots-integration-20261012/README.md). | These are another plant part, not radish. Keep raw shoots separate from the fermented-shoot reference; do not treat every `pickled`, `sour`, acidified, or brined shoot as fermented. No shoot term is an alias or substitute for pickled radish. |
| Hunan pickled white-radish peel | Hunan's provincial government names `Crispy pickled white radish peel` among regional dishes. It supplies a regional product lead, not a general pickle specification or a Québec French name. [S11] | Keep the Hunan product source-scoped. Do not use the dish name as an alias for generic pickled radish or daikon. The earlier [WP06 East Asia sweep](wp06-east-asia-cuisine-gap-sweep-20261011.md) routes it back to this WP05 review. |
| Zha cai and Yibin ya cai | Active `ingredient-ref-zha-cai` and `ingredient-ref-yibin-ya-cai` are separately named Chinese preserves. Their existing source records bound zha cai to the mustard-tuber product and Yibin ya cai to its geographically registered product; neither is radish. See the [Section W integration README](../batches/wp05-section-w-east-asian-condiment-identities-20261011/README.md). | Retain each exact identity. Do not alias either preserve, generic preserved mustard greens, or generic `ya cai` to the pickled-radish proposal. |

This decision follows the earlier Section W disposition to hold broad preserved/pickled radish while separately reviewing named products. It narrows that hold to an actionable salt-and-vinegar class; it does not close the wider regional-preserve line in the plan. The active [takuan integration record](../batches/wp05-section-w-takuan-integration-20261012/README.md) and [fermented bamboo-shoot integration record](../batches/wp05-section-w-fermented-bamboo-shoots-integration-20261012/README.md) remain authoritative for their current boundaries.
