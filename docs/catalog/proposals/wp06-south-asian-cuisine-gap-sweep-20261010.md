# WP06 South Asian cuisine gap sweep proposal

**Status:** root review complete; 12 identity-only references accepted (10 new identities and 2 legacy-candidate promotions), with 18 evidence-backed candidate records held unresolved. No forms, numeric data, recipe links, or substitutions were added.  
**Research date:** 2026-10-10.  
**Packet:** India by region, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Bhutan, and Maldives, as named in Section 05 of the global ingredient catalog plan.

## Scope and live-state audit

This is a bounded source-led gap sweep, not a claim that South Asian cuisine coverage is complete. The source sample includes Kashmir, South Indian plant-name evidence, Bengali material spanning Bangladesh and West Bengal, one Pakistani nihari recipe, selected Nepalese heritage recipes and spice terminology, two Bhutan recipes, Sri Lankan sour-fish and goraka material, and Maldivian tuna dishes. The sample is intentionally not treated as a single South Asian pantry.

The Section 05 checklist asks for source review across ordinary savory dishes, baking and breads, desserts, condiments, preserved foods, festive dishes, and cooking beverages where relevant. The sample reached savory meals, breads/dumplings, desserts, condiments/preserves, and festive recipes. No cooking-beverage ingredient emerged from the reviewed recipe sources; the Nepal spice overview mentions spice oils in soft-drink processing but does not name a usable recipe ingredient. Beverage coverage remains open.

Live package snapshot read before drafting: **727 active ingredient references, 34 ingredient forms, 916 research-ledger records, 84 legacy candidates, and 114 bundled recipes with 1,123 ingredient lines.** Candidate scans found no active rows or forms for the proposed South Asian identities below. The following existing identities and unresolved records were checked to avoid duplicate or false merges:

- Active and retained: atta; amchur powder; finger millet grain; curry powder; curry paste; garam masala; ginger-garlic paste; ground cumin; coriander and coriander seed; ground cardamom; mustard seeds; pandan leaves; cane jaggery; date syrup; tamarind fruit; red rice; leek; mango pickle; lime pickle; sweet mango chutney; tuna; dried bonito; and niter kibbeh.
- The **legacy** file already contains `ingredient-ref-cumin-seeds` and `ingredient-ref-caraway-seeds`; recommend using the former rather than minting a new cumin-seed ID. The legacy generic molasses candidate remains unresolved in the WP02 syrup/molasses review. Other relevant legacy keys include generic cardamom and mustard candidates; none should be used to absorb large cardamom, mustard oil, or the species-qualified spice names below.
- The active ground-cumin row has the broad English alias `cumin`; keep that collision visible if the legacy cumin-seed candidate is promoted. Do not give seed cumin the same unqualified alias without first resolving picker behavior and the existing alias.
- The active ground-cardamom row has the broad English alias `cardamom`; do not use bare `cardamom` to absorb large/black cardamom.
- Prior WP05 Section Y packets already cover ginger-garlic paste, amchur, sweet mango chutney, mango/lime pickles, a pending mixed-pickle review, kasundi as a previously identified but unintegrated candidate, and multiple MENA/Caucasus condiments. Those are not repeated here. The active Ethiopian niter-kibbeh identity from the WP06 African sweep is explicitly kept separate from ghee.
- Candidate-name scans across the prior WP05 South/Central Asia–MENA packets and WP06 cuisine packets found no earlier proposal for curry leaves, large cardamom, fenugreek seeds, ajwain, nigella seeds, mustard oil, ghee, date-palm jaggery, finger-millet flour, panch phoron, goraka, rihaakuru, timur, or jimbu. WP06 boundary material on Sichuan pepper, cassareep, and niter kibbeh was used only to check name and product boundaries.

The packet follows Section 05 and WP06 acceptance guidance: retain source-local names, classify each unmatched mention, make no formula-wide claims from one recipe, and report the recipe benchmark and its misses. Recipe prose is not reproduced.

## Recipe benchmark

The 114 bundled recipe files were checked at the 1,123 ingredient-line level. There are no exact ingredient-line hits for the proposed new South Asian names, regional transliterations, or the proposed tuna product forms. The small amount of South Asian-named content already in the corpus is concentrated in **Garam Masala**, **Curry Powder**, and **Butter Chicken**. Those recipes use existing rows such as ground cumin, coriander, ground cardamom, curry powder, garam masala, and curry paste; they do not evidence curry leaves, whole cumin, fenugreek seed, ajwain, nigella, ghee, or the other proposed additions. A tuna recipe elsewhere in the corpus does not match the source-specific Maldivian smoked/dried tuna forms. The corpus is therefore a benchmark with substantial regional misses, not proof of broad coverage.

## Measurement and substitution disposition

Measurement and substitution evidence are tracked independently above. No candidate receives a catalog density, unit mapping, package yield, general measurement conversion, ingredient-form conversion, or active substitution rule in this packet. Where a source gives recipe amounts, the amount belongs only to that recipe. Where a source offers a dish-level alternative—such as rice or chilli choices, momo filling choices, or a dessert filling choice—it remains context for that dish and does not become a global rule. Liquid versus solid nolen-gur evidence is an identity/form question; it does not establish a general conversion or replacement ratio.

For proposed identities not called out individually in the table, the catalog measurement state is `not_yet_researched` and the catalog substitution state is separately `not_yet_researched`. For source mentions marked unresolved, both states remain unset because identity/form resolution must precede numeric or substitution research.

## Prior-proposal overlap and exclusions

- The broad WP05 Section Y proposal and follow-ups already name ginger-garlic paste, tamarind paste, chutneys, kasundi, kewra, rose syrup, and other condiments. This packet neither reopens nor duplicates those recommendations. The source article’s kasundi/hilsa context is not promoted here.
- Active amchur, atta, curry paste/powder, garam masala, pandan leaves, mango/lime pickles, sweet mango chutney, and cane jaggery stay in their current identities. The recipe sources support usage context but do not justify broader aliases.
- The WP06 African sweep’s niter kibbeh remains separate from ghee. The WP06 Mexican/Central American and South American sweeps add no duplicate for the identities proposed here. The South American cassareep and other regional souring ingredients are not aliases for goraka.
- Existing `ingredient-ref-finger-millet` is a grain identity; neither a flour recipe nor a shared crop name converts that row into a flour identity. Generic millet flour in the Bhutan source remains species-unresolved.
- Proposed `timur` and `Himali jeera` do not alias to active Sichuan pepper, ground cumin, or legacy caraway seeds. `jimbu` and chhyapi do not merge into alliums generically: chhyapi has direct leek evidence, while jimbu remains a separate source-named product candidate.
