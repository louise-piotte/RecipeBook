# WP02 Mochiko Québec French display review

- **Reviewed:** 2026-10-11
- **Status:** proposal-only; terminology review only
- **Scope:** the proposed Mochiko display `farine de riz gluant Mochiko` for the named Mochiko product. This review does not assess the Shiratamako display or change catalog data.
- **Conclusion:** **ACCEPT for the internal fr-CA display.** The wording is attested by a Montréal-area Japanese grocery and corroborated by a French-language Canadian retailer. This is a source-backed catalog wording decision, not an OQLF ruling or a manufacturer-verified package translation.

## Display decision

Accept **`farine de riz gluant Mochiko`** as the French catalog display for this Mochiko product. Keep `Mochiko` as the distinguishing product name. The phrase accurately reflects Québec retail usage and reads as a descriptive French category followed by the product name.

The exact compound is not presented here as an OQLF-preferred term. OQLF records reviewed separately support `riz gluant` as a preferred name for the rice and `farine de riz` as the generic flour term. The Montréal listing directly uses the same words in its product title, in the order `Mochiko - Farine de riz gluant`.

## Limits and review disposition

- The strongest local attestation is a Québec retailer title whose word order differs from the proposed display but contains the same category phrase and product name. A Canadian French listing uses the category phrase before `Mochiko` directly.
- Québec sources also use `farine de riz doux` for Mochiko. OQLF’s `riz doux` record is for the rice grain; it does not by itself establish `farine de riz doux` as a preferred flour term. This review accepts the proposed display based on the direct Montréal listing and the Québec recipe usage of `farine de riz gluant`, without declaring the alternate retailer wording incorrect.
- No dedicated OQLF entry for Mochiko or for the compound display was located in this targeted review. No French manufacturer package panel was reviewed.
- The retailer pages provide product-listing language, not a verified cross-manufacturer definition. This review makes no claim that Mochiko is a universal synonym for all glutinous-rice flour, has one universal manufacturing process, or is generally interchangeable with another flour.
- Retail listings were online on the retrieval date, but the Miyamoto and Hungry Ninja listings displayed sold-out status and SUE Foods displayed “Bientôt disponible.” The evidence supports product naming, not current stock.

**Internal Québec French review:** supported. I recommend marking the fr-CA display review complete for this Mochiko wording. The approval is limited to the catalog display and is not formal OQLF approval, package-label verification, or a process/identity determination.
