# WP06 Southeast Asia cuisine gap sweep proposal

**Status:** Proposal for root review; four identity recommendations, two recipe-line variants, and seven unresolved/hold decisions. No seed or shared catalog data changed.
**Research date:** 2026-10-11.
**Scope:** Thailand, Vietnam, Laos, Cambodia, Myanmar, Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia, Philippines, Brunei, and Timor-Leste, as named in Section 05 of the global catalog plan.

## Scope and live-state audit

This sweep closes the still-unreviewed WP06 Southeast Asia packet as a source review only. The sample checks savory dishes, baking and desserts, condiments and preserved products, festive foods, and cooking or table beverages where the reviewed sources make them relevant. The sources represent selected regions and household traditions; they are not a census or a universal recipe standard.

The live split seed package was read before drafting: **912 ingredient references, 49 forms, 114 recipes, and 1,123 ingredient rows**. The recipe-line scan found no exact ingredient-line hits for the four recommended identities or the held names below. Two existing corpus recipes request generic fish sauce (recipes 093 and 095); recipe 095 also requests coconut milk and red curry paste. Recipe 086 requests curry paste. Those exact lines do not establish demand for the proposed new items. Télé-Québec's Lao and Thai pages and other regional sources below are **external cuisine evidence**, not bundled-recipe demand.

I checked proposed names and source-attested name candidates against active reference names and aliases, form labels and match terms, legacy candidate names and aliases, and IDs with the repository's `normalize_search_key`. The proposed IDs `ingredient-ref-thai-basil`, `ingredient-ref-vietnamese-coriander`, `ingredient-ref-yardlong-bean`, and `ingredient-ref-toasted-rice-powder`, plus their proposed display/alias keys, have no exact normalized collisions in that scope. This is an exact-key audit, not a fuzzy or picker test.

## Numeric, substitution, and next-action limits

No general quantity, density, unit mapping, conversion, form, yield, or substitution rule is proposed. Recipe measures in Télé-Québec or other sources remain tied to those individual recipes and product wording. The crop, fermentation, and recipe sources do not establish a reusable RecipeBook conversion or safe replacement. No source-specific method has been generalized into a preservation or fermentation instruction.

Next action: root reviews the four identity recommendations and the two variants, checks categories and proposed alias fields, and assigns the held names to later targeted reviews only where Québec naming or product scope can be resolved. Do not mark the full WP06 Southeast Asian coverage complete based solely on this sample; the source classes are represented, but many regional dishes and local product variants remain outside it.
