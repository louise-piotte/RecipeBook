# WP03 Section F: Québec French review for Korean tteokbokki rice-cake sticks

## Packet record

- **Reviewed:** 2026-10-11.
- **Candidate:** `candidate-korean-tteokbokki-rice-cake-sticks`.
- **Coordinator review:** Integrated as the narrow identity-only reference `ingredient-ref-korean-tteokbokki-rice-cake-sticks`; the candidate is marked merged. No recipe links or numeric behavior were added.
- **Recommendation:** Release the French naming hold for a narrowly scoped, identity-only reference. Current Canadian retail listings now connect the French retail wording *bâtonnet(s) de gâteau de riz* with Korean rice-cake sticks, and a Brossard listing uses the same French wording alongside English *rice cake sticks*. Targeted searches found no OQLF or federal terminology entry for this Korean product, so the proposed French display is a product description grounded in retail usage, not an official standardized term.
- **Proposed display:** EN `Korean rice cake sticks for tteokbokki`; FR `bâtonnets de gâteau de riz coréen pour tteokbokki`.
- **Scope:** plain packaged Korean rice-cake sticks intended for tteokbokki. Do not use a generic `rice cake` / `gâteau de riz` identity.

## Why the earlier naming hold can be narrowed

The earlier review found one Montréal French product title, `Gâteau de riz pour tteobokki`, but it did not identify the shape and was marked “Bientôt disponible.” At that point the local French wording did not securely identify the stick product.

The current Canadian evidence closes that gap enough for a descriptive catalog name:

1. A current French-language Walmart Canada product listing is titled `Bâtonnet de gâteau de riz Tokimi 1kg`; its French description calls them Korean rice-cake sticks. The corresponding English listing identifies the same item as sticks, describes a traditional cylindrical shape, and says it is intended for tteokbokki. Walmart notes that supplier and marketplace product information is not separately verified, so this supports retail naming and product class, not a verified ingredient declaration or printed package translation.
2. A current French Uber Eats listing for T&T Supermarket in Brossard is indexed with `bâtonnets de gâteau de riz - rice cake sticks - 900 g`. The opened merchant page confirms the Brossard store, while its fetched body does not expose this specific item row; treat the row as search-indexed local retail evidence rather than a fully inspected package listing.
3. SUE FOODS, a Montréal retailer, still presents `Gâteau de riz pour tteobokki`, 500 g, in French. Its page currently says “Bientôt disponible,” gives no shape or composition, and spells `tteobokki`; preserve that exact title only as evidence.

Together with the Montréal Korean grocer’s distinct `Rice Cake Tubular Type` listing and the Korean Tourism Organization’s description of tteokbokki using thin rice-cake sticks, the retailer language supports the qualified display above. The exact evidence is for retailer product listings; no physical bilingual package panel was verified.

## Decision

**Release the French naming hold only for the narrow plain packaged stick product.** Canadian French retail wording now supplies the stick term, and the associated Canadian English listing ties that product class to Korean cylindrical rice cakes used for tteokbokki; Québec retailer listings corroborate both the French form and local product category. The OQLF source does not establish an official term, so retain a fully qualified descriptive display and narrow aliases. Keep the record identity-only and separate from generic rice cakes, other tteok, oval soup slices, niangao, wheat or filled products, and prepared tteokbokki dishes or kits.
