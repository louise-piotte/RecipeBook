## Recommendation

The integrated generic leafy-green identity is **common lamb's-quarters / chénopode blanc**, limited to the young leaves and shoots of *Chenopodium album* L. The Québec Herbier and TERMIUM Plus support the bilingual species-name pair; Kew accepts the taxon; University of Maine Cooperative Extension documents its use as a cooked leafy green and includes two recipes. This supports a distinct plant-food identity, not a claim that the plant is currently sold in Québec or appears in the bundled recipe collection. [S01–S04]

Keep this identity separate from Good King Henry, garden orach, amaranth greens, spinach, and quinoa seed. Similar names, family membership, and spinach comparisons do not make these identities equivalent. In particular, omit the unqualified term “wild spinach”: the OQLF says it names several *Chenopodium* species, including both *C. album* and Good King Henry. [S05]
