# WP02 A7 R112: Hattai-ko / mugikogashi boundary review

## Disposition

Keep unqualified `Hattai-ko`, `hattaiko`, `mugikogashi`, `mugi-kogashi`, and `kōsen` off the global alias list for roasted barley flour. For a product or recipe that explicitly identifies barley, use the existing `ingredient-ref-roasted-barley-flour`; do not create a Japanese-origin identity or a separate grain form. Regional Hattai-ko naming includes corn and millet products, and no Québec or Canadian French product display was established for the unqualified names.

No catalog, ledger, recipe, or runtime data changed. No density, measure, yield, conversion, or substitution transfers are supported by this review.

## Evidence checked

- The [MEXT Food Composition Database](https://fooddb.mext.go.jp/details/details.pl?ITEM_NO=1_01010_7) classifies item 01010 under barley, names it “CEREALS/Barley/roasted flour,” and assigns *Hordeum vulgare*. MEXT's [official cereal explanation](https://www.mext.go.jp/component/a_menu/science/detail/__icsFiles/afieldfile/2017/12/20/1365346_1-0301r11.pdf) states that mugikogashi, also called kōsen or Hattai-ko, is made by roasting whole barley grain and milling it.
- Maehara's [regional Hattai-ko description](https://gishi.co.jp/home/item.asp?ct=28) says inputs can include barley, naked barley, corn, or millet, depending on region. Its current [120 g product](https://shop.gishi.co.jp/products/hattaiko-120g) is specifically 100% domestic barley.
- Maeda's [current Hattai-ko product](https://an-maeda.co.jp/products/hattaiko) likewise states 100% domestic naked barley and describes roasting and milling.
- OQLF's [`farine d'orge`](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/9489060/farine-dorge) supports the base bilingual barley-flour term. Les Moulins de Soulanges names its own functional product [`Farine d'Orge Torréfié`](https://moulinsdesoulanges.com/language/fr/ingredients-fonctionnels/), but does not connect it to Japanese Hattai-ko.
- The complete source limits and picker/corpus audit are recorded in the [R112 proposal](../../proposals/wp02-a7-hattai-ko-mugikogashi-identity-boundary-review-r112-20261014.md).
