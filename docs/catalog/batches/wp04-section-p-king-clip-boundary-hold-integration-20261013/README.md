# WP04 Section P — King Clip taxon boundary hold (R90)

Reviewed 2026-10-13. This closes the current product-to-species evidence screen; the broader Section P fish checklist remains open.

## Decision

Keep Seacore's current Canadian **King Clip** fillet product outside active references and the research ledger. Its [current seller page](https://www.seacoreseafood.com/product/King_Clip) names fresh, wild fillets from Costa Rica but does not identify a scientific species. OQLF applies `kingklip` to *Genypterus blacodes* (`abadèche rose`) and *G. capensis* (`abadèche du Cap`); [TERMIUM Plus](https://www.btb.termiumplus.gc.ca/tpv2alpha/alpha-eng.html?i=&index=frb&lang=eng&srchtxt=ABADECHE+CAP) explicitly notes that `kingklip` is also used for *G. blacodes*. The shared market name does not establish which taxon the Seacore item represents.

Do not create a taxon-free `King Clip` reference or attach the SKU to either species. Keep it separate from `cusk / brosme`, both *Genypterus* species, and generic fish/cusk-eel names until Seacore or the package identifies the taxon. The dated 2016 seller product list is not tied to the current listing and supplies no reusable measurement, yield, or conversion.

## Picker and recipe boundary

The current catalog has no exact owner for `King Clip`, `king clip`, `kingklip`, `kingclip`, or `king klip`. Do not add these shared market terms as aliases to a single species. A hypothetical `pink cusk-eel / abadèche rose` identity would be species-qualified, but the Seacore product is not linked to it. The 114-recipe / 1,123-row bundle has no candidate-term hits, so no recipe link or relink is proposed.

No active form, measure, unit mapping, conversion, package yield, edible/cooking yield, or substitution is supported. The product boundary is documentation-only: no catalog, ledger, recipe, or application data changed.

## Evidence and verification

- [OQLF, *abadèche rose*](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/1199126/abadeche-rose) identifies *G. blacodes* and includes `kingklip` among its English terms.
- [OQLF, *abadèche du Cap*](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/1199127/abadeche-du-cap) identifies *G. capensis* and includes `kingklip` / `South African kingklip`.
- [TERMIUM Plus](https://www.btb.termiumplus.gc.ca/tpv2alpha/alpha-eng.html?i=&index=frb&lang=eng&srchtxt=ABADECHE+CAP) records the cross-species common-name use.
- CFIA's [Fish List results](https://active.inspection.gc.ca/scripts/fssa/fispoi/fplist/fpresults.asp) for TSN 165000 are reused from the existing cusk packet, not duplicated into a new ledger record.
