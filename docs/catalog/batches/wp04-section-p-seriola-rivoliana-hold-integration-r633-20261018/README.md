# WP04 Section P R633 — Almaco jack naming hold

**Status:** Reviewed and held on 2026-10-18. No active catalog or ledger JSON changed.

## Decision

Keep *Seriola rivoliana* outside active references, forms, legacy identities, and the research ledger until a Québec-facing source supports a distinct French product name paired with the species. CFIA’s Fish List gives *S. rivoliana* both `Almaco Jack / Sériole` and `Jack / Sériole Limon`; it also assigns `Sériole` to other *Seriola* taxa. OQLF scopes the preferred Québec fish term `sériole` to *S. dumerili*. Current Canadian seafood pages establish market use of `Kona Kampachi`, `Almaco Jack`, and `Kanpachi`, but do not establish a bilingual Québec display for *S. rivoliana*.

The hold preserves the existing Greater amberjack and Japanese amberjack references, R334’s unallocated `kanpachi` wording, and the R85 Hiramasa hold. No recipe demand, form, measure, conversion, yield, or substitution was found.

## Evidence reviewed

- [CFIA Fish List](https://active.inspection.gc.ca/scripts/fssa/fispoi/fplist/fpresults.asp?cmbIn=e&q=Seriola%20rivoliana) and [CFIA Fish List guidance](https://inspection.canada.ca/en/food-labels/labelling/industry/fish/list): the first lists the taxon and Canadian label names; the second describes the list as acceptable fish-labelling terminology with scientific names and TSNs.
- [OQLF GDT entry for `sériole`](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/1198975/seriole): maps the preferred Québec fish term to *Seriola dumerili*.
- [Seafood Depot’s Kona Kampachi page](https://www.seafooddepot.ca/product-konakampachi): names *S. rivoliana*, `Almaco Jack`, and `Kahala` at its Vaughan, Ontario business.
- [True World Foods Canada’s fresh-seafood page](https://trueworldfoods.ca/fresh-seafood-7/): lists Hawaiian Kanpachi and Kanpachi fillet and has Montréal and Québec City locations, without a binomial or French product name.
- [U.S. FDA Seafood List](https://www.hfpappexternal.fda.gov/scripts/fdcc/index.cfm?id=Seriola_rivoliana&set=SeafoodList): scopes `Amberjack or Kanpachi` to ocean-farmed *S. rivoliana* for U.S. labeling only; this does not resolve Canadian naming.

The active picker projection remains unchanged: `sériole` returns the two existing visibly qualified references; `Almaco Jack`, `Sériole Limon`, `Kanpachi`, `Kampachi`, and the binomial return no new result. The 114-recipe scan found no matching candidate terms.

## Validation

- Proposal source and URL ownership, current reference and ledger ownership, picker projection, and all 114 bundled recipes were checked.
- No database catalog data changed. This documentation-only hold required no software tests under `AGENTS.md`.

Section P remains open. Reconsider only after a current Québec-facing product or terminology source pairs the species with a distinct French name.
