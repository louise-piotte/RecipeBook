# WP02 Section 04 B3 — pregelatinized sweet-potato-starch hold (R375)

## Disposition

Recorded a bounded hold on a separate `pregelatinized sweet potato starch` identity. Primary research demonstrates sweet-potato starch processed by gelatinization and spray drying and used in a noodle formulation. A current international supplier page advertises a product under the exact English name, but also lists foundry, feed, building-material, handicraft, and papermaking applications without a verified human-food grade specification. The evidence does not establish a current Canadian human-food product.

OQLF supports the Québec base term `fécule de patate douce` for extracted sweet-potato starch and the generic term `amidon prégélatinisé`; it does not attest the complete sweet-potato-specific French compound. Keep a possible `fécule de patate douce prégélatinisée` wording provisional and outside runtime search until a local product and Québec naming evidence qualify it.

The hold does not change `ingredient-ref-sweet-potato-starch`, its `amidon de patate douce` alias, the separate sweet-potato-starch-noodle identity, or whole-root flour. Add no active reference, alias, form, ledger candidate, measure, density, mapping, recipe link, conversion, yield, or substitution. The bundled recipe corpus has no matching demand.

## Evidence and reopen threshold

The proposal records two primary research papers, the supplier listing, the USDA’s explicitly unofficial translation of China’s edible-starch standard, and reused OQLF entries. Taken together, the evidence supports the existence of a food-research processing class but does not demonstrate a Canadian human-food product with product-matched Québec French wording. Reopen when a current Canadian or Québec source identifies the product as a human-food ingredient and supports a matching French name or authoritative Québec terminology.
