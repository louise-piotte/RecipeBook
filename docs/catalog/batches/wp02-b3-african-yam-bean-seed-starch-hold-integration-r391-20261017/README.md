# WP02 Section 04 B3 African yam-bean seed-starch hold — R391

Reviewed 2026-10-17. This integration records a documentation-only hold from the [R391 proposal](../../proposals/wp02-section-04-b3-african-yam-bean-seed-starch-boundary-review-r391-20261017.md).

Keep starch isolated from *Sphenostylis stenocarpa* seeds outside active references and the research ledger. Primary studies establish a technically distinct seed-starch fraction, but the reviewed current Canadian sources do not establish a standalone human-food product or product-matched Québec French name. This is a bounded evidence hold, not a claim that no supplier can offer it.

Keep the candidate distinct from African yam-bean seed flour (R261), tuber products, true-yam flour, and jicama starch (R119). OQLF's general `amidon` guidance does not provide a species-specific food name; do not build a French display or alias from unsupported plant-name compounds. Add no reference, candidate record, alias, form, measure, density, mapping, recipe link, conversion, yield, or substitution.

The R391 proposal records the primary studies, Canadian germplasm and product-search evidence, exact ownership and picker review, and the 114-recipe scan. No catalog or ledger data changed, so this documentation-only decision needed no software tests. Section 04 B3 remains open.
