# WP02 Section E R243 edible luster and glitter boundary hold (2026-10-16)

## Decision

Keep generic edible luster dust and edible glitter unallocated. Current Canadian decorating listings include non-toxic products that are explicitly not food as well as exact products labeled edible. Names such as luster, pearl, shimmer, and glitter do not establish interchangeable identity, composition, or food-use status. The bundle has no matching recipe demand, and a general Québec French term was not established. Do not add aliases or related numeric, form, conversion, yield, recipe-link, or substitution behavior.
