# WP03 Section F Tuo Zafi product boundary hold

## Decision

Keep the marketed `Tuo Zafi Corn & Rice Flour` product unallocated until a reliable ingredient/process
declaration and Québec-facing French name are available. Margaret African Market lists a current
Home Fresh product under that English title and a 1 kg package, though it was sold out when checked.
Feliza African Store uses a combined `Tuo Zafi Corn & Rice Flour | Banku Mix` title while also listing
Home Fresh Banku Mix separately. The retailer wording does not establish that the products are
equivalent or that the corn-and-rice product has one verified formula.

OQLF supports `farine de maïs` and `farine de riz` for the constituent flours only. Ghanaian research
describes Tuo Zaafi as prepared with different cereal bases; that dish name does not establish a
universal dry blend. Do not alias the product to the cooked dish, generic corn flour, rice flour, or
Banku mix. The recipe bundle has no matching demand. No reference, alias, form, measure, recipe link,
conversion, yield, substitution, or ledger candidate was added.

## Sources

- [Margaret African Market, Tuo Zafi Corn & Rice Flour – 1 kg](https://margaretafrica.com/products/tuo-zafi-corn-rice-flour-1kg): current Canadian product title and package size; sold out when checked and no ingredient panel is shown.
- [Feliza African Store, Flour](https://www.feliza.ca/flour): combined Tuo Zafi / Banku listing title and separate Home Fresh Banku Mix listing; does not establish synonymy.
- [OQLF, *farine de maïs*](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/8432786/farine-de-mais) and [*farine de riz*](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/8432782/farine-de-riz): constituent flour terms, not a composite-product name.
- [Annor et al., prepared Ghanaian foods](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40064-016-2202-9): describes millet as the main Tuo Zaafi base, with corn flour in some cases.
- [Bawa et al., Tuo Zaafi preparation study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2949824426002405): discusses maize, millet, and sorghum bases and a study-specific maize–okara–cassava composite flour.

The exact-ownership, picker, recipe-demand, and numeric review is in the [R44 proposal](../../proposals/wp03-section-f-tuo-zafi-corn-rice-flour-quebec-name-hold-20261013-r44.md).
