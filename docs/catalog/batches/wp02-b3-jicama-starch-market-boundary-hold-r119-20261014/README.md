# WP02 Section 04 B3 jicama-starch market-boundary hold

## Decision

Keep starch experimentally isolated from *Pachyrhizus erosus* outside active references, forms,
and the research ledger. The reviewed 2007, 2021, and 2023 studies characterize laboratory
extracts; they do not establish a current Canadian or Québec edible starch product or its market
name. The bounded Canadian/Québec search found fresh jicama root listings, not an isolated-starch
product. This is a scoped hold, not proof that no product exists.

Keep the existing whole-root `ingredient-ref-jicama`, its display, and aliases unchanged. Do not
activate `jicama starch` or provisional French constructions. Study-specific extraction yields and
bulk densities remain in the proposal and do not become a recipe measure, product density, or
substitution rule. No bundled recipe demand was found.
