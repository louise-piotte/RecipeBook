# WP02 B4 R278 — ripe banana flour product-boundary hold

## Decision

Held ripe banana flour outside active references, aliases, forms, and the research ledger. The research literature establishes ripe-fruit flour prepared for experiments, but the reviewed Canada-locale product is titled and described as ripe banana powder. Its “Ripe Banana Flour” common-name field does not clearly name a flour product. Keep the ripe terms unallocated and retain the existing green-banana-flour identity as the current owner of the broad `banana flour` picker query.

Reopen this question only when a current food-grade manufacturer, package, or Canadian/Québec retail listing clearly identifies ripe banana flour as the product, its source and composition are clear, and product-matched Québec French wording is available. Keep ripe plantain flour as a separate question.

## Scope and evidence

This is a documentation-only decision for the open [WP02 Section 04 B4 plan item](../../../RecipeBook-global-ingredient-catalog-plan.txt#L2955). It does not reopen green banana flour, green plantain flour, instant or pregelatinized products, isolated starch, or the existing mashed-ripe-banana form.

The [proposal](../../proposals/wp02-b4-ripe-banana-flour-product-boundary-review-r278-20261016.md) contains the ownership, picker, recipe-demand, measurement, conversion, and substitution audits, with proposal-local sources. The study literature confirms a research-prepared ripe-banana flour material, while FAO separately describes powder from fully ripe pulp and flour from green fruit. The OQLF's 1982 `farine de banane` entry is historical terminology and does not establish a current retail product or modern flour/powder equivalence. The current ZeeHERBS listing is clearly titled `Ripe Banana Powder`; the alternate common-name field does not establish a flour SKU.
