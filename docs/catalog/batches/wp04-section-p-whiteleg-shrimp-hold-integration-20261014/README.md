# WP04 Section P R94: Whiteleg Shrimp Picker Boundary Hold

Review date: 2026-10-14
Decision: documentation-only picker and identity hold.

## Decision

Keep *Litopenaeus vannamei* outside the active reference catalog and research ledger for now.
TERMIUM and Québec-facing products support the species and bilingual market wording, but CFIA
assigns `Whiteleg Shrimp` and `Crevette Pattes Blanches` to both *L. vannamei* and *L. setiferus*.
With only one row present, the phrase-first picker would make those shared names appear
species-exclusive. Do not shorten the Québec display to `crevette blanche`; OQLF assigns that name
to a different shrimp. Preserve `Vannamei` product titles as product-specific source evidence only.

The proposal's scan found no matching shrimp or species demand in the bundled recipes. Add no
reference, alias, form, measure, recipe link, conversion, yield, substitution, or ledger candidate.
Retail package sizes and product states remain SKU-specific.
