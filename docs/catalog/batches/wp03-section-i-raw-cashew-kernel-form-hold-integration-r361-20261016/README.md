# WP03 Section I R361 — raw cashew-kernel form hold

Recorded a documentation-only hold on `raw cashews / noix de cajou crues` and `unroasted cashews / noix de cajou non grillées`. UNECE DDP-17 (2023) describes the commercial cashew-kernel scope as kernels obtained by heating, shelling, and peeling, and excludes kernels subsequently roasted or otherwise processed. Québec retailer listings use raw-named labels in food-use contexts but do not document each product's process history or define the label. The proposed distinction is therefore not strong enough to create an active form.

Kept `ingredient-ref-cashews`, its forms, aliases, the research ledger, and recipe links unchanged. The 114-recipe / 1,123-row bundle has no cashew demand. No safety claim or home-shelling guidance is inferred from shell-hazard sources. The broader Section I processing-sensitive kernel checklist remains open.
