# WP02 A7 teff meal boundary hold — 2026-10-13

## Decision

Kept `semoule de teff` and the possible English terms `teff meal` / `teff semolina` unresolved and
unallocated. A Québec food-writing article mentions teff semolina alongside flour, but gives no
standalone product label, reproducible milling boundary, or process that distinguishes it from the
named flour product. OQLF's `semoule` entry covers wheat milling; it does not establish a teff product
class or validate `semoule de teff` as a product name.

The reviewed Québec and Canadian product pages identify teff flour. Keep this candidate distinct from
whole teff, `ingredient-ref-teff-flour`, R37's malted-teff-flour candidate, and prepared injera. The
114-recipe bundle has no matching teff or semolina demand. Add no active reference, alias, form,
measurement, recipe link, conversion, yield, or substitution. No ledger candidate was added.

## Evidence and data changes

- [Le Québec cuisine](https://quebecuisine.ca/book/laccompagnement-de-crepes-a-la-farine-ou-semoule-de-teff/)
  says the small teff seeds are dried to make semolina or flour. This is direct Québec French usage,
  but the article does not identify a producer, package, particle size, or milling process.
- The [OQLF GDT entry for `semoule`](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/8895941/semoule)
  defines the term in soft- and durum-wheat milling. It does not define teff semolina.
- The [OQLF entry for `tef`](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/8480763/tef)
  names the cereal and lists English `teff`; it does not name a milled product.
- [La Chèvre et le Chou](https://lachevreetlechou.ca/produits/farine-de-teff-bio) names a Québec
  product `Farine de teff bio` and lists teff as its ingredient.
- [VANA Africa](https://vanaafrica.ca/) shows its Calgary-milled own-label products as red and white
  teff flour, not meal or semolina.
- [Bob's Red Mill Teff Flour](https://www.bobsredmill.com/product/teff-flour) lists a 1/4 cup (35 g)
  serving and suggests replacing about one quarter of white flour with its whole-grain teff flour.
  These statements apply to that flour product and do not establish a meal measure or substitution.

file changed. No tests were needed for this documentation-only hold. See the
