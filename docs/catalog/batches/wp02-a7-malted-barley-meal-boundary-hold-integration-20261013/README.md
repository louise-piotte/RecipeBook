# WP02 A7 malted-barley-meal boundary hold

## Decision

Keep `malted barley meal` outside the food ingredient catalog. Wurzelwerk's exact-name product is a
gardening soil amendment; Post's hot cereal label lists `Malted Barley` separately from `Wheat Farina`
and does not identify a standalone barley-meal ingredient. OQLF defines malt as treated cereal, not a
ground food product. The reviewed sources do not establish a food-grade product boundary or a Québec
French consumer name.

The recipe bundle has no matching ingredient rows. Keep this phrase distinct from malted barley
flour, generic malt flour, and plain barley meal. Do not add an active identity, alias, form, measure,
density, mapping, recipe link, conversion, yield, substitution, legacy identity, or ledger candidate.
Reopen if a food manufacturer or technically accountable food supplier documents a standalone edible
product, its milling boundary, and a source-backed Québec French display.

## Sources

- [Wurzelwerk, Malted Barley Meal](https://wurzelwerk.bio/products/wurzelwerk-malted-barley-meal): the exact-name listing describes use for Living Soil, gardens, plants, and compost substrates; it gives gardening dosages and was marked sold out when reviewed.
- [Post Consumer Brands, Malt-O-Meal Original Hot Wheat Cereal](https://www.postconsumerbrands.com/brands/malt-o-meal-hot/products/malt-o-meal-original-hot-wheat-cereal/): the food label names `Wheat Farina, Malted Barley`; its 3 Tbsp (35 g) amount is the complete cereal serving, not a malted-barley-meal measure.
- [OQLF, *malt* (GDT)](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/26545137/malt): supports `malt` for cereal that has been soaked, germinated, and dried, but does not name a consumer food meal.
- Reused OQLF, [*farine d'orge maltée*](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/2497648/farine-dorge-maltee), and CFIA, [Canadian Food Compositional Standards](https://inspection.canada.ca/en/about-cfia/acts-and-regulations/list-acts-and-regulations/documents-incorporated-reference/canadian-food-compositional-standards-0), support the separate malted-barley-flour and generic meal boundaries; neither defines malted barley meal.

The source, ownership, picker, corpus, and numeric-boundary review is recorded in the [R45 proposal](../../proposals/wp02-a7-malted-barley-meal-food-use-boundary-review-20261013-round45.md).
