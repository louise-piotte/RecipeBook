# WP02 — pumpkin-seed-flour scope hold (2026-10-12)

## Decision

Keep the broad `pumpkin seed flour` identity and its aliases unallocated. Canadian-facing sources
apply that name to oil-press cake and to high-oil seed milled without oil removal. Those products do
not yet share a demonstrated culinary scope, and no process-matched Québec French product name was
found. Do not attach flour terms to the existing whole-seed, seed-butter, seed-oil, pumpkin-fruit, or
pumpkin-spice references.

The manifest-listed bundle has no pumpkin-seed-flour demand: 0 hits across 114 recipes and 1,123
ingredient rows. Package masses and a source-specific bread study do not establish a reusable
volume conversion, density, yield, or substitution. No active reference, alias, form, measurement,
recipe link, conversion, yield, substitution, or ledger record was added.

## Evidence reviewed

- [OQLF's `graine de citrouille` entry](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/18053242/graine-de-citrouille)
  supports the Québec French name for the seed; it does not define a flour product.
- [Second Harvest Canada's French article](https://www.secondharvest.ca/fr-ca/post/flour-power)
  describes milling the press cake left after oil extraction.
- [Naturitas Canada's Bio Planète listing](https://www.naturitas.ca/catalog/product/view/id/61689/)
  gives a Canadian-facing product name and press-cake process, but the listing is unavailable and
  its 250 g package is not a household measure.
- [Beyond the Equator's manufacturer page](https://beyondtheequator.com/collections/flours/products/pumpkin-seed-flour)
  calls its product pumpkin seed flour and describes milling pumpkin seeds.
- [Onset Worldwide's manufacturer page](https://www.onsetworldwide.com/nitro-milled-pumpkin-flour.html)
  states that its milled product retains the fats naturally present in the seeds.
- The proposal records additional French product and controlled bread-study sources, with their
  product-specific limits: [full WP02 review](../../proposals/wp02-next-open-section-b-candidate-review-20261012.md).

The reviewed picker terms currently return no flour result. Broader seed, oil, butter, and fruit
results remain owned by their existing identities. No candidate source IDs were added to the shared
ledger.

## Reconciliation

This hold completes one candidate review beneath the open WP02 Section B alternative-flour item; it
does not close Section B. The proposal contains the full ownership, picker, recipe-corpus, source,
numeric-limit, and pre-integration audit. Canonical JSON, recipe, ledger, generated code, and app
behavior were unchanged. No app tests or emulator checks were run; the reconciliation changed
documentation only.
