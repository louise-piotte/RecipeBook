# WP04 Section P gravlax-style semi-smoked salmon boundary hold — 2026-10-13

## Decision

Kept Fumoir Grizzly's `gravlax-style semi-smoked Atlantic salmon` SKU and bare `gravlax` outside
active references and forms. OQLF defines `gravlax` as raw salmon slices macerated with seasonings,
and Québec food-safety guidance describes gravlax as raw marinated or dry-brined fish. MAPAQ and
Grizzly identify a specific semi-smoked processed item. The maker's `style Gravlax` wording does not
establish that its SKU is the same product state as raw-cured gravlax.

The 100 g package and nutrition facts are specific to the MAPAQ product record. Manufacturer recipe
quantities are source-specific and are not reusable measures or portions. The recipe bundle has no
matching demand; the generic salmon-fillets reference remains unchanged. Add no active identity,
alias, form, measurement, recipe link, conversion, yield, substitution, or ledger candidate.

## Evidence and data changes

- The [OQLF GDT entry for `gravlax`](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/26502314/gravlax)
  defines the dish as raw salmon slices macerated with salt, sugar, white pepper, and dill; it gives
  `gravlax` as the preferred French term and English equivalent.
- [Québec food-safety guidance](https://www.quebec.ca/sante/alimentation/salubrite-aliments-prevention-risques/etablissements-alimentaires/precautions-aliments-dangereux/poisson/gravlax)
  describes gravlax generally as raw fish marinated or dry-brined.
- The [MAPAQ Québec Food Directory](https://www.repertoirealimentsquebecois.gouv.qc.ca/Produit/Index/8206019)
  lists `Gravlax style half-smoked sliced atlantic salmon`, prepared/processed fish, GTIN
  `775200007202`, a 100 g package, and ingredients including Atlantic salmon, sugar, salt, herbs,
  bacterial culture, and natural wood smoke.
- [Fumoir Grizzly's French product page](https://grizzly.qc.ca/produits/saumon-atlantique-mi-fume-style-gravlax-refresh/)
  names `saumon atlantique mi-fumé style Gravlax`, lists 100 g, and gives product-specific ingredients
  and handling directions. Its [English product page](https://grizzly.qc.ca/en/products/gravlax-style-semi-smoked-atlantic-salmon-refresh/)
  uses the corresponding `Gravlax style semi-smoked Atlantic salmon` wording.
- Grizzly's [salad recipe](https://grizzly.qc.ca/recettes/salade-gravlax-melon-et-fraise/) uses one
  package for four portions. The separate [gravlax tart recipe](https://grizzly.qc.ca/recettes/tarte-fine-gravlax-saumon-yogourt-aneth/)
  lists 150 g for four to six portions but does not identify the MAPAQ SKU. These amounts do not
  support a general portion or conversion.
