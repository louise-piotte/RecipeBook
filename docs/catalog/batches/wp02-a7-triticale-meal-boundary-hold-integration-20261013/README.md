# WP02 A7 Triticale Meal Boundary Hold

Integrated 2026-10-13 as a focused update to the unresolved triticale-meal boundary under the open [A7 checklist](../../../RecipeBook-global-ingredient-catalog-plan.txt#L1444). The earlier residual-meal review already recorded the hold; this review adds current supplier evidence and checks ownership and picker behavior.

## Decision

Keep `triticale meal` unresolved and unallocated. A Saskatchewan supplier lists Meal and Flour separately among triticale applications, and a Canadian cracker label uses `triticale meal`. These support a product-form signal, but neither source establishes a standalone meal specification, grind, milling fraction, or Québec French display. Keep the term separate from active triticale grain, flour, and flakes. Add no reference, alias, ledger candidate, form, measure, recipe link, conversion, yield, or substitution.

## Evidence and checks

- [InfraReady's triticale page](https://www.infrareadyproducts.com/products/triticale) lists Meal and Flour as separate applications, alongside whole, bumped, flaked, and cracked forms. It is an applications list, not a standalone SKU or specification.
- [Metro's Breton cracker listing](https://www.metro.ca/en/online-grocery/aisles/snacks/salty-snacks/crackers/original-multigrain-and-garden-vegetable-flavoured-crackers-variety-pack/p/055653672906) uses the exact ingredient phrase `triticale meal`; Metro marked the product out of stock and notes that manufacturers may change information. The composite label does not define the meal or its grind.
- [OQLF's `farine de triticale` entry](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/2497480/farine-de-triticale) supports the flour display only. Its technical [`gruau` entry](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/8430011/gruau) does not define triticale meal or validate a French product name.
- The proposal audit counted 1,367 active references, 100 forms, 64 legacy candidates, 1,699 research-ledger records, 114 manifest-listed recipes, and 1,123 ingredient rows. The three active triticale owners remain grain, flour, and flakes; no exact meal owner or bundled triticale demand was found.
- The checklist, roadmap, and progress log now record this focused recheck. No catalog or ledger data changed.

The full source register, exact ownership and picker projection, recipe scan, numeric limits, and next-action criteria are in the [R41 proposal](../../proposals/wp02-a7-triticale-meal-boundary-review-20261013-round41.md). No tests were run because this integration changes documentation only.
