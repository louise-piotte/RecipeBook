# WP04 Section P hwangtae boundary hold

## Decision

Keep hwangtae / `황태` outside active references and forms until a bundled recipe selects the needed
product presentation and a package label resolves fish identity. VisitKorea distinguishes winter-dried
hwangtae from other pollack states. Current Canadian listings identify different presentations, and
one listing has conflicting pollock/cod wording. OQLF's `pollack` and `goberge` terminology is scoped
to Atlantic pollock, so it is not a safe synonym for this unverified product.

The bundle has no matching recipe demand. A hypothetical row would make `pollack` and the explanatory
French phrase `poisson séché` single picker results; neither is a sufficiently narrow verified label
for hwangtae. No identity, alias, form, measure, recipe link, conversion, yield, substitution, or
research-ledger candidate was added. Preserve hwangtae as a distinct product family from generic dried
fish, other Korean pollack states, cod, Alaska pollock, Atlantic pollock, and prepared seafood products.

## Sources

- [VisitKorea, grilled hwangtaegui](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=178804): identifies hwangtae as dried pollack.
- [VisitKorea, meoktaegui and pollack naming](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/thingsToDo/foodTrip/special_view.do?menuSn=536&vcontsId=180105): distinguishes raw, dried, frozen, winter-dried, young, half-dried, and darkened pollack states.
- [H Mart Mall Toronto](https://hpack.ca/products/761898689080): current 70 g `Dried Pollack` listing with Korean product text `황태포`; sold out when accessed.
- [The K Mart](https://www.thekmart.ca/en/products/%ED%99%A9%ED%83%9C%EC%B1%84-1lb454g-42-99): current 454 g `Choripdongi Hwangtaechae` listing; page text says both Russian pollock and 100% cod, so it cannot establish species.
- [OQLF, *goberge*](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/1198637/goberge): identifies *Pollachius virens* and lists English `pollack` among its terms.
- [CFIA fish-labelling requirements](https://inspection.canada.ca/en/food-labels/labelling/industry/fish?wbdisable=true): explains common-name and product-state labelling requirements; it does not resolve either retailer listing.
