# WP05 South/Central Asia–MENA identity checkpoint: labneh and spices

- Integrated: 2026-10-08; coordinator-owned partial acceptance of four candidates from the MENA follow-up.
- Scope: labneh, za'atar, amchur powder, and sumac. Mixed Pickles and the wider Section Y list remain open.
- `labneh / labneh` uses the OQLF-supported French alias `lebneh` and is categorized as `DAIRY_AND_ALTERNATIVE`; OQLF places it under yogurt and Canadian dairy sources corroborate dairy placement. Do not assert one product formulation, texture, or moisture level. Keep distinct from plain yogurt and cream cheese.
- `za'atar / zaatar` promotes the existing stable legacy ID. OQLF supports `zaatar`/`zahtar` in French and `za'atar`/`zaatar`/`zahtar` in English. The canonical EN/FR labels cover `za'atar` and `zaatar`; `zahtar` is an alias in each language. Keep it as a composition-variable BAKING_AND_SPICE blend, not an alias of sumac, sesame, hyssop, thyme, or oregano.
- `sumac / sumac` promotes the existing legacy ID as a standalone culinary spice. OQLF defines the edible identity as aromatic purple powder from Mediterranean-shrub berries and notes its use in Middle Eastern cooking. Keep distinct from za'atar, ornamental/wild sumac, extracts, and seasoning blends.
- `amchur powder / amchur (mangue) en poudre` is a BAKING_AND_SPICE identity made from dried unripe green mango. Activate `amchoor` and `aamchur` only. Broader `mango powder`, `dry/dried mango powder`, and `sour mango powder` phrases remain inactive to prevent broad mango matches. A seller's qualitative tamarind comparison does not provide a substitution rule.
- All four identities have null density and empty unit mappings. No forms, package yields, measurement conversions, or substitutions were added.
- Recipe-line text audit across all 114 bundled recipes found no labneh, za'atar, sumac, or amchur matches.
