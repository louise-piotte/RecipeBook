# WP02 A7 Malted Amaranth Flour Boundary Hold

Integrated 2026-10-13 as a documentation-only hold under the still-open [A7 checklist](../../../RecipeBook-global-ingredient-catalog-plan.txt#L1444).

## Decision

Keep malted amaranth flour unresolved and unallocated. A peer-reviewed bread study prepares malted amaranth experimentally, but the reviewed Canadian and Québec sources do not establish a standalone retail product or a Québec French display. Keep the experimental term separate from active plain and sprouted amaranth flours. Add no reference, alias, form, measure, recipe link, conversion, yield, or substitution.

The study used a specific 70:30 wheat-to-malted-amaranth composite flour and a study-specific malting process. Everspring Farms currently lists sprouted amaranth flour as a distinct product. Neither source establishes equivalence between malted and sprouted products. OQLF supports the base term `amarante`, not a malted-flour product name.

## Evidence and checks

- Reviewed the primary 2022 bread study, [Everspring Farms' sprouted-amaranth-flour listing](https://www.everspringfarms.ca/product/sprouted-amaranth-flour/), [OQLF's *amarante* entry](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/26503667/amarante), and Québec plain-amaranth-flour listings. The study's formulation and supplier listings are product- or study-specific; they do not establish a reusable amount or substitution.
- The proposal audit counted 1,367 active references, 100 forms, 64 legacy candidates, 1,699 research-ledger records, 114 manifest-listed recipes, and 1,123 ingredient rows. No exact malted-amaranth owner or bundled recipe demand was found.
- Static picker projection: a hypothetical third flour choice would broaden `amaranth flour`, `farine d'amarante`, and `amaranth`; no picker change was made.
- The checklist, roadmap, and progress log now record this hold. No catalog, ledger, recipe, or generated data changed.

The source register, exact ownership review, search projection, and recipe-corpus audit are in the [R40 proposal](../../proposals/wp02-a7-malted-amaranth-flour-boundary-review-20261013-round40.md). No tests were run because this integration changes documentation only.
