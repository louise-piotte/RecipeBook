# WP02 B2 pecan-flour boundary hold (2026-10-12)

## Decision

Keep unqualified `pecan flour` and `pecan meal` unassigned. Current maker and research sources use
overlapping wording for finely ground whole pecans and for oil-press residue; the products differ in
composition and culinary behavior. The reviewed Canadian import category groups flour, meal, and
powder without resolving those process scopes. OQLF supports `pacane` / `noix de pacane` for the
whole nut, but does not establish a Québec French name for either flour product. The observed
`farine de noix de pécan` wording is only an ingredient in a Québec cracker listing.

## Boundaries and audit

- Do not attach flour or meal terms to the existing whole-pecan or pecan-butter identities.
- Keep product-specific press-cake evidence and tapped bulk-density measurements out of generic
  measures, density, mappings, and substitution rules.
- The manifest-listed corpus has no pecan-flour/meal phrase matches in 114 recipes and 1,123
  ingredient rows. Its six pecan rows are for whole or chopped pecans.
- No active reference, alias, form, ledger record, measurement, recipe link, conversion, yield, or
  substitution was added.

search, and measurement/substitution review: [WP02 B2 pecan-flour review](../../proposals/wp02-b2b3-next-gap-review-20261012-round3.md).
