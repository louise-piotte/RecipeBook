# WP02 / WP03 / WP04 R666–R669 catalog coverage integration

## Decisions

- **R666 — cornmeal:** Added six separate source-specific `portion_weight` observations to the existing cornmeal ledger record: CNF Food Code 4418’s 100 mL, 250 mL, and serving entries; King Arthur’s whole-cornmeal and yellow Quaker chart rows; and its Spoonbread formula amount. The earlier Bob’s Red Mill product-label serving remains separate. Recorded King Arthur durum semolina only as an alternative for dusting a bread sheet. No general measure, density, mapping, conversion, recipe link, yield, or active substitution rule was added.
- **R667 — fresh tomatoes:** Added a fresh-market form under the existing generic tomato reference and linked only recipe 096#1 (`4 large tomatoes, diced`). Its publisher prefers fresh tomatoes and names canned diced tomatoes as a fallback. The recipe text and amount are unchanged; raw-tomato and recipe comparison amounts remain source-specific.
- **R668 — Atlantic mackerel:** Held the proposed whole-thawed form outside active data. Metro’s English and Québec routes identify the same *Scomber scombrus* item and currently show it out of stock. DFO’s 2026 decision keeps the commercial fishery closed but does not establish that imported products are unavailable. Broad whole-mackerel phrases would narrow picker results to Atlantic mackerel, and the recipe bundle has no matching demand. This adds no catalog or ledger data.
- **R669 — chipotle:** Added whole smoked-and-dried chipotle as a form under the existing jalapeño reference. The separate `chipotle in adobo` condiment remains intact. Bare `chipotle` keeps both results visible; explicit `chipotle chili` and `chipotle chilli` phrases select the dried form. No amount, density, recipe link, or substitution was added.

The Section A7, Section L, and Section P parents remain open. R666’s finding is contextual to bread-sheet dusting, not a reusable cornmeal replacement rule. R667 does not assign a generic tomato amount to the fresh form. R669 leaves substitution research open.

## Integration and static checks

- Updated `ingredient-forms.v1.json` with the fresh-tomato and whole-smoked-dried-jalapeño forms. Updated only recipe 096#1 with the fresh-tomato form link. Added the R666 observations/finding and form-specific R667/R669 ledger records.
- Counts after integration: **1,837 references**, **166 forms**, **114 recipes / 1,123 ingredient lines**, **2,212 ledger records**, **10,355 source entries**, **8,745 distinct source IDs**, and **7,395 distinct source URLs**. The two new forms account for the increase from 164 to 166 forms; no reference was added.
- Static checks passed: bundled package validation and normalized-file check; ingredient-reference normalization check; generated catalog-index freshness check; ledger schema/custom validation (2,212 records, zero errors); form/source/observation ownership; recipe-line preservation and form-link scope; picker projections; UTF-8/no-BOM and escaped Unicode in bundled JSON; and local-link/whitespace checks.
- No software tests, build, emulator run, or deployment were performed. The data integration only adds database JSON data, so testing was not needed under the updated `AGENTS.md` rule. R668 is documentation-only.
