# R461 — Lemon curd identity integration

Integrated one source-backed identity-only reference for English **lemon curd** and Québec French **crème au citron**, with the exact Canadian retail French wording **crème de citron** as its only alias.

The reference is separate from lemon, lemon juice, lemon zest, jam, fruit purée, caramel sauce, and dulce de leche. The OQLF-listed English equivalents lemon cheese, lemon cheese curd, and lemon butter remain out of active picker aliases because they broaden unrelated cheese and butter searches. The corpus audit found no matching line among 114 recipes and 1,123 ingredient rows.

The Canadian PC product’s 250 ml package and 34 ml serving remain source-specific. No density, mapping, form, measurement observation, recipe link, conversion, yield, or substitution was added. Measurement research found no reusable value; substitution research remains not yet researched.

Package normalization, generated-index freshness, ledger-schema validation, source ownership, full-corpus matching, UTF-8/no-BOM, and scoped whitespace checks passed. No app or Android tests were run for this database-JSON addition under `AGENTS.md`.

The accepted source-backed decision and picker projection are documented in the [R461 proposal](../../proposals/wp02-section-d-lemon-curd-identity-review-r461-20261017.md). The broader Section D fruit-product checklist remains open.
