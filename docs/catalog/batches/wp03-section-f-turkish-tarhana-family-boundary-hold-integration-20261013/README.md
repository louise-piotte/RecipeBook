# WP03 Section F Turkish tarhana family boundary hold

## Decision

Integrated the R47 review as an evidence-only hold. Keep generic Turkish `tarhana` unallocated. Official Turkish sources distinguish fermented flour-based Uşak granules from Maraş Tarhanası, made with cracked wheat and yogurt, dried in sheets, and eaten in several ways, including as a snack or in soup. Canadian retailer pages use `tarhana` for multiple soup products, while no Québec-facing French product display was verified. A generic picker row would assign the family name to one product identity without resolving that boundary.

Keep Uşak and Maraş names product-specific. Do not alias the family to cracked wheat, bulgur, flour, or yogurt. The full bundled recipe package has no matching demand, and package sizes or fermentation periods do not support reusable measures or conversions.

## Evidence and limits

- The Turkish Ministry of Culture and Tourism describes generic tarhana as fermented flour, yogurt, vegetable, and spice dough that is dried and ground. Its Uşak specification names a wheat-flour-based granulated product and requires at least 21 days of fermentation: [Uşak Tarhanası](https://www.kulturportali.gov.tr/portal/usak-tarhanasi).
- The same ministry distinguishes Maraş Tarhanası, made from cracked wheat and yogurt, dried on woven mats, and eaten semi-dried, dry as a snack, in soup, or in other ways: [Maraş Tarhanası](https://www.kulturportali.gov.tr/portal/maras-tarhanasi). The European Commission protects the exact regional name *Maraş Tarhanası* as a PGI; this does not establish a generic Québec product name: [Regulation (EU) 2023/2706](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2023/2706/oj/eng).
- Turkish Bazaar Canada lists Ege soup tarhana, Maraş-style soup powder, and another tarhana soup mix with different product titles and package sizes. These retailer pages establish specific Canadian listings, not a uniform product composition or Québec French display: [Ege Tarhana Soup](https://www.turkishbazaar.ca/product/tarhana/), [Maraş-style Tarhana Soup](https://www.turkishbazaar.ca/product/hasiroglu-turkish-tarhana-soup/), and [Tarhana Soup mix](https://www.turkishbazaar.ca/product/tarhana-soup-toronto-canada/).
- The proposal's exact ownership audit found no active reference, form, legacy candidate, ledger entry, or earlier decision for tarhana. It scanned all 114 manifest-listed recipes and 1,123 ingredient rows with no matching phrase. No Québec French retail name, reusable measure, density, mapping, yield, or substitution was established.
