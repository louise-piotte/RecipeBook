# WP03 Section F Honey Nut Cheerios family picker hold (R186)

Integrated the review as a hold. Keep the Canadian standard-sugar Naturally Flavoured Honey Nut
Cheerios SKU outside active references, forms, legacy mappings, and the research ledger until the
separate Lower Sugar SKU can be reviewed in the same picker decision. General Mills Canada provides
distinct English and French product pages and describes the lower-sugar cereal as 50% lower in sugar
than the original. Adding only the standard SKU would make broad English and French Honey Nut
queries return one of two separate products.

Use the manufacturer titles if the family is later added together:

- Naturally Flavoured Honey Nut Cheerios / Au miel et aux noix Saveurs naturelles Cheerios
- Lower Sugar Naturally Flavoured Honey Nut Cheerios / Cheerios au miel et aux noix Saveurs
  naturelles moins de sucre

The 1 cup (37 g) serving belongs only to its manufacturer SKU and remains a possible product-specific
ledger observation if that SKU is later accepted. Do not infer a reusable cereal measure, density,
formula, form, yield, or substitution. The full 114-recipe / 1,123-row bundle has no matching demand.
No catalog, ledger, source register, recipe, or generated data changed in this hold.

Root rechecked exact ownership, picker projection, and recipe-corpus demand against the current
1,565-reference / 122-form / 1,910-record snapshot. The manufacturer’s English and French product
pages and both Lower Sugar product pages were opened on 2026-10-16; Metro’s indexed Québec listing is
secondary corroboration only because direct retrieval returned 403. See the [R186 proposal](../../proposals/wp03-section-f-honey-nut-cheerios-family-picker-hold-review-r186-20261016.md)
for the source register and full evidence limits.

No software tests were run because this integration changes documentation only. Section F remains
open; reconsider when both Honey Nut SKUs can be represented together or picker behavior can preserve
the family boundary.
