# WP03 Section U R327 — Chios mastic identity

Reviewed and integrated on 2026-10-16.

Added identity-only `Chios mastic / mastiha de Chios` in `BAKING_AND_SPICE`, with English aliases
`mastic`, `gum mastic`, and `Chios mastiha`, and French aliases `mastic`, `mastiha`, and
`gomme de mastic grecque`. The identity is limited to culinary Chios resin from *Pistacia lentiscus*;
the Chios growers use `Mastiha de Chios` in French. Keep the plant, other plant parts, oil, water,
liqueur, supplements, chewing gum, and composition-uncertain powder separate.

The ledger records the Greek-government-hosted rice-pudding formula's `1/2 teaspoon Chios mastic,
crushed with sugar` as evidence for that recipe only. It is not a density, conversion, or reusable
measure. No form, mapping, conversion, recipe link, yield, or active substitution rule was added;
substitution research remains open.

The [R327 proposal](../../proposals/wp03-section-u-regional-aromatic-review-r327-20261016.md)
contains the source and picker-boundary review. Key sources include [Kew's accepted taxon entry](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:70253-1),
the [Chios Mastiha Growers Association](https://www.gummastic.gr/fr/), its [culinary-use page](https://www.gummastic.gr/fr/le-chios-mastiha/mastiha-dans-notre-vie),
and the [Greek government-hosted recipe](https://portal.greekfarms.gov.gr/en/baked-rice-pudding-with-chios-mastic/).
The Montréal French retailer attestation is on its dev subdomain, as detailed in the proposal.

The growers' [French product page](https://www.gummastic.gr/fr/nos-produits/le-mastiha) lists resin
grades and a cooking-powder product without stating its formula. A [specific cooking-powder listing](https://www.masticha.gr/en/sugar-free/chios-mastic-powder-for-cooking-50g)
states that its 2 g sachet contains 1 g Chios mastic and 1 g maltodextrin; this SKU does not establish
the composition of other powders.
