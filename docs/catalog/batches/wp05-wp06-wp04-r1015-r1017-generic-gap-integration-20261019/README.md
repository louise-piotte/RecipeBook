# R1015–R1017 cross-category generic gap review

- **Date:** 2026-10-19
- **Status:** R1015 integrated; R1016 and R1017 held with reasons recorded.
- **Scope:** Generic prepared food, regional plant identity, and seafood-cut boundary.

## Decisions

- **R1015:** Added identity-only `oi-sobagi / oi-kimchi (oi-so-bagi)` in `SAUCE_AND_CONDIMENT`, with source-attested English aliases `oisobagi` and `cucumber kimchi`. The French display is provisional because the cited wording comes from an official Korean tourism page, not a Québec terminology ruling. Keep it distinct from generic kimchi, raw cucumber, other named kimchi types, and pickled radish. The source's texture and serving note remains source-specific; no measure, form, recipe link, conversion, yield, or active substitution was added.
- **R1016:** Kept pūhā out of active catalog data pending engagement with Māori or an appropriate Māori authority. The reviewed report specifically recommends Māori involvement in external decisions about these crops; this proposal is not that consultation. Preserve its source scope and the current *Tetragonia* New Zealand-spinach identity. Add no pūhā aliases, botanical crosswalks, forms, or recipe links.
- **R1017:** Held unqualified `salmon belly / ventre de saumon` outside references, aliases, forms, and ledger. OQLF defines the Québec term as a heavily salted ventral portion, while the other reviewed sources use belly-flap wording for a fillet part and belly wording for gravlax or a smoked product. The bundle has no recipe demand selecting one state. The seller's smoked, citrus-prepared product remains evidence about that named product only; none of its attributes were generalized.

See the [R1015](../../proposals/wp05-section-w-generic-pantry-gap-review-r1015-20261019.md), [R1016](../../proposals/wp06-regional-generic-gap-review-r1016-20261019.md), and [R1017](../../proposals/wp04-section-p-generic-seafood-gap-review-r1017-20261019.md) proposals for source registers and detailed ownership, recipe-demand, and boundary audits.

## Cross-category scope

The reviews selected generic culinary identity or evidence questions and did not pursue brand, product-line, flavour-family, SKU, or retailer-variant rosters. A queued item whose only deliverable is such a commercial roster is out of scope because **commercial-product counts do not add ingredient diversity**; record that reason and stop the roster research. Existing branded rows and their source ownership remain intact. Product-only claims and measurements remain with the named product unless a separate review establishes transferability.
