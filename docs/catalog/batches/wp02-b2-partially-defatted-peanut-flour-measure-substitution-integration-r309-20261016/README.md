# WP02 B2 R309 partially defatted peanut flour integration

Completed bounded measurement and substitution research on the existing `ingredient-ref-partially-defatted-peanut-flour` record. The Mill Creek listing reproduces a Protein Plus label serving of 1/4 cup (30 g); the observation is tied to that listing, with grade, cup standard, and filling method unspecified. Density and unit mappings remain empty.

The 2016 Labuckas et al. study supports two formula-specific findings: 10% and 20% replacement of wheat flour in one defined experimental bread formula, and replacement of the wheat-flour component in one cookie formula that retained cornstarch and increased shortening. Both findings have exact formula and product limitations and no `ruleId`. They do not create a reusable substitution rule. Aliases, forms, active rules, and recipes were not changed; no bundled recipe demands partially defatted peanut flour.
