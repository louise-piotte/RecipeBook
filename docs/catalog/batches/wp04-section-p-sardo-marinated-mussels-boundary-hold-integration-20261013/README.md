# WP04 Section P Sardo marinated-mussels boundary hold — 2026-10-13

## Decision

Keep Sardo's herb-and-garlic marinated mussels outside active references and forms until a bundled
recipe requests the product. The maker and Super C identify one finished, seasoned SKU and provide
English and Québec French product wording, but neither names its mussel species. OQLF's normalized
commercial term `moule` is scoped to blue mussel (*Mytilus edulis*); DFO's statement about the single
mussel species harvested in Canada does not identify the species in this Canadian-labelled SKU.

The bundled recipe corpus has no mussel request. Keep the product separate from blue and green-lipped
mussels, generic mussel wording, smoked mussels, rollmops, and other marinated seafood. Do not treat
the 250 ml package or Voilà's per-10-piece (55 g) nutrition serving as a recipe measure, piece mass,
density, or yield. Add no identity, alias, form, measurement, mapping, recipe link, conversion, yield,
or substitution; no ledger candidate was added.

## Evidence

- [Sardo, Select Herb & Garlic Marinated Mussels](https://sardofoods.ca/product/select-herb-garlic-marinated-mussels/)
  names the prepared product and provides its ingredient list and intended uses. It does not identify
  the mussel species or source fishery.
- [Super C's English listing](https://www.superc.ca/en/aisles/fish-seafood/prepared-fish-seafood/marinated-fish-seafood/herb-and-garlic-marinated-mussels/p/055823086458)
  and [French listing](https://www.superc.ca/allees/poissons-et-fruits-de-mer/poissons-et-fruits-de-mer-prepares/poissons-et-fruits-de-mer-marines/moules-marinees-aux-herbes-et-a-l-ail/p/055823086458)
  identify the same product number and 250 ml SKU with bilingual titles. The French listing's
  `Produit du Canada` designation is not a species statement.
- [Voilà's listing](https://voila.ca/products/sardo-marinated-mussels-herb-garlic-250-ml/437193EA)
  displays the nutrition serving as 10 pieces (55 g). That is specific to this product and does not
  establish whether the amount includes marinade or a reusable mussel conversion.
- [OQLF's `moule` entry](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/1198877/moule)
  specifies *Mytilus edulis* for the normalized Québec commercial term. [DFO's bivalve identification
  page](https://www.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/shellfish-mollusques/identification-eng.htm) says the single mussel
  species harvested in Canada is blue mussel; neither source ties the Sardo product to that species.
