Reviewed 2026-10-17. This integration records a documentation-only hold from the [R389 proposal](../../proposals/wp04-silkworm-pupae-identity-boundary-review-r389-20261017.md).

The reviewed Canadian grocery listing names frozen silkworm pupae without species or detailed product specification; the species-named human-food supplier is outside Canada. This is an evidence hold, not a conclusion that the food is novel, prohibited, unsafe, or unavailable.

Keep whole pupae, powders/flours, extracts/concentrates, supplements, and animal feed separate. Do not transfer Health Canada's non-novel determination for other insect species or processes to *B. mori*. OQLF terminology supports lexical components only, not a prescribed Québec food label. The recipe scan found no matching demand. No reference, alias, form, ledger candidate, measurement, conversion, yield, recipe link, or substitution was added; this documentation-only decision needed no tests.
