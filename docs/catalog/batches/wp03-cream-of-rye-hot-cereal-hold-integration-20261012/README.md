# WP03 — cream-of-rye hot-cereal identity hold (2026-10-12)

## Decision

Keep cream of rye outside the active catalog. U.S. FDA guidance names cream of rye as an example
within the hot-breakfast-cereal category, establishing the English product class. The reviewed
evidence does not establish a current Canadian or Québec dry-product label, its composition, or a
French product name. The former Roman Meal product's reported flaked-rye composition also leaves
the boundary from the active rye-flakes identity unresolved.

Keep `gruau de seigle` and `semoule de seigle` attached to the existing `rye meal / gruau de seigle`
identity. OQLF defines *gruau* as a prepared hot dish and allows the sense to extend to dishes made
with other cereals; RICARDO uses *gruau de seigle* for rye cooked with milk. TERMIUM's existing
rye-meal record supports the current milling-product owner. These sources do not attest a dry
cream-of-rye product name in Québec.

The live package has no complete cream-of-rye or rye-neighbor phrase in its recipe files or
ingredient lines: 0 hits in 114 recipes and 1,123 ingredient rows. The proposal records active/form/
legacy/ledger ownership, current picker results, the package fingerprint, and source limits.

## Evidence and limits

- The [FDA's nonbinding product-category guidance](https://www.fda.gov/media/102587/download)
  lists cream of rye among hot breakfast cereals. Its category reference amounts of 1 cup prepared
  and 40 g plain dry cereal are not package-specific measures, density, or a dry-to-cooked yield for
  a cream-of-rye product.
- The [OQLF entry for *gruau*](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/8363388/gruau)
  defines the term as a prepared dish and says its sense can extend to other cereals.
- [RICARDO's Québec hot-cereal article](https://www.ricardocuisine.com/chroniques/a-votre-sante/39-des-cereales-chaudes-au-petit-dejeuner)
  describes rye porridge as *gruau de seigle* cooked with milk; it does not name a packaged dry
  product.
- The proposal reviews the historical USDA records and product references, and cites the existing
  [TERMIUM rye-meal record](https://www.btb.termiumplus.gc.ca/tpv2alpha/alpha-fra.html?i=&index=frw&lang=fra&srchtxt=GRUAU+SEIGLE)
  for the active rye-meal naming boundary: [full WP03 review](../../proposals/wp03-next-section-f-residual-candidate-review-20261012.md).

The FDA category evidence supports English class membership only. It does not prove current Canadian
availability or a French display. Do not add an identity, aliases, forms, measures, density, mappings,
recipe links, yield, conversion, or substitution. Reopen if a current Canadian/Québec package or an
authoritative terminology source establishes a distinct bilingual dry-product identity.

## Reconciliation

This closes one candidate review beneath the open Section F regional-cereal and preprocessed-grain
item; that parent remains open. Root recorded the hold in the plan and roadmap. The shared catalog,
ledger, recipes, generated files, and application behavior were unchanged. No app tests or emulator
checks were run because this was a documentation-only reconciliation.
