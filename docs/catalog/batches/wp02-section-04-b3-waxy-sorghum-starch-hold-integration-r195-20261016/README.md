# WP02 Section 04 B3 R195 — Waxy Sorghum Starch Hold

- **Status:** Integrated as a documentation-only hold; the Section 04 B3 parent remains open.
- **Disposition:** Keep waxy sorghum starch out of active references and the research ledger until a current Canadian or Québec human-food source establishes a marketed starch grade and independent Québec terminology review supports its French display.
- **Existing identity:** Keep generic `sorghum starch / amidon de sorgho` unchanged. Keep waxy maize and waxy rice starch, sorghum grain, flour, grits, and semolina separate.

## Evidence and limits

The 2025 *Cereal Chemistry* study isolates starch from waxy sorghum grain using enzymatic and wet-milling methods. The 2008 comparison describes functional differences among waxy, heterowaxy, and normal starch samples. These primary studies establish a research subtype, but not a current Canadian retail product, food-service grade, French package name, kitchen measure, or recipe substitution. The Wiley author list identifies Xiaorong Wu; the proposal's original Xiaofei spelling was corrected.

OQLF defines the general terms `amidon de sorgho` and `amidon de grains cireux`; the latter's note permits origin-specific wording such as waxy maize starch. These entries support the existing generic sorghum-starch row and general cereal terminology, but do not verify `amidon de sorgho cireux` as a marketed Québec product name. Current adjacent Québec evidence includes sorghum flour in a gluten-free flour blend and a 700 g sorghum-flour product. Health Canada's 2021 NHP ingredient update lists Sorghum Starch with Dry and Powdered preparation methods, which is NHP terminology rather than evidence of a culinary food package.

## Ownership and recipe demand

Before this hold was recorded, the live catalog contained 1,574 references, 122 forms, 61 legacy candidates, and 1,919 research-ledger records. Exact ownership searches found no waxy-sorghum-starch reference, form, legacy candidate, or ledger allocation. The existing generic sorghum-starch reference remains the only source-class owner. The R195 source prefix is not added to the shared ledger: the three new proposal IDs each map to one URL, and the four OQLF/Québec product IDs are reused with their established URL mappings.

The manifest-listed bundle has 114 recipes and 1,123 ingredient rows. No recipe text or ingredient row requests waxy sorghum starch, sorghum starch, or sorghum. No recipe link or numeric behavior was added.

No density, measure, form, yield, conversion, or direction-specific substitution is supported. Laboratory extraction ratios and starch-water experiments remain study-specific.
