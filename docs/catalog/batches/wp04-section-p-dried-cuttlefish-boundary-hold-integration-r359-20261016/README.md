# WP04 Section P — dried cuttlefish boundary hold (R359, 2026-10-16)

## Review record

- **Status:** Integrated as a documentation-only hold on 2026-10-16; see the [proposal](../../proposals/wp04-section-p-next-gap-review-r359-20261016.md).
- **Selected boundary:** Human-food dried cuttlefish remains unallocated until a bundled recipe or a better-specified product request identifies the intended product.
- **Recommendation:** Keep dried cuttlefish outside active references, forms, legacy mappings, and the shared research ledger. The candidate French wording `seiche séchée` is provisional and is not verified as current Québec package wording.
- **Scope:** No active reference, legacy candidate, research-ledger record, form, measurement, recipe link, conversion, yield, or substitution was added.

## Evidence and decision

Canadian retailer pages reviewed on 2026-10-16 show two distinct contexts: T&T's page lists `Pacific Intl Dried Cuttlefish (100 g)` with little product detail, while Axia Station describes a seasoned CARADA product as cleaned, steamed, and sun-dried, with both direct eating and cooking uses. These are retailer page snapshots; availability may change, and neither page establishes the product species or a Québec French title. The ingredient list and preparation reported for the CARADA item remain specific to that product.

OQLF's GDT supports `seiche` as the Québec French base term for edible cuttlefish, and CFIA's Fish List shows that common names can be species-specific. CFIA labeling guidance says that product-condition descriptors such as `dried` may be needed to communicate a fish product's true nature. Agriculture and Agri-Food Canada's 2012 French report uses `seiche séchée ou salée` for 2011 Portugal imports, but this is historical trade wording and combines dried and salted product. Taken together, these sources do not establish one recipe-ready form or a current Québec retail name.

## Picker and recipe impact

- A hypothetical new `dried cuttlefish / seiche séchée` identity would change broad `cuttlefish` and `seiche` searches from two results to three; exact dried phrases would return the candidate.
- The 114 manifest-listed recipes and 1,123 ingredient rows contain no cuttlefish, seiche, *Sepia*, dried-cuttlefish, or snack demand. Recipe 023's sliced calamari remains a separate squid/calamari ingredient.
- Keep the current cuttlefish / seiche identity and the separate frozen cuttlefish-ball product unchanged. Do not infer an alias, form, package conversion, measure, yield, or substitution from the reviewed product pages.
