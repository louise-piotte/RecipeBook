# WP02 A7 round 43 — purple wheat meal hold

Integrated 2026-10-13 as a focused source refresh under the open [A7 checklist](../../../RecipeBook-global-ingredient-catalog-plan.txt#L1444). The earlier residual grain-meals review already kept purple wheat meal unresolved; no duplicate ledger or legacy-candidate record was added.

## Decision

Keep purple wheat meal unallocated. InfraReady's current Purple Wheat / AnthoGrain page lists meal and flour separately, and Metro's English Breton cracker label includes purple wheat meal. Those sources establish the phrase and a product-form distinction, but they do not define the meal grind or a standalone meal SKU. Canadian French evidence supports `blé pourpre` for the crop and `farine de blé pourpre` in other material; it does not establish a Québec French meal display.

The bundle has 114 recipes and 1,123 ingredient rows, with no purple-wheat terms. No active reference, form, legacy candidate, ledger record, exact-form measure, density, mapping, conversion, yield, or substitution was found. Keep the candidate distinct from active whole-wheat meal and from flour; add no row or alias.

## Evidence and checks

- [InfraReady Purple Wheat / AnthoGrain](https://www.infrareadyproducts.com/products/purple-wheat) separately lists Meal and Flour as applications.
- [InfraReady applications](https://www.infrareadyproducts.com/applications) says AnthoGrain can be processed into whole-grain meals and flours, but gives no meal grade or product specification.
- [Metro's English Breton variety-pack listing](https://www.metro.ca/en/online-grocery/aisles/snacks/salty-snacks/crackers/original-multigrain-and-garden-vegetable-flavoured-crackers-variety-pack/p/055653672906) includes purple wheat meal in its whole-grains list; the listing is a cracker ingredient statement and showed out of stock.
- The [AgriRéseau technical report](https://www.agrireseau.net/documents/Document_90797.pdf) uses `blé pourpre` and describes whole-grain purple-wheat flour prototypes. The Metro French listing uses `farine de blé pourpre` for a different product number and did not provide an accessible bilingual crosswalk.
- The proposal records source IDs, exact ownership, picker projection, corpus counts, and numeric/substitution limits in [`wp02-a7-purple-wheat-meal-boundary-review-20261013-round43.md`](../../proposals/wp02-a7-purple-wheat-meal-boundary-review-20261013-round43.md).

No tests or emulator checks were run because this was a documentation-only source refresh. WP02 A7 remains open.
