## Integrated decisions

- **WP02 B3 — mango-kernel starch:** Held outside active references and the ledger pending a current Canadian culinary product and product-matched Québec French name. Studies establish a lab-isolated kernel fraction, while supplier and terminology evidence do not establish this commercial ingredient. Keep mango fruit, amchur, kernel flour/powder, pulp starch, and kernel oil separate. No recipe demand was found.
- **WP03 Section F — Mini-Wheats Original Frosted:** Held outside active references and the ledger pending family-wide picker review. The Canadian English and French manufacturer pages list eight Mini-Wheats products; one Original row would make the broad `Mini-Wheats` search misleadingly select a single flavor. No recipe demand was found.
- **WP04 Section P — brined *Buccinum undatum*:** Held outside active references, forms, legacy mappings, and the ledger. Adding natural `whelk` / `buccin` product wording would make broad queries appear species-resolved, and the producer’s `bigorneau` wording conflicts with the active common-periwinkle name. Its French and English shelf-life statements also conflict. No recipe demand was found.
