# WP02 A7 deeply roasted durum flour boundary hold

## Decision

Integrated the R47 proposal as an evidence-only hold. Keep CerealVeneta's `Deeply roasted durum wheat flour / Farine de blé dur brûlé` outside the active catalog and ledger. The manufacturer's pages describe a whole-durum semi-finished product that is heavily but controllably toasted before cryomilling, but they do not establish a Canadian or Québec consumer product or a Québec French display.

The French wording also overlaps the manufacturer's description of traditional Apulian `blé brûlé`, made from durum grains left in the field after harvest and straw burning. CerealVeneta says its controlled process was inspired by that tradition and distinguishes the processes. OQLF supports the base name `farine de blé dur` for unqualified durum flour; it does not name this roasted product. Keep the candidate distinct from plain durum flour, durum semolina, malted/roasted wheat-malt flours, and the Québec mill's generic roasted wheat flour.

## Evidence and limits

- CerealVeneta's [English product page](https://cerealveneta.it/en/semi-finished/deeply-roasted-durum-wheat-flour/) names `Deeply roasted durum wheat flour`, calls it a semi-finished product made from whole durum wheat, and describes abundant but controlled toasting followed by cryomilling. Its product-sheet form asks for company and contact details. “Small quantities” is qualitative, not a recipe amount.
- CerealVeneta's [French product page](https://cerealveneta.it/fr/semi-finis/farine-de-ble-dur-brule/) names `Farine de blé dur brûlé` and describes whole durum, controlled `torréfaction douce`, and cryomilling. This is manufacturer wording, not a Québec label or OQLF ruling.
- The manufacturer's [process explanation](https://cerealveneta.it/fr/mild-roasting-how-to-enhance-the-aromatic-component-of-cereals-seeds-and-pulses/) distinguishes traditional Apulian `blé brûlé` from its controlled process inspired by the tradition.
- The [OQLF GDT entry](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/8357377/farine-de-ble-dur) supports `farine de blé dur` and `farine de blé durum` for unqualified durum flour, not a toasted product.
- Les Moulins de Soulanges lists [Roasted Wheat Flour](https://moulinsdesoulanges.com/language/en/roasted-wheat-flour/) / [Farine de Blé Torréfié](https://moulinsdesoulanges.com/language/fr/farine-de-ble-torrefie/) as a separate Québec-mill product. Those pages do not establish durum wheat, whole grain, or CerealVeneta's process.
- The proposal's exact ownership audit found no active reference, form, legacy candidate, ledger record, or earlier focused A7 decision for the candidate wording. Its pre-proposal snapshot was 1,369 references, 100 forms, 64 legacy candidates, and 1,699 ledger records. The 114-recipe / 1,123-row bundle had no candidate or adjacent-term hits and no links to the neighboring durum or roasted-wheat identities.
- No exact-form measure, density, unit mapping, conversion, yield, or substitution is supported. Do not infer one from plain durum flour, semolina, generic roasted wheat flour, or traditional `blé brûlé`.
