# WP04 Section P lumpfish roe / caviar de lompe hold — 2026-10-12

## Decision

Keep a species-qualified lumpfish-roe identity unallocated until a bundled recipe calls for it. The reviewed Québec product names `œufs de lompe` and `caviar de lompe` refer to roe from *Cyclopterus lumpus*, but they do not establish a generic roe or caviar identity. Keep this product separate from whole lumpfish, salmon roe, tobiko, masago, bottarga, and sturgeon caviar.

The recipe-conditioned checklist remains open for its other roe products. The current 114-file / 1,123-row recipe scan found no lumpfish, roe, fish-egg, or caviar demand. The live picker also shows why broad naming needs care: `caviar` currently returns finger lime through `lime caviar`; a lumpfish-roe display would add a second, unrelated result. Do not add bare `caviar`, `roe`, or `fish eggs` aliases.
