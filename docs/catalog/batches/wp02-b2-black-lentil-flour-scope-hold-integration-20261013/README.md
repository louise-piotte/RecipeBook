# WP02 B2/B3 black lentil flour scope hold — 2026-10-13

Integrated the [round-68 black lentil flour review](../../proposals/wp02-b2-black-lentil-flour-scope-quebec-search-hold-20261013-round68.md).

## Decision

Keep black lentil flour made from *Lens culinaris* unallocated. A food-science study uses the
phrase for *Lens culinaris*, while a flour manufacturer and Canadian retail listing use “black
lentil flour” for urad/black matpe (*Vigna mungo*). No Québec-facing flour label resolves the
product scope or French display. Do not add the phrase to either the whole black-Beluga lentil or
urid-flour references.

The current phrase-first picker would make `black lentil` return only the proposed flour, and the
Canadian Urad listing's exact “Black Lentil Flour” ingredient wording would land on the wrong
species-specific row. The recipe package has no matching ingredient demand. Research blend ratios
and measurements do not establish a kitchen measure, density, yield, conversion, or substitution.

No catalog, form, legacy candidate, or research-ledger data changed. Revisit if a Canadian
standalone product explicitly identifies *Lens culinaris*, Québec French product wording is
supported, and picker behavior preserves the Urad distinction.

## Checks

- Read-only exact-owner and archive search found no focused prior review or owner for the proposed identity or terms.
- The proposal reports no matching ingredient demand in the 114 manifest-listed recipes and 1,123 ingredient rows.
- No app tests or emulator checks were run; this is a documentation-only hold.
