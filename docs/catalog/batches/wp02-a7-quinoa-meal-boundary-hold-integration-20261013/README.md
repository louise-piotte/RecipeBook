# WP02 A7 quinoa-meal boundary hold — 2026-10-13

## Decision

Keep plain `quinoa meal` unresolved and unallocated. Graina uses the phrase for its whole-seed
quinoa flour; technical papers use `meal` and `flour` inconsistently. A Canadian retailer's “Quinoa
Meal, Basil Pesto” is a ready-to-eat dish made with cooked quinoa, not a dry milling product. OQLF
defines `quinoa` as the seed/pseudocereal, and the Québec producer names its flour `farine de quinoa`
without identifying a distinct meal product or French name.

The bundle has no matching recipe demand. Keep quinoa meal distinct from generic quinoa flour, other
quinoa-flour grades, and whole quinoa. Add no active reference, alias, form, measurement, density,
mapping, recipe link, conversion, yield, substitution, or ledger candidate.

## Evidence

- [Graina's Organic Quinoa Flour](https://graina.com.au/products/organic-quinoa-flour) page calls
  the item flour made from whole quinoa seeds; its indexed description also uses `quinoa meal` and
  `ground quinoa` as seller-specific synonyms.
- [Youssif et al.'s laboratory study](https://jfds.journals.ekb.eg/article_36010_a59ae867d590ace9f3acad5fc26610c1.pdf)
  calls its experimental sieved material “whole quinoa meal” and later calls the material quinoa
  flour. Its conditioning and screen details are study-specific, not a retail product standard.
- [The Québec quinoa producer](https://www.quinoaquebec.com/nosproduitprofessionnel) lists
  `Farine de quinoa` and `Flocons de quinoa`, but no separate meal product.
- [OQLF's quinoa entry](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/26541613/quinoa)
  supports `quinoa` for the seed and identifies it as a pseudocereal; it does not name a meal product.
- [Instacart Canada's Quinoa Meal, Basil Pesto listing](https://www.instacart.ca/products/385892-cucina-amore-quinoa-meal-basil-pesto-7-9-oz)
  describes a ready-to-eat food with cooked quinoa and pesto ingredients.

No matching candidate phrase was found in 114 bundled recipes or 1,123 ingredient rows. The proposal
records the exact ownership, picker, measurement, and substitution review.
