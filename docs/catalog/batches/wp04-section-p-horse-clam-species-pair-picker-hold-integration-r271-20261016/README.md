# WP04 Section P R271 — horse-clam species-pair hold

## Decision

Keep *Tresus capax* and *Tresus nuttallii* outside active references, forms, legacy mappings, and the research ledger. English sources distinguish fat gaper / *T. capax* from Pacific gaper / *T. nuttallii*, but the reviewed French names do not support a reliable, current whole-animal pair for Québec recipe search.

CFIA lists `Fausse-mactre` for *T. capax* but no French common name for its *T. nuttalli* row. DFO's French conservation and current fisheries material uses `fausse-mactre` across the pair. OQLF's 1993 `mactre du Pacifique` entry cites the historical combination *Tresus nuttali capax*; its `fausse mactre du Pacifique` wording elsewhere is scoped to a siphon. These sources do not justify choosing a current whole-animal alias or displaying both taxa under a shared broad French name.

The conditional species rows would give broad `clam` searches only two horse-clam results while the corpus has no clam recipe demand. Leave the held `ingredient-ref-clam` candidate unchanged. Add no identities, aliases, forms, measures, conversions, yields, recipe links, or substitutions. CNF 3111 covers mixed-species cooked/steamed clams and is not evidence for either horse-clam species. Do not infer absence of species-specific CNF 2026 data from the blocked file-access attempt recorded in [WP03 R270](../../batches/wp03-section-f-wild-rice-cnf-2026-row-access-review-integration-r270-20261016/README.md).

## Scope and sources

- [R271 proposal](../../proposals/wp04-next-section-p-gap-review-r271-20261016.md): full ownership and corpus audit, species-name evidence, conditional picker projection, limits, and source register.
- [CFIA Fish List](https://active.inspection.gc.ca/scripts/fssa/fispoi/fplist/fpresults.asp): *T. capax* has `Clam / Fausse-mactre`; the *T. nuttalli* row has `Horse Clam` and `Clam` but no French common name.
- [DFO 2026–27 Pacific geoduck and horse-clam fishery summary](https://www.pac.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/fm-gp/mplans/geoduck-panope-ifmp-pgip-sm-fra.html): current fishery/market context uses `fausse-mactre`, without distinguishing the two whole-animal species in French.
- [OQLF `mactre du Pacifique`](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/17017503/mactre-du-pacifique): 1993 entry with historical taxonomic wording; not adopted as a settled current pair name.
- [OQLF `siphon`](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/1199061/siphon): its *T. nuttalli* example is anatomy/product-part scoped.
- [DFO French sport-identification guide](https://www.pac.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/fm-gp/rec/identify-identifier-fra.html): calls *T. capax* `fausse-mactre` and describes its morphology, not recipe substitution.

The source IDs in the R271 proposal are proposal-local; this documentation-only hold adds no ledger sources or findings.
