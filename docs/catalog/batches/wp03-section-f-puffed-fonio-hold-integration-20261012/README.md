# WP03 — Puffed fonio identity hold (2026-10-12)

## Decision

Keep puffed fonio out of the active ingredient catalog pending a current Canadian or Québec product
label or specification that establishes the French product name, source species, and ingredient
scope. No active reference, alias, legacy candidate, research-ledger record, form, measure, recipe
link, conversion, yield, or substitution was added.

The reviewed sources establish that puffed fonio exists as a product class: Togo's Ministry of
Agriculture lists fonio soufflé among ready-to-consume fonio products, and the U.S. supplier Grainwell
offers a B2B “Puffed Fonio” product. Neither source establishes a Québec display name or whether its
product uses white or black fonio, a specific species, or a single-ingredient composition. The 2012
Canadian trademark journal lists “processed fonio puffs” / “fonio soufflé transformé” as proposed use;
that is not proof of a current product or Québec usage. A Benin catalogue also uses “Fonio soufflé” for
a multi-ingredient granola, so the phrase alone does not establish a plain puffed-grain identity.

Keep the candidate distinct from existing white and black fonio, their flours, precooked or parboiled
fonio, fonio flakes, and composite cereals or snacks. The full manifest-listed bundle contains 114
recipes and 1,123 ingredient rows, with zero matching recipe demand. Broad picker queries would also
gain another result for “fonio,” “puffed,” and “soufflé” without improving the unresolved product
scope.

## Evidence reviewed

The complete ownership audit, static picker comparison, full recipe scan, source limitations, and
proposal-time hashes are recorded in the [review proposal](../../proposals/wp03-section-f-puffed-fonio-identity-review-20261012.md).
Key sources include:

- Togo Ministry of Agriculture, [Plan d’investissement de la filière fonio](https://agriculture.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Plan_invest_Fonio_03_06_23_Valid-ok.pdf), which distinguishes white and black fonio and lists fonio soufflé as ready-to-consume without assigning a species to that product.
- Grainwell Foods, [Puffed Fonio](https://grainwellfoods.com/product/puffed-fonio/), a U.S. B2B product page without a consumer package, ingredient statement, or species.
- Canadian Intellectual Property Office, [Trade-marks Journal, Vol. 59, No. 2997](https://publications.gc.ca/collections/collection_2012/opic-cipo/RG42-2-59-2997.pdf), whose bilingual goods list is marked proposed use in Canada.
- Poulho, [2024 catalogue](https://poulho.my.canva.site/), which lists a composite granola called “Fonio soufflé.”
- Office québécois de la langue française, [fonio blanc](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/26529747/fonio-blanc), which supports white-fonio terms but does not establish a processed puffed-fonio term.

## Reconciliation

This closes only the named puffed-fonio candidate review beneath the open Section F checklist; grits,
polenta, other puffed grains, and the remaining Section F coverage stay open. Canonical JSON, recipe
data, research ledger, generated code, and app behavior were unchanged. This documentation-only
reconciliation required no app tests or emulator checks.
