# WP02 A7 — Einkorn malt flour boundary hold (R109)

Reviewed and integrated on 2026-10-14. A7 remains open.

## Decision

Kept einkorn malt flour unallocated. ATCO's wholesale range names `Einkorn malt flour`, but the
reviewed sources do not establish a Canadian or Québec product declaration, candidate-specific
specification, or Québec French display. The laboratory study establishes malted einkorn grain, not a
commercial flour product. OQLF defines `farine maltée` as flour with malt extract or ground malt
added; it does not name flour milled from malted einkorn. Do not invent a French alias from that term.

Keep the candidate distinct from plain einkorn flour, whole einkorn, malted wheat flour, generic malt
flour, malted spelt flour, and roasted malt flours. The Québec product-directory portion of 1/4 cup
(30 g) belongs only to its plain einkorn-flour listing. No form, measure, density, mapping, recipe
link, conversion, yield, or substitution is supported. The 114 manifest-listed recipes / 1,123 rows
have no matching demand.

No active reference, alias, legacy candidate, or ledger record was added. No database data changed;
this documentation-only hold required no tests.
