# WP05 Section Z piccalilli identity integration

Added identity-only `ingredient-ref-piccalilli` as `piccalilli / piccalilli` in `SAUCE_AND_CONDIMENT`. The OQLF Grand dictionnaire terminologique gives `piccalilli` as the preferred Québec French term and the same English equivalent. No aliases, forms, measures, mappings, recipe links, conversions, yields, or substitutions were added.

The identity remains distinct from generic relish, pickle relish, mustard pickles, chow-chow, chutney, and manufacturer-specific `sauce piccalilli` wording. The Heinz page documents one product formula, and Québec’s heritage record shows historical product variation; neither justifies broader aliases. The full recipe audit found zero piccalilli matches among 114 recipes and 1,123 ingredient rows.
