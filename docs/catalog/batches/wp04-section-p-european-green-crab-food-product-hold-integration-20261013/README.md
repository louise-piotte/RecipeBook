# WP04 Section P European green crab food-product hold — 2026-10-13

## Decision

Keep European green crab / `crabe vert` (*Carcinus maenas*) outside active ingredient references
and the research ledger until a defined Canadian human-food product is documented. Québec's 2024
identification guide directly pairs the French name, taxon, and English name. DFO's September 2026
Newfoundland and Labrador notice seeks expressions of interest in frozen or crushed crab for
composting, shell derivatives, or animal/human food; temporary licences would be offered only if
interest were sufficient, and details would follow later. The notice reports no result, food
specification, or current retail product. DFO's 2026 Public Notices index still lists PN26.008 and
contains no separate public outcome notice for the test case. DFO's separate December 2025 New Brunswick pilot lists
bait, bioplastics, and soil enrichment as potential uses.

A hypothetical qualified row would be uniquely searchable for `European green crab`, `green crab`,
`crabe vert`, and DFO's alternate `crabe européen`, but it would add a sixth result to the existing
broad `crab` / `crabe` searches. The 114 manifest-listed recipes (1,123 ingredient rows) contain no
green-crab request; the existing generic Jumbo Lump crab-meat recipe does not identify a species.
No food form, transferable measure, edible yield, conversion, or substitution is supported.

No active reference, alias, form, legacy candidate, ledger candidate, recipe link, measurement,
conversion, yield, or substitution was added. The Section P checklist remains open.

## Sources

- [Government of Québec — aquatic invasive-species identification guide](https://cdn-contenu.quebec.ca/cdn-contenu/faune/documents/exotiques/carnet-identification-especes-aquatiques-envahissantes.pdf), p. 4 (2024): pairs `Crabe vert`, *Carcinus maenas*, and `European green crab`; this is species/naming evidence, not food-product evidence.
- [Fisheries and Oceans Canada — Public Notice PN26.008](https://www.nfl.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/en/node/2916), issued 2026-09-08: describes an expression-of-interest test case for frozen/crushed product. It says temporary licences depend on sufficient interest and details will follow; it contains no result or food specification.
- [Government of Canada — Southwest New Brunswick commercial fishery pilot](https://www.canada.ca/en/fisheries-oceans/news/2025/12/government-of-canada-launches-commercial-fishery-pilot-to-help-control-european-green-crab-in-southwest-new-brunswick.html), 2025-12-15: announces a one-year control-fishery pilot and lists bait, bioplastics, and soil enrichment among potential uses.
- [DFO — 2026 Public Notices](https://www.nfl.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/en/Fisheries_Notices_2026): current index still lists PN26.008 and has no separate public test-case outcome notice; it does not establish private correspondence or licensing results.
- [DFO — Le crabe vert dans les eaux de Terre-Neuve](https://www.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/species-especes/publications/ais-eae/greencrab-crabevert/index-fra.html): pairs `crabe vert` / *Carcinus maenas*, gives `crabe européen` as an alternate French name, and distinguishes the species from native rock crab.
