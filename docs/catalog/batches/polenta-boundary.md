# WP03 polenta boundary review

- **Reviewed:** 2026-10-07
- **Status:** reviewed and unresolved for ingredient matching; no catalog identity was added.
- **Scope:** distinguish prepared polenta from dry corn products sold or described as polenta.

## Decision

Keep bare `polenta` unallocated. OQLF defines it in the food-dish domain as an Italian dish made from corn semolina cooked with liquid. OQLF separately defines `semoule de maïs` as a dry-corn milling product with fine, medium, and coarse grinds; that term is already used by the catalog's cornmeal identity.

Retail use does not establish a universal dry-product identity. TIPIAK Canada markets a quick, precooked corn-semolina polenta. Bob's Red Mill sells dry, coarsely ground yellow corn as polenta and describes brand-specific processing distinctions between its polenta, grits, and cornmeal. Keep the manufacturer's processing claims scoped to its own products.

Do not add a generic polenta identity, ingredient form, or alias to cornmeal, hominy grits, or durum semolina. If a future ingredient line clearly means a packaged dry product, resolve it from the exact label and process. If it means the prepared dish, retain it as preparation context rather than a dry ingredient reference.

The bundled seed contains no polenta display name or alias. A scan of the 114 bundled recipes found no polenta ingredient line; an archived RecipeKeeper note uses it as a serving suggestion. No recipe link, quantity, seed record, form, density, mapping, measurement, yield, or substitution changed.

- **WP03-S146 — Bob's Red Mill, [Yellow Corn Polenta](https://www.bobsredmill.com/product/corn-grits-polenta):** one dry, coarse-ground yellow-corn product sold as polenta.
