# WP02 A7 toasted glutinous rice flour boundary hold — 2026-10-13

## Decision

Keep **toasted glutinous rice flour** unallocated. Anrui Biotech names an English B2B sourcing
product, and San Vicente describes the flour in its espasol confection, but neither source establishes
a Canadian or Québec standalone product or a Québec French product name. OQLF supports only the base
terms `farine de riz` and `riz gluant` / `riz glutineux`; do not construct a French display by adding
`grillée` or `torréfiée`.

Keep this candidate separate from plain glutinous rice flour and its `sweet rice flour` aliases,
toasted rice powder / `poudre de riz rôti` / `khao khua`, roasted red rice flour, Mochiko,
Shiratamako, and flour toasted as a recipe step. The bundle contains no candidate or boundary-term
matches. Add no active identity, alias, ledger candidate, form, measurement, density, unit mapping,
recipe link, conversion, yield, or substitution.

## Evidence

- [Anrui Biotech, Toasted Glutinous Rice Flour](https://anruibio.org/products/toasted-glutinous-rice-flour):
  exact English name and a supplier-described soaking, toasting, milling, and sifting process; this is
  a B2B sourcing page, not a Canadian or Québec consumer label.
- [San Vicente's Homemade Kakanin, Espasol](https://sanvicentekakanin.com/index.php/product/espasol/):
  uses toasted glutinous rice flour in a prepared confection, not as a standalone flour SKU.
- [JETRO, Japanese Rice Flour recipes](https://japan-food.jetro.go.jp/riceflour/en/recipes/index.html),
  “Toasted Grain Custard”: uses toasted Japanese rice flour prepared in the recipe; it does not
  identify glutinous rice or a packaged flour equivalent.
- [OQLF, farine de riz](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/8432782/farine-de-riz)
  and [riz glutineux](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/8393595/riz-glutineux):
  support the base flour and rice-variety terms, not a toasted-flour compound.

## Audit and limits

The proposal records the 1,369-reference / 100-form / 1,699-ledger snapshot, exact ownership and
picker projections, and a scan of all 114 bundled recipes and 1,123 ingredient rows. It found no
candidate phrase, recipe link, household measure, density, conversion, substitution ratio, or Québec
French product display. A future review should wait for a current Canadian/Québec standalone product
and a source-attested French name that distinguishes it from existing rice products.

No catalog or ledger JSON changed. No tests were run because this was a documentation-only hold.
