# WP06 Europe R457 — Turrón de Jijona hold

Recorded on 2026-10-17 after root review of the [R457 proposal](../../proposals/wp06-europe-next-regional-identity-review-r457-20261017.md).

## Decision

Keep Turrón de Jijona unallocated under the current picker. The European Commission identifies Turrón de Jijona PGI and Turrón de Alicante PGI as distinct protected products. The Montréal storefront L'Española uses `turrón` as a broader family label and lists Jijona and Alicante styles separately. OQLF's older `touron` entry describes a generic Spanish almond confection; it does not identify Jijona as the Québec French name for this protected product.

A searchable display `Turrón de Jijona` would make accent-folded broad queries `turrón` and `turron` resolve to the Jijona subtype alone. The bundled corpus has no matching demand. Add no reference, alias, ledger record, form, measure, unit mapping, conversion, recipe link, yield, or substitution. Keep the existing generic `nougat / nougat` reference unchanged. Revisit after a broader turrón-family identity can coexist with regional styles or picker behavior can preserve the distinction.

## Evidence reviewed

- European Commission, [Turrón de Alicante PGI / Turrón de Jijona PGI](https://agriculture.ec.europa.eu/farming/geographical-indications-and-quality-schemes/geographical-indications-food-and-drink/turron-de-alicante-pgi_en), identifies both protected products and distinguishes the Jijona soft ground-almond style from Alicante's hard chopped-almond style.
- OQLF, [touron (GDT)](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/17049671/touron), records a generic Spanish almond confection, without a Jijona-specific designation or preferred-term status.
- Montréal retailer L'Española, [Turron et Polvorones](https://lespanola.com/fr/collections/turron), describes turrón as Spanish nougat and offers both Jijona and Alicante styles. The page is retail naming evidence, not certification of every listed product as PGI.

The proposal's full scan found no candidate matches across 114 manifest recipes / 1,123 ingredient rows. Europe-wide regional coverage remains open.
