**Date:** 2026-10-16

## Decisions

- **WP02 B2 — fenugreek seed flour:** Keep `fenugreek flour`, `methi flour`, and
  `farine de graines de fenugrec` out of active references, aliases, forms, and
  the research ledger. Research establishes experimental seed flour, while the
  Canada-facing products reviewed are spice powder or a seed-flour infusion
  product; no culinary flour identity with a supported Québec French display
  was established. See the [proposal](../../proposals/wp02-b2-fenugreek-seed-flour-boundary-review-r175-20261016.md).
- **WP03 Section F — Red River Cream of Whole Wheat:** Hold a separate identity
  until a process-matched Québec French product name is supported. Arva documents
  a distinct whole-wheat milling fraction with retained bran, but the local
  `crème de blé` evidence applies to the broader hot-cereal category. Keep the
  existing generic identity and Red River Original/7 Grain rows unchanged. See
  the [proposal](../../proposals/wp03-section-f-red-river-cream-whole-wheat-product-boundary-review-r175-20261016.md).
- **WP04 Section P — unagi kabayaki:** Hold an unqualified identity or form.
  Japanese sources establish a glazed-grilled eel preparation and one
  *Anguilla japonica* food-table entry; current Canadian and Québec examples do
  not establish a shared species or product scope. Do not attach these terms to
  American eel. See the [proposal](../../proposals/wp04-section-p-unagi-kabayaki-product-state-boundary-review-r175-20261016.md).

All three proposals report no matching bundled-recipe demand. No active
reference, alias, form, legacy identity, recipe link, measurement, conversion,
yield, substitution, or ledger record was added or changed. Proposal-local
source IDs remain unallocated.
