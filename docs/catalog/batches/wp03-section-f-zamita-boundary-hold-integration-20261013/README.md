# WP03 Section F Zamita Boundary Hold

Integrated 2026-10-13 under the open [regional cereal-mix and preprocessed-grain checklist](../../../RecipeBook-global-ingredient-catalog-plan.txt#L2555).

## Decision

Keep generic Moroccan Zamita/Zammita/Zemita outside active references and aliases. Current Moroccan public-marketplace listings and a named recipe describe different mixtures of roasted grains, legumes, seeds, nuts, and spices, with some preparations served after adding fat or liquid. The broader family does not have one stable dry-product boundary or a Québec French display. Keep it distinct from roasted barley or wheat flour and specific Sellou preparations. The recipe bundle has no matching demand. Add no identity, alias, form, measure, recipe link, conversion, yield, substitution, or ledger candidate.

## Evidence and checks

- Morocco's [High Commission for Planning](https://www.hcp.ma/region-drda/attachment/1125796/) groups sfouf, zemita, and sellou under a broad “other cereal-based products” code. This is a reporting category, not a composition or synonym standard.
- The public [Terroir du Maroc marketplace](https://terroirdumaroc.gov.ma/details-qui-sommes-nous), operated under the Agricultural Development Agency, lists [Zamita de blé](https://terroirdumaroc.gov.ma/fr_ma/catalog/product/view/id/257/s/zamita-ble-1kg-laarassi/category/12/) and [Zamita d’orge](https://terroirdumaroc.gov.ma/fr_ma/catalog/product/view/id/258/s/zamita-d-orge-1kg-laarassi/category/12/) from the Laarassi cooperative as variants of Sellou made from multiple ingredients. A separate [Lemima cooperative product](https://terroirdumaroc.gov.ma/fr_ma/catalog/product/view/id/1357/s/zamita-500-g-cooperative-lamima/category/2/) describes a roasted maize, wheat, and chickpea mixture with herbs and spices.
- [Choumicha's Zammita recipe](https://www.choumicha.ma/recette/175-zammita.html) uses roasted chickpea, barley, almond, flax, sesame, and millet plus spices; it lists honey, butter, and water for serving and notes that recipes vary by region. These recipe amounts do not define a reusable product composition.
- The cited [Rif cereal and bread study](https://doi.org/10.4000/ethnoecologie.3070) records related names and regional variation in roasted young-grain flour and richer multi-flour preparations. The journal's direct page was bot-blocked during this review; its contents were checked in indexed article text. A Moroccan seller's [Zemita and flour page](https://slamaagricole.ma/fr/categorie-produit/zemita-farines/) also describes varying cereal bases and dry or liquid-mixed uses. Neither source is a Québec terminology ruling.
- The proposal audit counted 1,367 active references, 100 forms, 64 legacy-import candidates, 1,699 research-ledger records, 114 manifest-listed recipes, and 1,123 ingredient rows. It found no exact Zamita/Zammita/Zemita/Sellou/Sfouf owner, no matching recipe demand, and no basis to attach the family to roasted barley or wheat flour.
- The checklist, roadmap, and progress log now record the hold. No catalog, form, measurement, recipe, or ledger data changed. No tests were run because this was a documentation-only change.

The full source register, exact ownership and picker projection, recipe scan, and numeric limits are in the [R42 proposal](../../proposals/wp03-section-f-zamita-zammita-product-family-boundary-hold-20261013-r42.md).
