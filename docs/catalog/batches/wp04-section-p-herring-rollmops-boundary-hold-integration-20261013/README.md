# WP04 Section P herring-rollmops boundary hold — 2026-10-13

## Decision

Keep Feature Foods **Herring Rollmops** / the retailer wording **Rollmop de hareng** outside active
references and forms until a bundled recipe requests the ready-to-serve product. OQLF defines
`rollmops` as a herring fillet marinated in vinegar and rolled around a pickle. Feature Foods lists a
current product with that presentation, and a Canadian French retailer listing uses `Rollmop de
hareng`. The manufacturer names a catch area in the Gulf of St. Lawrence and North Atlantic, but does
not identify a fish species. Do not attach the product to Atlantic or Pacific herring on catch-area
wording alone.

A hypothetical row would add a third result to both `herring` and `hareng` searches. The 114-recipe
bundle has no matching demand. Keep rollmops separate from plain herring, unrolled marinated herring,
smoked herring, and herring roe. Add no active identity, alias, form, measure, density, unit mapping,
recipe link, conversion, yield, substitution, or ledger candidate.

The French retail listing's **320 g** is package quantity only. Feature Foods gives no net weight,
drained weight, household volume, serving mass, or product-specific species. An external home-prepared
rollmops recipe is not evidence for a retail serving or substitute.

## Evidence

- [OQLF, rollmops](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/26500049/rollmops):
  preferred Québec French term and definition of the herring-fillet / vinegar / pickle product.
- [Feature Foods, Herring Rollmops](https://featurefoods.ca/products/0-herring-rollmops): current
  maker page gives the English title, ready-to-serve description, ingredients, and catch area; it does
  not name a scientific species or provide a portion measure.
- [Your Independent Grocer, Feature Foods Rollmop de hareng](https://www.yourindependentgrocer.ca/fr/rollmop-de-hareng/p/20764945_EA?source=nspt):
  French-language Canadian retail listing with a 320 g package. The listing is not a Québec-store
  label and its nutrition portion display is not adopted as serving evidence.
- [TERMIUM Plus, hareng mariné](https://www.btb.termiumplus.gc.ca/tpv2alpha/alpha-fra.html?i=&index=frb&lang=fra&srchtxt=HARENG+MARINE):
  distinguishes rollmops from broader marinated-herring products.
- [Seafood from Canada, Rollmops: Herring in Spiced Vinegar](https://seafoodfromcanada.ca/recipe/rollmops-herring-in-spiced-vinegar/):
  an external home-preparation recipe with an unclear fillet quantity; it does not establish a measure
  or equivalence for the retail product.
