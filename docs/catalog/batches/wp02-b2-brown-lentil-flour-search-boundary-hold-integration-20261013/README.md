# WP02 B2 brown lentil flour search hold — 2026-10-13

Integrated the [round-66 brown-lentil-flour review](../../proposals/wp02-b2-brown-lentil-flour-search-boundary-review-20261013-round66.md).

## Decision

Keep brown lentil flour outside the active catalog. Research papers establish whole-seed and
dehulled brown lentil flour samples, but current Canadian flour listings reviewed do not establish
one brown-specific product scope, and `farine de lentilles brunes` is not attested as a Québec flour
label. Canadian grading treats lentil class by cotyledon colour while seed coats span several
colours; a Canadian processor also uses `brown lentil` for whole red lentils.

No brown-lentil identity exists in the active catalog. Adding a flour row alone would make bare
`brown lentil` and `lentilles brunes` searches return flour as the only result. The recipe corpus has
no matching ingredient demand. Experimental recipe quantities and water-absorption values do not
support a general measurement, conversion, yield, or substitution.

No catalog or research-ledger data changed. Reopen only with a current product source that settles
whole/dehulled scope, Québec French product naming, and safe picker results.

## Checks

- Read-only reference, form, legacy, ledger, and recipe searches found no exact brown-lentil-flour allocation or recipe demand.
- No app tests or emulator checks were run; this is a documentation-only hold.

WP02 B2/B3 remains open.
