# WP04 Section P Fumoir d’Antan smoked scallop boundary hold

## Decision

Integrated the R44 review as an evidence-only hold. Keep Fumoir d’Antan's smoked scallop product unallocated until a bundled recipe requests it. La Mer's Québec listings use `Pétoncle fumé d’Antan` in French and `Smoked scallops d’Antan` in English; Metro lists the same maker's product as `Smoked Scallop`. Those labels establish a current product, but not a new ingredient identity or a general smoked form for scallops.

The active catalog already has generic and dried scallop identities. A new product row would add a third result for broad plural `scallops` and French `pétoncle` queries, while the full recipe bundle has no matching demand. Package evidence is inconsistent: La Mer collection cards show 150 g while its product detail shows 200 g. The maker's external crostini recipe uses one 200 g package without a serving count. Keep these as source-specific observations and add no reusable measure or conversion.

## Evidence and limits

- Montréal fishmonger La Mer's [French product detail](https://www.lamer.ca/products/petoncle-fume-200-gr) calls the frozen artisanal product `Pétoncle fumé d’Antan - 200 gr` and credits Fumoir d’Antan. Its [Pétoncles collection](https://www.lamer.ca/collections/petoncles), [French smoked-products collection](https://www.lamer.ca/collections/produits-fumes), and [English smoked-products collection](https://www.lamer.ca/en/collections/produits-fumes) list 150 g; the English listing uses `Smoked scallops d’Antan`.
- Metro lists [Le fumoir d’Antan Smoked Scallop](https://www.metro.ca/epicerie-en-ligne/allees/poissons-et-fruits-de-mer/poissons-et-fruits-de-mer-surgeles/emballes-et-en-vrac/petoncle-fume/p/627843771494) at 200 g and marks it out of stock in the reviewed page view. Its product-information note directs consumers to the package for current ingredients and allergen details. No species or ingredient panel was verified.
- A 2020 regional producer directory describes Fumoir d’Antan scallops as vacuum packed and lightly smoked, with 200 g and 1 kg formats. The maker's [smoked-scallop crostini](https://fumoirdantan.com/en/smoked-scallop-crostini/) recipe calls for one 200 g package but gives no serving count. These details do not support a current universal pack size, measure, or handling standard.
- OQLF's [pétoncle entry](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/1199064/petoncle) pairs Québec `pétoncle` with `scallop` and Pectinidae. It does not identify the product species. The full 114-recipe / 1,123-row bundle scan found no matching product or scallop phrase. No density, measure, yield, conversion, or substitution is supported.
