# WP03 Section F Barkoukch Product-Boundary Hold

Integrated 2026-10-13 under the still-open [Section F regional cereal checklist](../../../RecipeBook-global-ingredient-catalog-plan.txt#L2555).

## Decision

Keep Moroccan Barkoukch unallocated until a producer specification distinguishes it from the active pearl-couscous family and a Québec-facing French product name is supported. Moroccan sources describe large-grain durum- and whole-wheat products; they do not establish synonymy with the Québec pearl-couscous product. Keep `Barkoukch`, `berkoukes`, `berkoukech`, `Aïch`, `avazine`, `mardoud`, and `mhammsa` out of active aliases without a direct product crosswalk. Add no reference, ledger candidate, form, measure, recipe link, conversion, yield, or substitution.

## Evidence and checks

- Reviewed Moroccan seller listings from [Dar Sebti](https://darsebti.ma/fr/categories/semoules), [Ghalla](https://ghalla.ma/product/%D8%A8%D8%B1%D9%83%D9%88%D9%83%D8%B4-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D9%85%D8%AD-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B5%D9%84%D8%A8-barkoukch-ble-dur/), and [Khir Yemma](https://khiryemma.ma/faq/). They support a large-grain Moroccan cereal product and seller-specific wheat variants, not one shared specification.
- The [Québec Food Directory's large pearl-couscous record](https://www.repertoirealimentsquebecois.gouv.qc.ca/Produit/Index/8215655) names Israeli, Jerusalem, Mograbieh, and ptitim for one Québec-market SKU; it does not name Barkoukch. OQLF's [couscous entry](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/26502225/couscous) defines generic couscous, not Barkoukch.
- The proposal audit counted 1,367 active references, 100 forms, 64 legacy candidates, 1,699 research-ledger records, 114 manifest-listed recipes, and 1,123 ingredient rows. No exact Barkoukch owner or bundled recipe demand was found. Pearl couscous remains the exact owner of `pearl couscous / couscous perlé`; broad `couscous` currently returns six rows.
- The checklist, roadmap, and progress log now record this hold. No catalog or ledger data changed.

The full source register, ownership and picker projection, corpus audit, and numeric limits are in the [R40 proposal](../../proposals/wp03-section-f-barkoukch-product-boundary-hold-20261013-r40.md). No tests were run because this integration changes documentation only.
