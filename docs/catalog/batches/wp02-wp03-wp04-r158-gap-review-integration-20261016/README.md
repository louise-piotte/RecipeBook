# WP02/WP03/WP04 R158 gap-review integration

Integrated 2026-10-16 after root review of the proposals, current sources, catalog ownership, picker behavior, and bundled-recipe impact.

## Decisions

- **WP02 A6 — `jowari flour`: hold the alias.** Canadian sorghum-flour product pages describe sorghum as also known as `jowari`, and FSSAI material supports Jwari as a regional sorghum name. The Canadian pages do not title the product `Jowari Flour`. More importantly, adding that phrase to `ingredient-ref-sorghum-flour` would make a bare `jowari` query return only flour while the grain-sorghum row has no such alias. Keep the current displays, aliases, forms, measurement observations, and blocked substitution research unchanged. Append the sources and bounded unresolved decision to the existing sorghum-flour ledger record.
- **WP03 Section F — puffed teff: retain the naming hold.** Current sources establish whole-kernel puffed teff as a food product and attest English names. The French phrase `teff soufflé` was found on a Swiss supplier page, while OQLF, CFIA, and a Canadian French-facing raw-teff listing do not establish a Québec French name for the puffed product. Keep the existing `candidate-puffed-teff` unallocated, add six current source records and a new unresolved decision, and mark measurements and substitutions as researched with no reusable value or supported substitute.
- **WP04 Section P — smoked trout: add an identity-only reference.** Add `smoked trout / truite fumée` as `ingredient-ref-smoked-trout` in `PROTEIN`. Keep it separate from raw trout species and composite trout spread or pastry products. Québec listings show cold- and hot-smoked products, sliced product, fillets, and varying package sizes; keep those details product-specific. Add no aliases, forms, measures, density, unit mappings, conversions, yields, recipe links, or substitutions.

## Root source review

- For **jowari flour**, root checked Natural Focus's [Sorghum Flour](https://naturalfocus.ca/products/sorghum-flour) page and Westpoint Naturals' [Organic Sorghum Flour](https://westpointnaturals.shop/products/organic-sorghum-flour) page. Both name a sorghum-flour product and use `jowari` as a sorghum synonym in product description text. The current [FSSAI cereal standard](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_4_Cereals_and_Cereal_products.pdf) pairs Jowar Flour with Sorghum Flour. Québec wording remains `sorgho` / `farine de sorgho` in [OQLF](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/26506486/sorgho) and current local product records; none of these sources establishes a Québec Jowari product label.
- For **puffed teff**, root checked [Baktotaal's Teff Pops](https://www.baktotaal.com/teff-pops-140-grams), [Tradin Organic's teff portfolio](https://www.tradinorganic.com/organic-products/all/organic-teff), and KENTAUR's [Céréales soufflées](https://www.kentaur.ch/fr/produits/cereales-soufflees) page. These distinguish puffed kernels from raw grain and flour, but KENTAUR is Swiss. The [OQLF tef entry](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/8480763/tef) concerns the base cereal, and [CFIA cereal guidance](https://inspection.canada.ca/fr/etiquetage-aliment/etiquetage/industrie/cereales-produits-boulangerie) uses a general prepared-cereal category without a teff-specific Québec label. [Yupik's French-facing raw-grain page](https://yupik.com/fr/teff-seeds-p20075) supports `Graines de teff` for the raw grain only.
- For **smoked trout**, root checked OQLF's entries for [truite arc-en-ciel](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/1198854/truite-arc-en-ciel) and [truite steelhead](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/8409333/truite-steelhead), plus current Québec producer and retail pages from [Fumoir Beira Nova](https://www.beiranovafoods.ca/produit/truite-fumee-a-lerable-et-aux-poivres/), [Ferme Piscicole des Bobines](https://www.lesbobines.com/fr/produits/), [Aliments du Québec](https://alimentsduquebec.com/en/certified-products/fish-and-seafood/smoked-and-sliced-trout-refresh), and Montréal's [Poissonnerie La Mer](https://www.lamer.ca/en/products/truite-fumee-maison). The pages directly support the bilingual market wording while showing different species details and product states. OQLF does not standardize `truite fumée` as a taxonomic or process claim. The [CFIA Fish List overview](https://inspection.canada.ca/en/food-labels/labelling/industry/fish/list) is used only for its stated common-name and taxonomy scope.
