# WP04 Section P — preserved-fish product-state boundary review

**Date:** 2026-10-12  
**Disposition:** Reviewed and held as a product-boundary decision; the broader Section P checklist remains open.

## Decision

Keep species or named product, cut, preservation condition, packing medium, and readiness as separate
descriptors. Do not create one generic `preserved fish` identity or universal canned, smoked, salted,
dried, oil-packed, water-packed, or fermented forms on broad fish references. Retain the already
reviewed salt-cod, stockfish, dried-bonito, dried-shellfish, fish-powder, fish-sauce, anchovy, and
regional fermented-condiment boundaries.

The audit covered all 114 bundled recipes and 1,123 ingredient rows. It found no exact request for a
preserved fish or seafood product; the fish-sauce lines already link to the distinct condiment. The
review changed no catalog references, aliases, forms, ledger rows, recipe links, measures, conversions,
yields, or substitutions.

CFIA's current guidance treats fish cut/form, packing medium, and condition as distinct true-nature
descriptors. Its compositional standards separately define prepared and preserved fish. OQLF gives
`saumon fumé` for `smoked salmon` and identifies species-qualified `germon` terminology, while Québec-
facing product pages distinguish tuna cut, packing medium, and no-drain presentation. These sources
support product-specific labeling boundaries; they do not establish generic catalog forms or kitchen
conversions.
