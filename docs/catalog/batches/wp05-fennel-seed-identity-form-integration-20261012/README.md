## Evidence and boundary

OQLF directly pairs the preferred Québec French term `graine de fenouil` with English `fennel seed` and describes its culinary uses. The Canadian Food Compositional Standards define fennel or fennel seed, whole or ground, as the dried ripe fruit of *Foeniculum vulgare* Mill. [OQLF, graine de fenouil](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/2095228/graine-de-fenouil) · [CFIA, Canadian Food Compositional Standards, §6.1.18](https://inspection.canada.ca/en/about-cfia/acts-and-regulations/list-acts-and-regulations/documents-incorporated-reference/canadian-food-compositional-standards-0)

Québec product listings use `Fenouil en graine` and `graines de fenouil moulues`; the latter supports the French ground-form match term. Food Basics uses the English plural product title `Fennel Seeds`. Package weights and seller descriptions are not generalized into measures, density, or substitution.
