# WP02 A7 R415 / WP04 Section P R416 / WP06 Europe R417 integration

Integrated on 2026-10-17 after root review of [R415](../../proposals/wp02-section-a7-next-milling-gap-review-r415-20261017.md), [R416](../../proposals/wp04-sectionp-next-seafood-gap-review-r416-20261017.md), and [R417](../../proposals/wp06-europe-next-regional-identity-review-r417-20261017.md).

## Decisions

- **R415 — buckwheat grits:** add identity-only `buckwheat grits / gruau de sarrasin` as `ingredient-ref-buckwheat-grits`, category `GRAIN_AND_CEREAL`, with empty aliases. Canadian manufacturer ingredient declarations pair the English and French names; TERMIUM and Statistics Canada's NAPCS examples support the named milling product. Québec producer evidence distinguishes cracked grain from whole groats but does not define a universal particle size or hull state. Keep raw and roasted groats, flour, hot cereal, kasha, buckwheat meal, and buckwheat semolina separate. The broad French picker query `gruau de sarrasin` now returns both the raw-groat and grits references; `gruau de sarrasin cru` remains specific to raw groats. No reusable measure, form, recipe link, conversion, yield, or substitution is supported. [Oakrun English](https://oakrun.com/english-muffins/9-grains-english-muffins/), [Oakrun French](https://oakrun.com/muffins-anglais/muffins-anglais-aux-9-graines/?lang=fr), [TERMIUM Plus](https://www.btb.termiumplus.gc.ca/tpv2alpha/alpha-eng.html?i=&index=enw&lang=eng&srchtxt=GRITS), [Statistics Canada NAPCS](https://www23.statcan.gc.ca/imdb/p3VD.pl?CLV=3&CPV=1821294&CST=17022022&CVD=1386659&Function=getAllExample&MLV=4&TVD=1384502&V=447178&VST=17022022), [Aliments Trigone product](https://alimentstrigone.com/products/gruau-de-sarrasin-biologique-1-kg), [product-family notes](https://alimentstrigone.com/pages/sarrasin-noir-biologique).
- **R416 — herring roe:** hold `herring roe / œufs de hareng` unallocated as a reference, alias, form, legacy mapping, and ledger identity. OQLF prefers generic `rogue` for the commercializable female gonad and says `rave` is regional, while TERMIUM and Québec sources attest herring-specific terms without defining a common preparation state. A current Canadian processor labels its herring-roe form `Salted Roe (Kazunoko)`; a Maritime fishmonger lists `rave` products without a product specification. This overlaps the existing kazunoko hold, and the recipe bundle has no matching demand. Preserve the existing roe, milt, roe-on-kelp, Nenu Dieune, and sujiko boundaries. [OQLF GDT](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/1199042/rogue), [TERMIUM Plus](https://www.btb.termiumplus.gc.ca/tpv2alpha/alpha-eng.html?i=&index=frb&lang=eng&srchtxt=HARENG), [Gouvernement du Québec](https://www.quebec.ca/agriculture-environnement-et-ressources-naturelles/peches-aquaculture-commerciales/portraits-peche-aquaculture-commerciales/transformation-produits-marins), [North Delta Seafoods](https://www.ndseafoods.com/pacific-herring), [Poissonnerie du Creek](https://www.poissonnerieducreek.com/nos-produits).
- **R417 — Roquefort:** add identity-only `Roquefort / roquefort` as `ingredient-ref-roquefort`, category `CHEESE`, with empty aliases. OQLF supplies the Québec French and English names; the current 2026 EU PDO document defines the protected blue-veined cheese made exclusively from raw, whole sheep's milk. Keep the broad `blue cheese / bleu` row and other named blue cheeses separate. Add no forms, measurements, conversions, yields, recipe links, or substitutions; the specification's wheel dimensions and weight are product requirements, not recipe measures. Reuse the existing generic blue-cheese boundary record without duplicating its OQLF source. [OQLF GDT](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/26500662/roquefort), [European Commission PDO document](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:52026XC02834), [INAO](https://www.inao.gouv.fr/produit/roquefort-24302).

## Catalog and picker effects

- Active ingredient references: **1,719**, up from 1,717. Ingredient forms remain at **141**.
- The research ledger has **2,078 records**, **9,411 source entries**, **7,973 distinct source IDs**, and **6,691 distinct source URLs**. R415 adds six new source IDs plus one reused Statistics Canada source entry; R417 adds four new sources. R416 adds no ledger record.
- `buckwheat grits` and `buckwheat grit` resolve to `ingredient-ref-buckwheat-grits`. `gruau de sarrasin` returns raw buckwheat groats plus grits; the qualified `gruau de sarrasin cru` query remains on raw groats. Broad `grits` increases from 5 to 6, `gruau` from 10 to 11, `buckwheat` from 13 to 14, and `sarrasin` from 14 to 15.
- `Roquefort` and `roquefort` resolve to `ingredient-ref-roquefort`; `blue cheese` and `bleu` keep their existing results. Herring-roe queries remain unallocated and the existing picker rows stay unchanged.
- The manifest bundle remains **114 recipes / 1,123 ingredient rows**. The three reviews found no matching recipe rows or links.
