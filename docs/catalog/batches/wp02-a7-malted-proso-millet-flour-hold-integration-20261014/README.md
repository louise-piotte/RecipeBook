# WP02 A7 — malted-proso-flour boundary hold (R107)

Reviewed and integrated on 2026-10-14. A7 remains open.

## Decision

Keep malted proso millet flour unallocated. The reviewed studies establish experimental research uses for malted flour from *Panicum miliaceum*, but the Canadian seller pages reviewed name plain proso millet flour, and no reviewed Québec source names a malted-proso flour product in French. The hold is limited to the sources reviewed; it does not claim that no product exists elsewhere.

Keep the candidate distinct from common/proso millet grain, plain proso millet flour, generic millet flour, generic malt flour, and other species-specific malted millet flours. Do not create a French display by translating the English research term. OQLF’s `farine maltée` means flour with malt extract or ground malt added, which does not establish flour milled from previously malted proso grain.

No active reference, alias, legacy identity, ledger candidate, form, recipe link, measure, density, mapping, conversion, yield, or substitution was added. The reported 0.79 g/mL value belongs to intact proso grain, not flour; the study percentages belong to their tested *burfi*, bread, and cookie formulas.

## Picker and recipe boundary

The phrase-first picker has no result for `malted proso millet flour`, `malted proso`, or the exploratory French query `farine de millet commun maltée`. Current `proso millet flour` and `farine de millet commun` queries select the plain-flour row; `proso millet` and `millet commun` return both common-millet grain and proso flour. Broad `millet flour` and `farine de millet` remain multi-result sets.

The full manifest-listed bundle has 114 recipe files and 1,123 ingredient rows. The candidate terms have no recipe hits, and no recipe ingredient links to the common-millet grain or plain proso-flour references.

## Verification

The root review verified the primary research papers, OQLF terminology, the current Canadian plain-proso-flour listings, the grain-only bulk-density value, the ownership snapshot, the phrase-first picker projection, and the full recipe manifest. No shared reference, ledger, recipe, or generated index was changed. No app, Gradle, instrumentation, or emulator tests were run because this is a documentation-only hold.
