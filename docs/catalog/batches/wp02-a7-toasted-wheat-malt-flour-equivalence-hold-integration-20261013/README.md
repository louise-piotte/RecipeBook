# WP02 A7: Toasted wheat malt flour equivalence hold

Reviewed and held on 2026-10-13 from the [R58 equivalence review](../../proposals/wp02-a7-toasted-wheat-malt-flour-equivalence-boundary-review-20261013-round58.md).

## Disposition

Keep `toasted wheat malt flour / farine de blé malté toasté` unallocated. Do not add the toasted or IREKS `slightly roasted` wording as aliases to `ingredient-ref-roasted-wheat-malt-flour`, and do not create a new identity without a review that settles its product scope.

Current IREKS North America wording names `slightly roasted wheat malt flour / farine de blé malté légèrement grillée` for a Canadian-market product. Moulins Bourgeois lists `Toasted wheat malt flour / Farine de blé malté toasté` separately from `Farine de blé malté torréfié (noir) / Roasted wheat malt flour (black)`. The active Diversi ingredient declaration distinguishes ordinary and roasted wheat malt flour but does not name a roast grade or identify whether the malt or flour was roasted. These sources do not establish equivalence across the product labels.

Sources: [IREKS English product page](https://www.ireksnorthamerica.com/products/product-search/detail/ireks-aroma-wheat-malt-k), [IREKS French product search](https://www.ireksnorthamerica.com/fr/produits/recherche-de-produits), [Moulins Bourgeois 2024 French product catalogue](https://www.moulinsbourgeois.fr/pdf/Catalogue-produits-MoulinsBourgeois2024.pdf), and [OQLF, *farine maltée*](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/8432785/farine-maltee). The catalogue's distinct product labels are producer-specific and do not create a Québec terminology ruling.
