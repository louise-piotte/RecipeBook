# WP04 Section P R655 integration — White Sucker / meunier noir hold

Integrated on 2026-10-18 after root review of the [R655 proposal](../../proposals/wp04-section-p-next-regional-fish-gap-review-r655-20261018.md).

## Decision

Held White Sucker / meunier noir (*Catostomus commersonii*) outside active picker data. CFIA and OQLF support the species-level English/French name pair, and Québec's current nutrition page lists `Meunier noir, cru`. The hold is about current picker behavior: canonical `White Sucker` and `meunier noir` names would make broad `sucker` and `meunier` queries each return this one fish. The bundled corpus has no species-specific recipe demand to clarify those broad searches.

This hold does not claim that the fish lacks human-food evidence. Québec's sport-fish guide lists generic `Meunier`; a 2017–18 commercial-plan response includes the species but its asterisk indicates environmental characterization observation, not a species-specific catch or sale; and a 2009 commercialization report documents historical wholesale interest and a low-demand niche. None establishes current retail availability. DFO's 2016 profile says the species is not usually fished except as bait.

No catalog reference, legacy candidate, research-ledger row, alias, form, measure, recipe link, conversion, yield, or substitution was added. Reopen if picker behavior can preserve broad `sucker` / `meunier` scope or the bundled corpus gains a species-specific recipe.

## Sources and checks

- [CFIA Fish List results](https://active.inspection.gc.ca/scripts/fssa/fispoi/fplist/fpresults.asp) pairs TSN 553273 with White Sucker, Common Sucker / Meunier Noir, and Mullet, and uses sucker-family labels for other taxa.
- [OQLF GDT — meunier noir](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/1199912/meunier-noir) provides the preferred Québec fish-product term, English white sucker, Latin *C. commersonii*, and the edible-fish domain.
- [Gouvernement du Québec — fish consumption and health](https://www.quebec.ca/sante/alimentation/saines-habitudes-alimentaires/consommation-de-poisson-et-sante/recommandations) currently lists `Meunier noir, cru` in its omega-3 table and separately lists generic `Meunier` in sport-fishing consumption guidance.
- [Gouvernement du Québec — freshwater sport-fish guide](https://www.environnement.gouv.qc.ca/eau/guide/presentation.htm) lists generic `Meunier` in the four-meal-per-month general advice when no site-specific recommendation is available.
- [Fisheries and Oceans Canada — Meunier noir](https://www.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/species-especes/profiles-profils/white-sucker-meunier-noir-fra.html) gives the species profile and says it is not usually fished except as bait; it was last modified in 2016.
- [Québec environmental assessment response, July 2019](https://www.ree.environnement.gouv.qc.ca/dossiers/3211-14-040/3211-14-040-12.pdf) lists the species among fishery-plan species for the 2017–18 PLIO sector; its asterisk means observed during project-area characterization.
- [UQAC — Commercialisation du meunier noir (2009)](https://sdeir.uqac.ca/id/eprint/578/) records a human-consumption market evaluation and limited wholesaler interest, not current retail supply.
- Targeted Canadian and Québec retail searches found no verifiable current human-food SKU. The current picker and all 114 manifest-listed recipes / 1,123 ingredient rows were checked; no candidate-specific recipe row was found.

No software tests, build, emulator run, or deployment were needed for this documentation-only hold. Section P remains open.
