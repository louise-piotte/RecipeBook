# WP02 Section 04 B3 R131 — ginger-rhizome starch product hold

## Decision

Held starch isolated from ginger rhizome outside the active ingredient catalog and research ledger. Food-science sources establish experimental or process-derived ginger starch, but reviewed evidence does not establish a current Canadian or Québec standalone human-food product, a stable bilingual package name, or a validated Québec French product term. OQLF supports the component words `gingembre` and general `amidon`; that does not attest `amidon de gingembre` or `fécule de gingembre` as a product name.

No active reference, alias, form, candidate record, measure, recipe link, or substitution was added. Fresh/ground/candied ginger, ginger-garlic paste, and ordinary or modified starch identities remain unchanged. No bundled recipe demand was found. This documentation-only hold leaves WP02 B3 open.

Sources and limits: [CSIR–CFTRI annual report, p. 82](https://www.cftri.res.in/PDF/PERREPORT2014-15.pdf), [OQLF ginger](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/2095308/gingembre), [OQLF starch](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/8869736/amidon), [Madeneni et al.](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/star.201100011), [Pan et al.](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0308814625040841), and [Ogbete et al.](https://jfines.org/index.php/jfines/article/view/139). The proposal records the source-specific extraction and research limits, plus Canadian product examples that do not establish ginger-rhizome starch as a standalone product.

## Validation

No tests were run because no database JSON changed. The shared R131 package validation, normalization, reference, generated-index, recipe-corpus, UTF-8/no-BOM, local-link, whitespace, and scoped `git diff --check` checks passed.
