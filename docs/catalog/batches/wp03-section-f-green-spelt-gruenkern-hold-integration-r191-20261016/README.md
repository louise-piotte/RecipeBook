# WP03 Section F Grünkern / green-spelt hold integration (R191)

## Decision

Integrated a candidate-specific hold in the regional preprocessed-grains and Section F identity/terminology checklists. Grünkern / green spelt remains outside active ingredient references and the research ledger until a standalone Canadian or Québec product and a Québec-supported French product name can be verified.

The evidence establishes a distinct processed-spelt product, but not a local consumer product scope and title. The European Commission specification defines protected Fränkischer Grünkern as unripe-harvested spelt dried at the start of dough ripeness, with traditional beechwood drying. Davert currently sells a German 500 g Grünkern SKU described as half-ripe spelt dried over wood fire, usable whole or cracked. OQLF supports the base cereal term *épeautre* but does not name this processed product. Québec-grown and Canadian whole-spelt listings reviewed describe ordinary spelt grain, not the Grünkern harvest and drying process. The Swiss `épeautre vert` wording does not establish Québec usage.

The recipe corpus scan found no matching demand across 114 manifest-listed recipes and 1,123 ingredient rows. Keep Grünkern separate from ordinary spelt berries, freekeh, and farro. Add no alias, form, measure, conversion, yield, recipe link, or substitution while held.
