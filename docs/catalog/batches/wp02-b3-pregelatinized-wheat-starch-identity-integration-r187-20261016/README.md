# WP02 Section 04 B3 pregelatinized wheat starch identity (R187)

Added identity-only `pregelatinized wheat starch / amidon de blé prégélatinisé` as a
`FLOUR_AND_STARCH` reference. This is a source-qualified wheat-starch product class, distinct from
ordinary wheat starch, modified wheat starch, resistant wheat starch, pregelatinized rice starch,
and pregelatinized wheat flour. Retained only the English spelling variants `pre-gelatinized wheat
starch`, `pregelatinised wheat starch`, and ADM's product-category wording `pregel wheat starch`.
French aliases remain empty because the full display appears in a Québec ingredient declaration.

ADM's 2024 and 2023 product sheets separately list native wheat starch and pregel wheat starch, and
identify Paygel grades under the latter. ADM also lists its pregelatinized wheat flour separately.
Metro Québec's ingredient declaration names both `amidon de blé prégélatinisé` and ordinary `amidon
de blé`; OQLF supports `amidon prégélatinisé` and the English spelling variants. These sources
establish the product-class distinction and wording. The product descriptions do not supply a
reusable household measure, density, replacement ratio, conversion, or yield. The 114 manifest
recipes / 1,123 ingredient rows have no matching wheat-starch demand.

The ledger records the identity as verified and leaves measurement and substitution research in
`researched_no_supported_value` and `researched_no_supported_substitute`. It contains the source
register and accepted decision; it adds no numeric observations or findings. The Canadian Trade Index
entry is secondary supplier context only; its page was available in search indexing but direct
retrieval failed during root review. It does not establish a current Paygel SKU at that Québec
location.

2026-10-16. Static seed-package normalization/validation, reference normalization, ledger-schema
