# WP03 Section F R315 — whole-farro-flour boundary hold

Reviewed and integrated on 2026-10-16. Root checked current True Grain, Arva Flour Mills (English and French), Mayrand, and OQLF pages before accepting the proposal's boundary recommendation.

Keep the proposed species-neutral `whole farro flour` grade outside active references and the research ledger. The existing R148 `farro flour / farine de farro` row remains the species-neutral market class. Current Canadian whole-flour products checked here are identified as emmer; the Québec whole-flour listing is identified as spelt. Those source-specific names do not establish a common cross-species whole-farro grade or a Québec French display such as `farine de farro entière`.

Do not add a reference, alias, ledger candidate, form, measurement, conversion, yield, recipe link, or substitution. True Grain and Arva's product-specific whole-emmer baking advice and moisture guidance remain attached to their named products; Mayrand's 2 kg package size is not a measure. Picker projections and the 114-recipe / 1,123-row scan show no matching demand and no need to change active results. Keep the Section F farro-species and flour boundaries open for new evidence.

No app or database JSON changed in this hold decision. No software tests, Gradle builds, emulator checks, or deployment were run.
