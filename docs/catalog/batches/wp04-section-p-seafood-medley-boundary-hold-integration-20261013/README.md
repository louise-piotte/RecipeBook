# WP04 Section P frozen seafood medley picker hold — 2026-10-13

Integrated the [round-67 seafood-medley review](../../proposals/wp04-section-p-seafood-mix-quebec-label-boundary-review-20261013-round67.md).

## Decision

Keep the Anchor's Bay frozen seafood medley and a generic seafood-mix identity outside the active
catalog and research ledger. Metro's 360 g listing names squid, cooked mussel meat, shrimp, and
octopus, but does not establish species, proportions, the package display panel, or a stable recipe
blend. It was out of stock when reviewed, and the bundled recipes do not request this product.

CFIA reserves French `fruits de mer` for a defined all-invertebrate mix and says a mix including
cephalopods could use broader `produits de la mer` or `produits de la pêche`. OQLF also distinguishes
the narrower French term from English `seafood`. The retailer's French web title does not establish
package-label wording or a compliance finding. Any catalog term would make a broad category search
point to one mix. The package weight is not a measure or yield, and no substitution is supported.

No catalog or research-ledger data changed. The proposal records the picker projection, label limits,
and conditions for revisiting the product if a recipe names it.

## Checks

- Independent CFIA and OQLF source review confirmed the French seafood-mix wording boundary.
- Read-only exact ownership and recipe audits found no current allocation or product-specific recipe demand.
- No app tests or emulator checks were run; this is a documentation-only hold.

Section P remains open.
