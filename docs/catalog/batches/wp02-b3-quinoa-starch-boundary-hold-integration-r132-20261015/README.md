# WP02 Section 04 B3 R132 — quinoa-starch product-boundary hold

## Decision

Held quinoa starch outside the active ingredient catalog and research ledger. Primary studies establish isolated quinoa starch as a distinct research material, and current international supplier pages use quinoa-starch wording. The reviewed supplier materials do not establish one consistent product scope or a Canadian/Québec pantry ingredient with product-specific local wording. OQLF supports the component terms `quinoa` and general `amidon`, but does not attest `amidon de quinoa` as a product name; keep `fécule de quinoa` out because the OQLF note scopes it more specifically to tuber or underground-plant starch.

No active reference, ledger candidate, alias, form, measure, density, unit mapping, recipe link, conversion, yield, or substitution was added. Existing quinoa grain, flour, flakes, and puffed quinoa identities remain unchanged. The bundled corpus has no direct quinoa-starch demand. This documentation-only hold leaves WP02 Section 04 B3 open.

The proposal records product and French-name boundaries, picker effects, source-specific numerical claims, the recipe scan, and reconsideration criteria. Proposal-local source IDs are not ledger records and are not reserved.

## Validation

The proposal and its primary-source links were reviewed. No tests were run because no database JSON changed. No shared catalog or ledger data changed.
