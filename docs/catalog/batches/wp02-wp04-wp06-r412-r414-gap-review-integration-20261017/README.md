# WP02 A7 R412 / WP04 Section P R413 / WP06 Europe R414 integration

Integrated on 2026-10-17 after root review of [R412](../../proposals/wp02-section-a7-next-milling-gap-review-r412-20261017.md), [R413](../../proposals/wp04-section-p-next-seafood-gap-review-r413-20261017.md), and [R414](../../proposals/wp06-europe-next-regional-identity-review-r414-20261017.md).

## Decisions

- **R412 — barley grits:** add identity-only `barley grits / gruau d'orge` as `ingredient-ref-barley-grits`, category `GRAIN_AND_CEREAL`, with empty aliases. Québec product pages for the same Bon Matin item pair the English ingredient `barley grits` with French `gruau d'orge`; a second bilingual Ontario product specification pairs those names in a cracker ingredient declaration. [Metro English](https://www.metro.ca/en/online-grocery/aisles/bread-bakery-products/packaged-bread/whole-wheat-grain/multigrain-bread/p/061077940095), [Metro French](https://www.metro.ca/epicerie-en-ligne/allees/pains-et-patisseries/pains-emballes/ble-entier-et-cereales/pain-tranche-multigrain/p/061077940095), [Summer Fresh specification](https://syscoitems.ca/specifications/DIPSF71957_7264954x2.pdf). Keep barley meal, barley semolina, barley couscous, cracked barley, whole barley, and cream of barley cereal distinct. Product descriptions do not establish one universal grain-retention state, grind size, measure, or cooking behavior. Shiloh's page itself gives conflicting 46 g and 48 g whole-grain-per-serving figures; neither is a reusable kitchen measure. [Shiloh Farms](https://shilohfarms.com/barley-grits-organic/)
- **R413 — sujiko:** hold unallocated as a reference, alias, and form. MAFF distinguishes roe in the sac from separated ikura, while its sources describe both raw recipe input and salted finished product as sujiko. No current Québec French label or bundled recipe selects the intended product state. Keep the existing salmon-roe and ikura decisions unchanged; add no catalog or ledger entry. [MAFF salted-food classification](https://www.maff.go.jp/e/policies/market/dento_syoku/bunrui/siozuke.html), [MAFF Hokkaido recipe](https://www.maff.go.jp/e/policies/market/k_ryouri/search_menu/4769/index.html), [E&E Foods product description](https://www.eefoods.com/page/salmon-roe).
- **R414 — Tête de Moine AOP:** add identity-only `Tête de Moine AOP / PDO Tête de Moine cheese` as `ingredient-ref-tete-de-moine`, category `CHEESE`, with French alias `Fromage de Bellelay` and no English aliases. Use the current [Swiss Federal Office of Agriculture specification](https://www.blw.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/EoJ3kVwEIIXB/T%C3%AAte%20de%20Moine%20%C3%84nderung%20PH%202026%20f.pdf) for the protected identity boundary. Québec retailer Mayrand uses matching French and English titles for item 26390 and lists an 880 g package, but its 60-day aging description conflicts with the specification's minimum 75 days; treat the package and aging detail as SKU-specific and do not infer AOP compliance. [Mayrand French](https://mayrand.ca/fr/nos-produits/produits-laitiers/fromage/fromage-de-specialite/fromage-tete-de-moine-switzerland-26390), [Mayrand English](https://mayrand.ca/en/our-products/cheese-and-dairy/cheese/swiss-comte-gruyere-cheese/fromage-tete-de-moine-switzerland-26390). The BLW specification gives a 0.7–2 kg wheel range and a separate 3 kg protected-form maximum; the [Swiss AOP-IGP association](https://www.aop-igp.ch/fr/tete-de-moine-aop/page) says 700–900 g, while [Agroscope](https://www.agroscope.admin.ch/fr/tete-de-moine-aop-fr) says 0.7–2.5 kg. None is a recipe measure. Record one external [four-person fondue recipe](https://www.fromagesdesuisse.fr/inspiration/recettes/detail/fondue-a-la-tete-de-moine-aop) as a `portion_weight` observation of 300 g, retaining its 500 g Gruyère co-use and serving basis; do not derive an individual serving amount, conversion, package yield, or substitution.
