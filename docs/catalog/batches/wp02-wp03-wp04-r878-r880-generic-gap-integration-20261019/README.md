## R878 — garlic chive flowers

Added identity-only `garlic chive flowers / fleurs de ciboulette chinoise` for detached edible florets of correctly identified, food-grown *Allium tuberosum*. The OQLF source supports the plant name and culinary flower use; Colorado State Extension supports the edible-floret boundary. North Carolina State Extension's nonspecific toxicity note is recorded only as a source limitation, with no risk level or safe amount inferred.

Kept whole Chinese/garlic chives, common-chive blossoms (*Allium schoenoprasum*), flower stalks, scapes, and other *Allium* species separate. The French flower-part phrase is descriptive, composed from the OQLF plant name; it is not claimed as an independently standardized OQLF term. No aliases, forms, density, mappings, measurements, recipe links, conversions, yields, substitutions, or safety conclusions were added. The 114-recipe / 1,123-row corpus has no exact candidate phrase.

## R879 — rockfish / sébaste name group

Added identity-only `rockfish (name used for multiple fish species) / sébaste (nom utilisé pour plusieurs espèces de poissons)` as a visibly qualified `PROTEIN` name group. CFIA's Fish List pairs the broad term with *Sebastes* spp. and applies Rockfish wording to multiple named taxa. The qualifier describes the scope of a market name, not a single species or culinary equivalence.

Kept Québec redfish, named rockfish species, other redfish, snapper, rosefish, and ocean-perch identities separate. The French parenthetical is catalog-authored search scope and is not represented as an OQLF-approved consumer label. No aliases, forms, measurements, recipe links, conversions, yields, or substitutions were added. The recipe corpus has no candidate-term matches.

## R880 — sorghum-bran French display

Completed the planned Québec French display review for existing generic `sorghum bran / son de sorgho`. Retained `son de sorgho` as a transparent but provisional display: the reviewed evidence supports the sorghum-bran relation and the French base noun `son`, but not a Québec-preferred or local human-food label. Added no broad `son` alias and changed no catalog or ledger data. Preserve the existing sorghum bran entry and its source-scoped evidence.
