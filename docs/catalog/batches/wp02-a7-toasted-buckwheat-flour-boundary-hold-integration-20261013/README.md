# WP02 A7 toasted-buckwheat-flour boundary hold

## Decision

Keep `toasted buckwheat flour` unallocated. Eurogerm's bakery catalogue and Flanquart's French and
English supplier pages establish manufacturer use of the phrase, but the reviewed material does not
establish a current Québec product, a Québec French display, or an unambiguous single-ingredient
consumer flour. Castle Ingredients also lists a toasted-kasha flour product, but its B2B brochure does
not establish a Québec retail identity or make that name a general synonym. OQLF supports `sarrasin`
for the base ingredient; it does not name a toasted-flour product.

The recipe bundle has no matching ingredient rows. Keep the candidate distinct from plain and sprouted
buckwheat flour and from roasted buckwheat groats. Add no active identity, alias, form, measure,
density, unit mapping, recipe link, conversion, yield, substitution, legacy identity, or ledger
candidate. Reopen when a Québec-facing food product and its ingredient/process boundary are documented.

## Sources

- Eurogerm, [product catalogue](https://eurogerm-kb.com/images/Eurogerm-product-catalog.pdf): lists `Toasted buckwheat flour` as a B2B bakery product, without an ingredient statement or Québec display.
- Flanquart, [English buckwheat-flour page](https://www.flanquart.fr/en/range/flour-range/buckwheat-flour/) and [French page](https://www.flanquart.fr/notre-gamme/gamme-farines/farine-de-sarrasin/): describe natural and toasted flour plus thermal treatments for the food industry; the pages do not identify a dedicated consumer SKU or Québec label.
- Castle Ingredients, [product-range brochure](https://www.castle-group.eu/web/content/209206?unique=7087bc05a4657ee9c96d76ed4d1fe65acb7b7768): lists `Toasted Kasha flour (Toasted buckwheat flour)` as a product, but supplies no Québec-facing display or ingredient statement.
- OQLF, [*sarrasin* (GDT)](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/8889520/sarrasin), supports the base French ingredient name. OQLF, [*kacha* (GDT)](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/26506505/kacha), defines a prepared buckwheat gruel, not flour.

The exact ownership, picker, corpus, measurement, and substitution review is recorded in the [R46 proposal](../../proposals/wp02-a7-toasted-buckwheat-flour-boundary-review-20261013-round46.md).
