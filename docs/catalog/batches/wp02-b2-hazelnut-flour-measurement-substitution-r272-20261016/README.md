# WP02 B2 R272 integration: hazelnut-flour measures and substitution evidence

## Ledger update

Updated the existing ingredient-ref-hazelnut-flour research record. Added King Arthur Baking's 1 cup = 89 g chart portion and Bob's Red Mill's 2 tablespoon (14 g) product serving as separate source-specific observations. Neither source specifies a transferable fill method, so defaultDensity remains null and unitMappings remain empty.

Recorded Bob's manufacturer suggestion to replace 30% of wheat flour and Pycia and Juszczak's 5%, 10%, and 15% wheat-flour replacement experiments as bounded substitution evidence. The maker's amount basis is unstated, and the primary study measures rheology in dough made with a specific flour and test conditions. Neither supports a general finished-recipe rule; no active substitution or recipe link was added. The bundled corpus has no ordinary hazelnut-flour demand.

The ordinary hazelnut-flour identity, aliases, forms, and existing meal/defatted boundaries remain unchanged. The B2 parent checklist remains open.
