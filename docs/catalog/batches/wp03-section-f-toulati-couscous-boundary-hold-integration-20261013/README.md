# WP03 Section F — Toulati couscous boundary hold (round 39)

**Date:** 2026-10-13

**Decision:** Keep Southern Moroccan `Couscous Toulati / Toulati couscous` unallocated until a
producer specification names its three cereal flours and a Québec-facing French source supports the
display. Current Moroccan marketplace listings establish a named regional product and describe
three cereal flours, but do not identify the cereals or establish one formula across producers.
Keep it distinct from protected five-cereal Khoumassi and generic `couscous tri-céréales`.

## Evidence and limits

- [Terroir du Maroc's Couscous toulati – 900 g listing](https://terroirdumaroc.gov.ma/fr_ma/catalog/product/view/id/1287/s/couscous-toulati-900-g-cooperative-assahra-dahabya/) identifies the Laâyoune cooperative product, describes three cereal flours and hand-agglomeration with salted water, but does not name the cereals.
- [The cooperative profile](https://terroirdumaroc.gov.ma/fr_ma/marketplace/seller/profile/shop/CooprativeAssahraAddahabia) lists Toulati, Khoumassi, and bran couscous separately.
- [CoopMarketplace's Kheir Zemmour profile](https://coopmarketplace.ma/An/cooperative/kheir-zemmour/487) calls it Toulati couscous made with three selected grains, separately from Khoumassi. The [Kouss•Kouss culinary account](https://kousskouss.com/graines-mayalen-zubillaga/) places *toulati* among southern Moroccan multi-cereal couscous products without giving a common formula.
- The targeted search found no Québec/OQLF product name for Toulati. The Moroccan French product label alone does not establish the Québec-facing bilingual display.

## Catalog and recipe effects

No reference, alias, form, measure, mapping, recipe link, conversion, yield, substitution, or ledger
candidate was added. No exact Toulati owner was found in active references, forms, legacy identities,
or the research ledger. The existing generic couscous identity remains the general owner; the
Khoumassi and Baddaz rows remain separate regional identities.

The documented picker projection remains at zero results for `Toulati`, `Toulati couscous`, and
`couscous Toulati`; `couscous` remains at six results. The proposal records a scan of all 114
manifest-listed recipes and 1,123 ingredient rows with no Toulati-specific demand.
