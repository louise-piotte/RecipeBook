# WP04 Section P — gizzard-shad market and picker hold (R128)

Keep *Dorosoma cepedianum* outside active references, forms, and the shared research ledger until a
species-identified Canadian or Québec human-food product or bundled recipe supports activation.
OQLF supports `alose à gésier` for the taxon, but the entry is from 2012 and describes commercial
potential rather than a current sale. A current Toronto listing uses `Kohada – Gizzard Shad` without
a scientific name; identifying that SKU as *Konosirus punctatus* is an inference from its Kohada and
Konoshiro wording, so it establishes a naming collision, not the species in the product.

The Québec regulation prohibits sale of dead gizzard shad except through specified commercial-fishing,
fishing-pond, aquaculture, or imported commercial-source pathways. This is a qualified restriction,
not a complete sales ban. The recipe bundle has no matching demand. Because adding a row with the
unqualified display `gizzard shad` would make that phrase select *D. cepedianum* in the current picker,
keep the candidate unallocated and do not add aliases, forms, measures, mappings, recipe links,
conversions, yields, or substitutions.

Root reviewed the proposal's scoped source evidence and picker projection. Its 13 source IDs remain
proposal-local; no active catalog, form, legacy-candidate, or research-ledger JSON changed. The broad
Section P regional-fish checklist remains open. This documentation-only hold required no tests.
