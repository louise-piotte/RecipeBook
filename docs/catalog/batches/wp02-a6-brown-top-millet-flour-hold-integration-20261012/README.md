# WP02 A6 brown-top millet flour hold — 2026-10-12

## Decision

Keep brown-top millet flour (*Urochloa ramosa*) as an unallocated A6 candidate. Current manufacturer
pages establish a named flour product, and FSSAI identifies brown top (Korale) as *U. ramosa* in its
millet standard. The current evidence does not establish a Québec French display for this flour.

Do not add `ingredient-ref-brown-top-millet-flour`, alias the phrase to generic millet flour, or use
`farine de millet brun`: a current French retail product with that name declares *Panicum miliaceum*.
Keep `farine de millet à tête brune`, regional names, and activated/soaked/cold-milled wording
inactive. No catalog or ledger data, recipe links, measurements, forms, conversions, yields, or
substitutions changed. The complete 114-recipe corpus has no phrase hit.

## Evidence reviewed

- FSSAI, [Food Safety and Standards Regulations, Chapter 2.4: Cereals and Cereal Products](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_4_Cereals_and_Cereal_products.pdf), version 4 dated 2025-05-07, lists brown top (Korale) as *Urochloa ramosa* and defines a mixed millet flour class. It does not provide a specific single-grain brown-top flour name or Québec terminology.
- Dhatu Organics, [Activated Brown Top Millet Flour](https://www.dhatuorganics.com/products/activated-brown-top-millet-flour-500g), names *U. ramosa* and describes one maker-specific soaked, dried, cold-milled product. Its process and claims remain product-specific.
- B&B Organics, [Healthy Brown Top Millet Flour](https://bnborganics.com/products/brown-top-millet-flour), names brown-top flour and regional product wording but supplies no botanical name or Québec French label.
- OQLF, [millet (GDT)](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/26506455/millet), lists `millet`, `mil`, and `petit mil` as terms for a generic grouping; it does not identify brown-top millet.
- Naturalia, [Farine de millet brun](https://www.naturalia.fr/produit/farine-millet-brun-500g), declares *Panicum miliaceum* as the ingredient. It is used to document the species boundary, not as Québec terminology.
- Desi Chakki, Ontario, [Our Products](https://desichakki.com/products), includes Brown Top Millet in an Ontario-milled multigrain flour blend, not a standalone product or French label.

The six source IDs remain proposal-local in
[`wp02-a6-next-regional-flour-gap-sweep-20261012.md`](../../proposals/wp02-a6-next-regional-flour-gap-sweep-20261012.md);
no flour-specific ledger record was created. The existing held grain record remains separate.
