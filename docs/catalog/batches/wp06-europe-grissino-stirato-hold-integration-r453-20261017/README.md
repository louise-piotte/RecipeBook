# WP06 Europe R453 — Piedmontese grissino stirato hold

Recorded on 2026-10-17 after root review of the [R453 proposal](../../proposals/wp06-europe-next-regional-identity-review-r453-20261017.md).

## Decision

Keep Piedmontese `grissino stirato` outside active references and the evidence ledger for now. Regione Piemonte's official PAT page describes the regional stretched breadstick, its production territories, and its hand-stretching method. OQLF supports generic `gressin / breadstick`; Québec-facing `Grissini Torino/Torinesi` product titles do not establish Piedmontese origin or the traditional method.

Under current search, even the singular display `grissino stirato` would make broad `grissino` resolve to this lone regional subtype. Adding `grissini stirati` would also make broad `grissini` a singleton; omitting it still leaves the singular collision and loses the source-attested plural search phrase. The recipe corpus has no matching demand. Add no reference, alias, ledger record, form, measure, recipe link, conversion, yield, or substitution.

## Evidence reviewed

- Regione Piemonte, [PiemonteIS — Grissino Stirato](https://www.piemonteagri.it/qualita/it/prodotti/paste-e-dolci/306-grissino-stirato), identifies the PAT product, production territory, dough-stretching method, and a recipe-specific use in Supa barbëtta. This supports the regional product, not PDO/PGI status or a universal formula.
- OQLF, [gressin (GDT)](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/17042580/gressin), defines the generic product and translates it as `breadstick`; it does not give a Québec French term for the regional Piedmontese subtype.
- Montréal-area shelf titles include `Grissini Torino` and `Grissini Torinesi`, but the cited listings do not establish product origin or method.

The Europe checklist remains open; this is a documented hold, not a completed regional identity.
