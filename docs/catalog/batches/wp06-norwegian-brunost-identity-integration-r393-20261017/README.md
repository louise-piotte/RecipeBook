# WP06 Norwegian brunost identity integration — R393

## Decision

Added one identity-only `CHEESE` reference: `Norwegian brown cheese (brunost) / brunost (fromage brun norvégien)`. The French parenthetical is a provisional descriptive gloss; no OQLF or TERMIUM preferred term was found. Both alias arrays are empty.

TINE describes brunost as a family made by caramelizing whey with cow’s and/or goat’s milk and cream. Its examples include bread, waffles, brownies, and sauces. Visit Norway places Gudbrandsdalsost and Heidalsost in the Gudbrandsdalen regional context. A Montréal-based Canadian distributor lists Ski Queen in French as a Norwegian whey cheese and describes its product uses. These sources support the family identity and current Canadian naming context without making a single product’s formula representative of all brunost.

Kept `Gjetost` / `Geitost`, `Gudbrandsdalsost`, `Heidalsost`, and `Ski Queen` outside the global alias arrays as product-specific names. Whey, goat cheese, caramel sauce, dulce de leche, and sweetened condensed milk remain separate. Added no forms, measures, density, unit mappings, conversions, recipe links, yields, or substitutions. Measurement research found no reusable value; substitution research remains not yet researched. The complete bundled-recipe scan found no matching ingredient demand.

Seven sources and the accepted identity decision are recorded under `ingredient-ref-brunost` in the research ledger. Product formulations, product-specific package data, and one recipe’s 100 g amount were not generalized into reusable catalog behavior.
