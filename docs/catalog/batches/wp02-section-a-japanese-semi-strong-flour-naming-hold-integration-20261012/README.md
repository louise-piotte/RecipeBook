# WP02 Section A Japanese semi-strong flour naming hold

Keep 準強力粉 (*jun-kyōrikiko*), Japan's practical semi-strong wheat-flour class, outside the active catalog until a Québec French name or Canadian bilingual label establishes how to represent it. MAFF and the Japan Flour Milling Promotion Foundation distinguish it from strong, medium, and weak flour and describe its use for Chinese noodles, but neither provides a Québec/Canadian flour-grade crosswalk. The reviewed French terms concern strong flour or Canadian wheat classes and do not name this Japanese class.

Keep the class separate from Japanese strong, medium, and weak wheat flour and from Canadian high-gluten, bread, and all-purpose flour. Do not create an English gloss-only row, invent a French display, or add aliases, forms, measures, conversions, substitutions, or recipe links. This is a naming hold, not a denial that the Japanese flour class exists.

Documentation-only reconciliation; no tests or emulator checks were needed.
