# WP03 Section L jalapeño fresh form — R629 — 2026-10-18

## Accepted changes

Kept `ingredient-ref-jalapeno-pepper`, changed its French display to OQLF-preferred `piment jalapeno`, added French plural alias `jalapenos`, and added the fresh-market form `piment jalapeno frais / fresh jalapeno pepper`.

- [OQLF's GDT entry](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/8869222/piment-jalapeno) prefers the unaccented French spelling and discourages the Spanish tilde. It notes that pronunciation is the same in singular and plural. `jalapenos` is the regular French plural form.
- The [CFIA 2026 final report](https://publications.gc.ca/collections/collection_2026/acia-cfia/A104-701-2026-fra.pdf) studies whole fresh hot peppers sold at Canadian retail and lists 253 samples under `Piment jalapeno`; it supports the fresh-market identity but no reusable amount.
- Health Canada's [CNF raw jalapeño entry](https://aliments-nutrition.canada.ca/cnf-fce/serving-portion?id=4860&wbdisable=false) corroborates the raw name. Metro's [Québec produce listing](https://www.metro.ca/epicerie-en-ligne/allees/fruits-et-legumes/legumes/poivrons-zucchini-et-aubergines/piment-jalapeno/p/4693) and paired [French](https://www.metro.ca/recettes-et-occasions/recettes/salade-de-pates-du-sud-ouest) / [English](https://www.metro.ca/en/recipes-occasions/recipes/tex-mex-pasta-salad) recipe pages show fresh-market usage.

Chipotle, pickled/canned peppers, and ground jalapeño remain outside the fresh form. The single bundled recipe line remains unlinked because it does not specify fresh. Its existing USDA SR28 count observation remains recipe-specific and unchanged. No numeric behavior, package yield, conversion, recipe link, or substitution was added.
