# WP04 Section P stonecat food-market hold — 2026-10-13

## Decision

Keep stonecat / `barbotte des rapides` (*Noturus flavus*) outside active ingredient references and
the research ledger. OQLF supplies the Québec species name, English name, and taxon, but that
zoological entry is not a food-product source. The current Québec species list places *N. flavus*
among vulnerable species. A 2005 Québec report describes low sport and commercial interest and
sparse local records; this is historical context, not a current market assessment or a determination
of product provenance, harvest rules, or legal status.

The review found no current Canadian food-product specification, bundled recipe demand, transferable
kitchen quantity, or supported substitution. The 114 manifest-listed recipes contain 1,123 ingredient
rows and no stonecat match. Keep the species distinct from brown, yellow, and black bullheads and
channel catfish. Add no active reference, form, alias, measurement observation, ledger candidate,
recipe link, conversion, package yield, or substitution. Section P remains open.

## Sources

- [OQLF — *barbotte des rapides*](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/9488777/barbotte-des-rapides): gives the Québec zoological name, English `stonecat`, and *Noturus flavus*.
- [Government of Québec — list of threatened and vulnerable wildlife](https://www.quebec.ca/agriculture-environnement-et-ressources-naturelles/faune/gestion-faune-habitats-fauniques/especes-fauniques-menacees-vulnerables/liste): lists *Chat-fou des rapides* / *N. flavus* as vulnerable; the separate susceptible list is under review.
- [Government of Québec — 2005 report on *barbotte des rapides*](https://mffp.gouv.qc.ca/documents/faune/rapport-barbotte.pdf): describes low sport/commercial interest and limited Québec records. Its finding is historical.
- [Government of Canada — Québec Fishery Regulations, Schedule 3](https://laws.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-90-214/page-8.html): identifies Stonecat / Barbotte des rapides as *N. flavus* under the bullhead group. Its existing ledger source ID was reused in the proposal and no new ledger source was added.

The exact ownership, picker projection, recipe-demand, and numeric/substitution audit is in the
