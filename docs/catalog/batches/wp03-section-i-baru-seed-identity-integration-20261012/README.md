# WP03 Section I baru seed identity integration

Added identity-only **baru seeds / graines de baru** for the edible seed of *Dipteryx alata*. The row has no aliases, null density, and empty unit mappings. Keep `baru nut`, `baru almond`, `Brazilian almond`, `cumaru`, `cumbaru`, and tree-name wording out of aliases; keep fruit pulp, oil, meal/flour, and prepared products outside the whole-seed identity. No form, recipe link, measure, conversion, yield, or substitution was added.

Kew and Embrapa support the species and edible-seed boundary; a Québec-facing product page directly uses the English and French seed names. The retailer's roasted 150 g package is not a recipe measure, and its sensory comparisons do not support substitution. The audited recipe bundle has no baru demand.

Static bundled-package and generated-catalog checks passed. No app tests or emulator checks were run for this database-data addition under the JSON-only rule.
