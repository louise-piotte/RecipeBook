# WP02 A7 — malted Kodo millet flour boundary hold (R105)

Reviewed and integrated on 2026-10-14. A7 remains open.

## Decision

Keep malted Kodo millet flour unallocated. Do not add an active reference, alias, legacy or ledger candidate, form, recipe link, reusable measure, conversion, yield, or substitution.

The 2017 study malts Kodo and little-millet grains, then makes a 50:50 flour blend with milk powder, fruit powder, and sugar or jaggery. The 2024 study evaluates germinated Kodo flour in rusk formulations; it does not describe malted flour. Current Canadian-facing listings identify plain Kodo flour, not a standalone malted-Kodo flour. OQLF defines `farine maltée` as flour with malt extract or ground malt added, a different product boundary. These findings do not establish a stable Canadian product identity or a Québec French display.

Keep the existing Kodo grain, plain Kodo flour, and malt-flour identities unchanged. Keep the candidate distinct from composite malt mixes, germinated Kodo flour, and other cereal-specific malted flours. No source supports transferring the existing plain-Kodo-flour observations to a hypothetical malted product.

The 114-recipe / 1,123-row bundle has no Kodo or Varagu demand. No active references, forms, ledger records, recipes, or numeric data changed. No tests were run because this is documentation-only.
