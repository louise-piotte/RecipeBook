# WP04 Section P R308 — gray snapper picker-boundary hold

## Decision

Kept *Lutjanus griseus* / gray snapper outside active references and the research ledger. OQLF
supports the bilingual species name `gray snapper / vivaneau gris`, but the names necessarily match
broad `snapper` and `vivaneau` picker queries. CFIA's Fish List uses those broad labels for multiple
species. No matching demand appears in the 114-recipe / 1,123-row bundle.

The reviewed U.S. sources support the species/name relationship but do not establish Canadian label
use. A Vaughan supplier lists a source-specific mangrove snapper product as *L. griseus*; this does
not establish a Québec retail listing, a reusable form, or recipe demand. A Montréal wholesaler lists
broad `Vivaneau` and separate `Vivaneau américain` and `Vivaneau b-liner` labels without scientific
names. Keep the latter products unidentified.

No reference, alias, form, measure, recipe link, conversion, yield, or substitution was added. Keep
`mangrove snapper` and `lawyer snapper` outside aliases and do not confuse *L. griseus* with the CFIA
aquatic-animal-health `mangrove red snapper` name for *Lutjanus argentimaculatus*. Reconsider only
if picker behavior can preserve broad multi-species results or a model can represent the specific
taxon without capturing generic market terms.

## Sources

- OQLF, [vivaneau gris, Grand dictionnaire terminologique](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/1199104/vivaneau-gris): preferred Québec French name `vivaneau gris`, English `gray snapper`, taxon *L. griseus*, other English terms, and the note that `lutian gris` was not retained for Québec fish-product commercialization. The entry was updated in 2012 and does not establish current local stock.
- Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission, [Gray Snapper species account](https://myfwc.com/research/saltwater/codes/food-bait-finfish/gray-snapper/): gives *L. griseus* and common names Gray Snapper and Mangrove Snapper.
- Seafood Depot, [Mangrove Snapper](https://www.seafooddepot.ca/product-snappermangrove): names *L. griseus* and describes a source-specific whole, fresh, wild product of the U.S.A.; not a Québec retail listing.
- U.S. FDA, [Seafood List entry for *Lutjanus griseus*](https://hfpappexternal.fda.gov/scripts/fdcc/index.cfm?id=Lutjanus_griseus&set=SeafoodList): acceptable U.S. market name `Snapper`, common name `Gray Snapper`, and vernacular cross-references `Mangrove Snapper` and `Lawyer`.
- CFIA, [Susceptible species of aquatic animals](https://inspection.canada.ca/en/animal-health/aquatic-animals/diseases/susceptible-species): lists *L. argentimaculatus* as `mangrove red snapper` and says the names serve animal-health purposes only, not fish labelling.
- Can-Am Fruits et Légumes, Montréal, [fish and seafood products](https://www.canamfruits.com/produits/poissons-etbrfruits-de-mer/): lists generic `Vivaneau` and whole-fish names `Vivaneau américain` and `Vivaneau b-liner`, without scientific names.
- The existing CFIA [Fish List overview](https://inspection.canada.ca/en/food-labels/labelling/industry/fish/list) and [snapper search results](https://active.inspection.gc.ca/scripts/fssa/fispoi/fplist/fpresults.asp?cbShowAll=on&cmbIn=e&lang=e&q=snapper) are reused from the red-snapper review: multiple species share the broad English and French names.
