## Reason for the hold

Current Canadian Quaker manufacturer pages show nine English Harvest Crunch variants and seven French Croque Nature variants. A lone Canadian Maple identity would make `Harvest Crunch` and `Croque Nature` each return one apparently complete product choice while the family contains other differently formulated cereals. The two rosters do not align fully: the English page includes two Protein variants absent from the French roster. Review the sibling products and their French names together before allocating any family members.

The exact Canadian Maple product is supported by current bilingual manufacturer pages, and both pages show a product-specific ½ cup / 63 g nutrition serving. Those facts do not resolve the family-level picker problem or provide a reusable measure. The full manifest-listed recipe scan found no matching product demand in 114 recipes / 1,123 ingredient rows.
