# WP02 A7 — malted quinoa flour boundary hold (round 38)

**Date:** 2026-10-13

**Decision:** Keep experimental malted quinoa flour outside the active catalog and ledger.

Food-science papers establish experimental malted quinoa flour, including black and white quinoa
malting and a tested muffin formulation. They do not establish a current standalone Canadian or
Québec malted-flour product or a Québec French product label. Québec product sources support plain
and roasted quinoa flour as separate identities; they do not document a malted-quinoa flour product.
Keep the candidate separate from plain, roasted, color-qualified quinoa flour, whole quinoa malt, and
generic malt flour. Do not infer `farine de quinoa maltée` as an established display term.

The 2021 study's 24–96 hour germination, 50 °C drying, and grinding steps are experimental process
conditions. The Québec directory's 1/4 cup (30 g) portion is specific to a plain Cuisine Soleil
product. The muffin study's 30% flour replacement is recipe-specific. None is a general household
measure, density, conversion, cooking yield, or substitution for malted quinoa flour.

The 114 manifest-listed recipes contain 1,123 ingredient rows. None asks for quinoa flour or
malted quinoa flour. One row asks for two cups of cooked quinoa and links to the whole-quinoa cooked
form; it does not support a flour identity. A hypothetical row would expand `quinoa flour` from five
to six results and `quinoa` from eight to nine. No catalog or ledger entry was added.
