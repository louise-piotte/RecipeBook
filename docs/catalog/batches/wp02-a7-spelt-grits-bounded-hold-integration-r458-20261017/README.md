# R458 — Spelt-grits bounded hold

Held standalone **spelt grits** / **spelt grit** outside active references and the research ledger. MEGRO and Ecoagri establish supplier use of grits wording, but the reviewed sources do not establish a current Canadian grits product, a Québec-facing product-matched French name, or a common milling boundary between medium grits and wholegrain grits.

Keep `spelt grits`, `spelt grit`, `wholegrain spelt grits`, and `spelt grits – medium` unallocated. Do not assign `gruau d’épeautre`, `gruau d’épeautre complet`, `semoule d’épeautre`, or `épeautre concassé`. No active row, alias, ledger record, form, measure, recipe link, conversion, yield, or substitution was added. The full recipe bundle has no matching demand.

This documentation-only decision required no tests. See the [R458 proposal](../../proposals/wp02-section-a7-next-milling-gap-review-r458-20261017.md). The broader Section A7 review remains open.
