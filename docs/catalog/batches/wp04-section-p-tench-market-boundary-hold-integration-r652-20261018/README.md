# WP04 Section P R652 integration — Tench market-boundary hold

## Decision

Held Tench / Tanche (*Tinca tinca*) outside active references, legacy-import candidates, and the research ledger. CFIA's Fish List and the Gouvernement du Québec species profile pair the names `Tench / Tanche` with this taxon. The Québec profile describes an invasive freshwater species and says an angler may keep a catch for personal consumption; it does not establish a retail product or commercial supply. A targeted search for Canadian/Québec consumer listings found no verifiable product, and the bundled recipe corpus has no matching demand.

OQLF fiche 1198965 identifies Cunner / tanche-tautogue (*Tautogolabrus adspersus*) and notes that bare `tanche` has not been retained for Québec fish-product marketing for that species. That note remains scoped to Cunner; it does not negate CFIA and Québec's pairing for Tench. No alias is assigned across the two species. Keep the hold limited to missing Québec-facing culinary/product evidence; it does not make a legal or safety claim about eating Tench.

No reference, form, alias, legacy candidate, ledger record, recipe link, measurement, density, conversion, yield, or substitution was added. The Section P finfish parent remains open.

## Integration and checks

- Marked the R652 bounded hold complete in the Section P nested checklist and progress log and updated `roadmap.md`, the workflow, and the proposal status.
- Reviewed CFIA's Fish List name/taxon row and overview, the current Québec species profile, OQLF's GDT Cunner entry, current picker behavior, and the manifest-listed recipe corpus. The Québec page was updated 2026-10-15; it names Tench/Tanche and limits its consumption guidance to a personally caught fish.
- Static UTF-8/no-BOM, local-link, whitespace, picker-projection, source/ID ownership, and 114-recipe / 1,123-row demand checks passed. No software tests, build, emulator run, or deployment were performed for this documentation-only hold.
