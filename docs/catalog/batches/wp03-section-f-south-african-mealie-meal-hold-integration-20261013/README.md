# WP03 Section F: South African mealie-meal naming hold

Reviewed and held on 2026-10-13 from the [R57 boundary review](../../proposals/wp03-section-f-south-african-mealie-meal-boundary-review-20261013-r57.md).

## Disposition

Keep South African `mealie meal / mielie meal` outside the active reference catalog. South African grading sources establish a regional maize-meal family and separate meal grades from samp, maize rice, maize grits, and maize flour. OQLF defines broad dry-ground maize as `semoule de maïs`, allows fine, medium, and coarse grinds, and lists `maize meal` as a United Kingdom English term for that broad entry. The French term is already owned by `ingredient-ref-cornmeal`; no distinct Québec French display or safe crosswalk was found.

Keep `mielie pap`, `mealie pap`, `pap`, and `putu` out of aliases because they can name prepared foods or product uses. Preserve the existing maize-rice, samp, hominy-grits, cornmeal, and corn-flour ownership unchanged.

Sources: [South African Government maize-product grading regulation](https://www.gov.za/documents/agricultural-product-standards-act-regulations-grading-packing-and-marking-maize-0), [Department of Basic Education 2024 food specifications](https://www.education.gov.za/Portals/0/Documents/Publications/2024%20NSNP%20Food%20Specification%20Guidelines.pdf), [OQLF, *semoule de maïs*](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/17031243/semoule-de-mais), and [Canadian Iwisa Super Maize Meal listing](https://southafricanstore.ca/products/iwisa-super-maize-meal-1-kg).
