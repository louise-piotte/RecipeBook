# WP02 Section B R261 — African yam bean seed-flour hold

Reviewed the [R261 proposal](../../proposals/wp02-section-b-gap-review-r261-20261016.md) and kept the proposed flour outside active catalog and ledger data.

## Decision

Hold an African yam bean seed-flour reference and ledger candidate until a review can pair the whole *Sphenostylis stenocarpa* seed identity with a product-matched Québec French name. The proposal's food-science and FAO evidence supports a seed-flour concept. The bounded Canadian product scan found generic bean-flour wording and true-yam flour, but no consumer listing identifying *S. stenocarpa*. This is not a market-wide absence claim.

Keep `yam bean`, `yam-bean`, and `yam pea` unassigned as aliases. Kew lists African Yam Bean and Yam Pea as common names for *S. stenocarpa*. OQLF's jicama entry for *Pachyrhizus erosus* lists `yam bean` and `yam-bean` among its English terms and also distinguishes jicama from yam-bean. Do not transfer those terms to a flour row or create a broad alias that erases the plant-part distinction.

The 114-recipe, 1,123-row bundle has no matching candidate name or reference link. Keep flour names, forms, measurements, conversions, yields, and substitutions inactive until the paired review.
