Integrated the [round-65 malted-fonio review](../../proposals/wp02-a7-malted-fonio-flour-boundary-review-20261013-round65.md).

## Decision

Keep malted fonio flour unallocated. Primary studies use the English phrase for laboratory-made
flour and experimental blends, but the reviewed sources do not establish a current Canadian or
Québec standalone product or a product-specific Québec French name. Do not add an active reference,
alias, legacy identity, form, measure, recipe link, conversion, yield, density default, or
substitution.

Keep it distinct from generic fonio flour, black fonio flour, malt flour, and the separately named
roasted or precooked fonio-flour products. The study bulk-density values describe blended
formulations and are not transferred to this candidate. The bundled recipe corpus has no matching
ingredient phrase.

No catalog or research-ledger data changed. The proposal records the source evidence, current
neighboring search ownership, and conditions for a future review.

## Checks
