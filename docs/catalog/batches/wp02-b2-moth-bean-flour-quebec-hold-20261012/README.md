# Moth-bean flour Québec naming hold — 2026-10-12

## Decision

Keep moth-bean flour as an unallocated candidate. Research studies and product listings establish
flour made from moth bean (*Vigna aconitifolia*), but the reviewed sources do not establish a stable
Canadian product scope or a Québec French flour name. The English `moth bean flour` phrase is
attested in one Canadian menu listing; `moth atta` and `matki flour` are supported by Indian maker
or marketplace wording, not Québec product labels. Keep `farine de haricot papillon`, `farine de
haricots papillon`, and `farine de haricot mat` as research wording only.

Do not add flour terms to the existing whole-seed `moth beans / haricots papillon` identity. Keep
sprouted/cooked flour and extracted moth-bean starch separate. No catalog or ledger data, active
aliases, forms, measurements, recipe links, or substitutions changed. The review found no bundled
recipe demand. The [proposal](../../proposals/wp02-b2-moth-bean-flour-quebec-boundary-hold-20261012.md)
contains the exact ownership audit, source register, and reopening criteria.
