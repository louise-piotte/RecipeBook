# WP04 Section P round 40 — mentaiko hold

Integrated 2026-10-13 as a documentation-only hold under the open roe checklist at [plan line 4154](../../../RecipeBook-global-ingredient-catalog-plan.txt#L4377).

## Decision

Keep mentaiko / karashi-mentaiko outside active references and forms until a bundled recipe identifies the required product presentation. MAFF distinguishes karashi-mentaiko from shio-tarako. Current Canadian listings include both seasoned roe paste and membrane-sac products, and one mixed-roe SKU does not establish a universal pollock or cod identity. OQLF supports generic `œufs de poisson` but supplies no mentaiko-specific Québec French name.

The bundled corpus has 114 recipes and 1,123 ingredient rows, with no matching mentaiko, tarako, or fish-roe demand. A descriptive `seasoned fish roe` display would make mentaiko the phrase-first result for generic `roe`, replacing an accidental Greenland-halibut substring match. Keep it separate from tarako, other fish roe, salmon roe, tobiko, masago, bottarga, caviar, sea-urchin gonads, fish sauce, fish balls, and surimi.

No active reference, alias, form, measure, recipe link, conversion, yield, substitution, or ledger candidate was added. The proposal records only package-specific sizes and labels; none supports a transferable measure, serving, density, or conversion.

## Evidence and checks

- [MAFF salted-food classification](https://www.maff.go.jp/e/policies/market/dento_syoku/bunrui/siozuke.html) distinguishes shio-tarako from karashi-mentaiko and describes the latter as Alaska-pollock roe in liquid seasoning.
- [MAFF regional-food entry](https://www.maff.go.jp/j/nousin/kouryu/kyodo_ryouri/pdf/english.pdf), p. 7, describes spicy Alaska-pollack roe marinated in red-chili-spiced liquid and lists common uses.
- [OQLF fish-roe entry](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/10435851/oeufs-de-poisson) supports generic `œufs de poisson`; it does not name mentaiko.
- [Azuma Foods Canada](https://azumafoods.ca/products/mentai-paste/), [Daily Seafood](https://shop.dailyseafood.ca/products/6165aadf68dadc002d7c8b39/shirako-cod-semen-500-gm), [Hungry Ninja](https://hungry-ninja.ca/products/fzn-kaikobo-mentaiko-spicy-cod-roe-100g), and [PriceSmart Foods](https://www.pricesmartfoods.com/product/shirakiku-mentaiko-spicy-roe-paste-id-00074410167721) show distinct current product labels and presentations.
