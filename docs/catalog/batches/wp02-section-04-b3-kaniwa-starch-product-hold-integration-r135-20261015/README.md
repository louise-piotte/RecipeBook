# WP02 Section 04 B3 R135 — Kaniwa starch product-boundary hold

## Decision

Keep isolated kaniwa starch (*Chenopodium pallidicaule*) outside active references and the research
ledger until a current Canadian or Québec food product establishes both the standalone starch
product scope and product-specific French wording. The primary study isolates starch from kaniwa
flour for laboratory characterization; that establishes a research fraction, not a local consumer
product. [Steffolani et al.](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/star.201200286)

OQLF prefers `kaniwa` and `canihua` for the grain and `amidon` as the general food-starch term. It
does not establish a Québec product name for kaniwa starch and discourages `bébé quinoa` because
quinoa is a different plant. Do not use `fécule de kaniwa`; OQLF uses `fécule` more specifically for
starch in tubers or other underground plant organs. [OQLF kaniwa](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/26532687/kaniwa),
[OQLF amidon](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/8869736/amidon)

The current Canadian bilingual Yupik listing is for whole kaniwa seeds, with the same SKU, Peru
origin, and a whole-seed serving observation on both language pages. Metro's Québec listing is a
mixed flour containing organic kaniwa flour and other flours, not isolated starch. [Yupik English](https://yupik.com/en/kaniwa-seeds-p20072),
[Yupik French](https://yupik.com/fr/kaniwa-seeds-p20072),
[Metro mixed flour](https://www.metro.ca/epicerie-en-ligne/allees/garde-manger/ingredients-pour-cuisson-et-preparation/farines-et-essentiels-de-cuisson/farine-tout-usage-biologique/p/896899000449)

No reference, alias, ledger candidate, form, measurement observation for starch, density, unit
mapping, conversion, yield, recipe link, or substitution was added. Keep the existing whole-seed
identity and its source-specific serving unchanged. The complete recipe corpus has no matching
kaniwa or kaniwa-starch demand. This documentation-only hold required no tests.

See the [proposal](../../proposals/wp02-section-04-b3-next-gap-review-r135-20261015.md) and
[global catalog plan](../../../RecipeBook-global-ingredient-catalog-plan.txt). Section 04 B3 remains
open.
