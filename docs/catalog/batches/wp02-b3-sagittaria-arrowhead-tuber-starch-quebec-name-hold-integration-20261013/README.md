# WP02 B3 arrowhead tuber starch Québec naming hold — 2026-10-13

Integrated the [round-67 arrowhead-starch review](../../proposals/wp02-b3-sagittaria-arrowhead-tuber-starch-quebec-name-hold-20261013-round67.md).

## Decision

Keep starch isolated from *Sagittaria sagittifolia* tubers outside the active catalog and research
ledger. Primary studies establish an experimental starch material and extraction work, but the
review found no current Canadian/Québec standalone food product or supported Québec French product
name. OQLF supports botanical `sagittaire` and the general starch term `amidon`; these do not attest
`amidon de sagittaire`. TERMIUM's `sagittaire à larges feuilles` names *S. latifolia*, not the
research species.

Keep this candidate distinct from whole arrowhead tubers, arrowroot, and other tuber starches. The
reported extraction yield and lab bulk-density results do not establish a kitchen measure, cooking
yield, density default, conversion, or substitution. No bundled recipe contains the candidate.

No catalog or research-ledger data changed. The proposal records current ownership, picker behavior,
source limits, and conditions for reopening the candidate.

## Checks

- Independent source review confirmed the OQLF plant-name scope, OQLF generic starch wording, and the study-specific nature of published starch extraction results.
- Read-only ownership and recipe audits found no exact candidate allocation or bundled recipe demand.
- No app tests or emulator checks were run; this is a documentation-only hold.

WP02 B3 remains open.
