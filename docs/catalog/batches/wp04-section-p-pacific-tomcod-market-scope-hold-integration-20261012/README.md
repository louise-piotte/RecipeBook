# WP04 Section P Pacific tomcod market-scope hold (2026-10-12)

Kept Pacific tomcod (*Microgadus proximus*) out of the active ingredient catalog pending current
Canadian food-market evidence. The [review proposal](../../proposals/wp04-section-p-next-gap-review-20261012-round4.md)
records the species, naming, picker, source, and recipe audits. The current Pacific Fishery
Regulations pair `Pacific tomcod / Poulamon du Pacifique` with *M. proximus* in a bilingual
regulatory schedule, and CFIA's aquatic disease list repeats the pair; neither establishes consumer
labeling or Québec retail availability. The CFIA Fish List result for `Tomcod` identifies Atlantic
tomcod (*M. tomcod*) and does not list *M. proximus* in the reviewed result. Historical DFO market
and landing records do not establish current supply.

The full 114-recipe / 1,123-row corpus has no candidate or broader fish-term demand. Bare `tomcod`
and `poulamon` remain with the existing Atlantic-tomcod identity; no active reference, alias, form,
legacy candidate, shared ledger candidate, recipe link, measurement, conversion, yield, or
substitution was added. Keep the candidate only in the review record until a current Canadian
processor, retailer, or other authoritative food-market source identifies the species as a food
product, or a recipe explicitly requests it.

This was a documentation-only hold. No tests, emulator checks, or deployment were needed; no app or
database JSON files changed. Section P remains open.
