# WP02 A7 millet-meal boundary hold

## Decision

Keep `millet meal` unallocated. Ozery Bakery's current Canadian product page uses the phrase for one
component in its Morning Rounds multigrain mix, without a separate meal product, millet species, or
grind specification. The reviewed Walmart Marketplace listing has `Millet Meal` in English and
seller-supplied `Farine de Millet` in French for one listing; Walmart says third-party product details
are not separately verified. The listing does not establish that meal and flour are generally
equivalent. OQLF's generic `millet` entry names the cereal, not a processed meal product or a Québec
French meal term.

The bundle has no matching millet ingredient demand. Keep the phrase distinct from millet grain,
generic and species-qualified millet flours, millet flakes, and millet couscous. Do not add a reference,
alias, legacy or ledger candidate, form, measure, density, unit mapping, recipe link, conversion, yield,
or substitution. The prior grouped disposition is refreshed with the current manufacturer evidence;
no duplicate candidate record was created.

## Sources

- [Ozery Bakery, Morning Rounds – Multi Grain](https://ozerybakery.com/our-products/morning-rounds-multi-grain/): current maker page lists `millet meal` in a multigrain mix, but no standalone meal specification; its 75 g value is for one finished bun.
- [Walmart Canada, English listing](https://www.walmart.ca/en/ip/Millet-Meal-Stone-Ground/6NRGALCVE0DH) and [French listing](https://www.walmart.ca/fr/ip/Pierre-de-Millet-Moulue/6NRGALCVE0DH): same SKU with `meal` and `farine` titles; marketplace seller data are not separately verified.
- [OQLF, *millet*](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/26506455/millet): supports generic cereal naming, not a processed meal name.
- [Bob's Red Mill, Millet Flour](https://www.bobsredmill.com/product/millet-flour): manufacturer identifies a current stone-ground whole-grain millet flour; its serving and flour-replacement advice apply only to that flour.
- [Canadian Intellectual Property Office, trademark application 1978912](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/cipo/trademark-search/1978912): historical goods list separates meal and flour wording, but the application is abandoned and does not establish a current product or grind specification.
