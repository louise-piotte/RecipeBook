# WP03 Section F R94: Sprouted Sorghum Grain Hold

Review date: 2026-10-14
Decision: documentation-only boundary hold.

## Decision

Keep dried whole sprouted sorghum grain outside the active reference catalog and research ledger
until a Québec French name distinguishes the cook-before-eating product and product-specific
evidence describes the relevant sprouting and processing controls. The reviewed retail page
establishes a dried whole-grain product intended to be cooked, but not those missing boundaries.
This evidence gap is not a finding that the cited product is unsafe.

Keep ordinary grain sorghum, fresh living sprouts, seed for germination, malted sorghum, flour,
grits, and composite cereal products separate. Add no reference, alias, form, measure, density,
mapping, recipe link, conversion, yield, substitution, or ledger candidate. The proposal's scan
found no matching bundled recipe demand.

  provide general sprout-processing context, not validation of the cited commercial product.
