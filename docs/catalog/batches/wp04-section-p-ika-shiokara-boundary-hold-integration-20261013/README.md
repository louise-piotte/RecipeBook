# WP04 Section P Ika Shiokara Boundary Hold

Integrated 2026-10-13 under the open [preserved-seafood checklist](../../../RecipeBook-global-ingredient-catalog-plan.txt#L4207) and [fish-powder, fish-balls, and surimi checklist](../../../RecipeBook-global-ingredient-catalog-plan.txt#L4237).

## Decision

Keep ika/squid shiokara outside active references and forms until a bundled recipe selects the product. TERMIUM, MAFF, OQLF, and current Canadian retail listings support a squid-specific salted-ferment name, while the broader shiokara family includes other seafood. The recipe bundle has no matching demand, and a new row would broaden `squid` and `calmar` searches. Keep it separate from squid species, generic shiokara, fish sauce, dried squid, fish balls, and surimi. Add no alias, form, measure, recipe link, conversion, yield, or substitution.

## Evidence and checks

- Government of Canada's [TERMIUM Plus shiokara entry](https://www.btb.termiumplus.gc.ca/tpv2alpha/alpha-eng.html?fchrcrdnm=19&i=&index=frr&lang=eng&sg_kp_wet=1063198&srchtxt=CHAIR+POISSON) defines shiokara as salted fermented seafood and identifies `Ika-Shiokara` as squid meat and viscera. The entry also names other seafood variants.
- Japan MAFF's [fishery fermented food overview](https://www.maff.go.jp/e/policies/market/dento_syoku/bunrui/suisan-hakkou.html) lists squid shiokara alongside several shiokara products made from other seafood. It includes fish sauce in the broad salted-fermented category and describes it separately as a liquid seasoning. OQLF's [calmar entry](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/1198881/calmar) gives the preferred French base term `calmar` and English `squid`; it does not define a French name for shiokara.
- Canadian listings call the product `Squid Shiokara` or `Ika Shiokara`. The [Takewa listing](https://hungry-ninja.ca/products/fzn-takewa-ika-shiokara-marinated-squid-200g) names a 200 g product and gives squid, guts, sugar, salt, vinegar, honey, and other ingredients; its French page uses the retailer wording [`Calamar mariné Takewa Ika Shiokara`](https://hungry-ninja.ca/fr/products/fzn-takewa-ika-shiokara-marinated-squid-200g). [SEABORN](https://delivery.seaborn.ca/products/squid-shiokara-%E5%92%B8%E9%AD%B7%E9%AD%9A-%E3%82%A4%E3%82%AB%E3%81%AE%E5%A1%A9%E8%BE%9B) lists 200 g; [Seaborn-Toronto](https://www.seaborntoronto.com/product-page/ika-shiokara-%E7%83%8F%E8%B3%8A%E5%A1%A9%E8%BE%9B) uses `Ika shiokara`; [Blackjack Foods](https://blackjackfoodtrading.com/products/squid-shiokara) describes a frozen, thaw-to-serve 200 g pack; and [Cityfish](https://www.cityfish.ca/seafood-frozen) lists a 400 g product. Package quantities are not recipe portions.
- The proposal audit counted 1,367 active references, 100 forms, 64 legacy candidates, 1,699 research-ledger records, 114 manifest-listed recipes, and 1,123 ingredient rows. No shiokara-specific owner or matching recipe demand was found; the only related corpus line was 140 g of plain sliced calamari. The full ownership, picker, corpus, numeric-limit, and source audit is in the [round-39 proposal](../../proposals/wp04-section-p-ika-shiokara-product-boundary-review-20261013-round39.md).
- The checklist, roadmap, and progress log now record the hold. No catalog or ledger data changed. No tests were run because this was a documentation-only change.
