# WP04 Section P R336 — blue marlin picker-boundary hold

Reviewed on 2026-10-16. Kept *Makaira nigricans* / blue marlin / `makaire bleu` outside active ingredient references, aliases, legacy mappings, and the research ledger. This decision adds no catalog or recipe data.

CFIA identifies *M. nigricans* as `Blue Marlin / Makaire Bleu` and separately assigns broad `Marlin / Makaire` to *Tetrapturus* spp. OQLF supports the Québec name `makaire bleu` for the species, while Usito distinguishes Atlantic `makaire bleu` (*M. nigricans*) from Indo-Pacific `makaire bleu` (*M. mazara*). Product pages show Canadian food-market context, but they do not remove the broad-name collisions. A blue-only row would make both `marlin` and `makaire` look species specific and would make the unqualified French display appear exclusive. Revisit if the picker can represent broader groups alongside qualified species or a bundled recipe establishes a narrower product request.

The live snapshot contained 1,683 references, 129 forms, 58 legacy candidates, and 2,031 ledger records. No active owner or prior focused decision was found for the candidate ID or qualified bilingual names. The manifest scan covered 114 recipe files and 1,123 ingredient rows and found no matching term in the files or ingredient rows.
