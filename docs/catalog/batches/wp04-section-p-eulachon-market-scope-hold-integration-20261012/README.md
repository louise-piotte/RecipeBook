# WP04 Section P eulachon market-scope hold (2026-10-12)

Kept eulachon / eulakane (*Thaleichthys pacificus*) outside the active catalog pending a current,
species-matched Canadian consumer product or a bundled recipe request that resolves product state and
label scope. CFIA identifies the species as Eulachon but has no French common-name value; OQLF and
DFO support eulakane as a Québec French name. These establish a defensible bilingual species name,
not an ordinary retail listing.

The 2026 DFO plan covers Fraser River eulachon: commercial and recreational fisheries are closed in
that management area, while limited Indigenous food, social, and ceremonial harvest remains authorized
under communal licences. Do not generalize those Fraser-specific conditions to all B.C. populations
or treat them as overriding Indigenous food harvest and trade. Do not infer retail availability or
unavailability from this limited source review.

No active reference, alias, legacy candidate, ledger record, form, measure, recipe link, conversion,
yield, or substitution was added. Current ownership checks found no match across 1,312 references,
98 forms, 64 legacy entries, and 1,620 ledger records. The full 114-file / 1,123-row recipe corpus
has no candidate or sibling-term match. Static JSON parsing, source-prefix, exact-key ownership, picker
projection, recipe scan, UTF-8/no-BOM, and whitespace checks passed. This was a documentation-only
hold, so no app tests or emulator checks were needed. Section P remains open.
