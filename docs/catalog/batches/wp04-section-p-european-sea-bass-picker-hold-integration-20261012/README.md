# WP04 Section P European sea bass / bar commun picker hold

## Decision

Keep the prospective *Dicentrarchus labrax* row unallocated. OQLF and Canadian fish-naming evidence support **bar commun** / **sea bass**, and Québec listings show species-qualified products. The current phrase-first picker would nevertheless make broad `sea bass`, `bar`, and `bass` queries return this single species if the proposed row were activated. Health Canada's historical retail surveillance also cautions that products labelled “sea bass” may have represented more than one taxon. The bundled recipe corpus has no matching demand.

Reconsider the candidate if picker behavior can preserve broad fish choices or a recipe or product request supplies a species-qualified term. Do not add short or broader names as aliases, and keep European sea bass separate from other bass species and Patagonian toothfish.
