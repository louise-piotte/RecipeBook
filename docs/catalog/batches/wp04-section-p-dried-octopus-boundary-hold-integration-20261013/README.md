# WP04 Section P — dried octopus boundary hold (round 37)

**Date:** 2026-10-13

**Decision:** Keep dried octopus / `pieuvre séchée` outside active references and forms until a
bundled recipe identifies the product state it needs. A separately searchable dried-octopus row
would add a second result to the generic `octopus` and `pieuvre` queries. Leaving the candidate
unallocated preserves the existing generic `octopus / pieuvre` identity and its `poulpe` French
alias.

## Evidence and limits

- OQLF's [GDT entry for *pieuvre*](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/8402783/pieuvre) lists `pieuvre` and `poulpe` as preferred French terms and `octopus` as the English term. The entry is dated 1992 and does not describe a dried product.
- A 2021 [CFIA bilingual recall record](https://recalls-rappels.canada.ca/en/alert-recall/canada-uncle-bill-seafood-brand-dried-octopus-recalled-due-undeclared-sulphites) names `Dried Octopus / Pieuvre séchée` for a 142 g retail package. This supports the product wording but is historical evidence, not a current product or recipe.
- The current indexed [T&T listing](https://www.tntsupermarket.com/eng/75713601-pacific-intl-dried-octopus.html) names Pacific International Dried Octopus in a 114 g package. A [Souperwell listing](https://www.souperwell.com/products/dried-octopus) describes its own 77 g product as sun-dried and salted for soups and stews, and currently marks it sold out. These listings do not establish one species, salt level, cut, or preparation across products.

## Catalog and recipe effects

No reference, alias, form, measure, mapping, recipe link, conversion, yield, substitution, or ledger
candidate was added. The existing octopus reference remains identity-only. The proposal's static
ownership audit found no dried-octopus owner in active references, forms, legacy candidates, or the
research ledger. Its picker projection leaves the current `octopus` and `pieuvre` searches at one
result each and finds no result for `dried octopus` or `pieuvre séchée`.

The proposal records a scan of all 114 manifest-listed recipes and 1,123 ingredient rows. It found no
dried-octopus wording, no octopus/pieuvre/poulpe/tako ingredient row, and no recipe link to the
generic octopus reference. No product-specific amount, rehydration ratio, yield, or substitution can
be inferred from the available package descriptions.
