# WP02 Section A R234: white winter wheat flour hold

Accepted the bounded hold on La Milanaise’s `Organic Unbleached White Winter Wheat Flour` / `Farine de blé Winter blanche non blanchie biologique`. The manufacturer identifies a marketed flour milled from winter wheat, but it does not explain whether “white” describes grain class or flour appearance, or name the wheat class, variety, or grain origin. Do not assign the product to Canada Eastern White Winter (CEWW), merge it with soft-white or winter-red flour, or treat `T55` as a cross-country class equivalence.

The exact product phrases remain outside active references and the research ledger. No aliases, candidate mapping, form, measurement, recipe link, conversion, yield, or substitution was added. The bundled recipe corpus has no matching demand. Reopen after the mill clarifies the product’s grain class or variety and what “white” identifies, followed by independent Québec French review.

The full source, picker, namespace, and evidence audit is in the [R234 proposal](../../proposals/wp02-section-a-white-winter-wheat-flour-boundary-review-r234-20261016.md). No software tests or emulator checks were needed because this hold changed documentation only.
