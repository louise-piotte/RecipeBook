# WP02 Section A7 R465 — amaranth-grits hold

Recorded on 2026-10-17 after root review of the [R465 proposal](../../proposals/wp02-section-a7-next-milling-gap-review-r465-20261017.md).

## Decision

Keep `amaranth grits / amaranth grit` unallocated. Amarant Ukraine identifies a crushed amaranth product, but the same code's English product pages conflict between wholegrain, groats, and grits wording. No reviewed Canadian or Québec source identifies this product with a matching French display or a stable milling boundary. Do not assign the phrases to whole amaranth grain or amaranth flour, and leave R29's separate amaranth-meal hold unchanged.

The manifest scan found no matching phrase in the 114 bundled recipes or 1,123 ingredient rows. No active reference, alias, form, legacy candidate, ledger record, measure, mapping, conversion, yield, recipe link, or substitution changed. This was a documentation-only hold and needed no tests. Section A7 remains open.

## Evidence reviewed

- [Amarant Ukraine's Ukrainian product page](https://amarantshop.com.ua/uk/amarantovaya-krupa/amarantovaya-krupa-900-gr.html) calls code 025 crushed amaranth groats and gives only product-specific preparation directions.
- The same supplier's English page calls code 025 wholegrain; its English category and indexed product wording vary between crushed groats and grits. The proposal records the exact language-version conflict and its limits.
- The [CFIA 2015–2018 grain survey](https://inspection.canada.ca/en/food-safety-industry/food-chemistry-and-microbiology/food-safety-testing-reports-and-journal-articles/viruses-imported-frozen-scallops-0) lists amaranth flour and grain, while listing grits for some other grain samples. This historical targeted survey is not a current retail census or evidence of absence.
- Current Québec sources reviewed identify whole amaranth or amaranth flour but not an amaranth-grits product with a matching French label. The proposal documents those sources and reuses existing OQLF and Québec flour records without duplicating ledger entries.

## Reconciliation

The A7 checklist, roadmap, and workflow now record this bounded hold. No catalog or ledger data changed. The detailed source register, current ownership audit, phrase-first picker projection, recipe-demand scan, and measurement boundaries remain in the [R465 proposal](../../proposals/wp02-section-a7-next-milling-gap-review-r465-20261017.md).

No software tests or emulator checks were run; this reconciliation changed documentation only.
