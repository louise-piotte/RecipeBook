# WP02 Section 04 B3 chickpea-starch product-boundary hold

## Decision

Keep isolated chickpea (*Cicer arietinum*) starch outside active references, forms, and the research
ledger pending a current Canadian or Québec human-food product source and supported local product
name. Food-science research and a current international supplier distinguish isolated starch from
flour, but do not establish Canadian or Québec availability. The Canadian pulse-fractionation project
reports **starch-rich flour**, and a Canadian trademark goods statement is not proof of a current sale.
The bounded search found chickpea flour products, not a standalone isolated-starch product; this is
not proof that no Canadian supplier exists.

Keep chickpea flour, starch-rich chickpea flour, and isolated chickpea starch distinct. Do not add a
new display, `amidon de pois chiche`, aliases, a ledger candidate, measures, conversions, yields,
recipe links, or substitutions until product evidence supports the identity and Québec wording. The
bundled corpus has no matching demand.
