- English: `orange roughy`

to the taxon. The 114 bundled recipes contain no matching fish line. `Slimehead` and
`hoplostète rouge` remain inactive as aliases.

## Product-specific observations

The Québec-facing Capone Gourmet page reports frozen fillets at 200–230 g each and approximate
counts of 4–5, 10–11, 20–22, and 40–45 fillets in its 907 g, 2.27 kg, 4.54 kg, and 9.07 kg packs.
The ledger preserves these as vendor-specific observations because the count varies with natural
fish size; they are not species-wide measures, standard servings, or reusable conversions. The
listing itself does not state the scientific name.

Health Canada mercury guidance is retained as source context only. Its population-specific combined
fish-consumption limits are not stored as recipe quantities or ingredient measurements.
