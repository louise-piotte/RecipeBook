# WP03 Section F R115: brown-top millet Québec-name recheck

Reviewed and integrated on 2026-10-14. Section F remains open.

## Disposition

Kept `candidate-brown-top-millet` in the research ledger and outside active ingredient references.
The candidate remains unresolved for a Québec French display. The newer Canadian French retail
wording `millet brun` is tied to an Ottawa seller, while the same phrase appears on a French
proso-millet flour product; it does not establish a safe Québec grain label for *Urochloa ramosa*.
OQLF's entry remains generic and does not name this species. The proposal supplies the full source
review and exact current ownership checks.

No reference, alias, form, measurement, conversion, yield, recipe link, or substitution changed.
The existing seven-source candidate record and its research states remain unchanged. Conditional
English display and spelling aliases remain proposal-only; no French display or alias is approved.

## Evidence boundary

- FSSAI's human-food millet standard names Brown top (Korale), *Urochloa ramosa*, within whole and
  dehulled grain standards. This supports the species/product boundary, not a Canadian or Québec
  consumer name.
- Spice Divine's indexed French listing pairs the Browntop product with `millet brun SV` and
  `Korale`; its pickup page identifies an Ottawa, Ontario location. Direct retrieval of the French
  product URL returned a cache-miss response during this review, so this remains indexed seller
  wording rather than a directly verified label.
- OQLF's [millet entry](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/26506455/millet)
  lists generic preferred terms and says `millet` can refer to several species or abbreviate a
  specific one. Its [millet commun entry](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/26506451/millet-commun)
  identifies *Panicum miliaceum*.
- Naturalia's [French flour listing](https://www.naturalia.fr/produit/farine-millet-brun-500g)
  labels a *Panicum miliaceum* product `farine de millet brun`. This is a different species and
  product state, but confirms that the French wording is not a safe, species-specific alias by
  itself.
- The [R115 proposal](../../proposals/wp03-section-f-brown-top-millet-quebec-name-recheck-review-r115-20261014.md)
  records all twelve sources, the candidate boundary, picker projection, complete recipe scan, and
  numeric/form/substitution limits.
