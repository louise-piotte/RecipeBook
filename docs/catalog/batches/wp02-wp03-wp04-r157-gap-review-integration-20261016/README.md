# WP02/WP03/WP04 R157 gap-review integration

Integrated 2026-10-16 after independent root review of the proposals, current source pages, catalog ownership, and bundled-recipe impact.

## Decisions

- **WP02 A6 — white teff flour:** Keep the candidate outside active references and the research ledger. VANA's Calgary listing names a white-teff product but says its current French sack text incorrectly reads `FARINE DE FEFF ROUGE` and is awaiting correction. A Toronto listing pairs “White/Ivory” for its own product, while Azmera lists its own white-teff flour. Those sources do not establish that every white-teff listing is equivalent to the existing Ontario-made ivory-teff identity. No standalone Québec French product name was verified. Keep generic, ivory, and brown teff-flour rows unchanged; add no alias or separate row.
- **WP03 Section F — soft white winter wheat berries:** Add identity-only `soft white winter wheat berries / grains de blé tendre blanc d'hiver` as `ingredient-ref-soft-white-winter-wheat-berries` in `GRAIN_AND_CEREAL`. The Ontario mill's current product page names whole, unmilled soft white winter wheat berries. Canadian Grain Commission sources distinguish the soft-white-winter class from soft-white-spring wheat, but do not assign this seller product to a designated variety, class, or grade. OQLF supports `blé tendre` as a component; the full French display is provisional, not an OQLF ruling or verified Québec retail title. Keep it separate from the season-unspecified soft-white row and leave independent Québec review open. Add no aliases, forms, measures, density, unit mappings, conversions, yields, recipe links, or substitutions.
- **WP04 Section P — horse mackerel / chinchard:** Hold the unqualified market label and a hypothetical *Trachurus trachurus* row outside the active catalog and research ledger. OQLF maps *chinchard* to *T. trachurus*, but the current CFIA Fish List assigns `Horse Mackerel` and `Chinchard` across multiple taxa. Timelia's Québec fresh-fish listings use `Chinchard / Horse Mackerel` and `Chinchard Afrique / Horse Mackerel Africa` without supplying a binomial. Do not attach these phrases to existing mackerel rows.

None of the three candidate groups has matching bundled-recipe demand. The accepted wheat row adds no recipe links or numeric behavior.

## Independent source review

- For **white teff flour**, root confirmed the current English product name and label-correction note on [VANA's wholesale catalogue](https://vanaafrica.ca/catalogue), the seller-specific “White/Ivory” wording on [Zula Market](https://zulamarket.ca/products/mama-white-teff-flour), and the Canadian-made white-flour wording on [Azmera's listing](https://global.afrikartt.com/product/azmera-white-teff-flour-25lb). [Berhan's Ontario Made ivory-flour listing](https://supportontariomade.ca/explore-products/5-lb-ivory-teff-flour) confirms the existing row's product identity but does not establish a cross-seller equivalence. The existing [OQLF teff entry](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/8480763/tef) concerns the base cereal, not white flour.
- For **soft white winter wheat berries**, root confirmed the exact whole-kernel product title on [1847 Stone Milling](https://1847.ca/product/soft-white-winter-wheat-berries/), the current [Canadian Grain Commission wheat-class table](https://www.canada.ca/en/grain-commission/services/grain-quality-system/wheat-classes.html), its current [French CEWW variety order](https://www.canada.ca/fr/commission-grains/services/systeme-qualite-grains/arretes/lvd-ble-ceww.html), the [2025 CEWW quality report](https://www.grainscanada.gc.ca/en/grain-research/grain-harvest-export-quality/wheat/2025/harvest-quality-report-ceww-2025.html), and OQLF's [blé tendre entry](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/8357378/ble-tendre). The class evidence does not establish the seller product's class or cultivar; agricultural test weight is not kitchen density.
- For **horse mackerel / chinchard**, root confirmed OQLF's [chinchard entry](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/1198926/chinchard), the overlapping taxa in the [CFIA Fish List](https://active.inspection.gc.ca/scripts/fssa/fispoi/fplist/fpresults.asp?cbShowAll=on&cmbIn=e&lang=e&q=MACKEREL), CFIA's [common-name guidance](https://inspection.canada.ca/en/food-labels/labelling/industry/fish/common-names), and Timelia's current [Québec fresh-fish menu](https://order.online/store/timelia-qu%C3%A9bec-2862751). The source set supports a taxonomic hold for the unqualified local product wording.
