# WP03 Section F R513 integration — Inno Foods Blueberry Oat Puffs hold

**Date:** 2026-10-17
**Review:** [R513 proposal](../../proposals/wp03-section-f-next-gap-review-r513-20261017.md)

## Disposition

Keep Inno Foods Blueberry Oat Puffs outside active references, forms, legacy mappings, and the
research ledger until a product-matched Québec-facing French name is verified. The manufacturer
confirms a distinct formulated cereal, but its linked Canadian package mockup image text was not
readable in the available extraction. The retailer evidence is English-only and does not establish
Québec availability. Do not invent the French display.

Keep this finished cereal separate from plain puffed oat kernels, rolled oats, blueberry, coconut,
and seeds. Add no aliases, forms, measurements, recipe links, conversions, yields, or substitutions.
The 680 g package size remains retailer-specific packaging evidence.

## Evidence and static review

The manufacturer page gives the English title and ingredient declaration; the manufacturer
collection lists the item as a cereal. Instacart/Costco and a recent Costco East listing use an
alternate English title and list 680 g, but do not establish a Québec French product name or
availability. The full manifest scan found no matching files or ingredient rows across 114 recipes
and 1,123 ingredient rows.

The conditional picker projection finds the manufacturer title with one result, while the
alternate retailer word order and `puffed oats` remain unmatched. Adding only the English title
would raise `Blueberry` from one to two, `oat` from nine to ten, and `cereal` from 56 to 57; it
would leave `oats`, `céréales`, and `Inno Foods` unchanged. These projections support keeping the
bilingual product identity unallocated until its French title is verified.

The plan, roadmap, and workflow now record this documentation-only hold. No catalog, ledger,
generated-index, or recipe data changed. No software tests, build, emulator check, or deployment
were needed.
