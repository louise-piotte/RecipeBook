# WP02 Section 04 B3 R363 — pregelatinized tapioca-starch hold

Recorded a documentation-only hold on a general `pregelatinized tapioca starch / amidon de tapioca prégélatinisé` identity. Fineberry’s Canadian product page presents a single-ingredient food-use pre-gel tapioca product but does not specify a native or modified grade. Nepra’s current portfolio lists both native and modified pre-gel tapioca starch. Ingredion’s Canadian NATIONAL 104 sheet describes a tapioca starch as both pregelatinized and modified and labels it “Modified Tapioca Starch” in Canada. These sources show that “pregelatinized” and “modified” overlap for at least one commercial tapioca grade; they do not establish a clean general boundary for a new row.

No reference, ledger candidate, alias, form, measurement, recipe link, conversion, yield, or substitution was added. The existing tapioca-starch identities remain unchanged. A hypothetical general row would expand broad `tapioca starch` search from four existing identities to five; exact pregelatinized phrases currently have no result. The 114-recipe / 1,123-row bundle has no tapioca demand. Reopen with a grade-specific Canadian specification and independent Québec naming review, or after a picker-level decision for overlapping process and modification descriptors. The broader B3 checklist remains open.
