# WP02 A7 — malted rice flour boundary hold (round 39)

**Date:** 2026-10-13

**Decision:** Keep malted rice flour unresolved and unallocated. Research papers establish it as an
experimental ingredient, while the reviewed Canadian and Québec sources do not establish a
standalone product specification or Québec French display. A research term alone does not establish
a consumer product. Keep it separate from plain, brown, glutinous, roasted, or toasted rice flour;
rice malt syrup; fermented rice malt/koji; and generic malt flour.

## Evidence and limits

- A 2025 [Food Chemistry study indexed by PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40378781/) explicitly names malted rice flour in an experimental weaning mix; its optimized formula uses 5% in a multi-ingredient formulation.
- A 2010 [Rice Science paper](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1672630808601043) studies malted rice flour in bread and reports 35% wheat-flour replacement for a specific three-day-germinated rice flour and formula. Neither study supports a general substitution or retail specification.
- OQLF's [GDT entry for *farine de riz*](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/8432782/farine-de-riz) supports the Québec term for plain rice flour from white or brown rice, but does not name malted rice flour.
- The Québec Food Directory lists [La Milanaise organic whole brown rice flour](https://www.repertoirealimentsquebecois.gouv.qc.ca/Produit/Index/8204185) as a plain brown-rice product and reports its SKU-specific 1/3 cup (60 g) serving. Do not transfer that portion to malted rice flour.
- Reviewed Canadian supplier catalogues from [Port Royal Mills](https://www.portroyalmills.com/) and [Snow Cap](https://snowcap.com/product-category/ingredients/flours-and-grains/flour-misc-alternative/) list rice-flour and malted-grain products separately, without a malted-rice-flour SKU in the public listings. These catalogues are limited source checks, not proof of market-wide absence.

## Catalog and recipe effects

No active reference, alias, form, legacy identity, ledger candidate, measure, density, mapping,
recipe link, conversion, yield, or substitution was added. The proposal's ownership audit found no
exact candidate owner in active references, forms, legacy candidates, or the research ledger.

The proposal's picker projection leaves the candidate queries at zero and estimates that a separate
row would add one result to `rice flour` and `farine de riz` (each currently seven) and `rice`
(currently 51). All 114 manifest-listed recipes and 1,123 structured ingredient rows were scanned;
none requests malted rice flour.
