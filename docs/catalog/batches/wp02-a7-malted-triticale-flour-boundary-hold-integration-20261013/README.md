# WP02 A7 malted triticale flour boundary hold

Accepted the documentation-only hold for experimental malted triticale flour. The 2023 biscuit and 2024 pasta studies establish malted triticale flour as a research ingredient, but the reviewed Canadian and Québec sources do not establish a standalone consumer product or product-specific Québec French display. Keep it outside active references, forms, legacy candidates, and the research ledger. Preserve the existing plain triticale flour identity; do not attach malted wording to it.

The recipe bundle has no matching triticale demand. No measurements, forms, conversions, yields, recipe links, or substitutions were added. No tests were needed for this documentation-only decision.

The proposal snapshot of 1,378 references and 1,708 ledger records predates the same-day integration of South African samp (WP03 Section F R54). Current counts are 1,379 references and 1,709 ledger records; the triticale findings remain unchanged.
