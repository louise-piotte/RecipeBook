# WP02/WP03/WP04 round-31 boundary holds

Reviewed 2026-10-13. The three proposals remain unallocated. No shared catalog JSON, ledger, recipe,
generated index, or code changed.

## Decisions

- **WP02 A7 — rice grits:** Keep the generic phrase unallocated. Anson Mills and Tidewater Grain use
  it for broken Carolina Gold rice kernels, while Safa Agro describes a ground-and-sieved rice
  product. Statistics Canada's classification example lists the phrase but does not define its
  process, and no Québec French name establishes the generic product boundary. Leave the existing
  broken-rice row unchanged. See the
  [proposal](../../proposals/wp02-a7-rice-grits-boundary-review-20261013-round31.md), [Anson Mills](https://ansonmills.com/products/105),
  [Tidewater Grain](https://www.tidewatergrain.com/retail/p/rice-grits),
  [Safa Agro](https://safaagro.com/rice-grits-semolina/), and
  [Statistics Canada](https://www23.statcan.gc.ca/imdb/p3VD.pl?CLV=3&CPV=1821294&CST=17022022&CVD=1386659&Function=getAllExample&MLV=4&TVD=1384502&V=447178&VST=17022022).
- **WP03 Section F — Ogi Baba powder:** Keep the Canadian Guinea Corn Powder (Ogi Baba) unallocated.
  Retail listings identify the dry product but do not establish its ingredients/process or a Québec
  French name. Food-science sources describe sorghum Ogi Baba as a fermented preparation, with
  experimental powdered products having variable cereal/legume formulas. An active `Ogi Baba` term
  would capture bare `ogi` and replace the current unrelated `ogiri-egusi` substring result. The
  recipe bundle has no matching demand. See the
  [proposal](../../proposals/wp03-section-f-ogi-baba-powder-boundary-review-20261013.md),
  [FoodBasket Canada](https://foodbasketcanada.com/product/guinea-corn-powder-ogi-baba/),
  [Beauty For Ashes](https://africanmarket.ca/product-category/pap/),
  [Otunba et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8646963/),
  [Omah et al.](https://www.ajol.info/index.php/ajfand/article/download/231635/218787), and
  [FAO's French-language nutrition text](https://www.fao.org/4/T0818F/T0818F0d.htm).
- **WP04 Section P — cod tongues:** Keep the unqualified cut outside active references and forms.
  OQLF pairs `langue de morue` with “cod tongue” and says the product can be fresh or salted. Current
  sources establish fresh/frozen Atlantic cod products, but do not confirm both states under the
  existing Atlantic-cod and salt-cod identities; a single fresh Atlantic-cod form would hide salted
  intent. See the
  [proposal](../../proposals/wp04-section-p-cod-tongues-cut-form-boundary-review-20261013-round31.md),
  [OQLF](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/9488142/langue-de-morue),
  [Capone Gourmet](https://caponegourmet.com/products/langue-de-morue-surgele-islande),
  [Salties](https://www.salties.ca/products/fresh-cod-tongues), and
  [Coup de Pouce / ITHQ](https://www.coupdepouce.com/cuisine/entrees-et-accompagnements/recette/langues-de-morue-vinaigrette).

All proposals report zero matches across 114 bundled recipes and 1,123 ingredient rows. No app,
unit, instrumentation, emulator, or other tests were run for this proposal-only documentation
update.
