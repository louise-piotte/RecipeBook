# WP02 B2 Brazil-nut flour identity hold — 2026-10-12

## Decision

Keep Brazil-nut flour as an unresolved product candidate and outside the active catalog for now.
Current product descriptions use flour wording for ground Brazil nuts without disclosing whether the
kernels were pressed. Embrapa and a 2026 food-science study describe a separate process that presses
Brazil-nut kernels into cake and mills that cake into partially defatted flour. The sources do not
resolve whether these products should share one reference or receive separate identities/forms, and
the French flour display remains unverified in Québec consumer use.

No ingredient reference, alias, legacy candidate, ledger record, form, measure, density, unit mapping,
recipe link, or substitution was added. The exact English and French flour phrases have no active
owner, and the complete 114-recipe corpus scan found no matching demand. The existing Brazil-nut and
Brazil-nut-butter references remain unchanged.

## Evidence reviewed

- [OQLF, noix du Brésil](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/2296932/noix-du-bresil): supports the nut name and species, but not a flour name.
- [CFIA, ACIA-SARI update (2024-12-02)](https://inspection.canada.ca/fr/importation-aliments-vegetaux-ou-animaux/vegetaux-produits-vegetaux/sari/mises-jour-acia-sari-2024-12-02): lists `Farine, semoule et poudre : Noix du Brésil` import codes, grouping several product types without specifying process or consumer wording.
- [Embrapa, partially defatted Brazil-nut flour study](https://www.infoteca.cnptia.embrapa.br/infoteca/bitstream/doc/998892/1/BPD43.pdf): describes pressing kernels to obtain oil and cake, then making partially defatted flour from the cake.
- [Fabri et al., Brazil Nut Flour as a Functional Ingredient (2026)](https://aocs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/aocs.70038): describes three presses followed by grinding and sieving the press cake; its cookie results are study-specific.
- [Oh! Nuts, Ground Brazil Nut Flour](https://ohnuts.com/products/ground-brazil-nut-flour): describes finely ground nuts, without a pressing specification.
- [Empório Manjericão, IBY Brazil-nut flour](https://www.loja.emporiomanjericao.com.br/farinhas-6.html/farinha-de-castanha-do-brasil-iby-foods--p): lists Brazil nut as the ingredient and culinary uses, but does not state whether the product was pressed.

## Remaining work

Before promotion, resolve the product boundary between whole-kernel milling and press-cake flour,
find a Québec-facing French consumer term for the selected product scope, and review any supported
household measures or recipe-specific substitutions. Broader WP02 B2/B3 review remains open.

for exact-key ownership, the full recipe scan, measurement/substitution review, source IDs, and
remaining questions. This documentation-only hold required no tests.
