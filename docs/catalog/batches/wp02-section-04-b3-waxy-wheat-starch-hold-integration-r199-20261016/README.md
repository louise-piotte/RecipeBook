# WP02 Section 04 B3 R199 — Waxy Wheat Starch Hold

- **Status:** Integrated as a documentation-only hold; the Section 04 B3 parent remains open.
- **Disposition:** Keep waxy wheat starch outside active references and the research ledger until a current Canadian or Québec human-food product and product-matched Québec name are documented.
- **Existing identity:** Keep generic, modified, pregelatinized, and resistant wheat starches separate. Do not add `amidon de blé cireux` as a display or alias based only on component terminology.

## Evidence and limits

The Canadian Grain Commission's 2026–2027 CWSP order lists CDC Vosk. The University of Saskatchewan describes CDC Vosk as waxy/low-amylose spring wheat adapted to central Canada. An AAFC-affiliated study fractionates waxy wheat into flour and starch. These establish a cultivar and experimental starch fraction, not a current food-grade waxy-wheat-starch product.

Allzone's reviewed Canadian pages quote generic native food-grade wheat starch and waxy maize, rice, and tapioca starch. They do not list waxy wheat starch; this is a bounded supplier-page finding, not evidence of market-wide nonavailability. OQLF defines general waxy-starch terminology but does not attest a wheat-specific product name. The Québec Food Directory entry supports generic `amidon de blé` in a finished food only.

Keep waxy wheat flour, whole waxy wheat, and laboratory starch fractions distinct from ordinary wheat starch. Add no reference, aliases, forms, measures, density, mappings, recipe links, conversions, yields, or substitutions.

## Ownership and recipe demand

At review, the live package contained 1,579 references, 122 forms, 61 legacy candidates, and 1,924 ledger records. The existing generic wheat-starch reference and its modified, pregelatinized, and resistant neighbors remain unchanged. The R199 source prefix and proposed ID were unallocated; the five proposal-local source IDs map to five URLs, with existing OQLF and Allzone sources reused by their registered IDs.

The manifest-listed bundle has 114 recipes and 1,123 ingredient rows. The audit found no waxy-wheat-starch phrase hits and no ingredient rows linked to the four existing wheat-starch references.
