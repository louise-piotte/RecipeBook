# WP04 Section P R467 — shrimp-cracker hold

Recorded on 2026-10-17 after root review of the [R467 proposal](../../proposals/wp04-section-p-next-seafood-review-r467-20261017.md).

## Decision

Keep `shrimp crackers / craquelins de crevettes` unallocated. Québec and Canadian listings support the product wording, but adding the English display would make broad `crackers` return only this seafood snack. `Prawn crackers`, `shrimp chips`, and `chips aux crevettes` remain proposal-only phrases because they would divert broad `prawn` or `chips` searches. The reviewed products also include snack-ready and uncooked states without a shared preparation or conversion rule.

The manifest scan found no matching phrase in the 114 bundled recipes or 1,123 ingredient rows. No active reference, alias, form, legacy candidate, ledger record, measure, mapping, conversion, package yield, recipe link, or substitution changed. This was a documentation-only hold and needed no tests. Section P remains open.

## Evidence reviewed

- [Metro Québec](https://www.metro.ca/epicerie-en-ligne/allees/collations/collations-salees/bretzels-batonnets-au-fromage-et-amuse-gueules/craquelins-de-crevettes/p/031146200122) and [Poissonnerie Les Trois Pêcheurs](https://www.troispecheurs.fish/produit/chips-aux-crevettes/) use different Québec French product titles; the fishmonger's listed bag sizes conflict on the page.
- [Nongshim USA](https://nongshimusa.com/product-detail?pid=53) identifies its product as a snack with a SKU-specific formulation. [Holland Store Toronto](https://hollandstore.ca/products/flower-kerupuk-small) labels a particular Flower product “Un-fried Shrimp Crackers.”
- The Canadian Food Inspection Agency's [2025 recall notice](https://recalls-rappels.canada.ca/en/alert-recall/various-brands-crackers-recalled-due-undeclared-egg) names one recalled Ny.Siok Shrimp Crackers SKU; it is historical recall evidence, not current availability.
