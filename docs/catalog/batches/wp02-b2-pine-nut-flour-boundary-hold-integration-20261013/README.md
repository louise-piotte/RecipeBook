# WP02 B2 pine-nut flour boundary hold — 2026-10-13

Integrated the [round-69 pine-nut flour review](../../proposals/wp02-b2-pine-nut-flour-boundary-review-20261013-round69.md).

## Decision

Keep generic pine-nut flour unallocated. Current product descriptions use the phrase across
different pine sources and processes, and the Québec-facing French flour name is not established
for a matching product scope. The Canadian seller's page says its nuts are pressed and ground but
does not specify defatting or press-cake use. Keep whole pine nuts and pine-nut butter separate;
add no flour alias to either.

The recipe corpus has no flour demand. Product pack sizes and study results do not establish a
household measure, density, yield, conversion, or substitution. Revisit when a product source
establishes the material and process and a Québec-facing source names that same flour.

No catalog, form, legacy-candidate, or research-ledger data changed. No tests or emulator checks
were run; this is a documentation-only hold.

WP02 B2/B3 remains open.
