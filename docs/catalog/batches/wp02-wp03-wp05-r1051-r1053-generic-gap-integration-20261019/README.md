# WP02/WP03/WP05 R1051–R1053 generic-gap integration

**Date:** 2026-10-19
**Scope:** Generic grain milling, edible flowers, and regional preserved foods.

## Decisions

- **R1051 — hold common buckwheat bran flour.** Milling studies identify crop-specific bran-flour fractions, but the reviewed human-food evidence does not establish that those samples are a transferable culinary grade of common-buckwheat bran flour. Keep the candidate out of active references and the ledger; preserve generic buckwheat bran and Tartary-buckwheat bran flour as separate existing identities, with all sources and claims unchanged. The Anson Mills wheat-meal lead remains out of scope as a commercial-only item.
- **R1052 — add common lilac flowers / fleurs du lilas commun.** Add one identity-only reference bounded to flowers of *Syringa vulgaris*. Keep the French compound descriptive and provisional, aliases empty, and flower structure unspecified. The Télé-Québec flower-to-alcohol amount remains specific to its infusion recipe; no reusable measurement, density, form, recipe link, or substitution was added.
- **R1053 — add Bettara-zuke (Tokyo rice-koji daikon pickle) / bettara-zuke (radis daïkon de Tokyo mariné au koji de riz).** Keep this named Tokyo preserve distinct from raw daikon, takuan, generic pickled radish, and the broader koji-zuke class. The French gloss remains provisional. MAFF timings and preparation notes stay source-scoped; no forms, measures, recipe links, conversions, yields, or active substitutions were added.

## Cross-category commercial scope

The assigned work selected only generic ingredient identities or reviewed a generic ingredient boundary. It pursued no brand, product-line, flavour-family, SKU, retailer-variant, or commercial roster. A scan of all unchecked plan lines matching brand, product-line, flavour, SKU, retailer, and manufacturer terms found no commercial-only deliverable; remaining matches are generic flavour/extract identities or evidence, measurement, and source rules. The exact-spec allulose status gate remains separate and untouched. The previously dispositioned Section F commercial cereal rosters and named Anson Mills, Nu Life Market, Dempsey, and Mochiko/Shiratamako tasks remain out of scope because adding another commercial product does not add ingredient diversity. Preserve their existing rows and product-scoped evidence. Manufacturer evidence may support a generic claim when transferable; product-specific claims and measurements remain limited to their named product or source.

No existing reference was deleted, merged, or relabeled. No existing source row was reused or broadened. The Colorado State Extension edible-flower page and NIBN classification appendix retain their prior source IDs, locators, owners, and claims; R1052 and R1053 received separate source IDs with identity-specific locators. R1051’s held candidate introduced no data or source-owner changes.
