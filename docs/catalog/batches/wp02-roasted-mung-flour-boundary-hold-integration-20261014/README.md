# WP02 R113: roasted-mung-flour boundary hold

## Disposition

Keep Niru's `Roasted Mong Flour` outside the active reference catalog. The Canadian manufacturer
describes it as both a “very fine grain starch” and “this flour,” but does not establish a botanical
species, whole-bean flour versus extracted starch, roasting and milling order, or French product name.
Keep the existing `candidate-roasted-mong-flour` ledger record separate from the generic
`candidate-mung-bean-flour` candidate, the active mung-bean identity, and the active mung-bean-starch
identity. No catalog or ledger data changed.

Niru lists `Roasted Mung` separately and describes that item as roasted mung beans. Walmart Canada's
PTI Moong Flour listing describes a different, split/dehulled, roasted, ground product; that
product-specific process does not resolve Niru's listing. OQLF uses `haricot mungo` for both
*Vigna radiata* and *V. mungo*, while no source establishes a Québec French name for Niru's product.
No alias, form, measure, conversion, yield, recipe link, or substitution was added. The 114-recipe /
1,123-row corpus scan found no matching text or direct links.

## Evidence

- Niru's [Roasted Mong Flour page](https://niru.com/product/roasted-mong-flour/) describes a fine-grain starch made from mong beans and calls it flour, without specifying species or processing sequence.
- Niru's separate [Roasted Mung page](https://niru.com/product/roasted-mung/) calls that product roasted mung beans.
- Walmart Canada's [PTI Moong Flour page](https://www.walmart.ca/en/ip/PTI-Moong-Flour/6000196867429) describes split green gram with the seed coat removed, roasted, and ground. This is one product-specific listing.
- OQLF identifies *Vigna radiata* under [haricot mungo](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/2082840/haricot-mungo) and notes that the same French term also names *Vigna mungo* in its [second entry](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/2082823/haricot-mungo).

contains the full source register, product ownership and picker review, recipe scan, and reopen criteria.
