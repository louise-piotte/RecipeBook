# WP04 Section P clam product-state holds (2026-10-13)

## Decision

Keep Clover Leaf clam juice and whole yellow baby clams in water outside the active catalog and
forms. Preserve the existing unresolved `ingredient-ref-clam` ledger candidate; add no duplicate
candidate or source records. The French manufacturer and Québec retailer pages establish the two
product names and product-specific package/serving observations. Activating either by itself would
make broad `clam` and `palourde` searches return that specialized product as a singleton, while the
bundled recipes contain no clam product request.

Keep the manufacturer's 1 tbsp (15 mL) clam-juice portion and 1/4 cup drained (55 g) baby-clam
serving scoped to those products. The 236 mL and 142 g package quantities are not recipe measures or
general conversions. Add no active identity, alias, form, density, unit mapping, measure, conversion,
yield, recipe link, or substitution.

The full product boundary, species/name limits, picker projection, and source register are in the
[round-70 proposal](../../proposals/wp04-section-p-clam-product-state-boundary-review-20261013-round70.md).
This documentation-only review makes no catalog or ledger changes and needs no app tests or emulator
checks.

Section P remains open.
