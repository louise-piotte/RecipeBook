# WP04 Section P Arctic wedge clam picker hold — 2026-10-13

## Decision

Keep Arctic wedge clam / `clovisse arctique` (*Mesodesma arctatum*) outside the active ingredient
catalog and research ledger. Fisheries and Oceans Canada lists the bilingual species names and taxon
among Canadian harvested bivalves. Québec seafood sources also establish a food and occasional local
commercial-fishing context. They do not resolve the generic clam identity or create recipe demand.

The picker has no active generic clam result. Adding this one species would make broad `clam` and
`clovisse` searches return it alone. The current 114-recipe bundle has 1,123 ingredient rows and no
clam or candidate mentions. Preserve the unresolved generic-clam candidate. Add no active reference,
alias, form, ledger candidate, recipe link, measurement, conversion, yield, or substitution. Revisit
when a recipe or exact product request selects this species, or picker behavior can preserve multiple
appropriate clam choices.

## Sources

- [Fisheries and Oceans Canada — Identifying bivalve shellfish](https://www.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/shellfish-mollusques/identification-eng.htm) and [Identification des mollusques bivalves](https://www.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/shellfish-mollusques/identification-fra.htm): pair Arctic wedge clam / `clovisse arctique` with *M. arctatum* and describe it among harvested clams.
- [Government of Canada — Known concentration areas of Arctic wedge clam](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/b0633101-18e6-483a-b668-4e1f76789696?wbdisable=true): maps known harvested or unharvested Québec concentrations using data through 2020; it does not show the full species distribution or where fishing is permitted. The record was modified in March 2026.
- [De l’eau à la bouche — Clovisse arctique](https://deleaualabouche.ca/especes/clovisse-arctique/): gives the local food context, generic kitchen wording `palourde`, resource status as undetermined, and intermittent commercial fishing in the Moyenne-Côte-Nord.
- [Exploramer — Clovisse arctique / Fourchette bleue](https://fourchettebleue.ca/produits/clovisse-arctique/): current species/product information with the same taxon and occasional-fishery context; it is not a vendor SKU.
