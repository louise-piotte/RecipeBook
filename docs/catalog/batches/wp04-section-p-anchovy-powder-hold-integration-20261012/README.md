# WP04 Section P anchovy powder product-boundary hold

## Decision

Keep generic `anchovy powder / poudre d’anchois` unallocated as a reference, alias, or form. Korean
culinary sources describe pure ground anchovy used for broth, while current Canadian prepared-food
ingredient lists use the same unqualified phrase for an anchovy-extract, maltodextrin, and salt
blend. No reviewed Canadian or Québec standalone powder label resolves the product scope, and no
bundled recipe requests anchovy powder.

The existing `anchovy / anchois`, anchovy-paste, and source-scoped Bonga dried-fish-powder identities
remain unchanged. Fish balls and surimi remain separate open work. No catalog, ledger, recipe,
generated, measurement, form, or substitution data changed. The new source IDs remain proposal-local.
See the [source review](../../proposals/wp04-section-p-anchovy-powder-product-boundary-review-20261012.md)
for the exact-name audit and source limits.

## Reconciliation

- KBS World records the Korean spelling `멸치 가루` and explains that anchovy and other powders are
  sold for convenient broth-making; a separate cooking source describes 100% anchovy powder.
- Walmart Canada and Costco Canada list anchovy powder inside Caesar-dressing ingredient panels as
  maltodextrin, anchovy extract, and salt. Those prepared-food statements do not establish a
  standalone powder SKU or equivalence to ground whole anchovy.
- No matching phrase appears in the 114 manifest-listed recipes or 1,123 ingredient rows.
- Keep the fish-powder / fish-balls / surimi checklist open.
- No tests or emulator checks were run because this is a documentation-only hold.
