# WP04 Section P R649 integration — Blue Whiting boundary hold

## Decision

Held *Micromesistius poutassou* outside active references and the research ledger. CFIA assigns the exact English label `Blue Whiting` to both *M. poutassou* and *M. australis*. It pairs *M. poutassou* with `Merlan` and also lists `Whiting / Merlan Bleu`; for *M. australis* it lists `Blue Whiting / Morue Bleue`, `Southern Blue Whiting`, and `Blue Cod / Merlan Bleu Austral`. CFIA’s common names are Canadian fish-labelling nomenclature, not evidence that the taxa are interchangeable.

OQLF’s edible-fish entry `merlan` identifies *Merlangius merlangus*, and Usito distinguishes `merlan bleu` from `merlan bleu austral`. Montréal-based Lagoon Seafood lists frozen `Morue Bleue ... (Merlan bleu Austral)` products. Together, the sources do not establish a safe single-species bilingual picker row for *M. poutassou*. A hypothetical row would make the shared `blue whiting` term a singleton result for one taxon and would also cross the Québec French name boundaries. The 114-recipe corpus has no matching ingredient demand.

No catalog or ledger data changed. No aliases, forms, measures, recipe links, conversions, yields, or substitutions were added. R641 Bowfin and R645 Blue Runner remain unchanged; Section P's broader finfish item remains open.

## Review updates and checks

- Marked the R649 boundary review complete in the Section P checklist and progress log.
- Updated the active roadmap and catalog workflow.
- Reviewed CFIA's Fish List, the OQLF and Usito entries, and Lagoon Seafood's product catalogue; verified the name and taxonomy collision.
- The proposal's static picker projection and 114-recipe / 1,123-row scan found no candidate demand. No software tests, build, emulator run, or deployment were needed for this documentation-only hold.
