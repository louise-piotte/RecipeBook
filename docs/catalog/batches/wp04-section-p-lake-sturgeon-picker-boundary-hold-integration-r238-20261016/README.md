# WP04 Section P R238 lake-sturgeon picker-boundary hold (2026-10-16)

## Decision

Keep lake sturgeon (*Acipenser fulvescens*) / `esturgeon jaune` outside active references, forms, legacy mappings, and the research ledger. OQLF supports the Québec-preferred edible-fish term and its English counterpart. The CFIA Fish List uses unqualified `sturgeon` / `esturgeon` for several taxa; adding one species row would make those broad searches appear resolved to a single taxon. The reviewed Québec seller listings do not identify their flesh by binomial, and the bundled recipe corpus has no matching candidate demand. No active measures, forms, mappings, recipe links, conversions, yields, or substitutions were added.

The review found zero matching ingredient rows in the 114-recipe / 1,123-row bundle. The Québec government page identifies *A. fulvescens* and its St. Lawrence commercial fishery. SIMMER and Capital Fish offer Québec sturgeon flesh but do not state the species on the reviewed listings. A generic Québec recipe does not supply app-bundle demand or species-specific substitution evidence.

This integration is documentation-only. No catalog or research-ledger data changed; no software tests or emulator checks were needed. The regional-fish checklist remains open.
