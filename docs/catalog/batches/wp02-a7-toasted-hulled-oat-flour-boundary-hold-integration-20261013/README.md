# WP02 A7 toasted hulled oat flour boundary hold

## Decision

Integrated the R48 review as an evidence-only hold. Keep CerealVeneta's `Toasted hulled oat flour / Farine d’avoine décortiquée toastée` outside the active catalog. Its manufacturer pages describe a semi-finished product that is gently heat-treated and milled with liquid nitrogen, but do not establish Canadian or Québec availability or a Québec product display. OQLF supports the base name `farine d’avoine`, not this process-specific product.

Keep this candidate separate from generic oat flour, Castle Oat 80 roasted oat malt flour, and the distinct Moulins thermal-grade naming conflict. The recipe bundle has no oat-flour demand; the public manufacturer page gives no transferable measure, density, process setting, or substitution.

## Evidence and limits

- CerealVeneta's [English product page](https://cerealveneta.it/en/semi-finished/toasted-hulled-oat-flour/) names the product and says it is gently heat-treated and milled with liquid nitrogen. The manufacturer describes it as a semi-finished product for bakery and vegetable-drink applications; its product sheet requires company and email details.
- The manufacturer's [French page](https://cerealveneta.it/fr/semi-finis/farine-davoine-decortiquee-toastee/) supplies source-specific French wording but is not a Québec consumer label or terminology ruling. OQLF's [farine d’avoine entry](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/2497598/farine-davoine) supports the base term only.
- The proposal keeps this product separate from Les Moulins de Soulanges' pregelatinized/roasted wording conflict and Castle Oat 80 roasted oat malt flour. The full 114-recipe / 1,123-row bundle scan found no candidate-phrase hits or oat-flour recipe line. No exact-form measure, density, mapping, conversion, yield, or substitution is supported.
