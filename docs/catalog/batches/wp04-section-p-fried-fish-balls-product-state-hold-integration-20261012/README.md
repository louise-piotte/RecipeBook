# WP04 Section P fried fish-ball product-state hold

## Decision

Keep the live `fish balls / boulettes de poisson` identity unchanged. Do not add `fried fish balls` or
`boulettes de poisson frites` as aliases, a separate identity, or a form. Canadian listings show
assorted and filled variants, while a Toronto manufacturer lists plain, crab, fried, and crab-flavoured
fish balls with varied ingredients. These sources do not establish one Québec French label, shared
formulation, species, universal cooked state, or recipe use.

The 114-recipe corpus has no fish-ball request. No measure, piece count, density, yield, conversion,
recipe link, or substitution is supported. Actual crab meat, surimi paste, surimi shreds, fish cakes,
croquettes, shrimp balls, and squid balls remain separate.

See the [source review](../../proposals/wp04-section-p-fried-fish-balls-product-state-review-20261012.md)
for exact-name ownership, source limits, and the recipe scan. Metro Canada illustrates an English/French
generic frozen product name; Ocean Food lists its fried variants while explicitly describing fish balls
as a varied family.
