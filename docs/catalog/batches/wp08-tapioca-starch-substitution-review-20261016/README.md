# WP08 tapioca-starch substitution review — 2026-10-16

## Decision

Recorded one directional, qualitative alternative from `tapioca starch / fécule de manioc` to cornstarch when tapioca starch is used only to thicken a cooked liquid. Bob's Red Mill says cornstarch can work in this role, should be added at the beginning rather than the later stage used for tapioca, and produces a matte rather than glossy finish. It gives no replacement amount or ratio. [Bob's Red Mill](https://www.bobsredmill.com/articles/5-tapioca-flour-substitutes-to-test-out)

The ledger retains the substitution research as evidence only. It adds no `SubstitutionRule`, recipe-line link, amount, mapping, or conversion. `substitutionResearchState` is `researched_supported` for this bounded finding; measurement research stays `not_yet_researched`.

Iowa State Extension distinguishes tapioca pearls, powders/flakes, and instant/minute granules, and says the forms are not interchangeable. Its 2 tablespoons tapioca powder per 1 tablespoon cornstarch statement is for the opposite direction; the separate 1:1 statement concerns instant tapioca. Neither ratio was reversed or transferred. King Arthur's fruit-pie chart uses quick-cooking tapioca with fruit-specific quantities; those data do not establish a substitution using extracted tapioca starch. [Iowa State Extension](https://blogs.extension.iastate.edu/answerline/tag/cornstarch-substitution/), [King Arthur Baking](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/learn/guides/pie-thickener)
