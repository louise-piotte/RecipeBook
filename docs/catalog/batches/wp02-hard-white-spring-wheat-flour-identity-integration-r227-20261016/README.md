# WP02 Section A R227 — hard white spring wheat flour

Added identity-only `hard white spring wheat flour / farine de blé de force blanc de printemps` as `ingredient-ref-hard-white-spring-wheat-flour`, using Rogers Foods' current White Peaks product as the source-specific product evidence. The French display remains a transparent construction from Canadian Grain Commission and OQLF wording; independent Québec review remains open.

The new row has no aliases, forms, default density, unit mappings, conversions, package yields, recipe links, or active substitutions. It stays separate from hard-white-spring berries, white whole-wheat flour, generic all-purpose flour, hard-red-spring flour, and the formal CWHWS class. The Charlevoix French phrase is kept out of aliases because its technical sheet describes a blend of white spring and red autumn wheat. Rogers' 20 kg package and product specifications remain product-specific.

The bundled corpus has 114 recipes and 1,123 ingredient rows; no candidate phrase or ingredient reference match was found. No recipe was linked or changed.

## Sources

- [Rogers Foods, White Peaks](https://rogersfoods.com/shop/commercial-products/white-flours/white-peaks/): Canadian flour product explicitly made from hard white spring wheat; manufacturer calls this product all-purpose. It does not identify a formal CGC class or wheat variety.
- [Canadian Grain Commission, Canadian wheat classes](https://www.grainscanada.gc.ca/en/grain-quality/grain-grading/wheat-classes.html): hard-white-spring class boundary and stated uses; it does not assign Rogers' product to that formal class.
- [Canadian Grain Commission, Blé : Classes et variétés](https://www.grainscanada.gc.ca/fr/qualite-grains/guide-officiel-classement-grains/04-ble/classes-varietes.html): official French class wording used to construct the provisional display.
- [OQLF, farine de blé de force](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/8357376/farine-de-ble-de-force): Québec French base term; the OQLF does not attest the complete white-spring compound.
- [Moulin de Charlevoix BBCT20 technical sheet](https://mayrand.ca/hubfs/13690%20Farine%20Bl%C3%A9%20Conventionnel%20Tradition.pdf): local phrase and blend-boundary evidence only; the sheet describes white spring wheat milled together with red autumn wheat.
